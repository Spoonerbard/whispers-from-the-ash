# **The Adventuring License**

When the party first arrives in Salvation, Sheriff Karr will approach them, introduce himself, and demand to see their "[[The Adventuring License]]." This is a formality, a way for House Tharashk to keep track of who is operating in their territory and to establish their authority.

- **The License:** The license is a simple document, stamped with the seal of [[House Tharashk]], that grants the bearer permission to explore [[the Mournland]] and engage in "salvage operations" (i.e., looting).
- **Requirements:** To obtain a license, the party must:
    - Register their names and a brief description of their abilities.
    - Pay a small fee (e.g., 5 gp per person).
    - Agree to abide by the laws of Salvation and to respect House Tharashk's authority.
    - Promise to report any significant discoveries or encounters to the Sheriff.
- **Consequences of Not Having a License:** Operating without a license could lead to fines, imprisonment, or even expulsion from Salvation. Sheriff Karr might be lenient on a first offense, especially if the party seems capable and willing to cooperate.
- **Opportunity for Roleplaying:** This interaction provides an opportunity for the players to introduce their characters, establish their motivations, and get a feel for the town's power dynamics.
