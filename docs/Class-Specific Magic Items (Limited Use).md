#rewards 


**1. Rogue:**

- **Item:** [[Shadow Band]]
- **Description:** A simple, dark leather band worn on the wrist, etched with subtle runes.
- **Effect:** Once per day, the rogue can use an action to become invisible, as per the _invisibility_ spell, for up to 1 minute. The invisibility ends if the rogue attacks, casts a spell, or takes any other action besides moving, hiding or searching. The band is clearly visible, even when the player is not.
- **Flavor:** "Forged in the shadows, for those who walk between them."

**2. Barbarian:**

- **Item:** [[Flameheart Ring]]
- **Description:** A thick, iron ring set with a rough, uncut fire opal that glimmers with inner heat.
- **Effect:** Once per day, as a bonus action while raging, the barbarian can activate the ring to gain resistance to fire damage until the end of their next turn. While this is in effect, they can also imbue their melee weapon attacks with an additional 1d4 fire damage, but also take 1 fire damage themselves at the end of each of their turns.
- **Flavor:** "A spark of the inferno, bound to your will."

**3. Wizard:**

- **Item:** [[Scribe's Quill of Recall]]
- **Description:** An ordinary-looking quill pen crafted from raven feather and bone.
- **Effect:** Once per day, the wizard can use the quill to instantly transcribe a spell they are casting into their spellbook without expending the usual time or gold required, as long as they have the materials on hand. The spell must be of a level they can normally cast.
- **Flavor:** "Knowledge is fleeting, but this quill remembers."

**4. Druid:**

- **Item:** [[Seed of the Whispering Woods]]
- **Description:** A small, smooth seed that pulses with a faint green light and whispers faintly when held to the ear.
- **Effect:** Once per day, the druid can plant the seed in the ground and speak a command word to cause a small tree to sprout rapidly. The tree acts as per the spell _speak with plants_ for up to 10 minutes, allowing the druid to communicate with nearby plants. The seed is then consumed.
- **Flavor:** "Nature's secrets, whispered on the wind, heard by those who listen."

**6. Undecided Class:** Here are a few options, depending on what the final player chooses:

- **Paladin/Cleric:**
    - **Item:** Acolyte's Censer
    - **Description:** A small, brass censer that hangs from a chain.
    - **Effect:** Once per day, the paladin or cleric can use an action to light the incense within the censer (using a special, inexpensive incense). For 1 minute, they and their allies within 10 feet have advantage on saving throws against fear and charm effects. The incense can be found in settlements or crafted with a DC 10 Wisdom (Nature) check.
    - **Flavor:** "Faith's flame burns bright, banishing doubt and fear."
- **Fighter/Monk:**
    - **Item:** [[Veteran's Sparring Gloves]]
    - **Description:** A pair of well-worn leather gloves, reinforced with metal studs.
    - **Effect:** Once per day, when the fighter or monk makes a melee weapon attack and misses, they can use a bonus action to make one unarmed strike against the same target.
    - **Flavor:** "Even in defeat, a lesson is learned."
- **Bard:**
    - **Item:** [[Minstrel's Tuning Fork]]
    - **Description:** A small, silver tuning fork that hums faintly when struck.
    - **Effect:** Once per day, when the bard casts a spell that requires a saving throw, they can strike the tuning fork as part of the action. One target of the spell has disadvantage on their first saving throw against it.
    - **Flavor:** "The perfect note, to strike the heart's true chord."
- **Ranger:**
    - **Item:** Pathfinder's Arrowhead
    - **Description:** An arrowhead crafted from a strange, green-glowing stone found in [[the Mournland]].
    - **Effect:** Once per day, the ranger can nock this arrowhead onto an arrow and fire it at the ground. The arrowhead will magically point towards the direction of the nearest source of potable water within a 1-mile radius. The arrowhead is then consumed, and must be recrafted.
    - **Flavor:** "Nature's guidance, a beacon in the wild."
- **Artificer:**
    - **Item:** [[Tinker's Multitool]]
    - **Description:** A compact set of tools that can magically reconfigure itself into various shapes.
    - **Effect:** Once per day, the artificer can use the multitool to gain proficiency in any one tool for 1 hour.
    - **Flavor:** "Necessity is the mother of invention, and this tool is its child."

**Important Notes:**

- **Power Level:** These items are intentionally designed to be relatively low-powered, providing useful but limited benefits. They are meant to be "budget" magic items, reflecting the scarcity of resources in Sanctuary.
- **Upgrade Potential:** As the campaign progresses, these items could be upgraded or modified using the modular crafting system. For example, the barbarian's ring could eventually grant full immunity to fire damage, or the wizard's quill could be used to transcribe higher-level spells.
- **Flavor and Roleplaying:** Encourage players to embrace the flavor of these items and incorporate them into their roleplaying.
- **Balance:** Monitor how these items are being used in play and adjust their effects if necessary to maintain balance.
