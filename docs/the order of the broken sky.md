**The Order of the Broken Sky: Seekers of the Shattered Truth**

**Overview:**

- **Purpose:** The Order of the Broken Sky is a secretive society dedicated to uncovering the truth behind the Mourning, understanding the magical forces that caused it, and potentially finding a way to heal the land or prevent a similar catastrophe from happening again.
- **Methodology:** They are scholars, arcanists, and explorers, meticulously gathering information, studying artifacts, and venturing into [[the Mournland]]] to conduct research. They value knowledge above all else, but they are not afraid to use force to protect their secrets or achieve their goals.
- **Structure:** The Order is organized in a hierarchical structure, with a council of elders or archons at the top, various ranks of researchers and agents, and a network of informants and contacts throughout Eberron.
- **Secrecy:** They operate in the shadows, as their research is often considered dangerous or heretical by established institutions. They are wary of outsiders and fiercely protective of their knowledge. They will often communicate in code or through intermediaries. Their members are located across the continent, but their operations are more concentrated around [[the Mournland]], and they likely have a hidden base of operations somewhere within or near it.
- **Moral Ambiguity:** The Order is not inherently good or evil. They are driven by a thirst for knowledge and a desire to prevent another Mourning, but their methods can be ruthless, and their pursuit of power could lead them down a dangerous path. They might see themselves as the only ones capable of understanding the true nature of the Mourning and therefore justified in taking any measures necessary to achieve their goals.

**Notable NPCs:**

1. **Vaelia Moonwhisper (Leader):**
    
    - **Appearance:** An elderly human woman with long, silver hair, piercing blue eyes, and a calm, almost ethereal demeanor. She often wears simple, grey robes adorned with celestial patterns. She is never seen without a staff made from an unknown, dark metal that hums faintly with power.
    - **Personality:** Vaelia is a powerful archmage and a skilled diviner. She is wise, patient, and deeply knowledgeable about the Mourning, but also ruthless and secretive. She believes that the Order is the only hope for understanding and potentially reversing the Mourning's effects. She is the one who foresaw the importance of the Sky Song Tavern.
    - **Motivation:** To uncover the complete truth behind the Mourning, to prevent another such catastrophe, and to secure the Order's position as the foremost authority on arcane knowledge.
    - **Secret:** Vaelia was once a member of a different organization that was involved in the events leading up to the Mourning (possibly even the cabal that performed the "Final Sanction," or a precursor to it). She carries the guilt and burden of that past, which fuels her current actions. She might have been the one that gave the warrior the weapon to give to Gyda.
    - **Quote:** "The Mourning shattered more than just a nation. It broke the sky itself. We are the ones who must pick up the pieces, no matter the cost."
2. **Kaelen "The Raven" Rhen (Field Agent):**
    
    - **Appearance:** A young, agile half-elf with dark hair often tied back in a braid, sharp features, and a haunted look in his eyes. He favors dark clothing and carries a variety of tools and weapons, including a modified hand crossbow and several daggers. He has a tattoo of a raven on his left forearm.
    - **Personality:** Kaelen is a skilled scout, infiltrator, and information gatherer for the Order. He is cynical and distrustful of outsiders but fiercely loyal to Vaelia and the Order's goals. He is haunted by something he witnessed in [[the Mournland]].
    - **Motivation:** To carry out Vaelia's orders and gather the information the Order needs. He is also secretly searching for a way to atone for a past mistake. He is often tasked with observing the party and reporting on their actions.
    - **Secret:** Kaelen was once a member of a different faction (possibly a Cyran loyalist group or a band of mercenaries) that was wiped out in [[the Mournland]]. He blames himself for their deaths and carries a deep-seated fear of the wasteland.
    - **Quote:** "[[the Mournland]] whispers secrets to those who know how to listen. Most of them are lies, but some... some are terrifyingly true."
3. **Archivist Theron (Scholar):**
    
    - **Appearance:** A middle-aged gnome with spectacles perched on his nose, ink-stained fingers, and a perpetually distracted air. He is usually surrounded by stacks of books, scrolls, and strange artifacts.
    - **Personality:** Theron is a brilliant but eccentric scholar obsessed with cataloging and understanding pre-Mourning magic. He is the Order's chief archivist and spends most of his time in their hidden library, poring over ancient texts and গবেষণাাগার notes. He is often oblivious to social cues and can be difficult to communicate with.
    - **Motivation:** To acquire and preserve as much knowledge as possible, particularly about the magic that existed before the Mourning. He believes that this knowledge is the key to understanding and potentially healing [[the Mournland]].
    - **Secret:** Theron is secretly experimenting with Croydonite, believing it holds the key to unlocking lost magical techniques. He is aware of the dangers but is driven by his insatiable curiosity. He hides this from the rest of the order, but Vaelia is beginning to suspect.
    - **Quote:** "The past is not dead, it's merely buried. And within these pages... within these artifacts... lie the keys to understanding our present, and perhaps even shaping our future."

**Relationship to the Plot:**

- **Guardians of Knowledge:** The Order acts as both a source of information and a potential obstacle for the players. They possess a great deal of knowledge about [[the Mournland]], the Mourning, and the events leading up to it, but they are not always willing to share it freely.
- **Interested in the Sky Song Tavern:** Vaelia's vision has led the Order to believe that the Sky Song Tavern holds a key to understanding the Mourning, perhaps through the Singing Stones, Fizzwick's research, or some other hidden secret within the ruin.
- **Monitoring the Party:** The Order will likely monitor the party's progress, especially after they learn of their involvement with the Sky Song Tavern and their discoveries related to Croydonite.
- **Potential Allies:** If the party's goals align with the Order's, they might find them to be valuable allies, gaining access to their knowledge, resources, and contacts.
- **Potential Antagonists:** If the party's actions threaten the Order's secrets or their plans, they could become dangerous adversaries. The Order might try to manipulate the party, steal their discoveries, or even eliminate them if they are deemed a threat.
- **Moral Grey Area:** The Order's methods and ultimate goals are not clearly defined as good or evil. This allows for complex interactions and moral dilemmas for the players.

**Important Historical Items and Knowledge:**

- **[[The Final Sanction]]:** The Order knows about the "Final Sanction," the desperate ritual used to close the eldritch portal. They might be searching for information about the specific magic used, the identities of the cabal members, or the location of any surviving artifacts related to the ritual.
- **The Disciples of the Unwritten Name:** The Order is aware of this proto-sect of the Becoming God cult and their role in the events leading up to the Mourning. They might be searching for the cult's hidden bases, research notes, or any surviving members.
- **The Progenitor Dragon Bone:** The Order knows about the bone's involvement and is likely searching for its current location (or its remnants, if it was destroyed). They might believe it holds the key to understanding the Mourning or even reversing its effects. They are very interested in the properties of Croydonite, and what it reveals about the nature of the bone.
- **Pre-Mourning Magic:** The Order is obsessed with recovering lost magical knowledge from before the Mourning. They believe that this knowledge is essential to understanding the present and preventing future catastrophes. They would be very interested in studying any of Fizwick's magical creations, especially his notes.
- **The Nature of Croydonite:** The Order is actively researching Croydonite, trying to understand its properties, its dangers, and its potential uses. They are likely aware of the soul fragments trapped within it, though their opinion on this matter might be divided.

**Possible Plot Hooks Involving the Order:**

- **The Stolen Artifact:** The Order contacts the party, claiming that a valuable artifact related to the Mourning has been stolen from their archives. They offer a reward for its return and might reveal some information about the artifact's significance in the process.
- **The Race to the Ruin:** The Order learns of a newly discovered ruin in [[the Mournland]]] that might contain information about the Disciples of the Unwritten Name. They race against the party (and potentially other factions) to reach the ruin first.
- **The Prisoner Exchange:** The Order has captured a member of a rival faction who possesses knowledge about the Mourning. They offer to exchange the prisoner for something the party has found, such as a piece of [[Croydonite]] or a specific artifact.
- **The Secret Mission:** Vaelia Moonwhisper contacts the party directly, offering them a dangerous but potentially rewarding mission into the heart of [[the Mournland]]. She might reveal some of her secrets to gain their trust, but she will always keep her true motives hidden.
