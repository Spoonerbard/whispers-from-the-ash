**Shadow's Embrace (Revised - Tier 1)**

**Description:**

- **Appearance:** The rapier has a slender, elegant blade. The top third of the blade is made of a dark, matte metal that seems to absorb light. The lower two thirds are made from a strange, black material that is as hard and sharp as steel, but has a slightly porous, almost bone like texture, this is the Croydonite, but the players won't know that. The hilt is wrapped in worn, black leather, and a single, tear-shaped piece of polished obsidian is set in the pommel. It hums faintly when drawn, and the air around it seems to grow slightly colder.
- **Aesthetic:** The weapon retains its sleek and menacing design, reflecting Morticia Addams' aesthetic. The two different materials will make it stand out.

**Base Stats:**

- **Type:** Rapier (can change form as Gyda's pact weapon)
- **Damage:** 1d8 piercing damage
- **Properties:** Finesse, Light

**Unique Properties:**

1. **Shadowfell's Chill:**
    
    - **Effect:** Once per day, [[Gyda]]a]]can imbue the blade with the chilling energy of the Shadowfell. The next successful attack with the weapon deals an additional 1d4 cold damage, and the target must make a Constitution saving throw (DC equal to Gyda's spell save DC). On a failed save, the target's speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of their next turn.
    - **Flavor:** "The cold grip of the Shadowfell, a reminder of what you left behind."
2. **Whispers of the Past:**
    
    - **Effect:** When [[Gyda]]a]]a]]a]]a]]a]]deals a critical hit with the weapon, she hears a brief, fragmented whisper from the soul of someone slain by the cult or her patron. This whisper might offer a cryptic clue about her patron, the cult, or a way to break her pact. It could also be a plea for help or a warning. This whisper is only audible to Gyda.
    - **Flavor:** "The echoes of lost souls, trapped between worlds, seeking solace or revenge."
3. **Tethered to the Shadowfell (Drawback):**
    
    - **Effect:** While attuned to this weapon, [[Gyda]]has disadvantage on saving throws against effects that would banish her or send her to another plane of existence. Additionally, if she spends more than 24 hours outside the Shadowfell without using the weapon, she gains a level of exhaustion that can only be removed by using the weapon to successfully deal damage to a creature or by returning to the Shadowfell for at least an hour.
    - **Flavor:** "A lingering connection to the darkness, a price for the power you wield."

**Croydonite Integration:**

- **Hidden Nature:** The lower portion of the blade being made from [[Croydonite]] is not immediately apparent. The material is described as a strange, black, bone-like substance.
- **Later Revelation:** The players will learn the truth about the blade's composition later in the campaign, perhaps through:
    - **[[Troxler]]'s Analysis:** As [[Troxler]]'s knowledge grows, he might be able to identify the material.
    - **Research in the Order's Archives:** If the players gain access to the Order of the Broken Sky's research, they might find information about [[Croydonite]] and its properties.
    - **Psychic Visions:** [[Gyda]]might experience visions or dreams triggered by the weapon, revealing its connection to the Mourning and the trapped souls.
- **Implications:** The revelation that Gyda's weapon is partially made from [[Croydonite]] will raise ethical questions and potentially lead to conflict or difficult choices.

**Upgrade Paths:**

The upgrade paths remain largely the same as before (Path of Redemption, Path of Shadow, Path of Balance), but the specific upgrades might be adjusted to reflect the weapon's slightly reduced power at Tier 1.

- **Example: Path of Redemption (Tier 2):** Instead of casting _lesser restoration_, [[Gyda]]might gain the ability to cast _protection from evil and good_ once per day, using the weapon as a focus. The cold damage from the weapon could also increase when used against fiends and undead.
- **Example: Path of Shadow (Tier 2):** The cold damage from Shadowfell's Chill could increase to 2d4 and it can now be used twice per day.
- **Focus on The whispers:** You could also place more emphasis on the "Whispers of the Past" ability in the early tiers, making it a more reliable source of information or giving [[Gyda]]more control over the whispers she hears.

**Why This Change?**

- **Tier Appropriateness:** Removing the initial magical damage makes the weapon more appropriate for low-level play, preventing it from being overpowered.
- **Mystery and Intrigue:** The unknown composition of the blade adds an element of mystery and encourages investigation.
- **Delayed Ethical Dilemma:** Delaying the [[Croydonite]] revelation allows the players to become attached to the weapon before confronting the moral implications of its nature.
- **Storytelling Potential:** The gradual discovery of the weapon's secrets creates a more compelling narrative arc.
