**The Lurker of the Stillwater: A Goldfish Gone Ghastly**

**Lore:**

- **Origin:** Once a prized pet goldfish, kept in a wealthy Cyran's estate, the Lurker was transformed by the Mourning into a monstrous predator. The time-accelerated magic warped its body, amplified its predatory instincts, and granted it the bizarre ability to survive, and even thrive, out of water. It has spent the last century terrorising the lake, and anything that comes near it.
- **Nature:** The Lurker is a solitary creature, driven by a ravenous hunger and a territorial instinct. It's not particularly intelligent but is cunning and opportunistic, using its unique abilities to ambush and overwhelm its prey. It has an instinctive hatred for anything that enters it's lake.
- **Appearance:** The Lurker still retains some features of its goldfish origins, but grotesquely distorted. It's now the size of a horse, with a thick, muscular body covered in shimmering, golden scales that have hardened into armor plating. Its fins have become ragged, leathery appendages, and its once-innocuous mouth is now a বিশাল maw filled with rows of razor-sharp teeth. Its eyes are large and black, with a predatory gleam. Most notably, it has developed two large, inflatable air bladders on either side of its body, which it uses to propel itself out of the water and briefly "fly" across land. These bladders are a pale, sickly green color and are covered in thin, translucent veins. It has developed a powerful tail, that it uses to propel itself through the water.
- **Habitat:** The Lurker makes its lair in the deepest part of the Stillwater, likely in an underwater cave or a submerged ruin. It considers the entire lake its territory and will aggressively defend it against any intruders. It often hunts by cruising just below the surface, it's golden scales just visible in the water, before launching itself at prey.

# **Stat Block:**

**The Lurker of the Stillwater** _Large monstrosity, unaligned_

**Armor Class:** 16 (natural armor) **Hit Points:** 85 (10d10 + 30) **Speed:** 10 ft., swim 60 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover, while airborne)

**STR** 18 (+4) **DEX** 14 (+2) **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 4 (-3) **WIS** 10 (+0) **CHA** 5 (-3)

**Saving Throws:** Dex +5, Con +6 **Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +5 **Damage Immunities:** Poison **Condition Immunities:** Poisoned **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13 **Languages:** — **Challenge:**1 5 (1,800 XP)

**Traits:**

- **Amphibious:** The Lurker can breathe both air and water.
- **Water Dependency:** The Lurker needs to be submerged in water for at least 1 hour each day or it gains one level of exhaustion.
- **Keen Smell:** The Lurker has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
- **Contaminated Water:** The Lurker is immune to the negative effects of the Stillwater's water.

**Actions:**

- **Multiattack:** The Lurker makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its tail slap.
- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 15 (2d10 + 4) piercing damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature,2 it is grappled (escape DC 15). Until this grapple ends, the3 target is restrained, and the Lurker can't bite another target.
- **Tail Slap:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
- **Water Jet:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, range 30/60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 10 (3d6) cold damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Bonus Actions:**

- **Inflate/Deflate Air Bladders:** The Lurker can inflate its air bladders, gaining a flying speed of 30 feet (hover) until the end of its next turn. While airborne, it can't use its swim speed, and it falls to the ground at the end of its turn unless it lands in water. The Lurker can use a bonus action to deflate the air bladders.
- **Submerge:** If the Lurker is in water, it can use a bonus action to submerge itself, becoming heavily obscured until the start of its next turn or until it attacks or uses an action.

**Weakness:**

- **Air Bladders:** The Lurker's air bladders are vulnerable. While inflated, attacks against the Lurker have advantage, and if the Lurker takes 15 or more damage in a single turn while airborne, one of its air bladders ruptures. The Lurker can still use this ability with one air bladder, but it's flying speed is halved. If both air bladders are ruptured, it loses its flying speed and falls. It can reinflate its air bladders after finishing a short or long rest.

**Tactics:**

- **Hit and Run:** The Lurker will use its superior swimming speed and its ability to briefly fly to launch surprise attacks from the water, then retreat to safety.
- **Drag Down:** It will try to grapple its prey and drag it into the water, where it has the advantage.
- **Water Jet:** It will use its Water Jet to knock enemies prone, making them easier to attack.
- **Airborne Assault:** It will use its air bladders to leap out of the water and attack enemies on land, using its bite and tail slap attacks. It might also try to grapple enemies and then carry them into the air before dropping them.
- **Exploit Weakness:** If the party damages its air bladders, it will become more cautious and rely on its swimming speed and underwater advantage.

**Encounter Design:**

- **The Stillwater:** The encounter should take place at or near the Stillwater, making use of both the water and the surrounding terrain.
- **Dark Humor:** Emphasize the absurdity of the creature's appearance and its goldfish origins, even as it poses a deadly threat. You can describe its attacks with a mix of horror and humor, highlighting the incongruity of a giant, mutated goldfish terrorizing the party.
- **Environmental Hazards:** The area around the lake could have patches of toxic algae, unstable ground, or other hazards that the Lurker can use to its advantage.
- **Multiple Stages:** The encounter could have multiple stages:
    - **Stage 1:** The party is lured to the lake or approaches it to collect the water sample. The Lurker attacks from the water, using hit-and-run tactics.
    - **Stage 2:** The party damages the Lurker's air bladders, forcing it to fight them in the water or on land.
    - **Stage 3:** The Lurker, enraged or desperate, might retreat to its lair, leading the party to a final confrontation in a more confined and dangerous environment.
- **Alternative Solutions:** The party might find ways to defeat the Lurker without killing it. They could try to trap it, drive it away with powerful magic, or even find a way to communicate with it and appeal to its dim, goldfish-like intellect.

**Rewards:**

- **Lurker Scales:** The Lurker's scales are tough and beautiful, and could be used to craft armor or other items.
- **Air Bladders:** If carefully removed, the Lurker's air bladders might be used to create a magical item that grants limited flight or underwater breathing.
- **Bite Venom:** A skilled alchemist might be able to extract venom from the Lurker's teeth, which could be used to create potent poisons or antitoxins.
- **Information:** Defeating the Lurker and exploring its lair might reveal clues about the Mourning, the nature of the Stillwater's contamination, or the activities of other factions in the region.

**The Lurker of the Stillwater is a unique and challenging encounter that blends horror, humor, and the bizarre nature of the Mournland. Its unusual abilities and its vulnerability create opportunities for creative tactics and strategic thinking. Remember to play up the dark humor of the situation, emphasize the creature's monstrous nature, and reward your players for their ingenuity in dealing with this mutated menace.**
