


**Tier 2: The Safehouse**

- **Description:** A more substantial building, perhaps a former shop or a small house, in a better location. It's been repaired and reinforced, offering decent security and comfort.
- **Requirements:**
    - Town must reach Tier 2 (Frontier Town).
    - Party must achieve a certain level of renown or complete a specific quest for the town.
    - Cost: 500 gp (to purchase or lease the building).
- **Starting Features:**
    - **Separate Bedrooms:** Small, private rooms for each party member.
    - **Common Area:** A larger space for meetings, meals, and relaxation.
    - **Reinforced Door & Windows:** Providing better security.
    - **Larger Storage:** Multiple chests or a small storage room.
- **Upgrades:**
    - **Workshop (Cost: 200 gp, requires tools and materials):** A dedicated space for crafting, repairs, and tinkering.
    - **Library/Study (Cost: 150 gp, requires bookshelves and writing materials):** An area for research, mapmaking, and storing knowledge.
    - **Basic Alchemy Lab (Cost: 250 gp, requires specialized equipment):** Allows for the creation of simple potions and alchemical items.
    - **Secure Storage (Cost: 300 gp, requires a strong lock and reinforced container):** A hidden or heavily fortified area for storing valuables.
