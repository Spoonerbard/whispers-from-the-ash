**The Steel Stoners: Outlaw Miners of [[[[the Mournland]]]]]**

**Overview:**

- **Who They Are:** A clan of dwarf prospectors and miners who have turned their backs on the authority of House Kundarak and established their own independent operation in [[the Mournland]]. They are all criminals, having stolen something of great value from House Kundarak, which has put a price on their heads.
- **What They Do:** They are primarily focused on mining Croydonite, which they believe to be a valuable, though strange, mineral. They are unaware of its true nature and the dangers it poses. They smuggle the [[Croydonite]] out of [[the Mournland]] and sell it to the highest bidder, likely unknowingly contributing to dangerous experiments or the creation of unstable weapons.
- **Base of Operations:** They have a hidden mining operation set up in a location relatively near [[npcs]], likely in a geographically unstable area like the Scarred Peak or within the Howling Caves, where they've discovered a significant vein of Croydonite. Their base camp is located in [[npcs]] in an abandoned building on the edge of town.
- **Relationship with [[npcs|npcs]]:** They are tolerated in [[npcs]] because they bring in a significant amount of wealth and are willing to trade for supplies and services. They maintain a relatively low profile and try to avoid drawing too much attention to themselves. They are friendly enough to the townsfolk but keep mostly to themselves.
- **Relationship with the Party:** They are initially friendly and welcoming towards the party, seeing them as potential customers or allies. However, they are also wary and watchful, especially if the party starts asking too many questions about their mining operation or their past. They will do whatever it takes to protect their secret and their [[Croydonite]] supply.

1. **[[Dwalin "The Mole" Kettlebottom]] (Scout/Lookout):**
    
    - **Appearance:** A small, wiry dwarf with a nervous demeanor, constantly looking over his shoulder. He wears dark, nondescript clothing and carries a set of thieves' tools along with a shortbow.
    - **Personality:** Dwalin is the Steel Stoner's scout and lookout. He's responsible for monitoring the area around their mining operation and keeping an eye out for potential threats. He's easily spooked and prone to exaggeration, but he has a knack for spotting trouble before it arrives. He can often be found up the tallest structure in town, and he is known for leaving messages in seemingly random places.
    - **Motivation:** To avoid getting caught by House Kundarak and to keep his fellow Steel Stoners safe. He's also motivated by a desire for excitement and adventure, though he'd never admit it.
    - **Secret:** Dwalin is secretly sending information to House Phiarlan/Thuranni about the Steel Stoners' activities, in exchange for protection and a possible escape route if things go south. He is also deathly afraid of birds.
    - **Quote:** "I saw something out there... something big. And scary. We should probably, uh, go the other way."

**The Mining Operation:**

- **Location:** The mine is located in a hidden area within one of the squares near [[npcs]], chosen for its rich [[Croydonite]] deposits and its defensibility. The exact location should be something the players can discover through exploration, investigation, or by following the Steel Stoners.
- **Security:** The Steel Stoners have fortified the mine entrance and set up traps to deter intruders. They also have guards posted at strategic locations. They are on constant alert for both House Kundarak agents and the dangers of [[the Mournland]].
- **Croydonite Processing:** The dwarves are mining raw [[Croydonite]] and doing some basic processing on-site to make it easier to transport. They are likely unaware of the dangers of prolonged exposure and are not taking proper precautions. They are selling it to various buyers, including those that the party interacts with (such as [[Troxler]]).

**Plot Hooks:**

- **The Missing Miners:** Several Steel Stoners have gone missing in the mine. The party is hired to investigate, leading them to discover the dangers of [[Croydonite]] exposure or a new threat within the mine.
- **The House Kundarak Investigation:** House Kundarak agents arrive in [[npcs]], searching for the stolen item and the Steel Stoners. The party might be caught in the middle, forced to choose a side or play both ends against the middle.
- **The [[Croydonite]] Sickness:** Miners in [[npcs]] start exhibiting strange symptoms – mutations, madness, or magical instability – due to [[Croydonite]] exposure. The party is tasked with finding a cure or containing the outbreak.
- **The Rival Buyer:** A mysterious buyer (potentially an agent of the Order of the Broken Sky or House Cannith) approaches the party, offering a large sum of money for raw Croydonite. This could lead to a confrontation with the Steel Stoners.
- **The Secret of the Stone:** The party discovers clues about the true nature of the item the Steel Stoners stole from House Kundarak, revealing its power and its potential to change the balance of power in [[the Mournland]].

**Using the Steel Stoners:**

- **Moral Ambiguity:** The Steel Stoners are criminals, but they are also a close-knit group trying to survive in a harsh environment. They are not inherently evil, but their actions have consequences. This allows for complex interactions and moral dilemmas for the players.
- **Source of Conflict:** They can be a source of conflict with House Kundarak, other factions interested in Croydonite, or even among themselves if their secrets are revealed.
- **Source of Information:** They have firsthand knowledge of [[the Mournland]] and its dangers, particularly regarding Croydonite. They might be willing to share information with the party, for a price.
- **Potential Allies:** If the party plays their cards right, they might be able to gain the Steel Stoners as allies, though this would likely require them to help the dwarves against House Kundarak or other enemies.
