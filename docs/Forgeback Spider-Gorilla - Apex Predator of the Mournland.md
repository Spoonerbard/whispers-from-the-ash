**Forgeback Spider-Gorilla: Apex Predator of the Mournland**

**Lore:**

- **Origin:** A terrifying fusion of nature's savagery and the remnants of war, the Forgeback Spider-Gorilla is a unique abomination created by the twisted magic of the Mournland. It's a creature born from a gorilla that stumbled upon a battlefield littered with warforged remains and, driven by a combination of instinct and the Mourning's influence, began to incorporate the metallic parts into its own body. It is a relatively young creature, being a new species to the Mournland, but it is quickly making it's mark.
- **Nature:** The Forgeback is a cunning and brutal predator, combining the strength and agility of a gorilla with the enhanced resilience and weaponry of a warforged. It's a solitary creature, fiercely territorial, and driven by a primal hunger. It views other creatures as either prey or a threat. It is highly intelligent and learns from each encounter. It is known for setting traps and ambushes for it's prey.
- **Appearance:** Imagine a massive gorilla, its fur matted and scarred. Fused to its body are plates of warforged armor, bolted and welded onto its flesh in a grotesque mockery of a soldier's uniform. These plates are concentrated around its chest, shoulders, and forearms, providing significant protection. One of its arms might end in a warforged hand, the fingers replaced with wicked, blade-like claws. Its eyes glow with an eerie red light, and its roar is a terrifying mix of animalistic rage and the grinding of metal. It's lower back has several long, sharp, metal spider legs, fused to it's body, that it uses to climb and to attack.
- **Habitat:** The Forgeback Spider-Gorilla can be found in various parts of the Mournland, but it prefers areas with dense cover, such as ruined cities, twisted forests, or mountainous regions, where it can use its climbing ability and ambush tactics to its advantage. It is known to make lairs in high places. It is an excellent climber, able to scale walls and trees with ease.

**Stat Block:**

**Forgeback Spider-Gorilla** _Large monstrosity, unaligned_

**Armor Class:** 17 (natural armor, warforged plating) **Hit Points:** 95 (10d10 + 40) **Speed:** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

**STR** 20 (+5) **DEX** 16 (+3) **CON** 18 (+4) **INT** 11 (+0) **WIS** 12 (+1) **CHA** 7 (-2)

**Saving Throws:** Dex +6, Con +7 **Skills:** Athletics +8, Perception +4, Stealth +6 **Damage Resistances:** Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks Not Made with Adamantine Weapons **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.,1 passive Perception 14 **Languages:** Understands Common but can't speak **Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP)

**Traits:**

- **Warforged Resilience:** The Forgeback has advantage on saving throws against being poisoned, and it is immune to disease. It doesn't need to eat, drink, or breathe. It is also immune to sleep spells and effects.
- **Brute:** A melee weapon deals one extra die of its damage when the Forgeback hits with it (included in the attack).
- **Keen Senses:** The Forgeback has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell or hearing.
- **Spider Climb:** The Forgeback can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check.

**Actions:**

- **Multiattack:**2 The Forgeback makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws. It can replace one claw attack with its Grab action, or it can replace it's bite attack with it's Web ability.
- **Bite:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 16 (2d10 + 5) piercing damage.
- **Claw:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 143 (2d8 + 5) slashing damage.
- **Grab:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ The target is grappled (escape DC 16). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the Forgeback can't use its Grab attack on another target. The Forgeback can make a bite attack against the grappled target as a bonus action.
- **Web (Recharge 5-6):** The Forgeback shoots a thick strand of webbing at a point it can see within 60 feet. The webbing fills a 10-foot cube from that point. The area becomes difficult terrain, and any creature that starts its turn in the webbing or enters it for the first time on a turn must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is restrained. A creature can use its action to make a DC 15 Strength check, freeing itself or another creature within its reach on a success.4 The webbing is flammable and can be destroyed by dealing 5 fire damage to it.

**Reactions:**

- **Metallic Rebuke:** When a creature hits the Forgeback with a melee attack, the attacker takes 1d4 piercing damage from the sharp, jagged warforged plating on the Forgeback's body, if they are within 5 feet of the Forgeback.

**Legendary Actions (If used as a solo boss against a higher-level party):** The Forgeback can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The5 Forgeback regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

- **Detect:** The Forgeback makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
- **Claw Attack:** The Forgeback makes a claw attack.
- **Leap (Costs 2 Actions):** The Forgeback jumps up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

**Tactics:**

- **Hunter:** The Forgeback will stalk its prey, using its stealth and climbing ability to stay hidden until it can launch a surprise attack. It will attempt to grapple a target and then use its bite attack to deal extra damage.
- **Hit and Run:** It will use its superior speed and climbing ability to attack and then retreat, wearing down its opponents.
- **Webbing:** It will use its webbing to create difficult terrain, trap enemies, or block off escape routes. It might also use the webbing to create a barrier between itself and its enemies, forcing them to come to it.
- **Armor Focus:** It will prioritize attacking enemies who are not wearing heavy armor or who are not proficient with heavy armor.
- **Learning:** The Forgeback is intelligent and will learn from its mistakes. If the party defeats it once, it will be more cautious and use different tactics in subsequent encounters. It might even start setting traps or ambushes specifically designed to counter the party's strengths.

**Weaknesses:**

- **Fire:** While resistant to many forms of damage, the Forgeback is vulnerable to fire, taking double damage from fire attacks.
- **Adamantine Weapons:** Weapons made of adamantine bypass its resistance to nonmagical piercing and slashing damage.
- **Powerful Magic:** High-level spells and magical effects can overwhelm its defenses.

**Encounter Design:**

- **Level 3 Encounter:**
    - **The Hunt:** The party is being hunted by the Forgeback. They might encounter signs of its presence (tracks, warforged remains, partially eaten carcasses) before the creature attacks. The encounter should emphasize stealth, evasion, and potentially setting traps or preparing an ambush for the Forgeback. The goal might be to escape the creature's territory rather than defeat it.
    - **Terrain:** A ruined city, a dense forest, or a rocky canyon would all be suitable locations, providing opportunities for the Forgeback to use its climbing and stealth abilities.
- **Level 5 Encounter:**
    - **The Lair:** The party tracks the Forgeback to its lair, perhaps to recover a stolen item, rescue a captured NPC, or simply to eliminate the threat. The lair could be a high, ruined building, a cave in the side of a cliff, or a network of tunnels beneath a dead forest.
    - **Boss Fight:** The encounter should be a challenging boss fight, with the Forgeback using all of its abilities to its advantage. It might have additional hazards in its lair, such as pits, unstable structures, or even captured creatures that it releases to fight alongside it.
- **Multiple Encounters:** The Forgeback could be a recurring enemy, harassing the party throughout their early explorations of the Mournland. Each encounter could reveal more about the creature's intelligence, its tactics, and its connection to the warforged.

**Rewards:**

- **Warforged Components:** The Forgeback's body contains valuable warforged components that can be salvaged and used for crafting or sold to Troxler.
- **Trophy:** The Forgeback's skull or claws could be a valuable trophy, proving the party's skill and bravery.
- **Information:** Defeating the Forgeback and examining its lair might reveal clues about the Mourning, the Lord of Blades, or other secrets of the region.
- **Reputation:** Defeating such a powerful creature will undoubtedly increase the party's reputation in Salvation and beyond.

**The Forgeback Spider-Gorilla is a formidable opponent, designed to be a recurring threat that embodies the dangers of the Mournland. Its unique combination of abilities, its terrifying appearance, and its intelligent tactics will make it a memorable and challenging foe for your players. Remember to adjust its stats and abilities based on your party's level and to use it in a way that enhances the story and creates exciting gameplay opportunities.**
