**House Cannith: Makers of Magic and War**

- **Who They Are:** House Cannith is one of the thirteen  [[dragonmarked houses]] , holding the **Mark of Making**. They are renowned for their mastery of artifice, crafting, and invention. Historically, they were the creators of mundane and magical items, including the warforged, constructs that were initially built to fight in the Last War.

- **Pre-Mourning Power:** Before [[the Mourning]], House Cannith was one of the most powerful and influential houses in Khorvaire. Their creation of the warforged revolutionized warfare, and their magical innovations shaped many aspects of daily life. They held a monopoly on the creation of many types of magical items, as well as being the foremost experts on the use of dragonshards in their creations.

- **The Split:** The Last War, and particularly the destruction of [[Cyre]], led to a schism within House Cannith. The house is now divided into three main branches, each led by a different scion of the family, each claiming to be the true heir to the Cannith legacy:
    - **Cannith South (Merrix d'Cannith):** Based in Sharn, focused on the development of warforged and other constructs. They are considered the most radical branch.
    - **Cannith West (Zorlan d'Cannith):** Based in Korth, focused on traditional Cannith craftsmanship and the creation of magical items. They are considered the most conservative branch.
    - **Cannith East (Jorlanna d'Cannith):** Based in Aundair, focused on research and development of new magical technologies. They are considered the most moderate branch.

- **Post-Mourning Activities:** Since the Day of Mourning, House Cannith has been struggling to regain its former power and influence. They are still major players in the magical marketplace, but the schism has weakened them, and they face competition from other houses and independent artisans. They have been very careful to follow the rules set down by [[the treaty of Thronehold]], but there have been rumors that some factions within the house are secretly violating the treaty.

**House Cannith's Role in Your Campaign So Far:**

- **Secret Agents:** House Cannith has a clandestine presence in Salvation, the frontier town on the edge of the Mournland. **Deputy "Whisper" Elara** is a Cannith double agent embedded within the town's Tharashk-controlled law enforcement. Her mission is to monitor the situation, gather intelligence, and report back to her superiors. It is likely that she is working for Cannith East.
- **Interest in the Mournland:** House Cannith, like other powerful groups, is intensely interested in the Mournland. They see it as a potential source of lost knowledge, powerful artifacts, and unique resources, particularly **Croydonite**. They are especially interested in any surviving Cannith research facilities or workshops that might be found in the ruins of Cyre, such as the city of Making. They are also interested in recovering any warforged that might still be active in the Mournland.
- **The City of Making:** As the former center of magical innovation and likely the location of advanced Cannith research facilities, the ruins of Making are a prime target for House Cannith. They would be eager to recover any surviving technology, blueprints, or prototypes, especially anything related to warforged creation or the creation of a "warforged god."
- **Competition with Other Factions:** House Cannith is not the only group interested in the Mournland. They are in competition with the Order of the Broken Sky, House Tharashk, and potentially other factions, each with their own agendas. This creates opportunities for conflict, espionage, and uneasy alliances.
- **Potential for Weaponization:** House Cannith's history of weapon development makes them a likely candidate for wanting to exploit the military applications of Croydonite or other Mournland discoveries. They might be secretly developing new weapons or constructs powered by the corrupted magic of the wasteland. They might also be interested in the activities of the Lord of Blades, and his attempts to create a warforged god.
- **Double Agents among the Deputies:** The presence of Cannith agents within the ranks of the town's deputies suggests a deeper level of involvement. These agents are likely gathering information, monitoring the activities of other factions, and potentially manipulating events from behind the scenes. The brother of Deputy "Deadeye" Dal is also a Cannith agent.
- **Jorun d'Cannith:** A young, ambitious scion of House Cannith has recently arrived in town, eager to prove himself. He is in league with the Order of the Broken Sky.

**Possible Future Involvement:**

- **Retrieval Missions:** House Cannith might hire the party to retrieve lost technology, artifacts, or research data from the Mournland. These missions would be dangerous but potentially lucrative.
- **Escort or Protection:** The party might be hired to escort Cannith researchers or protect their interests in Salvation.
- **Investigation into Rivals:** House Cannith might task the party with investigating the activities of their rivals, such as House Tharashk or the Order of the Broken Sky.
- **The Croydonite Question:** House Cannith's interest in Croydonite could put them at odds with the party, especially if the players decide that the material is too dangerous to be exploited.
- **Internal Conflict:** The party might become embroiled in the internal power struggles between the different branches of House Cannith, each vying for control of the house's resources and influence.

**In summary, House Cannith is a powerful and influential force in your campaign, even if their presence is mostly felt behind the scenes at this early stage. They are driven by a desire to reclaim their lost glory, to unlock the secrets of the Mournland, and to maintain their dominance in the field of magical technology. Their involvement adds a layer of political intrigue, corporate espionage, and potential conflict to the story, and their interest in Croydonite and the ruins of Making places them at the heart of the campaign's central mysteries.**
