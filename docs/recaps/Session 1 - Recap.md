# **Session One Recap: Into the Grey**

The mists have parted, and the Mournland, a land scarred by a century of silence and deadly fog, lies open for the first time in living memory. Drawn by whispers of forgotten treasures and the allure of the unknown, three adventurers found themselves in the frontier town of Salvation, a ramshackle settlement clinging to the edge of this blighted land.

The party, consisting of **Raymar**, a spore druid, **Rei**, an assassin rogue, and **Gyda**, a hexblade warlock, arrived in Salvation and were promptly subjected to the gruff authority of **Sheriff Hardin "Hard-Eye" Karr**. After the mandatory (and somewhat exploitative) process of acquiring their "adventuring licenses" from House Tharashk, they were assigned to **Shack E**, a less-than-luxurious dwelling that would serve as their base of operations.

Their exploration of Salvation proved... eventful. While the intention was to familiarize themselves with the town's key locations and NPCs, the experience felt somewhat disorganized. **(Note:** _Consider having a designated NPC, like Deputy Dal, provide a guided tour in the future to streamline this process._)

Despite the disjointed exploration, the party did encounter some of Salvation's notable residents. They met **"Old Man" Silas**, the curmudgeonly proprietor of the general store, **Mara "Fingers" Keen**, the sly purveyor of illicit goods, and **"Mama" Elara Thorne**, the stoic landlady of their humble abode.

The adventurers then ventured into the **Crooked Tankard**, where Rei, perhaps seeking to test her limits, indulged in Grelga's infamous "Mournland Moonshine" and, against all odds, managed to hold it down, passing her constitution saving throw. Gyda, on the other hand, had a less successful encounter with the local ale. A natural 1 on her constitution check led to a rather embarrassing (and swift) intoxication, requiring the care of the town's gentle but inexperienced centaur cleric, **Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight**.

During her time at the clinic, Gyda learned about **Anya "Swiftbow"**, the sole survivor of a recent expedition into the Ashen Wastes, a small desert region northwest of Salvation. Anya's harrowing tale of a heavily armed party, reduced to a single, half-mad survivor muttering about creatures that "swim" and "breathe" within the black sands, piqued the party's interest.

Intrigued by the mystery and perhaps eager to prove themselves, the adventurers decided to venture into the Ashen Wastes. Reaching the edge of the Mournland, they were confronted by a sheer cliff face, a dramatic drop into the swirling grey mist that marked the true beginning of the wasteland. A lone, ominous sign warned, "If you value your lives, be somewhere else." Undeterred, Raymar, displaying the versatility of his druidic abilities, wild-shaped into a giant eagle and expertly ferried his companions down the 200-foot precipice, showcasing a practical and impressive use of his powers. They are now at the bottom of the cliff, and have taken their first steps into the Mournland.

**Session Highlights:**

- Successful introduction of the setting, the Mournland, and the town of Salvation.
- The party met several key NPCs and learned about the adventuring licenses.
- The "Mournland Moonshine" and ale incident provided some early roleplaying opportunities and character development.
- The cliff descent using wild shape was a creative and effective way to overcome a significant obstacle.
- The party has a clear objective for the next session: explore the Ashen Wastes and uncover the fate of Anya's expedition.

**Areas for Improvement:**

- **Town Exploration:** The exploration of Salvation felt disorganized. Using a designated NPC guide or providing a more structured introduction to the town's locations and services would improve the flow. You could have used note cards, with each location, and the NPCs present at those locations.
- **NPC Interaction:** While the party met several NPCs, the interactions could have been more focused. Consider preparing specific information or quest hooks that each NPC can offer.
- **Early Stakes:** While the cliff descent was exciting, the session lacked a strong sense of immediate danger or urgency. Consider introducing a more pressing threat or a time constraint to motivate the party's actions. Perhaps the acquisition of their licence was tied to them investigating the fate of the missing party.

**Next Steps:**

- **Prepare for the Ashen Wastes:** Plan the encounters and challenges the party will face in the Ashen Wastes, including the creatures that "swim" and "breathe" beneath the sand.
- **Develop Anya's Role:** Decide how Anya will figure in the next session. Will she recover enough to provide more information? Will the party encounter her in the wastes?
- **Consider the Steel Sentinels:** The arrival of the warforged is scheduled for around level 4, which isn't too far off. Start thinking about how you will introduce them and tie their mission into the ongoing narrative.

Overall, it sounds like a solid first session! You've introduced the setting, established the basic mechanics, and given your players a taste of the dangers and mysteries that await them in the Mournland. With a little more structure and focus, your next session is sure to be even better.

# **Session One, Part Two Recap: Lost and Found in the Mournland**

Following their initial foray into the Ashen Wastes, the party, consisting of **Raymar** (spore druid), **Rei** (assassin rogue), and **Gyda** (hexblade warlock), returned to Salvation with more questions than answers.

**Anya's Revelation:**

Their first stop was to check on **Anya "Swiftbow,"** the half-elf ranger and sole survivor of the previous expedition. Having regained consciousness, Anya was able to provide a fragmented account of her party's fate. She confirmed the existence of the creatures within the black sands, and spoke of being separated from her companions in the wastes. She could not remember much about the time, only that she had been running for a long time, before collapsing, only to awaken in the care of Kaelen.

**Silas's Secret:**

Seeking further information, the party turned to **"Old Man" Silas**, the general store owner. Perhaps revealing more than he intended, Silas disclosed that he had been secretly scouting the Mournland in his spare time. He was, after all a former adventurer. He confirmed the location of the Ashen Wastes where Anya's party had gone missing, providing the party with rough directions and a warning about the dangers of the area. He marked a location on their map, a place he had seen from a distance but had not yet explored.

**Into the Mournland (Again):**

Venturing beyond the cliff and into the Mournland proper, the party braved the Ashen Wastes once more, eventually finding themselves at the edge of the Silent Grove. There, nestled amongst the petrified trees, they discovered the ruins of the **Sky Song Tavern**, a building that seemed impossibly displaced from its original location.

**Discoveries and Battles:**

Inside the tavern, the party made a peculiar discovery: several barrels filled with disassembled warforged parts, all bearing markings that suggested they belonged to House Cannith. This discovery raised questions about potential Cannith involvement in the area and the purpose of these warforged.

Their investigation was interrupted by an **animated warforged hand**, which attacked the party. They also found a cellar, hidden beneath the ruined tavern. Within they found two bottles of wine, worth a combined 700gp, and the body of a male, presumed to be the tavern owner. Upon the body they found a journal, and 1800gp in gold and platinum.

Further exploration was cut short as they were attacked by a creature made entirely of fire, a **Fire Worm** (adapted from the **Sand Fire Worm**), seemingly drawn to the commotion, or perhaps guarding the ruins. A fierce battle ensued, culminating in the party's victory, though not without sustaining injuries. It should be noted that the party did not find the secret room, detailed in the Sky Song Tavern description.

**Unexpected Guests:**

As they battled the Fire Worm, they noticed two figures emerge from the shadows: a **warforged** and a **dwarf**. The warforged, to the party's astonishment, displayed sorcerous abilities, and both were speaking in an unfamiliar language, seemingly agitated and confused. Even more বিস্ময়কর, both the warforged and the dwarf appeared significantly younger than they should have been, based on Anya's descriptions of her missing companions. Anya later confirmed that these were indeed her companions, though their ages and the language barrier presented a perplexing mystery. The dwarf was identified by a tattoo of his deity on his arm.

**Shelter from the Storm:**

With a fierce Mournland storm raging outside, the party, along with the two disoriented adventurers, took shelter within the partially intact ruins of the Sky Song Tavern. They were forced to huddle together for warmth and protection as the storm battered the building throughout the night.

**Vanishing Barrels:**

During the night, under the cover of the storm, the barrels containing the warforged parts sprouted legs and mysteriously fled the tavern, presumably continuing some unknown, pre-programmed mission. This added another layer of intrigue to the already strange events.

**Return to Salvation:**

The next morning, the storm had passed, leaving behind an eerie calm. The party returned to Salvation with Anya's two companions in tow, the mystery of their altered state and the animated barrels heavy on their minds.

**Session Highlights:**

- **Unveiling More of the Mournland:** The party experienced the dangers of the Mournland firsthand, encountering both mutated creatures and the remnants of its magical corruption.
- **The Sky Song Tavern:** The discovery of the displaced tavern and its secrets provided a strong focal point for the session.
- **Combat Encounters:** The battles with the animated hand and the Fire Worm were engaging and challenging.
- **The Time-Displaced Adventurers:** The introduction of the younger warforged and dwarf creates a compelling mystery and potential plot complications.
- **The Walking Barrels:** A bizarre and humorous element that adds to the weirdness of the Mournland and hints at a larger, potentially sinister, plan.
- **Discovery of the Journal:** The party have in their possession, a journal that could contain valuable information.

**Areas for Improvement:**

- **The Tour:** As you mentioned, the initial exploration of Salvation felt disorganized. A guided tour by Deputy Dal (or another NPC) would help streamline this in the future.
- **Clearer Stakes:** While the party explored and fought, the immediate stakes could have been higher. Perhaps the storm could have been approaching faster, forcing them to find shelter quickly, or maybe they could have been racing against another group to reach the tavern first.

**Next Steps:**

- **Investigate the Time Discrepancy:** The party will likely want to figure out how Anya's companions became younger and why they are speaking a strange language. This could involve research, questioning the NPCs in Salvation (especially Silas, who might have knowledge of time magic), or even seeking help from the Order of the Broken Sky.
- **Decipher the Journal:** The journal found on the tavern owner is a crucial piece of the puzzle. The party will need to find a way to translate it and understand its contents.
- **Track the Barrels:** The party might try to track the animated barrels, leading them to another location in the Mournland or revealing more about the nature of the warforged parts.
- **Deal with the Arrival of the Houses:** Now that the party is level 3, and they have returned from the Mournland for the second time, it is time for the Houses to take notice.
