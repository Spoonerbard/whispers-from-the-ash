# **Session One Recap: Into the Grey**

The mists have parted, and the Mournland, a land scarred by a century of silence and deadly fog, lies open for the first time in living memory. Drawn by whispers of forgotten treasures and the allure of the unknown, three adventurers found themselves in the frontier town of Salvation, a ramshackle settlement clinging to the edge of this blighted land.

The party, consisting of **Raymar**, a spore druid, **Rei**, an assassin rogue, and **Gyda**, a hexblade warlock, arrived in Salvation and were promptly subjected to the gruff authority of **Sheriff Hardin "Hard-Eye" Karr**. After the mandatory (and somewhat exploitative) process of acquiring their "adventuring licenses" from House Tharashk, they were assigned to **Shack B**, a less-than-luxurious dwelling that would serve as their base of operations.

The adventurers then ventured into the **Crooked Tankard**, where Rei, perhaps seeking to test her limits, indulged in Grelga's infamous "Mournland Moonshine" and, against all odds, managed to hold it down, passing her constitution saving throw. Gyda, on the other hand, had a less successful encounter with the local ale. A natural 1 on her constitution check led to a rather embarrassing (and swift) intoxication, requiring the care of the town's gentle but inexperienced centaur cleric, **Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight**.

During her time at the clinic, Gyda learned about **Anya "Swiftbow"**, the sole survivor of a recent expedition into the Ashen Wastes, a small desert region northwest of Salvation. Anya's harrowing tale of a heavily armed party, reduced to a single, half-mad survivor muttering about creatures that "swim" and "breathe" within the black sands, piqued the party's interest.

**Anya's Revelation:**

Their first stop was to check on **Anya "Swiftbow,"** the half-elf ranger and sole survivor of the previous expedition. Having regained consciousness, Anya was able to provide a fragmented account of her party's fate. She confirmed the existence of the creatures within the black sands, and spoke of being separated from her companions in the wastes. She could not remember much about the time, only that she had been running for a long time, before collapsing, only to awaken in the care of Kaelen.

**Silas's Secret:**

Seeking further information, the party turned to **"Old Man" Silas**, the general store owner. Perhaps revealing more than he intended, Silas disclosed that he had been secretly scouting the Mournland in his spare time. He was, after all a former adventurer. He confirmed the location of the Ashen Wastes where Anya's party had gone missing, providing the party with rough directions and a warning about the dangers of the area. He marked a location on their map, a place he had seen from a distance but had not yet explored.

**Over The Edge**
Intrigued by the mystery and perhaps eager to prove themselves, the adventurers decided to venture into the Ashen Wastes. Reaching the edge of the Mournland, they were confronted by a sheer cliff face, a dramatic drop into the swirling grey mist that marked the true beginning of the wasteland. A lone, ominous sign warned, "If you value your lives, be somewhere else." Undeterred, Raymar, displaying the versatility of their druidic abilities, wild-shaped into a giant spider and expertly ferried his companions down the 200-foot precipice, showcasing a practical and impressive use of their powers. They are now at the bottom of the cliff, and have taken their first steps into the Mournland. They discovered veins of a mysterious glistening black rock substance running through the cliff face and all along the ground. Eventually venturing beyond the cliff and into the Mournland proper, the party braved the Ashen Wastes, after several hours travel, they discovered the ruins of a once flying Tavern, that had crashed a hundred years previously.

**Discoveries and Battles:**

Inside the tavern, the party made a peculiar discovery: several barrels filled with disassembled warforged parts, all bearing indicating that they were recently placed there in the last few weeks. 

Their investigation was interrupted by an **animated warforged hand**, which attacked the party. They also found a cellar, hidden beneath the ruined tavern. Within they found two bottles of wine, worth a combined 700gp, and the body of a male, presumed to be the tavern owner and successful recovered 1800gp in gold and platinum.

Further exploration was cut short as they were attacked by a creature a ash worm, seemingly drawn to the commotion, or perhaps guarding the ruins. A fierce battle ensued, culminating in the party's victory, though not without sustaining injuries and the Druid dropping to death saves.

**Unexpected Guests:**

As they battled the Ash Worm, they noticed two figures emerge from the shadows: a **warforged** and a **dwarf**. The warforged, to the party's astonishment, displayed sorcerous abilities, and both were speaking in an unfamiliar language, seemingly agitated and confused. Both the warforged and the dwarf appeared significantly younger than they should have been, based on Anya's descriptions of her missing companions. Anya later confirmed that these were indeed her companions, though their ages and the language barrier presented a perplexing mystery. The dwarf was identified by a tattoo of his deity on his arm.

**Shelter from the Storm:**

With a fierce Mournland storm raging outside, the party, along with the two disoriented adventurers, took shelter within the partially intact ruins of the Tavern. They were forced to huddle together for warmth and protection as the storm battered the building throughout the night.

**Vanishing Barrels:**

During the night, under the cover of the storm, the barrels containing the warforged parts sprouted legs and mysteriously fled the tavern, presumably continuing some unknown, pre-programmed mission.

**Return to Salvation:**
The next morning, the storm had passed, leaving behind an eerie calm. The party returned to Salvation with Anya's two companions in tow, the mystery of their altered state and the animated barrels heavy on their minds.
