# Salvation's Circus



The party, battered and weary from their recent expedition into the Mournlands and the perilous storm they endured, limped back into Salvation.  Despite their exhaustion, rest was not on the cards just yet. The vibrant and chaotic "Grand Menagerie of Monsters" circus had arrived in town, demanding their attention.

  

Ignoring their weary limbs, the adventurers plunged into the heart of the circus, a sensory overload of sights, sounds, and smells.  They navigated the bustling crowds and colourful tents, encountering a host of peculiar characters:

  

    Barnabus Bumble: The flamboyant and energetic ringmaster, orchestrating the circus's bizarre spectacles.

    Lux Magstaggle le Man: A captivating magical contortionist with the uncanny ability to detach and control her limbs independently. Her performances left the party both amazed and slightly unnerved.

    Nibble: A mischievous and giggling goblin clown, adding to the circus's chaotic atmosphere with his pranks and antics.

    'Thunderclap': A forest gnome with a fiery passion for pyrotechnics, specialising in creating dazzling and explosive fireworks displays.

    Seaphina: A mysterious and enigmatic fortune teller, offering glimpses into the future (for a price, of course).

    Lorrim: A gruff but efficient roadie, busily preparing a series of cages backstage. He revealed that the circus was planning an expedition into the Mournlands to capture new and exotic creatures for their menagerie.

  

This encounter with the circus and its inhabitants marks the end of this session.  The party, still exhausted but now intrigued by the circus's plans, are left with a number of questions. What are the circus's true intentions in the Mournlands?  Will the party aid or hinder their expedition? And will they ever get a chance to rest?

  

## Salvation's Circus - Part 2 - Whack a Mole

  

The party, now thoroughly immersed in the strange world of the Grand Menagerie of Monsters, decided to split up to better investigate the circus's activities.

  

Talon, the rogue, succumbing to exhaustion, retreated to Shack B (their home base) to catch some much-needed sleep.  Meanwhile, the rest of the party continued their explorations as the circus's festivities stretched late into the night.

  

    Lady Gyda sought out Seaphina for a glimpse into her future, receiving a cryptic fortune.

    Raymar, perhaps finding a kindred spirit in the liminal space between life and death, developed a burgeoning affection for the extraordinary contortionist, Lux Magstaggle le Man.

    Rei, ever the cunning one, engaged Thunderclap in conversation, cleverly manipulating the gnome into believing they were working alongside Sheriff Karr on "the plan".

  

Cassie the paladin, Buu the barbarian, and Rei then regrouped at Shack B. Rei shared the information gleaned from Thunderclap, hinting at some sort of clandestine operation.  Seeking further clarification, they returned to the circus and sought out Lorrim, the roadie.

  

Rei and Glonk, using their combined skills of persuasion and deception, convinced Lorrim that they were also involved in Sheriff Karr's "plan".  From Lorrim, they unearthed a disturbing truth: the "plan" was a plot to detonate a massive firework, designed to disable the teleportation shield that protected the town and its surrounding area.  This act would leave Salvation vulnerable to an invasion by the House Thuranni army, who intended to use the town as a staging ground for a full-scale assault into the Mournlands.  The party also learned that this action would be a direct violation of a treaty between the Dragonmarked Houses, potentially igniting a devastating war.

  

However, Lorrim, a keen observer, saw through Glonk's disguise.  Just as the massive firework detonated overhead, casting the town in a brilliant but ominous light, Lorrim attacked! The session ended in a dramatic cliffhanger, with the party facing immediate danger amidst the chaos of the exploding firework and the revelation of a potentially catastrophic war.

  

## Salvation's Circus - Part 3 - The Aliphox is Summoned

  

The detonation of the massive firework unleashed chaos upon Salvation.  As the town was plunged into disarray, a House Thuranni army teleported in, descending upon the town like a swarm of locusts.  Their objective: the cliff face entrance to the Mournlands.

  

But the explosion had also triggered something far more sinister. The great explosion summoned, the Aliphox, the first of the corrupted beasts mentioned in the old letters – a creature from the late Queen of Cyre's collection of monstrous experiments. This mythic, corrupted celestial being, radiating an aura of malevolent power, made short work of the invading army, tearing through their ranks with horrifying ease.

  

Amidst this pandemonium:

  

*   Lorrim, his treachery and warforged nature revealed, engaged Rei and Glonk in a fierce battle.

*   Buu, Cassie, and a still-groggy Talon found themselves confronted by a group of Thuranni soldiers.

*   Raymar, heartbroken by unrequited love, struggled to cope with the emotional fallout, his romantic dreams shattered.

*   Gyda, unfortunately, suffered blindness as a result of the explosive blast and then was attacked by the fortuneteller.

  

The battle raged, a chaotic melee of soldiers, monsters, and adventurers.  Soon, the party and a small band of surviving soldiers found themselves as the last line of defence between the corrupted celestial creature and the complete annihilation of Salvation.  The fate of the town, and perhaps even more, hung precariously in the balance.

  
  

# Salvation's Circus - Part 4

  

The desperate battle against the corrupted celestial creature, the Aliphox, raged on.  The party, battered and bruised, faced seemingly insurmountable odds.  Many suffered grievous injuries, each close call bringing them nearer to defeat.  But amidst the chaos, Glonk seized an opportunity, managing to cast *sleep* upon the monstrous Aliphox.  The creature, momentarily subdued, became vulnerable.  Lady Gyda, recovered from her blindness, displayed incredible resilience and precision, guided perhaps by instinct or a surge of magic, and plunged her weapon into the Aliphox's heart.

  

The corrupted celestial being, its power waning, finally succumbed. The Thuranni army, its ranks decimated, lay in ruins.  Their carefully laid plans were exposed, their leaders humiliated. Their circus gambit, designed to exploit the Mournlands, had failed spectacularly, utterly outmatched by the very power they sought to control.

  

Just as all seemed lost, a surprising saviour emerged.  Silas, the unassuming shopkeeper, revealed his true nature: a powerful retired wizard.  With a flick of his wrist and the utterance of a single, potent word, Silas cast *wish*, mending the fabric of reality itself.  The Aliphox was healed and resurrected, its corrupted nature purged.

  

In a stunning turn of events, the now-benevolent Aliphox, grateful for its redemption, used its celestial powers to heal the party of their injuries, mending broken bones and curing lingering ailments.  The creature then bestowed upon them the gift of a truly restful night's sleep, a balm for their weary bodies and troubled minds.

  

The following morning dawned bright and clear. The party, refreshed and revitalised, gathered to discuss their next steps.  They had faced down a corrupted celestial being, thwarted an invasion, and uncovered a conspiracy.  Now, with some well-earned downtime ahead of them, they could finally take a breath and plan their future adventures.
