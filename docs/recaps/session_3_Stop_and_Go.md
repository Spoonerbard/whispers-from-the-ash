#recap 

Session three marked the solidifying of the adventuring party, a motley crew forged in the crucible of the Mournlands' unpredictable dangers. The core group - Raymar, the Spore Druid, whose connection to the blighted nature of the Mournlands was both a strength and a source of unsettling power; Gyda, the female Warlock, her pact with an otherworldly entity granting her arcane abilities, but at a cost yet to be fully understood; and Rei, the Rogue Assassin, a master of stealth and deadly precision, whose recent transformation into a toad had been a significant setback - remained.

Thankfully, the combined efforts of Meadowlight, the town's harried cleric, and Troxler, the eccentric gnome artificer, had yielded a temporary solution for Rei's amphibian predicament. A specially crafted magical amulet now suppressed the toad transformation, restoring Rei to her humanoid form, albeit with a noticeable and likely permanent penalty to her speed. While she was no doubt relieved to be out of her toad form, the lingering effects served as a constant reminder of the Mournlands' bizarre and capricious magic.

These three were joined by the ever-more-perplexing Talon, who had somehow managed to multiclass into a Bard, in addition to his Rogue skills, despite his claims of boredom. His motivations remained as unclear as ever, his actions a strange blend of surprising competence and baffling choices. His new bardic abilities added another layer to his already enigmatic persona.

The party gained two entirely new members as well: Cassie, a human Paladin, radiating an aura of righteous determination, likely a stark contrast to the morally ambiguous landscape of the Mournlands; and Buu, a Barbarian, whose imposing physique and raw power promised to be a valuable asset in the dangers ahead. Cassie and Buu were fresh arrivals in Salvation, newly minted adventurers after receiving their licenses from the stern Sheriff Karr. Their arrival brought a surge of fresh energy and determination, coupled with their eagerness to prove themselves.

This newly formed party of seven, a mix of seasoned veterans bearing the physical and psychological scars of their previous encounters, and eager newcomers, stood poised on the edge of the Mournlands. The bonds between them, forged in the shared experience of survival and the pursuit of common goals, however varied those goals might be, were beginning to solidify. The trials ahead would undoubtedly test their strength, their loyalty, and their sanity. They had survived the chaos of their previous foray into the Mournlands, but the question remained: could this newly expanded party withstand the horrors that awaited them in the depths of the fallen kingdom of Cyre? Their next adventure would be the true test of their mettle.

___

Their ranks bolstered and their toad-related troubles temporarily resolved, the party turned their attention to the task at hand. Silas, the enigmatic shopkeeper, once again urged them to investigate the abandoned lightning rail station to the north, hinting at potential rewards and, perhaps, furthering his own mysterious agenda.

Before setting out, they made a stop at Troxler's workshop. The gnome artificer, ever eager to test her inventions, presented them with her latest creation: a sentient, talking rope ladder, reinforced with mythril for added durability. This ladder, christened "Shoelace" by the party, possessed an exuberantly cheerful personality, expressing an almost unsettling joy at its own existence. It was certainly a unique, if somewhat unnerving, addition to their equipment.

They deployed Shoelace at the edge of the crater, the chatty ladder helpfully offering advice (and unsolicited commentary) as they descended into the Mournlands. Once on the crater floor, however, the dangers of their environment quickly became apparent. Raymar, the ever-vigilant Spore Druid, spotted something large and menacing flying through the thick fog that blanketed the area. Simultaneously, Rei, perhaps still affected by the lingering magic of the Mournlands or by the trauma from her toad transformation, became fixated on a vein of glowing Cyrodnite in the crater wall, losing her footing and tumbling onto Gyda below.

The sudden commotion and the unknown threat in the fog put the party on high alert. They rallied, readying themselves for a potential attack as they pressed northward. Their caution was soon justified. As they advanced, a creature circled overhead before dropping a collection of ornate masks in their path. These were no ordinary masks; they were the visages of the Royal Guard, the elite protectors of the last Queen of Cyre. And they were clearly cursed.

The masks attempted to possess the party members, their ancient magic seeking to dominate the minds of the adventurers. A tense and desperate battle ensued, with some of the party nearly succumbing to the insidious influence of the cursed artifacts. It was a close call, a fight where the line between victory and defeat was razor-thin. But in the end, they managed to overcome the possessed masks, their combined strength and skill prevailing against the lingering magic of the fallen kingdom.

As the dust settled, Raymar, aided by her bat familiar Derek, caught a glimpse of a magnificent creature soaring through the fog: the Aliphox. This majestic, two-headed bird, with its vibrant plumage and insect-like wings, was a creature of legend, known to have served as the royal signal bird for the last Queen of Cyre. Its appearance was both awe-inspiring and ominous, a reminder of the lost glory and lingering magic of the fallen kingdom. The sighting suggested that their journey was far from over, and that even greater mysteries, and perhaps greater dangers, awaited them in the depths of the Mournlands. The Aliphox's presence was a clear sign: they were venturing deeper into the heart of a land steeped in powerful, unpredictable magic, and their every step would be fraught with peril and wonder in equal measure.

___

Following their encounter with the cursed masks and the sighting of the Aliphox, the party finally reached their destination: the abandoned lightning rail station. What they found was not just a derelict station, but the ruins of a suburban town, a haunting testament to the devastation wrought by the Mourning. Long-dead ghouls, once commuters and rail guards, now roamed the area, driven by an insatiable hunger. Adding to the eerie atmosphere were the figures of townsfolk, petrified mid-stride, frozen in time by strange temporal storms that still lingered in the area.

As the party investigated the ruins, they quickly discovered the danger of these time storms. Several members found themselves abruptly frozen, trapped in a moment of suspended time. The battle that ensued was a chaotic dance of avoiding the time storms, while also fending off the relentless ghouls. It became a fight not just for survival, but also for freedom of movement.

It was Buu, the Barbarian, who came up with a clever solution. Using his immense strength, he hurled showers of rocks, revealing safe paths through the treacherous time storms by triggering the freezing effect at a distance. His quick thinking allowed the party to navigate the dangerous area and finally overcome the ghouls.

Despite their victory, the battle had taken its toll. As night fell, a vicious magical storm, a common occurrence in the Mournlands, swept through the area. Forced to take shelter within the ruins of the train station, the party endured a long and harrowing night. The magical maelstrom tore through their makeshift camp, destroying tents, bedrolls, and even damaging some of their weapons. Sleep was a fitful, near-impossible luxury.

Exhausted but alive, the battered party finally limped back to Salvation the following day. They were greeted by an unexpected sight: a traveling circus had arrived in town, its colorful tents and lively music a stark contrast to the grim realities of the Mournlands. The circus, with its promise of entertainment and unusual wares, offered a brief respite from their ordeal. The fanfare and the presence of exotic goods hinted at a chance to resupply, recover, and perhaps even find something to lift their spirits after the harrowing experience in the ruined town. However, the memory of the ghouls, the time storms, and the destructive magical maelstrom lingered, a stark reminder that even within the relative safety of Salvation, the shadow of the Mournlands loomed large. Their journey was far from over, and the dangers they faced were as unpredictable and relentless as the land itself.
