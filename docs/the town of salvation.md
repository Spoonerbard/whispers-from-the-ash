# **I. Salvation's Growth & Tiers of Play**

[[npcs]]'s development will be directly tied to the party's progress and the treasures they bring back. We'll use the concept of **Town Tiers** that roughly correspond to the tiers of character play in D&D 5e.

**Town Tiers:**

- **Tier 1: Outpost (Levels 1-4):** Salvation is a struggling settlement, barely holding on. Resources are scarce, and the focus is on survival.
- **Tier 2: Frontier Town (Levels 5-10):** [[npcs]] has established itself and is starting to grow. More traders arrive, and the town's defenses are improved.
- **Tier 3: Thriving Hub (Levels 11-16):** [[npcs]] becomes a major center for trade and exploration in the region. Specialized services and facilities become available.
- **Tier 4: Regional Power (Levels 17-20):** [[npcs]] has become a powerful and influential settlement, potentially even a political force in [[the Mournland]].

**Triggering Town Growth:**

The party's actions will directly influence [[npcs]]'s growth. Here are some ways to trigger advancement to the next tier:

- **Major Discoveries:** Finding a significant lost city, uncovering a powerful artifact, or defeating a major threat in [[the Mournland]] could all trigger growth.
- **Resource Influx:** Bringing back large quantities of valuable resources (e.g., rare metals, magical components, treasure) will stimulate the economy and attract new settlers.
- **Establishing Trade Routes:** If the party establishes safe and reliable trade routes to other settlements (if any exist in your setting), it will boost [[npcs]]'s prosperity.
- **Reputation:** As the party's fame grows, so too will [[npcs]]'s renown, attracting more people and investment.
- **Population:** [[npcs]]'s population could be a quantifiable metric for growth. Each tier could have a population threshold.
- **Side Quests:** Specific quests related to improving the town (e.g., building a well, repairing the walls, recruiting a skilled craftsman) could contribute to its growth.

# **II. Goods and Services**

**A. Basic Equipment (Tier 1 & Beyond):**

- **Quality:** At Tier 1, most equipment is of poor quality, reflecting the scarcity of resources and skilled labor.
    - **Tents:** One-use only in the harsh [[the mournland]] environment.
    - **Rations:** Bland and unappetizing, but they keep you alive.
    - **Tools:** Prone to breaking or wearing out quickly in the harsh environment of [[The Mournland]]
    - **Weapons & Armor:** Simple weapons (clubs, spears, daggers) and basic armor (leather, hide) are available, but they are often poorly made and offer minimal protection.
- **Availability:** Common items are generally available, but supplies might be limited.
- **Cost:** Prices are relatively high due to scarcity.

**B. Improved Equipment (Tier 2 & Beyond):**

- **Quality:** As [[npcs]] grows, the quality of goods improves.
    - **Tents:** More durable tents become available, lasting for multiple uses.
    - **Rations:** More palatable and nutritious rations are offered.
    - **Tools:** Sturdier and more reliable tools can be purchased.
    - **Weapons & Armor:** Better quality weapons (swords, axes, bows) and armor (studded leather, chain shirts) become available.
- **Availability:** A wider range of items is in stock.
- **Cost:** Prices might remain high but are more justified by the improved quality.

**C. Specialized Equipment (Tier 3 & Beyond):**

- **Quality:** [[npcs]] starts attracting skilled artisans who can craft specialized gear.
    - **Mournland-Adapted Gear:** Equipment specifically designed for the challenges of [[the Mournland]] might become available (e.g., specialized clothing to protect from the elements, gas masks to filter out toxins, goggles to improve visibility in dust storms).
    - **Tools for Specific Trades:** Advanced tools for mining, crafting, or other specialized tasks can be found.
- **Availability:** More specialized items are offered, but they might be made to order or in limited supply.
- **Cost:** Prices are high, reflecting the skill and resources required to make these items.

**D. Magical Crafting Materials (All Tiers):**

- **Availability:** Troxler (or another similar NPC) is the primary source for these materials.
- **Source:** The party must bring back materials from [[the Mournland]]:
    - **Monster Parts:** Scales, claws, fangs, internal organs, etc., from specific creatures.
    - **Rare Minerals:** Unusual ores, crystals, or other geological finds.
    - **Magical Residue:** Dust, powder, or other remnants found at sites of powerful magic.
- **Cost:** The party provides the materials. Troxler charges a fee for their services, based on the complexity of the crafting and their own skill level.
- **No Magic Item Sales:** [[npcs]] _never_ sells pre-made magic items. They must always be crafted or found.

# **III. Town Expansion & Services**

As  grows, new buildings, NPCs, and services will appear:

**Examples:**

**Tier 1: Outpost**

- **General Store:** Sells basic supplies of poor quality.
- **Makeshift Tavern:** A rough-and-tumble establishment serving watered-down drinks and questionable food.
- **Basic Blacksmith:** Can repair simple weapons and armor but cannot craft anything complex.
- **Troxler's Workshop:** A small, cluttered workshop where Troxler tinkers and experiments.
- **Crude Wall:** A basic palisade or earthen rampart offering minimal defense.

**Tier 2: Frontier Town**

- **Improved General Store:** Offers a wider selection of goods, including some higher-quality items.
- **Proper Inn:** Provides more comfortable lodging and better food.
- **Upgraded Blacksmith:** Can craft better quality weapons and armor.
- **Expanded Troxler's Workshop:** Troxler has more space and tools, allowing them to take on more complex projects.
- **Guard Barracks:** A small contingent of guards is stationed in the town, improving security.
- **Sturdy Wall:** A more robust defensive wall is built around the town.

**Tier 3: Thriving Hub**

- **Specialty Shops:** Shops catering to specific needs (e.g., a cartographer, a herbalist,  provider) appear.
- **Larger Inn & Tavern:** Offers a variety of rooms and entertainment.
- **Masterwork Crafters:** Highly skilled artisans set up shop, offering masterwork quality items.
- **Troxler's Emporium:** Troxler's workshop becomes a renowned center for magical crafting.
- **Town Hall:** A central location for town meetings and record-keeping.
- **Reinforced Walls & Watchtowers:** The town's defenses are significantly improved.
- **Temple or Shrine:** A place of worship is established, potentially offering healing services.

**Tier 4: Regional Power**

- **Marketplace:** A bustling hub for trade from across the region.
- **Luxury Accommodations:** High-end inns and other services for wealthy travelers.
- **Guildhalls:** Organizations for various trades and professions are established.
- **Troxler's Academy:** Troxler might establish a school for aspiring artificers.
- **Fortified Keep:** A strong keep is built to protect the town and project its power.
- **Embassy:** Representatives from other settlements or factions might establish embassies in [[npcs]].

**IV. Connecting Growth to Exploration**

- **Blueprints:** The party could find blueprints or schematics in [[the Mournland]]] that unlock new crafting options for Troxler or other artisans in town.
- **Rare Resources:** Certain buildings or upgrades might require specific, rare resources that can only be found in dangerous locations.
- **Lost Knowledge:** The party might uncover lost knowledge or ancient techniques that can be used to improve the town's defenses, technology, or magical capabilities.
- **NPC Recruitment:** The party could encounter skilled NPCs in [[the Mournland]] who can be persuaded to relocate to [[npcs]], bringing their expertise and expanding the town's services.



#  Example Progression: Troxler's Tiers

- **Tier 1 (Outpost):** Troxler can only create common magic items and struggles with repairs. Their workshop is small and poorly equipped.
- **Trigger:** The party brings back a significant amount of Magical Essence and defeats a powerful creature in [[the Mournland]].
- **Tier 2 (Frontier Town):** Troxler can now create uncommon magic items and handle most repairs. They have an expanded workshop with better tools. They can start taking on simple custom orders.
- **Trigger:** The party discovers a lost forge or workshop in [[the Mournland]], providing Troxler with new knowledge and materials. They also establish a trade route with another settlement, bringing in more resources.
- **Tier 3 (Thriving Hub):** Troxler can create rare magic items, and their reputation for quality spreads. They have a large, well-equipped workshop and can handle complex custom orders. They begin experimenting with new techniques.
- **Trigger:** The party recovers a powerful artifact or ancient text containing lost magical knowledge. They also help [[npcs]] establish itself as a major trading hub.
- **Tier 4 (Regional Power):** Troxler is a master artificer, capable of creating very rare and potentially even legendary items. They might establish an academy to train the next generation of artificers.
