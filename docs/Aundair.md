#fantasticlocations 



- **Geography:** A nation in western [[Khorvaire]], known for its rolling plains, fertile farmlands, and arcane academies. It borders [[the Mournland]] to the east. It is a land of magic and learning. It's capital is Fairhaven.
    - **Culture:** Aundairians value intelligence, education, and magical prowess. They are known for their skilled wizards, their sophisticated agriculture, and their refined culture. They are also known for their fierce independence and their strong national pride.
    - **Post-War:** Aundair emerged from [[the Last War]] relatively unscathed, though they lost a significant portion of their territory to the Eldeen Reaches. They are focused on rebuilding their magical infrastructure and maintaining their position as a center of arcane learning. They are ruled by Queen Aurala ir'Wynarn, a direct descendant of [[King Jarot]], the last king of Galifar.
    - **Connection to the Campaign:**
        - **Proximity:** Being close to the Mournland, Aundair is likely concerned about the potential dangers it poses and might be interested in research or expeditions into the wasteland.
        - **Refugees:** Salvation might receive refugees from Aundair, fleeing [[the Mournland]] or seeking opportunities in the frontier town.
        - **[[The Order of the Broken Sky]]:** This organization, with its focus on arcane knowledge and the Mourning, could have ties to Aundair's magical academies or be seeking to recruit Aundairian mages. Aundair could also be secretly funding the Order.
        - **[[House Cannith]]:** Cannith East, led by Jorlanna d'Cannith, is based in Aundair and might have interests in the Mournland.
        - **Questlines:** The party might be hired to escort Aundairian scholars into [[the Mournland]], investigate magical anomalies near the border, or deal with threats from the Mournland that spill over into Aundairian territory.
