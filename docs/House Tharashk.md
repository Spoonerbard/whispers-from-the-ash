[[House Tharashk]] is known as the House of Finding. They're relative newcomers to the  [[dragonmarked houses]] , with their mark appearing about a thousand years ago. Here's what I can tell you about them:

**Focus:**

- **Finding:** Their dragonmark grants them abilities related to locating creatures, objects, and even uncovering secrets. This makes them excellent trackers, investigators, and bounty hunters.
- **Prospecting:** They are skilled in finding valuable resources, from rare minerals to hidden caches.

**Structure:**

Unlike other houses that are more unified, Tharashk is comprised of three main clans:

- **Clan Thuranni:** Focuses on finding people. They often work as bounty hunters and trackers.
- **Clan Ruken:** Specializes in finding objects. They're prospectors, archaeologists, and treasure hunters.
- **Clan Vadalis:** Focuses on uncovering secrets. They tend towards investigation and espionage.

**Notable Persons:**



- **[[Thuranni]]:** A renowned bounty hunter with a reputation for never failing to capture their quarry.
- **[[Ruken]]:** A master prospector who has discovered numerous valuable mineral deposits and lost treasures.
- **[[Vadalis]]:** A skilled investigator who can uncover any secret, no matter how well hidden.

**Important Notes:**

- **Independent:** The clans within House Tharashk are quite independent and often compete with each other.
- **Secretive:** Vadalis especially is known for its secrecy, making its members difficult to identify.
- **Recent Rise:** Tharashk is the youngest Dragonmarked House, so they are still establishing their power and influence.



# **House Tharashk: The Finders of Eberron**

- **Who They Are:** House Tharashk carries the **Mark of Finding** and holds a unique position in Eberron. They are prospectors, inquisitives, and bounty hunters, specializing in finding things that are lost, hidden, or otherwise hard to obtain. They are also known for their employment and training of monstrous races, particularly orcs, half-orcs, and goblinoids. They are a relatively young house, having only come to prominence during the Last War, and are considered by many to be upstarts.
- **Areas of Expertise:**
    - **Prospecting and Mining:** They locate and extract valuable resources, including minerals, dragonshards, and other rare materials.
    - **Bounty Hunting:** They track down and capture fugitives, often operating in areas where traditional law enforcement is weak or nonexistent. They are the best bounty hunters in the world.
    - **Investigation:** They employ inquisitives who specialize in finding lost objects, uncovering secrets, and solving mysteries, using a combination of mundane and magical techniques. They have a rivalry with House Medani in this regard.
    - **Monstrous Labor:** They are known for employing and training monstrous races, using them as laborers, guards, and even soldiers. They often form close bonds with these creatures, treating them as valued members of their house.

**House Tharashk's Role in Your Campaign So Far:**

- **Employers of the Law:** House Tharashk effectively controls law and order in Salvation. They employ the Sheriff and his deputies, ensuring that their interests are protected and that the town remains a stable base of operations. They are the ones who issue the "adventuring licenses."
- **Interest in the Mournland:** House Tharashk sees the Mournland as a vast, untapped resource. They are particularly interested in:
    - **Croydonite:** They see it as a valuable mineral, despite its dangers, and are actively mining it through the Steel Stoners. They may not yet know about the souls trapped within.
    - **Other Minerals:** They are likely searching for other rare minerals and resources exposed by the Mourning.
    - **Salvage:** They are interested in recovering valuable artifacts and lost technology from the ruins of Cyre.
    - **Dragonshards:** While more common in other regions, they would be on the lookout for any dragonshard deposits uncovered by the Mourning.
- **The Steel Stoners:** This group of outlaw dwarven miners is secretly working for House Tharashk, smuggling Croydonite out of the Mournland. They are unaware of the true nature of Croydonite. The party has yet to interact with them, but their presence adds another layer to the intrigue surrounding the town.
- **Sheriff "Hard-Eye" Karr:** The sheriff is a loyal Tharashk employee, tasked with maintaining order in Salvation and protecting the house's interests. He is a gruff but mostly fair enforcer of the law. His main concern is keeping the town safe and orderly.
- **Potential for Conflict:** House Tharashk's activities in the Mournland could bring them into conflict with other factions, such as House Cannith, the Order of the Broken Sky, or even the Lord of Blades. They are also likely to clash with anyone who opposes the exploitation of Croydonite.

**Possible Future Involvement:**

- **Expansion into the Mournland:** House Tharashk might try to expand their operations in the Mournland, establishing new mining outposts or settlements. This could lead to conflict with the native creatures or other factions. They would be very interested in finding a way to safely mine Croydonite, and would likely be willing to pay a high price for any information that could help them do so.
- **The Search for the "Warforged God":** If they learn about the Disciples of the Unwritten Name and their attempt to create a warforged god, they might see it as an opportunity to acquire a powerful weapon or a source of unique magical power. They might even try to recreate the experiment, using Croydonite to enhance the process.
- **Dealing with the Steel Stoners:** House Tharashk might eventually discover the Steel Stoners' theft and send agents to retrieve their stolen property and punish the dwarves. This could create a difficult situation for the party if they have befriended or allied with the miners. Alternatively, the party might be hired to hunt down the Steel Stoners.
- **The Croydonite Market:** House Tharashk is likely involved in the trade of Croydonite, supplying it to various buyers (perhaps unknowingly). The party might uncover this trade and be forced to make a choice about whether to expose it, shut it down, or somehow profit from it.
- **Competition with House Cannith:** The rivalry between House Tharashk and House Cannith could play out in the Mournland, with both houses vying for control of resources and territory. The party might be caught in the middle, forced to choose a side or navigate the complex political landscape.
