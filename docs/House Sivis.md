# **House Sivis: The Speakers of Truth**

- **Who They Are:** House Sivis carries the **Mark of Scribing** and dominates the field of communication across Khorvaire. They are the scribes, notaries, translators, and messengers of Eberron, facilitating communication and ensuring the integrity of the written word. They operate the speaking stones, a network of magical communication devices that allows for instantaneous communication across vast distances. They also maintain a vast library of records and documents in their fortresses. They are known for their impartiality and discretion.
- **Areas of Expertise:**
    - **Communication:** They operate the speaking stones, maintain messenger guilds, and provide translation and interpretation services.
    - **Verification:** They notarize documents, authenticate contracts, and provide legal services related to documentation and testimony.
    - **Information Management:** They maintain vast libraries and archives, and they are experts at cataloging, storing, and retrieving information.
    - **Linguistics:** They are masters of language, both written and spoken, and they have developed unique magical techniques for encoding and decoding messages.
- **Post-Mourning Role:** The Day of Mourning and the destruction of Cyre undoubtedly disrupted House Sivis's operations, particularly their speaking stone network. They have likely been working to rebuild and expand their network in the aftermath of the Last War. They are also likely trying to find any records that survived the destruction of Cyre. They are interested in finding out what happened on the Day of Mourning.
- **Neutrality:** House Sivis traditionally maintains a neutral stance in political conflicts, providing their services to all who can afford them. This neutrality has allowed them to build a reputation for trustworthiness and discretion, which is essential to their business. However, this does not mean that they are not involved in the power struggles of Khorvaire. They are often privy to sensitive information, and their services can be used to manipulate events from behind the scenes.

**House Sivis's Role in Your Campaign So Far:**

- **Limited Presence:** Unlike Cannith and [[House Tharashk|Tharashk]], House Sivis has no established presence in Salvation. This reflects their focus on communication and information rather than direct involvement in exploration or resource extraction. They are unlikely to have any interest in Croydonite.
- **Potential Interest:** However, the events in the Mournland, particularly the mystery surrounding the Day of Mourning, would be of great interest to House Sivis. They would be eager to learn the truth behind the catastrophe, to acquire any surviving records from Cyre, and to understand the unique magical phenomena of the Mournland. They would be very interested in the magical properties of the different types of terrain in the Mournland.
- **Information Network:** House Sivis might be using their existing network of contacts (such as Mara "Fingers" Keen, if she is indeed working for Phiarlan/Thuranni) to gather information about the Mournland. They might also be employing independent agents or contracting with adventurers to investigate specific locations or rumors. They will likely avoid drawing attention to themselves.
- **The Order of the Broken Sky:** Given the Order's focus on knowledge and research, it's possible that House Sivis has a clandestine relationship with them. They might be providing the Order with information or using them as a proxy to investigate the Mournland. They would be careful to keep this relationship secret, to avoid any appearance of bias.

**Possible Future Involvement:**

- **Establishing a Communication Network:** As Salvation grows, House Sivis might see an opportunity to establish a speaking stone station in the town, connecting it to their wider network. This would be a significant development, increasing the town's importance and potentially attracting more settlers and traders. It would also give House Sivis a foothold in the region.
- **Investigating the Mourning:** House Sivis might hire the party to investigate specific locations in the Mournland, seeking to recover lost records, interview survivors (if any can be found), or document the strange phenomena of the region. They would be particularly interested in any information about the Final Sanction. They might task the party with finding out more about the Order of the Broken Sky.
- **The Search for Lost Libraries:** The party might discover the location of a hidden library or archive that belonged to House Sivis before the Mourning. This could contain valuable information about Cyre, the Last War, or the events leading up to the catastrophe. It might also contain records of communication between Cyre and the rest of the world, which could shed light on the events of the Day of Mourning.
- **Competition with Other Factions:** House Sivis's interest in information could bring them into conflict with other factions, such as the Order of the Broken Sky, who are also seeking knowledge about the Mourning. The party might be caught in the middle, forced to choose between competing interests.
- **The Role of Communication:** House Sivis's presence could highlight the importance of communication and information control in the aftermath of the Mourning. They could play a key role in shaping public perception of events or in spreading (or suppressing) rumors and information.
