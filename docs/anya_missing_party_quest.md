**Time Storms: A New Environmental Hazard**

- **Nature:** Time storms are a rare but dangerous phenomenon in the Mournland, a manifestation of the disrupted flow of time caused by the Final Sanction and the amplified chronomancy magic. They are localized, unpredictable, and thankfully short-lived. They are more common in areas with high concentrations of black Croydonite and near the epicentre of the Mourning.
- **Appearance:** A time storm might appear as a swirling vortex of black clouds, crackling with black lightning. The air within the storm might shimmer and distort, and objects might appear to age or de-age rapidly. They could also be heralded by a sudden change in the wind's direction, a drop in temperature, or a strange, metallic smell.
- **Effects:**
    - **De-Aging/Aging:** Creatures caught in a time storm risk being rapidly aged or de-aged. The effect could be temporary or permanent, depending on the intensity of the storm and the duration of exposure.
    - **Time Distortion:** Time might flow differently within the storm. A few minutes inside the storm could equal hours or even days in the outside world. This could disorient and confuse those trapped within.
    - **Temporal Anomalies:** Objects or creatures from different time periods might be briefly pulled into the present by the storm. This could result in encounters with ghosts, echoes of the past, or even creatures from Eberron's prehistory.
    - **Chronal Sickness:** Exposure to a time storm could cause "chronal sickness," a debilitating condition that might manifest as rapid aging, memory loss, or even cellular degeneration. This could be a lingering effect that requires magical healing or specialized treatment.
- **Detection:**
    - **Arcana Check:** A successful DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check might allow a character to recognize the signs of an approaching time storm or to identify an area where one has recently occurred.
    - **Nature Check:** A successful DC 15 Wisdom (Nature) check might allow a character to notice unusual weather patterns or disturbances in the environment that could indicate an impending time storm.
    - **Divination Magic:** Spells like _detect magic_ or _augury_ might provide warnings or insights into the nature of time storms.

**Anya's Party and the Time Storm:**

- **The Event:** Anya's party was caught in a time storm while traveling through the Mournland at night. They were likely seeking shelter or trying to reach a specific location when the storm hit.
- **The Effects:**
    - **De-Aging:** The warforged sorcerer and the dwarven wizard were significantly de-aged, reverting to a physical state from their past. This could have interesting implications for their personalities and memories. They might have fragmented memories of their time before the Mourning or might even revert to a younger mindset. They would also be physically weaker.
    - **Separation:** The party was likely separated during the storm, with Anya being thrown clear or finding shelter while her companions were trapped within the affected area. This explains why she was found alone and half-mad.
    - **Chronal Sickness:** Anya might be suffering from a mild form of chronal sickness, explaining her fragmented memories and her disoriented state. The others might also be suffering from this.
    - **The Language:** The unfamiliar language spoken by the de-aged adventurers could be an older dialect of their respective languages, a language from their childhood, or even a language from another plane of existence if they were briefly shunted through a portal during the storm.
- **The Mystery:** The party will need to piece together what happened to Anya's companions, understand the effects of the time storm, and potentially find a way to reverse the de-aging.

**Plot Hooks and Developments:**

- **The Search for a Cure:** The party might need to find a way to restore Anya's companions to their proper ages. This could involve researching ancient magic, seeking out a powerful chronomancer, or finding a location in the Mournland where time flows differently.
- **The Warforged Sorcerer's Memories:** The warforged sorcerer, due to their constructed nature, might have fragmented memories of the Mourning or even of events that predate their own creation. These memories could offer valuable clues about the Final Sanction or the nature of the Mournland. His memories may slowly return over time. He might also have to relearn his spells.
- **The Dwarven Wizard's Expertise:** The dwarven wizard, even in his de-aged state, might retain some knowledge of dwarven history, craftsmanship, or magic. This knowledge could be crucial in solving a puzzle, overcoming an obstacle, or understanding the secrets of a particular location. He might also have to relearn his spells.
- **The Order of the Broken Sky:** The Order might be very interested in the time storm and its effects, seeing it as a potential key to understanding the manipulation of time. They might try to recruit the party to help them study the phenomenon or to capture one of the de-aged adventurers for research.
- **House Cannith:** House Cannith might also be interested in the warforged sorcerer, particularly if he has memories of Cannith's pre-Mourning activities. They might try to claim him as their property or to extract his memories for their own purposes.
- **The Conspirator:** The time storm could be a part of the Conspirator's plan, a way to manipulate time or to access a specific point in the past. The party might discover evidence that the Conspirator is intentionally creating or manipulating these storms.

**Using the Time Storm in the Campaign:**

- **Environmental Hazard:** Time storms can be a recurring hazard in the Mournland, forcing players to be cautious and to develop strategies for dealing with their effects.
- **Plot Device:** Time storms can be used to introduce new mysteries, alter the course of events, or create unique challenges for the party.
- **Character Development:** Being caught in a time storm could have a profound impact on a character's development, forcing them to confront their past or their potential future.
- **Moral Dilemmas:** The party might encounter other individuals who have been affected by time storms, forcing them to make difficult choices about how to help them or whether to help them at all.
