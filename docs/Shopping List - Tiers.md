# **Tier 1: Outpost - Merchant Wares**

# **I. "Old Man" [[Silas]]'s General Store**

**(Prices are 1.5x standard PHB prices due to the remote location and Silas's )**

- **Adventuring Gear:**
    
    - **Backpack (7 gp):** A small, patched-up backpack made of coarse canvas, barely large enough to hold essential supplies.
    - **Bedroll (4 gp):** A thin, scratchy wool blanket rolled up and tied with twine. It offers minimal comfort and barely any warmth, and it's single use only in the Mournland.
    - **Crowbar (7 gp):** A rusty, slightly bent crowbar.
    - **Hammer (4 gp):** A small, poorly-balanced hammer with a chipped head.
    - **Mess Kit (1 gp):** A dented tin plate, a chipped wooden cup, and a rusty spoon.
    - **Rations (1 gp per day):** Dry, bland, and unappetizing. Hardtack, jerky that's more tough than tender, and dried beans that require hours of soaking.
    - **Rope, hempen (50 feet) (5 gp):** Coarse, frayed rope, prone to snapping under stress.
    - **Tinderbox (3 gp):** A damp tinderbox containing a few pieces of flint and some questionable tinder.
    - **Torch (1 sp):** A crudely made torch that sputters and smokes, providing dim light for about 30 minutes.
    - **Waterskin (1 gp):** A patched and leaky waterskin that can hold about half a gallon of water. It smells faintly of mildew.
    - **Shovel (7 gp):** A small, rusty shovel with a cracked wooden handle.
    - **Climbers Kit (75 gp):** Made up of mostly rope and a few homemade pitons.
    - **Fishing Tackle (4 gp):** A simple hook, line, and sinker.
    - **Healer's Kit (15 gp):** A very basic kit with limited uses.
- **Tools:**
    
    - **Pickaxe (7 gp):** A heavy, unbalanced pickaxe with a dull point, clearly used and poorly maintained.
    - **Miner's Lantern (5 gp):** This is just an oil lamp that is described as a "Miner's Lantern".
- **Weapons:**
    
    - **Club (2 sp):** A thick, knotty branch, roughly shaped into a club.
    - **Dagger (7 gp):** A poorly made, easily dulled dagger.
    - **Spear (4 gp):** A crudely fashioned spear with a fire-hardened wooden point, that is described as a "Mournland Hunting Spear".
    - **Handaxe (15 gp):** A small, dull handaxe.
    - **Light Crossbow (75 gp):** A simple, low-powered crossbow that is prone to jamming.
    - **Crossbow Bolts (1 gp per 5):** Poorly made and unbalanced.
- **Armor:**
    
    - **Leather Armor (30 gp):** Stiff, poorly tanned leather armor that offers minimal protection. It smells strongly of animal fat.
    - **Shield (30 gp):** A small, wooden shield reinforced with strips of scrap metal.
- **Other:**
    
    - **Candles (5 cp each):** Smoky, foul-smelling candles made from rendered animal fat.
    - **Chalk (1 cp per piece):** Dusty, easily crumbled chalk.
    - **Ink (1 oz. bottle) (30 gp):** Watery, faded ink.
    - **Parchment (1 sheet) (3 sp):** Rough, uneven parchment.
    - **Quill (1 cp):** A poorly cut quill that splatters ink.
    - **Clothes, Common (7 gp):** Roughspun, patched-up clothing made from coarse wool or linen.
    - **Blanket (winter) (1 gp):** Thin and itchy.

# **II. [[Mara 'Fingers' Keen]] (Black Market Trader)**

**(Prices are 2x standard PHB prices due to the illicit nature of her goods and her connections)**

- **Weapons:**
    
    - **Dagger (8 gp):** A well-made but clearly used dagger, easily concealed.
    - **Shortsword (20 gp):** A decent quality shortsword, showing signs of wear but still sharp.
    - **Hand Crossbow (150 gp):** A compact hand crossbow, perfect for stealthy attacks.
    - **Blowgun (20 gp):** A slender blowgun, good for delivering poisons.
    - **Thieves' Tools (50 gp):** Stolen from somewhere, used but in good condition.
- **Poisons:**
    
    - **Basic Poison (Vial) (200 gp):** A simple contact or ingested poison that deals 1d4 poison damage on a failed DC 10 Constitution saving throw.
    - **Crawler's Kiss (Vial) (300 gp):** A paralytic poison harvested from a creature in the Mournland. DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on1 a success.
- **Other:**
    
    - **Unidentified Magic Item (50-200 gp):** Mara always has a few unidentified magic items of questionable origin. These could be anything from a _driftglobe_ to a cursed ring. The price varies depending on the item's apparent quality and Mara's assessment of its potential value.
    - **Forged Documents (50 gp):** Mara can create forged documents, such as travel papers, letters of introduction, or even an "official" House seal.
    - **Information:** Mara is a valuable source of information, but her prices are steep, and she's not always trustworthy. She might charge anywhere from 10 to 100 gp for a piece of information, depending on its value and how much she thinks the party can afford to pay.
    - **Lockpicks (25 gp):** A set of lockpicks of decent quality.

# **III. [[Cleric Kaelen 'kai' Meadowlight]] (Cleric - Healing Services)**

**(Prices are standard PHB prices, but his services are limited by his abilities and fear)**

- **Services:**
    - _Cure Wounds_ (1st level): 10 gp (Heals 1d8 + his Wisdom modifier)
    - _Lesser Restoration_: 50 gp (Can only cure basic poisons and diseases)
    - _Identify_: 20 gp
    - Herbal remedies: 5 gp (Non-magical healing, equivalent to a Healer's Kit application, takes ten minutes to apply)
    - Can mend clothing, and other cloth items for a small fee.

# **V. [[Grelga]] (Barkeep at  [[the crooked tankard]] )**

- **Food:**
    
    - Stew (3 cp per bowl): A thick, hearty stew made with whatever ingredients are available.
    - Roasted Meat (5 cp per serving): Usually some kind of small game, roasted over the fire.
    - Bread (1 cp per loaf): A hard, crusty bread that's good for dipping in stew.
- **Drinks:**
    
    - Weak Ale (4 cp per tankard): A thin, watery ale that's barely alcoholic.
    - "Mournland Moonshine" (1 sp per shot): A potent, foul-tasting liquor that's guaranteed to knock you off your feet. (DC 8 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for one hour, disadvantage on all attack rolls, and ability checks for the duration).
    - Water (1 cp per cup): Water is available for purchase, it's almost drinkable.
- **Lodging:**
    
    - Common Room Floor: 1 sp per night.
    - Shared Room (4 beds): 5 sp per night per person.

# **VI. "Mama" [[Elara Thorne]] (Landlady)**

- **Services:**
    - Rent of Shack E: 5 gp per week (for the whole party).
    - Home-cooked meals: 5 sp per meal (simple but nourishing).
    - Laundry: 1 sp per item.



**VII. Other:** There are no other shops in Salvation at this tier.

**Remember**: These are just starting points. You can adjust prices, add or remove items, and change the quality of goods based on the specific circumstances of your campaign. As the town grows and the players progress, these merchants will expand their inventories and offer more advanced goods and services.
