# Cleric Services 



**Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight: Centaur Cleric of Lamarr (Life Domain)**

**Tier 1: Outpost**

**Services:**

- **Basic Healing:**
    - _Cure Wounds_ (1st level): 10 gp (Heals 1d8 + his Wisdom modifier)
    - _Healing Word_ (1st level): 10 gp
- **Minor Restoration:**
    - _Lesser Restoration_: 50 gp (Can only cure basic poisons and diseases, not those inflicted by magical effects from the Mournland).
- **Spiritual Guidance:**
    - _Guidance_ (Cantrip): Free (offered to those who seem genuinely in need)
    - _Thaumaturgy_ (Cantrip): Free
- **Identification:**
    - _Identify_: 20 gp (Can help identify magical effects and curses, but his inexperience might limit his accuracy).
- **Other:**
    - Mending: Simple repairs to clothing and cloth items. (1 gp per item)

**Limitations:**

- Kai is inexperienced and still fearful. He can only cast a limited number of spells per day and is hesitant to use magic that might attract unwanted attention. His connection to his deity might be weakened in the Mournland.
- He can, at this point, only cast up to 1st level spells.
- He relies heavily on readily available herbs and materials for his remedies.

**Items for Sale:**

- **Holy Water (25 gp per flask):** Created by Kai through a simple ritual. It's effective against undead and fiends, but the potency is somewhat diminished due to his inexperience and the Mournland's influence. Deals 1d4 radiant damage to undead and fiends, instead of the usual 2d6.
- **Herbal Poultices (5 gp each):** These can be applied to wounds to provide mundane healing, restoring 1d4 hit points after one minute. They can only be used once per day.
- **Antitoxin (50 gp per vial):** A basic antitoxin, effective against common poisons but not against magical or potent ones.
- **Incense of Arawai (10 gp per stick):** This incense, when burned, creates a sense of calm and peace. It provides advantage on saving throws against fear effects for one hour. While it is lit, it provides a faint light, in a 5ft radius.
- **Symbol of Arawai (5 gp):** A small, wooden holy symbol of Arawai, carved by Kai. It doesn't have any inherent magical properties but serves as a focus for prayer and a symbol of hope.
