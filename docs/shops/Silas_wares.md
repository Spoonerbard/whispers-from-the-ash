**Services:**


- **Rumors and Information:** He's a source of rumors and information about the surrounding area, but he's not always reliable and often exaggerates the dangers to discourage competition. He might charge a small fee for information, or he might trade it for favors or valuable items.
- **Appraisal:** [[Silas]] can appraise items for a fee (5 gp per item). He has a surprisingly good eye for value, despite his pretense of ignorance.

**Catalogue Ordering Service:**

- [[Silas]] has a tattered catalogue from a pre-Mourning merchant house.
- Players can order any simple or martial weapon from the PHB.
- Delivery time is one session per item.
- 50% non-refundable deposit required.
- Pricing: PHB Price + 50%, plus an additional 10-20% markup for rare or in-demand items.
- [[Silas]] will not order in any living creatures.
