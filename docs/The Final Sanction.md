#lore/history 



**The Final Sanction: A Desperate Gamble**

**The Goal:** To forcibly close [[the eldritch portal]] opened by [[the Disciples of the Unwritten Name]] before it could consume Eberron.

**The Method:** [[The cabal]] of powerful mages, clerics, and potentially other beings, employed a ritual of immense power that drastically accelerated time _within a localized area_ around the portal. Their intent was to speed up the portal's lifecycle, causing it to collapse and close in on itself before the horrors within could fully emerge.

**Components of the Ritual:**

1. **The Anchors:**
    
    - The cabal likely used powerful artifacts as anchors to focus and contain the time acceleration. These could have been relics of ancient power, specially constructed magical devices, or even [[the Singing Stones]] 
    - The anchors might have been strategically placed around the epicenter of the ritual, forming a circle or other geometric pattern designed to channel and control the time magic.
2. **The Conduit:**
    
    - A powerful being or object might have served as a conduit for the time-acceleration magic. This could have been:
        - **A willing (or unwilling) spellcaster:** A member of the cabal with exceptional magical ability, channeling the ritual's energy through their own body.
        - **A captured creature of time:** Perhaps a being from the plane of Dal Quor (the Region of Dreams) or a powerful chronomancer, forced to manipulate time against their will.
        - **The Progenitor Dragon Bone:** The very bone that created the portal could have been used to reverse the effect, though this would have been incredibly risky.
3. **The Invocation:**
    
    - The cabal would have used a complex incantation, a powerful spell unlike anything seen before or since. This spell would have drawn upon the combined magical energies of the anchors, the conduit, and the casters themselves.
    - The invocation might have involved invoking the names of powerful deities or even making a pact with a being of immense power, such as a Lord of Dust or a powerful archfey.

**The Unforeseen Interaction:**

The plan was to accelerate time only within a small, contained area. However, the cabal underestimated the volatile interaction between the time-acceleration magic and the eldritch energies of the portal.

- **Resonance and Amplification:** The portal, being a tear in the fabric of reality, resonated with the time magic in an unexpected way. Instead of simply collapsing, the portal acted as a lens, amplifying the time-acceleration effect and broadcasting it outwards in a catastrophic wave.
- **The Time Wave:** This wave of distorted time washed over the entire nation of Cyre. Within the affected area, time accelerated exponentially – one day became the equivalent of 100,000 years. This effect contained the explosion of energy from the portal's closure.
- **Uneven Distribution:** The time acceleration wasn't uniform. Areas closer to the epicenter experienced the most extreme effects, while those further away were subjected to slightly less drastic time distortions. This could explain the varied and unpredictable nature of [[the Mournland]]]'s landscape and its inhabitants.

**The Consequences: 100,000 Years in a Day**

The accelerated time within [[Cyre]]had a devastating and bizarre impact:

1. **Geological Upheaval:**
    
    - **Instant Mountains:** Mountain ranges rose and eroded in the blink of an eye.
    - **Canyons Carved:** Rivers carved deep canyons through the landscape, then dried up or changed course.
    - **Mineral Formation:** The intense pressure and heat, combined with the magical energies, created new and strange minerals, including Croydonite, which formed as the land itself absorbed the souls and the magical backlash.
2. **Climate Cataclysm:**
    
    - **Extreme Weather:** Weather patterns went haywire. Massive storms, droughts, and floods ravaged the land in rapid succession.
    - **Shifting Biomes:** Entire ecosystems shifted and changed in an instant, creating bizarre juxtapositions of desert, jungle, and arctic landscapes.
    - **The Dead-Gray Mist:** The thick, gray mist that blankets [[the Mournland]] could be a result of this accelerated climate change, a perpetual storm of dust and magical residue.
3. **Evolutionary Mayhem:**
    
    - **Hyper-Evolution:** Some creatures underwent incredibly rapid and bizarre evolutionary changes, adapting to the new, harsh environment in grotesque and unpredictable ways. This resulted in the mutated and monstrous creatures that now roam [[the Mournland]].
    - **Magical Fusion:** Other creatures were fused with raw magic or Croydonite, becoming living spells or elementals, warped and twisted by the chaotic energies.
    - **Extinction:** Many species were simply unable to adapt to the rapid changes and were instantly wiped out, their fossilized remains now littering the landscape, often found in bizarre and contorted positions.
4. **The Landscape of the Soul:**
    
    - **Psychic Scars:** The psychic trauma of the event, combined with the presence of shattered souls in Croydonite, left deep psychic scars on the land. This manifests as areas of intense emotional energy, haunted locations, and the "whispers" of the dead that can be heard throughout [[the Mournland]].
    - **Reality Distortion:** The fabric of reality itself was weakened by the time distortion, leading to areas where the laws of physics are bent or broken, creating the strange environmental hazards we discussed earlier.

**The Legacy of the Final Sanction:**

- **The Cabal's Fate:** Most, if not all, of [[the cabal]] members likely perished in the ritual, their bodies and minds consumed by the immense energies they unleashed or they were driven mad and are in hiding.
- **Lost Knowledge:** The specific knowledge of how the Final Sanction was performed was lost, making it difficult, if not impossible, to replicate.
- **A World Scarred:** [[[[the Mournland]]]] stands as a testament to the destructive potential of unchecked magic and the terrible cost of even the most desperate measures.
- **The Order's Mission:** The Order of the Broken Sky, founded by someone who may have been part of the original cabal, now seeks to understand what happened and potentially find a way to heal the land or prevent a similar catastrophe.

**Clues and Discoveries:**

- **[[Remnants of the Ritual]]:** The party might discover the remnants of the anchors used in the Final Sanction, perhaps still humming with residual time energy. These could be incredibly dangerous to handle.
- **[[Distorted Creatures]]:** Studying the mutated creatures of [[the Mournland]] could provide clues about the nature of the time acceleration and the types of magic used in the ritual.
- **[[Fossilized Moments]]:** The party might find areas where the rapid changes have fossilized moments in time – a tree that grew to an enormous size in an instant before being petrified, a creature caught mid-transformation, or even a group of people frozen in their final moments of terror.
- **[[Time Anomalies]]:** The party could encounter areas where time is still distorted, experiencing localized time loops, accelerated aging, or glimpses into the past or future.

**Plot Hooks:**

- **The Search for [[the Anchors]]:** [[The Order of the Broken Sky]] might task the party with finding the remnants of the anchors, believing they hold the key to understanding the Final Sanction.
- **[[The Time-Lost City]]:** Rumors of a city where time flows differently could lead the party to a location where they can experience the effects of the time acceleration firsthand.
- **The Chronomancer's Legacy:** The party discovers the notes or [[the hidden laboratory of a chronomancer]] who was involved in the Final Sanction (either as a member of [[the cabal]] or as [[the conduit]]), revealing more details about the ritual and its consequences.
