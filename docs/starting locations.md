[[npcs]] 



**Remember:** Each of these locations is within a square adjacent to Sanctuary, meaning travel is relatively safe and cost-free, encouraging early exploration.

**I. Desert Themed**

- **1. The Whispering Dunes:**
    - **Description:** A vast expanse of sand dunes where the wind constantly carries whispers and moans, said to be the voices of those lost in the Mourning. The area is rumored to contain hidden oases and buried ruins.
    - **Potential Encounters:**
        - **Sand Scavengers:** A nomadic tribe eking out a living in the dunes, potentially hostile to outsiders.
        - **Dune Stalkers:** Reskinned Giant Lizards or other desert predators adapted to [[the Mournland]].
        - **Sand Fire Worm Swarms:** As established in your earlier encounter.
        - **Ghostly Apparitions:** Flickering images of those who died in the Mourning, drawn to the whispers in the sand.
    - **Challenges:**
        - **Navigation:** The shifting dunes make it easy to get lost.
        - **Dehydration:** The intense heat increases water consumption.
        - **Sandstorms:** Periodic sandstorms reduce visibility and can inflict damage.
    - **Rewards:**
        - **Hidden Oasis:** A source of fresh water and rare desert plants.
        - **Buried Ruin:** A pre-Mourning structure containing valuable artifacts or historical documents.
        - **Lost Caravan:** The remains of a merchant caravan, potentially containing valuable goods.
- **2. The Sunken Bazaar:**
    - **Description:** The ruins of a once-thriving marketplace, now partially buried beneath the sand. Crumbling stalls, toppled columns, and faded mosaics hint at its former glory.
    - **Potential Encounters:**
        - **Animated Constructs:** Clay or stone golems, remnants of the bazaar's security, still patrolling the ruins.
        - **Dust Mephits:** Mischievous elementals that enjoy playing tricks on travelers.
        - **Carrion Crawlers:** Scavenging in the debris.
    - **Challenges:**
        - **Unstable Structures:** The ruins are crumbling and prone to collapse.
        - **Hidden Traps:** The bazaar might still contain functional traps designed to deter thieves.
        - **Illusions:** Residual magic could create illusory stalls or merchants, tempting the unwary.
    - **Rewards:**
        - **Pre-Mourning Coins:** Hidden stashes of gold and silver.
        - **Trade Goods:** Intact or partially damaged goods from the marketplace (pottery, textiles, spices).
        - **Merchant Records:** Documents detailing trade routes, valuable commodities, or the names of influential figures from before the Mourning.

**II. Mountain Themed**

- **3. The Scarred Peak:**
    - **Description:** A lone, jagged mountain scarred by the Mourning. Strange, crystalline formations jut out from its slopes, and the air crackles with residual energy.
    - **Potential Encounters:**
        - **Mountain Lions (reskinned as mutated, more aggressive versions):** Predators adapted to the harsh environment.
        - **Croydonite-Tainted Galeb Duhr:** Animated boulders infused with Croydonite, hostile and unpredictable.
        - **Harpies:** Their songs warped into maddening screeches.
    - **Challenges:**
        - **Difficult Terrain:** Steep slopes, treacherous paths, and falling rocks.
        - **Altitude Sickness:** The peak's elevation could cause altitude sickness (exhaustion, disadvantage on certain checks).
        - **Croydonite Exposure:** Areas with high concentrations of [[Croydonite]] pose a risk of contamination.
    - **Rewards:**
        - **Croydonite Deposits:** A rich source of raw Croydonite, but dangerous to mine.
        - **Rare Minerals:** Valuable ores and gemstones exposed by the Mourning.
        - **Observation Point:** The peak offers a panoramic view of the surrounding[[mournland]], potentially revealing other points of interest.
- **4. The Howling Caves:**
    - **Description:** A network of caves within a mountainside, known for the eerie howling sounds that emanate from within. The caves are rumored to be haunted or inhabited by dangerous creatures.
    - **Potential Encounters:**
        - **Stirges:** Mutated by the Mourning, these stirges are larger and more aggressive.
        - **Shadow Mastiffs:** Their howls echoing through the tunnels.
        - **Lost Miners:** The spirits of miners trapped during the Mourning, now haunting the caves.
    - **Challenges:**
        - **Darkness:** The caves are pitch black, requiring light sources to navigate.
        - **Confusing Layout:** The cave system is a labyrinth, making it easy to get lost.
        - **Cave-Ins:** The unstable environment poses a risk of cave-ins.
    - **Rewards:**
        - **Ancient Mineshaft:** A pre-Mourning mineshaft containing valuable ores or forgotten mining equipment.
        - **Hidden Chamber:** A secret chamber containing the remains of a hermit or a hidden shrine.
        - **Fungal Garden:** A cavern where strange, bioluminescent fungi grow, potentially possessing useful properties.

**III. Water Themed**

- **5. The Weeping Mire:**
    - **Description:** A stagnant marsh formed around a corrupted water source. The air is thick with the smell of decay, and strange, mutated plants grow in the murky water.
    - **Potential Encounters:**
        - **Bullywugs (corrupted):** Twisted by the Mourning, these bullywugs are more aggressive and might worship a dark entity.
        - **Shambling Mounds:** Composed of mutated vegetation and animated by the corrupted water.
        - **Will-o'-Wisps:** Luring travelers to their doom in the mire.
    - **Challenges:**
        - **Difficult Terrain:** The marsh is difficult to traverse, with areas of deep mud and hidden pools of water.
        - **Disease:** The stagnant water carries diseases (e.g., sewer plague).
        - **Poisonous Plants:** Contact with certain plants could cause poisoning or other harmful effects.
    - **Rewards:**
        - **Rare Herbs:** Unique plants with medicinal or alchemical properties grow in the mire.
        - **Sunken Relic:** An artifact from before the Mourning, lost in the murky depths.
        - **Hidden Grotto:** A secluded grotto that serves as a refuge for a reclusive NPC or a hidden spring of relatively pure water.
- **6. The Ghost Coast:**
    - **Description:** A stretch of coastline haunted by the spirits of sailors and fishermen who perished in the Mourning. The area is shrouded in perpetual fog, and the sound of crashing waves is mixed with mournful cries.
    - **Potential Encounters:**
        - **Specters and Ghosts:** Restless spirits of the drowned, potentially hostile or seeking aid.
        - **Giant Crabs (mutated):** Larger, more aggressive, and possibly with strange abilities.
        - **Waterlogged Zombies:** Animated corpses of sailors, rising from the depths.
    - **Challenges:**
        - **Limited Visibility:** The fog makes navigation difficult and provides cover for enemies.
        - **Treacherous Tides:** The coastline might have strong currents or hidden underwater hazards.
        - **Psychic Residue:** The intense sorrow of the drowned creates a psychic resonance that can affect the characters' minds (e.g., disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws, vulnerability to psychic damage).
    - **Rewards:**
        - **Shipwreck:** The remains of a ship that crashed during the Mourning, potentially containing valuable cargo or the captain's log.
        - **Hidden Cove:** A secluded cove where smugglers or pirates once operated, now containing hidden treasure or a secret escape route.
        - **Message in a Bottle:** A bottle containing a message from someone lost in the Mourning, offering a clue to a mystery or a plea for help.

**IV. Forest Themed**

- **7. The Silent Grove:**
    - **Description:** A small, eerily silent forest where the trees are petrified and the ground is covered in a layer of ash. No animals or insects can be heard, and the air is heavy with a sense of dread.
    - **Potential Encounters:**
        - **Needle Blights:** Awakened by the unnatural silence.
        - **Shadows:** Drawn to the stillness of the grove.
        - **Treant (corrupted):** A once-noble treant, now twisted and hostile due to the Mourning.
    - **Challenges:**
        - **Oppressive Silence:** The unnatural silence imposes disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and can be mentally unsettling (e.g., requiring Wisdom saving throws to avoid gaining levels of exhaustion).
        - **Petrified Forest:** The petrified trees are difficult to climb and might crumble if disturbed.
        - **Hidden Threats:** The silence makes it difficult to detect approaching enemies.
    - **Rewards:**
        - **Ancient Shrine:** A hidden shrine dedicated to a nature deity, potentially containing a powerful artifact or a source of restorative magic.
        - **Petrified Creatures:** The preserved remains of animals or people caught in the Mourning, potentially offering clues to the past or containing valuable items.
        - **Heart of the Grove:** A central location within the grove where the silence is most intense, potentially offering a unique opportunity for meditation or communion with nature spirits.
- **8. The Tanglewood:**
    - **Description:** A dense, overgrown forest where the vegetation has been warped and mutated by the Mourning. The trees are twisted into unnatural shapes, and strange, bioluminescent fungi grow in the shadows.
    - **Potential Encounters:**
        - **Giant Spiders (mutated):** Larger, faster, and possibly with venomous bites that cause strange effects.
        - **Blights:** Twisted, animated plants that attack any intruders.
        - **Displacer Beasts:** Their displacement abilities are enhanced by the strange magic of the forest.
    - **Challenges:**
        - **Difficult Terrain:** The dense undergrowth and twisted trees make movement difficult.
        - **Disorienting Spores:** The air is filled with spores that can cause hallucinations or other sensory distortions.
        - **Hidden Paths:** The forest is full of hidden paths and secret trails, some leading to danger, others to rewards.
    - **Rewards:**
        - **Rare Herbs:** The mutated flora might include rare and potent herbs useful for potion-making or other alchemical purposes.
        - **Lost Explorer's Camp:** The remains of a camp belonging to an explorer who ventured into the Tanglewood and never returned, potentially containing valuable notes or equipment.
        - **Fey Crossing:** A hidden portal to the Feywild, warped and unpredictable due to the Mourning's influence.

These are just a few ideas to get you started. Feel free to mix and match elements, add your own twists, and tailor the locations to your players' actions and interests. Remember to provide opportunities for roleplaying, combat, exploration, and puzzle-solving in each location. The goal is to make each square on the map feel unique and memorable, encouraging players to explore every corner of [[the Mournland]].
