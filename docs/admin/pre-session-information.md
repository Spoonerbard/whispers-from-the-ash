**Subject: Welcome to the Mournland - Your West Marches Adventure Awaits\!**

Hey everyone,

Thanks for your interest in joining our D\&D 5e West Marches campaign\! I'm excited to explore the mysteries and dangers of the Mournland with you.

This campaign will be a bit different from a traditional, ongoing storyline. In a West Marches game, the focus is on **player-driven exploration** in a shared world. Think of it like an adventuring guild based in the town of **Salvation**, poised on the edge of the Mournland – a vast, magically-warped wasteland that was once the magically and technologically advance nation of Cyre.

**Here's how it works:**

  * **You decide when and where to explore:** We'll have a map of the region, and you'll choose which areas to investigate. Each session will be a self-contained expedition into the Mournland.
  * **Flexible scheduling:** We'll aim for sessions every two weeks, alternating between Saturdays and Sundays. We will decide the day two sessions in advance. The real-world time will limit the scope of in-game exploration. When we hit 5.30 pm GMT, that's when the session will end.
  * **Sign-up System:**  **Sign-up for each session will be on a first-come, first-served basis.** I'll send out a sign-up sheet before each session.
  * **Character continuity:** You'll keep the same character throughout the campaign, leveling them up as you complete sessions.
  * **Open table:** Not everyone needs to be at every session. You can jump in and out based on your availability. Each session will have a recap at the beginning so that players who missed the last one can get caught up.

**Character Creation:**

  * **System:** We'll be using the **2014 D\&D 5e mechanics.**
  * **Level:** You'll start at **Level 3**.
  * **Stats:** Generate your ability scores by rolling **4d6, dropping the lowest die, and arranging the results as you choose.** You may **reroll 1s, or take the average.**
  * **Advancement:** You will gain a level every three sessions played.
  * **Sourcebooks:** We'll be sticking to official published material. **No homebrew content, please.** If you want to use any options from *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*, please let me know in advance so we can discuss it.
  * **Starting Gear:** You'll start with standard starting equipment for your class, plus **400 GP** to spend on additional gear. You can spend this gold before the first session. Just ask if you need help with prices, especially for items unique to Eberron, like those from House Cannith.
  * **Magic Item:** You also get to choose **one common magic item** to start with. Let me know your choice, and we'll make sure it fits within the campaign's setting.
  * **Backstories:** You can use originally created

**House Rules:**

  * **Encumbrance & Rations:** I won't be tracking these meticulously unless things get unrealistic. However, be mindful of what your character can realistically carry, especially when venturing into the Mournland.
  * **Critical Hits:** On a critical hit, roll the relevant damage dice, add your modifiers, and then multiply the total by two.
  * **Fumbles:**  Rolling a natural 1 on an attack roll will result in a fumble. This might mean dropping your weapon, hitting an ally by mistake, or some other unfortunate (and potentially humorous) consequence. I'll determine the effect based on the situation.
  * **Short Rests:** You can spend hit dice during a short rest to regain hit points. Roll the hit dice you choose to spend, and add your Constitution modifier to each roll. The total is how many hit points you regain.
  * **Long Rests:** You regain all your hit dice on a long rest. However, be warned: long resting in the Mournland is extremely dangerous without a secure location.
  * **Materials and Components:** Unless otherwise stated, assume that your character has any mundane equipment they need. Feel free to ask if you are unsure.

**Safety and Inclusivity:**

  * **X-Card:** We will be using the **X-Card** safety tool. If anything makes you uncomfortable, simply say "X-Card" or hold up an "X" (you can use a physical card, a piece of paper, or type it in the chat). We'll stop, adjust, and move on, no questions asked. Your comfort and safety are paramount.
  * **Content Rating:** This game is rated for an audience of **15+**. We'll be dealing with mature themes, including violence, war, and potentially some morally ambiguous situations.
  * **Lines and Veils:** If there are any specific topics or themes you'd like to avoid (lines) or handle off-screen (veils), please let me know privately. I want to ensure everyone feels safe and comfortable at the table.

**Venue Rules:**

  * **No Outside Food or Drinks:** Please be aware that the venue does not allow outside food or drinks. They have refreshments available for purchase.

**A Note About Lucy:**

My dog, Lucy, will usually be present during our sessions. She's very friendly and well-behaved. However, if anyone has allergies or is uncomfortable around dogs for any reason, please let me know, and I'll make sure Lucy stays home. She is more than happy to do so.

**New and Experienced Players Welcome:**

We have a mix of experienced and new players, and **new players are always welcome\!** Don't be afraid to ask questions – we're all here to learn and have fun together.

**The Setting:**

Our game takes place in **Eberron**, a world of magic, technology, and intrigue. We'll be based in **Salvation**, a small, struggling town on the border of the **Mournland**. A century ago, the nation of Cyre was destroyed in a magical cataclysm known as the Mourning. Now, it's a wasteland filled with strange creatures, twisted landscapes, and lost treasures. But beware, the Mournland is a dangerous place, and few who enter return unscathed.

**Next Steps:**

1.  **Create your character:** Send me your character sheet before our first session, along with a brief backstory and their reason for being in Salvation. What do they hope to find in the Mournland?
2.  **Join us for Session Zero:** We'll have a short Session Zero to discuss the campaign in more detail, answer any questions, and introduce our characters to each other.
3.  **Prepare for Adventure:**  Get ready to explore the Mournland, uncover its secrets, and face its dangers\!

I'm really looking forward to playing with you all. If you have any questions, feel free to ask\!
