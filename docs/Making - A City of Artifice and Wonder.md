**Before the Mourning:**

- **A Hub of Innovation:** Making was renowned throughout [[Cyre]] and across [[Khorvaire]] as a center of magical innovation, artistic expression, and cutting-edge artifice. It was a city where magic and technology seamlessly blended, pushing the boundaries of what was considered possible. It was a rival to the magical might of the city of Sharn.
- **[[The City of Making]]:** The city's name reflected its primary focus: the creation of new and wondrous things. It was a place where artificers, mages, and inventors from all over Khorvaire gathered to share ideas, collaborate on projects, and bring their most ambitious creations to life. It may have even been home to a creation forge, though it would have probably been destroyed.
- **Architectural Marvel:** Making was a city of soaring towers, intricate bridges, and gravity-defying structures, all powered by a sophisticated network of magical energy. Buildings might have shifted and reconfigured themselves based on the needs of their inhabitants, and the city was likely illuminated by a dazzling array of ever-bright lanterns and other magical lights. The city was known for having buildings made of a crystal like material, that was both beautiful and extremely strong.
- **Artistic Flourish:** The people of Making valued not only functionality but also aesthetics. The city was filled with public art, from animated sculptures to mesmerizing light displays. Even the most utilitarian objects were often designed with an artistic flourish.
- **House Cannith's Influence:** House Cannith had a strong presence in Making, and the city was likely home to some of their most advanced research facilities and workshops. It was a place where they tested new prototypes, experimented with new magical techniques, and pushed the boundaries of warforged design. The creation forge, if it existed would have belonged to House Cannith.
- **A Center of Learning:** Making was home to prestigious magical academies, attracting students and scholars from across Khorvaire. These institutions fostered a spirit of collaboration and innovation, driving the city's rapid advancements. The city was known for being one of the most scholarly cities of it's age.

**The Disciples of the Unwritten Name in Making:**

- **Secret Operations:** The Disciples of the Unwritten Name, the radical sect of the Becoming God cult, operated secretly within Making. They were drawn to the city's advanced magical knowledge and its concentration of skilled artificers.
- **Hidden Base:** They likely had a hidden base of operations somewhere within the city, perhaps disguised as a legitimate workshop or research facility, or even integrated into the lower levels of the city, where few dared to explore.
- **Recruitment:** They might have recruited members from among the city's artificers and mages, particularly those who were dissatisfied with the প্রচলিত limits of magical research or who were drawn to the idea of creating a new god. They would have been careful only to recruit those they felt they could trust.
- **The Ritual Site:** The ritual that opened the eldritch portal likely took place in a hidden chamber within their base or at a location of magical significance within or near the city. They may have been trying to use the creation forge in their ritual.

**The Day of Mourning:**

- **Ground Zero:** Making was at the epicenter of the magical explosion that created [[the Mournland]]. The city bore the full brunt of the time-accelerated cataclysm.
- **Instantaneous Devastation:** The city and its inhabitants were likely subjected to the most extreme effects of the time distortion. Buildings would have crumbled to dust in an instant, the landscape would have been radically reshaped, and the people of Making would have been subjected to the most gruesome fates.
- **The Portal's Remnants:** The site where the portal was opened would likely be the most heavily scarred and distorted area within [[the Mournland]], a place of unimaginable horror and potent, chaotic magic. It would be located in the most dangerous part of [[the Mournland]].

**Making in the Present:**

- **A Ruin Beyond Recognition:** What remains of Making is likely unrecognizable, a twisted landscape of fused metal, shattered crystal, and petrified remains. It's a place where the laws of physics are broken and the echoes of the past are সবচেয়ে strong.
- **A Source of Power and Peril:** The ruins of Making are undoubtedly one of the most dangerous places in [[the Mournland]], but also potentially one of the most valuable. The city likely contains powerful artifacts, lost knowledge, and significant [[Croydonite]] deposits, including the remnants of the Progenitor Dragon bone. However, many would fear the dangers of such a place.
- **A Graveyard of Innovation:** The twisted remnants of Making's magical creations might still be active, posing a threat to explorers. Imagine animated constructs driven mad by the time distortion, or magical defenses that have become unstable and unpredictable. The creation forge, if it exists might have it's creative energies twisted into a destructive force, spewing out a torrent of molten metal.
- **Psychic Echoes:** The psychic residue of the Mourning would be particularly strong in Making, with the screams and whispers of the dead echoing through the ruins. The area might be haunted by powerful specters or other undead, remnants of those who perished in the initial cataclysm.

**Possible Locations within Making:**

- **The Shattered Academy:** The ruins of one of Making's prestigious magical academies, now a twisted labyrinth of collapsed lecture halls, warped laboratories, and haunted libraries.
- **The Crystal Gardens:** A once-beautiful garden where the plants were made of crystal. Now, it's a deadly maze of razor-sharp shards and mutated, carnivorous flora.
- **The Foundry of Nightmares:** A former House Cannith research facility where the Disciples of the Unwritten Name might have conducted their experiments. It could be filled with twisted warforged, unstable magical constructs, and remnants of their dark rituals. It could be the location of the creation forge.
- **The Portal Scar:** The exact location where the eldritch portal was opened. This would be the most dangerous and distorted part of the city, a place where reality itself is unraveling.
- **The Plaza of Lost Voices:** A once-bustling public square, now a desolate space where the echoes of the city's final moments can still be heard.
