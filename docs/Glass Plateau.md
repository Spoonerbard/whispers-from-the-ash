## Geography[](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau?veaction=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Geography")

It is a vast highland plateau or tableland, composed entirely of glass. Although it is overall a smooth and flat expanse, there are also spikes, spires, peaks, and other formations of sharp and jagged glass protruding from the floor in places seemingly at random.[[1]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-FN-p83-1)[[2]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECS-p191-2)[[3]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-EPG-p130-3)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECG-p92-4)[[5]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-RftLW-p221-5) They resemble blown glass and can grow to huge sizes[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECG-p92-4) and some are bewildering and incomprehensible in shape.[[3]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-EPG-p130-3) Near the center, the glass is black, being obsidian, but as one gets closer to the edges, the glass starts to get lighter in color and more transparent, becoming pale. At the edge of the plateau are jagged cliffs where the glass is nearly white. These separate the lowlands from the top of the plateau[[1]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-FN-p83-1)[[2]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECS-p191-2)[[5]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-RftLW-p221-5) and stand around 100 feet (30 meters) high.[[1]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-FN-p83-1) Occasionally, deep in the dark obsidian there can be glimpsed fiery flashes of light[[2]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECS-p191-2)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECG-p92-4) and liquid glass and even lava can be exuded near the edges.[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECG-p92-4)

To the north lies the Field of Living Spells, to the southwest is the [Field of Ruins](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Field_of_Ruins "Field of Ruins"), and to the east is the [Crimson Water](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Crimson_Water "Crimson Water"). The Glass Plateau completely cuts the Saerun Road once linking Making to [Tronish](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Tronish "Tronish") in the east and [Lyrenton](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Lyrenton "Lyrenton") in the west.[[1]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-FN-p83-1)[[2]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECS-p191-2)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECG-p92-4)

## History[](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau?veaction=edit&section=2 "Edit section: History")

Some theorize that the [Mourning](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Mourning "Mourning") itself began in Making, at the center of what became the Glass Plateau. On the [Day of Mourning](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Day_of_Mourning "Day of Mourning"), 20 [Olarune](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Olarune "Olarune") in [994 YK](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/994_YK "994 YK"), it appears that something horrible happened within, nearby, or around the city, and from there it spread out to overwhelm and destroy the entire kingdom of Cyre.[[1]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-FN-p83-1)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECG-p92-4)[[5]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-RftLW-p221-5)[[7]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-FN-p77-7)[[8]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECG-p89-8)

All around Making, the land of southern [[Cyre]]was hugely reshaped by the upwelling of the Glass Plateau.[[1]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-FN-p83-1)[[5]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-RftLW-p221-5) It happened so fast and so abruptly that soldiers fighting sword to sword would be separated in moments, one standing on firm ground, the other atop a 100-foot-high cliff, before both were slain by the Mourning. Weeks of earthquakes followed.[[1]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-FN-p83-1)

In 997 YK, the tremors were repeated, seeing lava creep from cracks along the plateau's western side, in a manner likened to bleeding from a scab. This made the highland utterly inaccessible from the west. The lava has since cooled into pale gray glass. The plateau continues to grow, perhaps implying that whatever caused it is still going on.[[1]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-FN-p83-1)

## Inhabitants[](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau?veaction=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Inhabitants")

It's a desolate wasteland where nothing grows, few creatures roam, and only unnatural things live.[[2]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECS-p191-2)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECG-p92-4)[[5]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-RftLW-p221-5) [Living spells](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Living_spell "Living spell") appear to be attracted to the Glass Plateau, or are even still created here,[[2]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECS-p191-2)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECG-p92-4) with [living fireballs](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Living_fireball "Living fireball") seeming to originate there.[[9]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-9) Either way, the highest population of them in [[the Mournland]] is on and around this place.[[2]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau#cite_note-ECS-p191-2)
