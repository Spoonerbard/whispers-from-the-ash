**Croydonite: The Mourning's Tears**

**Appearance:**

- **Glassy and Jagged:** [[Croydonite]] typically appears as irregular, glassy masses, often with sharp, jagged edges. It's usually opaque but can sometimes have translucent patches.
- **Color Variations:** The most common color is a deep, swirling black, like solidified smoke, often with veins or inclusions of other colors running through it:
    - **Ember Red:** Indicates residual heat and a higher concentration of volatile magic.
    - **Sickly Green:** Suggests a corrupted or tainted form of Croydonite, potentially more dangerous.
    - **Veins of Blue:** Hints at latent arcane energy, often found in [[Croydonite]] closer to the epicenter of the Mourning.
    - **Oily Iridescence:** Some [[Croydonite]] displays a shifting, oily iridescence, like a soap bubble, indicating a more unstable and unpredictable nature.
- **Faint Glow:** Most [[Croydonite]] emits a faint, internal glow, especially in the darker varieties. The intensity of the glow can fluctuate, pulsing slowly or flickering erratically.
- **Size:** [[Croydonite]] deposits can range in size from small shards to large, boulder-sized masses.

**Flavortext:**

_"They call it Croydonite, the Mourning's Tears. Said to be the solidified essence of the land's pain, a physical manifestation of the magic that went wrong. Touch it, and you can almost hear the whispers of the dead. Use it, and you're playing with fire, or worse. But in the right hands... in the right hands, it's power. Unrefined, untamed, but power nonetheless."_ — Excerpt from [[the mournland]] scavenger's journal

**Properties:**

- **Magical Instability:** [[Croydonite]] is inherently unstable, a chaotic blend of residual magic and transformed matter. It radiates a faint aura detectable with _detect magic_.
- **Energy Source:** It contains a significant amount of latent magical energy, but extracting and controlling that energy is difficult and dangerous.
- **Environmental Effects:** Prolonged exposure to raw [[Croydonite]] can have unpredictable effects on living creatures, causing:
    - **Minor Mutations:** Strange skin patterns, glowing eyes, unusual hair growth.
    - **Magical Sickness:** A debilitating condition characterized by fatigue, nausea, and magical instability (e.g., wild magic surges).
    - **Corruption:** In extreme cases, exposure can lead to more severe mutations or even complete magical corruption, twisting creatures into monstrous forms.
- **Resonance:** [[Croydonite]] tends to resonate with other magical energies. It can amplify or distort spells cast in its vicinity, and it might react unpredictably to magical items.
- **Weight:** It's heavier than it looks. And is surprisingly hard to break.

**Potential Uses:**

- **Raw Power Source:**
    - **Unrefined:** In its raw form, [[Croydonite]] can be used as a crude but powerful energy source for magical devices or rituals. However, this is extremely dangerous due to its instability.
    - **Refined:** Skilled artificers, like Troxler, might be able to refine Croydonite, stabilizing it to some degree and making it safer to use. This refined form could be used to power magical items or create new types of spells. This process is difficult, and might take multiple attempts, potentially losing some of the material each time.
- **Weaponization:**
    - **Croydonite Shards:** Sharp shards of [[Croydonite]] e]] could be embedded in weapons, causing them to inflict additional magical damage or have unpredictable side effects on a hit. These weapons might degrade quickly or be dangerous to the wielder.
    - **Explosive Devices:** Its volatile nature makes [[Croydonite]] a potential component in explosives. However, controlling the blast would be extremely difficult.
    - **Magical Ammunition:** [[Croydonite]] could be incorporated into ammunition for ranged weapons. Arrows or bullets tipped with [[Croydonite]] might explode on impact or inflict lingering magical effects.
- **Magical Item Creation:**
    - **Component:** Refined [[Croydonite]] could be a crucial component in the creation of new and powerful magic items, granting them unique properties or enhancing their existing abilities.
    - **Focus:** It could be used as a focus for spells, amplifying their power or altering their effects, but potentially making them less predictable.
- **Construction:**
    - **Magical Wards:** In its raw or refined form, it could be used to create powerful magical wards, but these wards might have unintended side effects or attract unwanted attention. The larger the piece, the larger the potential for unintended side effects.
    - **Enhanced Structures:** Incorporating [[Croydonite]] into building materials could make structures stronger or imbue them with magical properties, but it could also make them unstable or dangerous to inhabit. It could potentially warp reality around it.
- **Research:**
    - **Understanding the Mourning:** Studying [[Croydonite]] could provide valuable insights into the nature of the Mourning and the magical forces that caused it.
    - **Developing New Magic:** Experimenting with [[Croydonite]] could lead to the development of new types of spells or magical technologies, but the risks would be immense.

**Risks and Challenges:**

- **Instability:** Handling [[Croydonite]] is always risky. Even refined [[Croydonite]] can be unpredictable.
- **Corruption:** The potential for corruption makes it dangerous to work with, especially for those who are not magically adept.
- **Attracting Attention:** Large quantities of Croydonite, or the use of [[Croydonite]] in powerful magic, could attract unwanted attention from creatures of [[the Mournland]] or other entities drawn to its power.
- **Ethical Concerns:** The use of [[Croydonite]] raises ethical questions. Is it right to exploit a material born from such a catastrophic event? What are the long-term consequences of tampering with such powerful and unstable magic?

Croydonite is a powerful and dangerous material that embodies the tragedy and mystery of [[the Mournland]]. It offers great potential for those brave enough to try and harness its power, but it also carries significant risks. Its use will undoubtedly shape the course of your West Marches campaign, forcing players to make difficult choices about how to utilize this potent and volatile substance.


# The Shattered Souls:

    Origin: When the Mourning occurred, the catastrophic magical energies didn't just destroy the land and its people; it shattered the souls of those killed, ripping them apart into countless fragments.
    Imprisonment: These soul fragments, each containing a sliver of a former life, were then infused into the very fabric of [[the Mournland]], becoming trapped within the newly formed Croydonite.
    Sentience: Each fragment retains a faint, residual sentience, a whisper of its former self. They are not fully conscious but exist in a state of perpetual torment, confusion, and longing.
    Number: Millions perished in the Mourning. With each soul shattered into a million pieces, the sheer number of trapped soul fragments within [[Croydonite]] is astronomical, almost beyond comprehension.
    The Source of Power: The magical power of [[Croydonite]] is derived, in part, from this trapped spiritual energy. It's the anguish, the fear, and the unfulfilled desires of these fragmented souls that fuel its volatile nature.

Discovering the Secret:

The players might uncover this horrifying truth through various means:

    Psychic Visions: A character using or experimenting with [[Croydonite]] might experience fragmented memories or visions, catching glimpses of the lives and deaths of those whose souls are trapped within.
    Whispers on the Wind: In areas with high concentrations of Croydonite, the players might hear faint whispers, cries, or moans on the wind – the voices of the shattered souls. These could be unintelligible or offer cryptic clues.
    Empathic Overload: A character with empathic abilities might be overwhelmed by the sheer amount of psychic pain and suffering emanating from Croydonite.
    Ancient Texts or Records: The party could discover ancient texts, hidden records, or the গবেষণাগার notes of a scholar who researched the Mourning and uncovered the truth about Croydonite.
    A Mad Prophet: An NPC driven mad by the knowledge of Croydonite's secret could reveal it to the party, ranting and raving about the "voices in the stone."
    Communication with the Dead: Through spells like speak with dead or other necromantic rituals, the party might be able to communicate directly with the soul fragments, though the experience would likely be fragmented and disturbing.
    Troxler's Discovery: As Troxler studies and refines Croydonite, they might stumble upon this horrifying truth, confronting the party with the ethical implications of their work.

Moral Dilemma:

The revelation of Croydonite's true nature creates a profound moral dilemma for the players:

    Is it ethical to use Croydonite, knowing that it is powered by the suffering of countless souls? Every use of Croydonite, even for seemingly good purposes, could be seen as perpetuating the torment of the innocent.
    Can the souls be freed? Is there a way to release the trapped soul fragments and grant them peace, or are they doomed to an eternity of suffering? This could become a major quest line for the party.
    What is the responsibility of the living to the dead in this context? Do the players have a moral obligation to try to help these souls, even if it means sacrificing their own goals or safety?
    The Greater Good: Can the use of [[Croydonite]] be justified if it helps protect the living or rebuild civilization, even if it means exploiting the suffering of the dead? This is a classic utilitarian dilemma.
    The Nature of the Soul: This discovery raises fundamental questions about the nature of the soul, the afterlife (or lack thereof), and the consequences of tampering with powerful magic.

Ramifications:

    Player Choice: The players' choices regarding [[Croydonite]] will have a significant impact on the campaign's narrative and their characters' development.
    Factional Divisions: Different factions within the setting might have different views on Croydonite. Some might see it as an abomination to be avoided at all costs, while others might view it as a necessary tool for survival or even a path to power. This could lead to conflict and alliances.
    Troxler's Arc: Troxler, as the primary crafter of Croydonite, will be forced to confront the ethical implications of their work. This could lead to a major turning point in their character arc. They might abandon their research, seek a way to refine [[Croydonite]] to remove the souls, or even become an antagonist if they choose to embrace the power of [[Croydonite]] regardless of the cost.
    Thematic Resonance: The shattered souls within [[Croydonite]] serve as a powerful reminder of the devastating consequences of unchecked ambition and the dangers of tampering with forces beyond human comprehension.
    Potential for Redemption: The quest to free the trapped souls could become a central theme of the campaign, offering the players a chance to atone for the sins of the past and bring a measure of peace to [[the Mournland]].
