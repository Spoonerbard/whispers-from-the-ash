Here's a breakdown of a longer campaign arc, incorporating these ideas and keeping the culprit's identity flexible:

**I. Campaign Arc: The Unveiling of the Rifts**

**Phase 1: Seeds of Doubt (Levels 1-5)**

- **Focus:** Exploration of the Mournland, establishing Salvation, encountering initial dangers, and meeting key NPCs.
- **Events:**
    - The party arrives in Salvation, gets their bearings, and begins exploring the nearby areas (Ashen Wastes, Jagged Peaks, Stillwater, Illusory মিথ্যে).
    - They encounter the Steel Stoners and learn about Croydonite.
    - They discover the Sky Song Tavern and potentially attract the attention of the Order of the Broken Sky.
    - They meet Dr. Vex and begin to understand the potential of alchemical experimentation with Mournland materials.
    - They might have their first encounter with the Forgeback Spider-Gorilla.
    - They start to piece together clues about the Mourning, the Final Sanction, and the different types of magical corruption.
    - They begin to upgrade their base and help Salvation grow.
- **Hidden Plot:** The secret villain (let's call them "The Conspirator" for now) is subtly manipulating events, using the party's explorations to gather information, acquire resources, and test the Mournland's properties. They might be providing the party with seemingly helpful advice, funding their expeditions, or even offering them quests that unknowingly further their own goals.
- **Clues:**
    - The Conspirator shows an unusual interest in the party's discoveries, particularly regarding Croydonite, ancient artifacts, and any mention of portals or planar travel.
    - They might express skepticism or fear about certain areas of the Mournland, subtly discouraging the party from investigating places that might reveal the truth too early.
    - They might have a hidden connection to one of the factions operating in the region (e.g., a secret agreement with the Order of the Broken Sky or a deal with a Lord of Dust).
- **Ending:** The party makes a significant discovery, such as finding a piece of the Progenitor Dragon bone, uncovering evidence of the Disciples of the Unwritten Name's rituals, or encountering a location with a strong connection to another plane. This discovery triggers the next phase of the campaign. The Steel Sentinels arrive in town.

**Phase 2: The Gathering Storm (Levels 6-10)**

- **Focus:** Uncovering the plot to open portals, identifying the Conspirator, and dealing with the Steel Sentinels.
- **Events:**
    - The party learns about the Conspirator's plan to gather large quantities of Croydonite and use it to open portals to other planes. They might discover evidence of the Conspirator's experiments, witness the opening of a small, unstable portal, or receive a warning from a concerned NPC (perhaps a redeemed Roric or a dissenting member of the Order of the Broken Sky).
    - The Steel Sentinels arrive in Salvation, and their mission to stop the Lord of Blades becomes a significant subplot. The party might be forced to choose a side or try to navigate the conflict between the Sentinels and the Lord of Blades' forces. The Sentinels are destroyed or scattered by an encounter with the Lord of Blades or a powerful entity from the Mournland.
    - The party's actions might inadvertently help the Conspirator gather the resources they need or weaken the defenses against planar incursions.
    - The party might face moral dilemmas related to the use of Croydonite and the potential consequences of opening portals to other planes.
    - They might have a confrontation with the Forgeback Spider-Gorilla in its lair.
- **Hidden Plot:** The Conspirator continues to manipulate events from the shadows, using their influence in Salvation to further their goals. They might start to become more open about their interest in Croydonite, potentially even trying to recruit the party to their cause. They will be working towards opening larger, more stable portals.
- **Clues:**
    - The party discovers the Conspirator's hidden lab or a cache of their research notes, revealing their experiments with Croydonite and their plans for interplanar travel.
    - They encounter creatures or entities from other planes, evidence of the Conspirator's successful portal experiments.
    - They learn more about the entity trapped beneath the Glass Plateau and the Conspirator's desire to release it.
    - They uncover evidence of the Conspirator's past, their connection to the Day of Mourning, and their personal motivation for reopening the portal.
- **Ending:** The party uncovers the Conspirator's identity and their ultimate plan. They might have a confrontation with the Conspirator, but they are not yet powerful enough to defeat them. The Conspirator escapes, leaving the party to deal with the consequences of their actions. The party also witnesses the arrival of a small group of agents from House Cannith, House Tharashk, and House Sivis. They are initially here to investigate the activities of the Steel Sentinels, but they will soon take an interest in the party's activities.

**Phase 3: The Breach (Levels 11-16)**

- **Focus:** Stopping the Conspirator from reopening the eldritch portal and freeing the Devourer of Dreams.
- **Events:**
    - The Conspirator succeeds in opening a large, stable portal to another plane (or multiple portals to different planes). This could be a plane of elemental chaos, a realm of madness, or a prison dimension where dangerous entities are held.
    - Creatures and entities from the other plane begin to pour into the Mournland, creating new dangers and threatening to destabilize the region. The land around the portal begins to warp and change, reflecting the nature of the plane it is connected to.
    - The party must race against time to find a way to close the portal before it expands further or before the Devourer of Dreams can break free. They might need to gather powerful artifacts, forge alliances with other factions, or even venture into the other plane to find a way to shut it down from the other side.
    - They might have to make difficult choices about which factions to trust and which to oppose. They might even have to choose between stopping the Conspirator and saving Salvation from the planar incursion.
- **Hidden Plot:** The Conspirator's ultimate goal is revealed: to reopen the original eldritch portal and free the Devourer of Dreams. They believe that this entity holds the key to ultimate power or that its release will usher in a new age of chaos that they can control. Their motivations are deeply personal and tied to their secret.
- **Clues:**
    - The party learns more about the Final Sanction and the cabal that performed it. They might discover the location of the ritual's epicenter, the remnants of the anchors used, or even records left behind by the cabal members.
    - They discover the true nature of the Glass Plateau and the entity trapped beneath it. They might even find a way to communicate with the Devourer of Dreams, though this would be incredibly dangerous.
    - They uncover the Conspirator's connection to the entity and their plan to release it.
- **Ending:** The party confronts the Conspirator at the site of the original eldritch portal, just as they are about to complete their ritual to reopen it. This will be a climactic battle, with the fate of Eberron hanging in the balance. The party might have to make a great sacrifice to close the portal and re-imprison the Devourer of Dreams. They might even have to sacrifice one of their own, or make a deal with a powerful entity to achieve victory.

**Phase 4: The Aftermath (Levels 17-20)**

- **Focus:** Dealing with the consequences of the previous phase and rebuilding. The choices the party made will determine the state of the Mournland and their own future.
- **Possible Outcomes:**
    - **The Portal is Closed:** The party succeeds in closing the portal and re-imprisoning the Devourer of Dreams, but the Mournland is forever changed. They might have to deal with lingering planar incursions, corrupted creatures, and the lasting effects of the Conspirator's actions. They will be hailed as heroes.
    - **The Devourer is Released:** The party fails to stop the Conspirator, and the Devourer of Dreams is unleashed upon Eberron. This could lead to a catastrophic war against a being of unimaginable power, potentially plunging the world into a new age of darkness.
    - **A New Balance:** The party finds a way to partially close the portal or to appease the Devourer of Dreams, creating a new, uneasy balance in the Mournland. This could lead to a more stable but fundamentally altered world, where the boundaries between planes are weaker and the influence of other realities is more pronounced.
- **Rebuilding and Healing:** Whatever the outcome, the party will likely play a key role in rebuilding Salvation and helping the people of the Mournland recover from the Conspirator's actions. They might even find a way to begin healing the land itself, though this would be a long and arduous process.
- **Legacy:** The party's actions will determine their legacy. Will they be remembered as heroes who saved Eberron from a terrible fate, or as villains who unleashed a new era of chaos?

**The Conspirator's Identity and Secret:**

Here are some possibilities for the Conspirator's identity, their secret, and their motivation, along with how they might have been manipulating the party:

**1. "Old Man" Silas (The Disgraced Scholar):**

- **Secret:** Edgar ir'Wynarn, a disgraced Cyran noble and scholar who was present during the Mourning. He was part of a group researching chronomancy and its potential for causing harm. He witnessed the "Final Sanction" but was unable to prevent it. His wife, Elara, and child were caught in the ritual and pulled into the portal. He's been studying the event ever since.
- **Motivation:** To reopen the portal and rescue his family, no matter the cost. He believes that the Devourer of Dreams holds the key to reversing time or opening a gateway to the realm where his loved ones are trapped.
- **Manipulation:** Silas has been using the party to gather information about the Mournland, test the properties of Croydonite, and potentially locate artifacts related to the Final Sanction. He might have even planted the idea of exploring the Ashen Wastes, knowing about the creatures there and hoping they would clear a path for him. He would not reveal any of this information until it was too late. He would use his knowledge of the party's strengths and weaknesses to manipulate them into doing his bidding.

**2. "Mama" Elara Thorne (The Grieving Widow):**

- **Secret:** Elara is not just a former adventurer, but the widow of a powerful Cyran mage who was part of the cabal that enacted the Final Sanction. He sacrificed himself to close the portal, but Elara believes he is still alive, trapped on the other side. She was not present at the time, but has pieced together what happened from his notes.
- **Motivation:** To reopen the portal and reunite with her lost love, even if it means unleashing the Devourer of Dreams upon the world. She is driven by grief and a desperate hope that she can somehow bring him back.
- **Manipulation:** Elara has been using her position as the party's landlady to gather information about their discoveries, subtly influencing their decisions and steering them towards locations that might hold the key to reopening the portal. She might have even planted the keepsake that led them to the Mournland in the first place, hoping they would find a way to reopen the portal.

**3. Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight (The Traumatized Healer):**

- **Secret:** Kai is not just fleeing from a danger in the Feywild. He is from the original Cyre, and his family were killed when he was young, pulled into the portal. His parents pushed him away, sacrificing themselves to save him. He has suppressed the memory, but it manifests in his fear of using powerful magic. He arrived via a manifest zone to Thelanis, as his powers manifested, seeking refuge from the horrors of his past. He eventually found his way back to Eberron and to Salvation. He is drawn to the Mournland, seeking a way to undo what happened to his family.
- **Motivation:** Driven by a subconscious desire to reunite with his lost family. He believes that by understanding the nature of the portals, he might be able to find a way to bring them back or at least find peace.
- **Manipulation:** Kai has been subtly influencing the party, using his healing abilities to gain their trust while secretly studying their explorations of the Mournland. He might be drawn to areas with strong planar connections, hoping to find a way to reopen the portal. He might offer seemingly helpful advice that actually leads the party closer to his hidden goal. He would try to steer the party towards helping him, perhaps by suggesting that the portal could be used to heal the Mournland.

**Remember**:

- **Flexibility:** Keep the Conspirator's identity flexible until you're ready to reveal it. This allows you to adapt to the players' choices and actions, making the story more dynamic and responsive.
- **Subtlety:** The Conspirator's manipulation should be subtle, at least initially. They should appear to be a helpful and trustworthy NPC, someone the party would never suspect.
- **Moral Complexity:** The Conspirator's motivations should be understandable, even sympathetic. They are driven by a personal tragedy and a desperate desire to undo a terrible loss. This will make the eventual betrayal even more impactful.

This detailed outline provides a framework for a compelling and multi-layered campaign arc. The mystery of the Mourning, the threat of the Devourer of Dreams, and the personal stakes of the Conspirator will keep your players engaged and invested in the story. The choices they make throughout the campaign will have a profound impact on the world, shaping the fate of Salvation, the Mournland, and potentially all of Eberron.
