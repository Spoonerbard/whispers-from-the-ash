**Croydonite Contamination:**

As you suggested, the specific type of Croydonite contamination can be linked to the colors and schools of magic:

- **Yellow Croydonite:** Associated with necromancy, found near undead or areas infused with death energy. The Conductor Husks are a prime example.
- **Black Croydonite:** Linked to chronomancy and the time distortions of the Mourning. This type of Croydonite might be found near areas with extreme time anomalies or near the epicenter of the Final Sanction. It is the most common type of croydonite in the Mournland.
- **Other Colors:** The other colors of Croydonite (blue, orange, purple, pink, red, green, brown) could be found in areas where the corresponding school of magic was used or is still present in a corrupted form. These are much rarer, and would be more powerful and unstable.

**Using the Colors in the Game:**

- **Visual Clues:** The colors provide immediate visual clues about the type of magic that was used in an area or the nature of the corruption that afflicts it. Players can learn to recognize the different colors and anticipate the types of challenges they might face.
- **Environmental Storytelling:** The color-coded manifestations of magic can be used to tell stories about the past. For example, a ruined city filled with pink, petal-like growths might suggest that enchantment magic was used extensively there, perhaps for entertainment or mind control. A region covered in red ash might indicate a battle where powerful evocation spells were unleashed.
- **Investigative Tool:** Players can use their knowledge of the color-coding system to investigate the Mourning, deduce the types of magic used in different locations, and potentially piece together the events that led to the catastrophe.
- **Crafting and Research:** Troxler or other NPCs might be able to use the different colors of Croydonite in their crafting or research, potentially unlocking new recipes or discovering ways to counter the negative effects of the Mourning.
- **Character Interactions:** Certain characters might react differently to different colors of magic. For example, a character sensitive to psychic energy might be particularly affected by areas infused with divination (purple) or enchantment (pink) magic. The druid might find that areas infused with transmutation magic are particularly disturbing, as they represent a fundamental alteration of the natural world.

**Examples:**

- **The Ashen Wastes:** The black sand (chronomancy) is a clear indication of the time distortion that occurred during the Mourning. The presence of yellow acid on the Conductor Husks also suggests a strong necromantic influence, perhaps from whatever being created the portal, or as a result of the souls being ripped apart.
- **The Sky Song Tavern:** The faint, lingering magic within the tavern might manifest as subtle color cues. For instance, the enchanted murals might shimmer with green (illusion) or pink (enchantment) hues, while the brewery in the basement might have traces of brown (transmutation) or orange (conjuration) residue, depending on the types of magic used to create the Sky Song Ale.
- **The Stillwater:** The lake's unnatural green color could be a result of illusion magic, or perhaps a combination of transmutation and necromantic influences, given the presence of mutated creatures and the oily sheen.
- **The Jagged Peaks:** These mountains might have veins of red (evocation) Croydonite running through them, indicating the use of powerful fire magic in the area, potentially during the Final Sanction. The presence of the Steel Stoners and their Croydonite mining operation adds another layer to the region's dangers.
- **The Glass Plateau:** While seemingly devoid of color, the Plateau's black, reflective surface could be interpreted as a manifestation of chronomancy on a massive scale. The shadowy reflections and the whispers of madness might hint at the presence of other colors, representing the different types of magic used to create and reinforce the prison.

By consistently using this color-coded system, you create a visually engaging and thematically rich environment for your players to explore. The colors become clues, warnings, and story elements, adding depth to the mystery of the Mournland and providing a framework for understanding the forces that shaped it.
