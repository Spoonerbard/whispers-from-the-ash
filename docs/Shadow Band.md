Okay, let's flesh out the **Shadow Band** for the rogue player, developing its upgrade paths and connection to the campaign's themes.

**Rogue's Shadow Band: A Tool for Stealth and Subterfuge**

**Current Abilities (Tier 1):**

- **Shadow's Embrace:** Once per day, the rogue can use an action to become invisible, as per the _invisibility_ spell, for up to 1 minute. The invisibility ends if the rogue attacks, casts a spell, or takes any other action besides moving, hiding or searching. The band is clearly visible, even when the player is not.

**Upgrade Paths:**

This item will focus on enhancing the rogue's stealth, mobility, and ability to manipulate shadows. We'll offer three paths: **Whisper, Umbral, and Gloom.**

**1. Path of the Whisper (Focus: Deception and Information Gathering):**

- **Theme:** This path emphasizes stealth, infiltration, and gathering secrets. It's about becoming a shadow, unseen and unheard.
- **Tier 2 Upgrade:**
    - **Shadowy Misdirection:** Once per day, the rogue can use the bonus action to cast the _minor illusion_ cantrip, without expending a spell slot. The illusion is always some form of visual or auditory distraction, such as a faint whisper, a fleeting shadow, or a deceptive sound.
    - **Requirement:** To unlock this upgrade, the rogue must successfully infiltrate a location or gather a piece of sensitive information without being detected. They could also gather materials from an area with strong ties to illusion magic.
- **Tier 3 Upgrade:**
    - **Whispers of the Shadowfell:** The rogue can now cast the _pass without trace_ spell once per day without expending a spell slot, using the Shadow Band as a focus.
    - **Requirement:** The rogue must spend time studying the nature of shadows, perhaps by meditating in a location infused with shadow magic or by deciphering an ancient text related to stealth and deception.
- **Tier 4 Upgrade:**
    - **One with the Shadows:** While invisible from the _invisibility_ effect of the ring, the rogue can take the dash, disengage, or hide action as a bonus action. The ring can be used twice per day.
    - **Requirement:** The rogue must successfully complete a challenging mission that requires them to rely heavily on stealth and deception, such as infiltrating a secure location, stealing a valuable item, or eavesdropping on a secret meeting.

**2. Path of the Umbral (Focus: Mobility and Shadow Manipulation):**

- **Theme:** This path enhances the rogue's ability to move through shadows and manipulate darkness, turning them into a swift and elusive predator.
- **Tier 2 Upgrade:**
    - **Shadow Step:** Once per day, the rogue can use the bonus action to cast the _misty step_ spell without expending a spell slot, using the Shadow Band as a focus.
    - **Requirement:** The rogue must spend time training in areas of deep shadow or darkness, honing their ability to move through them. They might also need to acquire a rare material, such as a piece of solidified shadow or a gem infused with shadow energy.
- **Tier 3 Upgrade:**
    - **Umbral Shroud:** While wearing the Shadow Band, the rogue gains a +5 bonus to Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
    - **Requirement:** The rogue must successfully evade a powerful enemy or escape from a dangerous situation using their stealth and agility.
- **Tier 4 Upgrade:**
    - **Shadow Jaunt:** The rogue can now use the _misty step_ spell twice per day. In addition to this, once per day, they can use the Shadow Band to cast the _dimension door_ spell without expending a spell slot, though the range is reduced to 50 feet. The Shadow Band can be used to cast misty step three times per day.
    - **Requirement:** The rogue must master a technique for manipulating shadows, perhaps by studying with a master assassin, a creature of shadow, or by deciphering an ancient text on shadow magic.

**3. Path of Gloom (Focus: Combat and Shadow-Based Attacks):**

- **Theme:** This path enhances the rogue's combat abilities, allowing them to strike from the shadows with deadly force and use darkness to their advantage.
- **Tier 2 Upgrade:**
    - **Gloom Strike:** Once per turn, when the rogue hits a creature with a weapon attack while they are in dim light or darkness, they can deal an extra 1d6 necrotic damage.
    - **Requirement:** The rogue must defeat a certain number of enemies using stealth attacks or by striking from the shadows. They might also need to acquire a weapon that has been used in darkness for a prolonged period of time.
- **Tier 3 Upgrade:**
    - **Shadow Sight:** The rogue can see normally in darkness, both magical and nonmagical, out to a distance of 60 feet.
    - **Requirement:** The rogue must spend time training their senses in complete darkness, learning to rely on other senses besides sight.
- **Tier 4 Upgrade:**
    - **Embrace the Void:** Once per day, the rogue can use the Shadow Band to cast the _darkness_ spell without expending a spell slot, centering it on themselves. They can see through this darkness, and it lasts for 1 minute. While within this darkness the rogue can use their **Gloom Strike** ability twice per turn. The ring can be used to cast _darkness_ in this way twice per day.
    - **Requirement:** The rogue must defeat a powerful enemy in complete darkness or overcome a challenge that requires them to embrace the shadows.

- **Tier 3:** The changes become more pronounced. The band might become partially translucent, as if made of solidified shadow. It could also start to emit a faint aura of darkness. It could also more closely resemble a ring.
- **Tier 4:** The band might transform completely, becoming a fully-fledged ring crafted from shadow metal or adorned with a dark, powerful gemstone. It could also project a visible aura of darkness or shadow, reflecting the rogue's mastery over the shadows.
