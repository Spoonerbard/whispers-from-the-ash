# Encounter 1: The Living Quake
#encounters/theambush 

- **Challenge:** As the party navigates a narrow pass, the ground begins to shake violently. Cracks appear in the rock face, and boulders tumble down towards them.
- **Source:** The tremors are caused by a Living Quake, a creature of elemental earth infused with the chaotic energies of the Mourning 1. It burrows beneath the surface, causing localized earthquakes and destabilizing the terrain.
- **Possible Actions:**
    - **Strength (Athletics) checks** to avoid falling rocks and maintain their footing.
    - **Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks** to leap across widening fissures and climb unstable ledges.
    - **Intelligence (Nature) checks** to predict the Quake's movements and find stable ground.
    - If the party has a spellcaster, they could try to use spells like **Earth Tremor** or **Stone Shape** to influence the Quake or stabilize the terrain.
- **Consequences of Failure:** Falling rocks could deal bludgeoning damage and knock characters prone. If the tremors are severe enough, a landslide could occur, burying the party or forcing them to make difficult **Strength (Athletics) checks** to escape.
- **Eberron Twist:** The Living Quake could be drawn to metal, making it particularly aggressive towards warforged or characters wearing heavy armor.

# **Encounter 2: The Whisperwind Ravine**


- **Challenge:** The party reaches a deep ravine spanned by a precarious natural bridge. As they cross, a howling wind whips through the chasm, carrying strange whispers and unsettling visions.
- **Source:** The ravine is a focal point for the lingering psychic echoes of the Mourning. The whispers are fragments of memories, screams, and pleas for help from those who perished in the disaster.
- **Possible Actions:**
    - **Wisdom (Insight) checks** to resist the unsettling effects of the whispers and discern any useful information.
    - **Charisma (Performance) or (Persuasion) checks** to calm panicked party members or try to reason with the echoes.
    - **Intelligence (Arcana) checks** to understand the nature of the psychic phenomenon and potentially find a way to shield themselves from it.
- **Consequences of Failure:** The whispers could inflict psychic damage or induce madness, causing characters to become confused, frightened, or even hostile towards each other.
- **Eberron Twist:** The whispers could manifest as visions of past battles or glimpses of the party's own potential demise, playing on their fears and insecurities.

# **Encounter 3: The Gnarled Grove**

- **Challenge:** The party stumbles upon a grove of gnarled, mutated trees. The trees seem to be alive, their branches reaching out like grasping claws.
- **Source:** The trees have been warped by the magical energies of the Mourning, becoming predatory and hostile. They might be animated by lingering spirits or simply driven by a primal instinct to consume.
- **Possible Actions:**
    - **Strength (Athletics) checks** to hack through dense undergrowth and climb the twisted trees.
    - **Dexterity (Stealth) checks** to avoid detection by the trees and move through the grove unnoticed.
    - **Wisdom (Survival) checks** to identify the most dangerous trees and find a safe path through the grove.
- **Consequences of Failure:** The trees could attack with their branches, dealing slashing damage and possibly grappling or restraining characters. They might also release poisonous spores or emit hallucinogenic fumes.
- **Eberron Twist:** The trees could be infused with elemental energy, allowing them to unleash blasts of fire, ice, or lightning.

# **Encounter 4: The Ghostly Patrol**

- **Challenge:** As the party climbs higher, they encounter a patrol of spectral soldiers, still marching as they did during the Last War. The soldiers seem unaware of their surroundings and hostile to any living creatures.
- **Source:** These are the ghosts of Cyran soldiers, trapped in [[the Mournland]]] and forever reliving their final moments. They might be bound to a particular location or driven by a lingering sense of duty.
- **Possible Actions:**
    - **Charisma (Religion) checks** to turn the undead or attempt to communicate with them.
    - **Intelligence (History) checks** to identify the soldiers and potentially learn something about their past or their mission.
    - **Combat:** If diplomacy fails, the party will have to fight the spectral soldiers. Remember that ghosts have resistance to most weapon attacks and can possess living creatures.
- **Consequences of Failure:** Being possessed by a ghost could have a variety of negative effects, depending on the ghost's personality and motivations.
- **Eberron Twist:** The ghostly soldiers could be accompanied by spectral warforged or even ghostly siege engines, adding an eerie technological element to the encounter.

# encounter 5: the vending machine
#encounters/thepuzzle 
#fantasticlocations/waypoint 

- **Glyph of Shielding:** Dispenses a small, shimmering disc that grants the user +1 AC for one hour.
- **Glyph of the Hound:** Releases a spectral hound that follows the party for one day, acting as a scout and providing advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks.
- **Glyph of the Lantern:** Produces a small, ever-burning lantern that sheds bright light in a 15-foot radius, unaffected by the dim light of [[the Mournland]].
- **Glyph of Featherfall:** A single feather floats out, granting one creature the effect of the _feather fall_ spell for one hour.
- **Glyph of Warning:** Instead of a reward, this glyph activates a shimmering illusion showing a brief, cryptic warning about a nearby danger (e.g., a hidden chasm, a roaming monster, a trapped area).
- **Glyph of Sustenance:** Dispenses a small, tasteless biscuit that magically provides the nourishment of a full meal.
- **Glyph of Guidance:** A small, carved arrow emerges, pointing towards the nearest source of fresh water.
- **Glyph of Mischief:** This glyph plays a harmless prank on the activator (e.g., a sudden gust of wind ruffles their hair, their voice changes pitch for a few minutes, a small, harmless illusionary creature appears and mocks them).
- **Glyph of the Past:** Shows a fleeting vision of the monument as it was before the Mourning, perhaps offering clues about its purpose or the civilization that created it.

**Eberron Twist:**

- Some glyphs could be linked to specific dragonmarks, only activating for those who possess the corresponding mark.
- The monument could be a remnant of a lost giant civilization, with glyphs depicting strange symbols and dispensing items infused with elemental energy.
- The monument could be a creation of House Cannith, with glyphs dispensing small, malfunctioning magical devices or prototypes of wondrous items.

This ancient magical vending machine adds a unique element to your[[mournland]] exploration, offering both useful rewards and intriguing mysteries for your players to uncover.

# Encounter 6: The Oasis Above


#encounters/thepuzzle #potentialscene/encounter #fantasticlocations 


**Location:** A dried-up oasis in [[the Mournland]], now a desolate hollow filled with withered plants and cracked earth.

**The Scene:**

- **The Bubble:** A large, perfectly spherical bubble of water (approximately 50 feet in diameter) floats silently about 30 feet above the center of the dead oasis. The water is crystal clear, and strange, luminescent plants can be seen growing inside.
- **Stepping Stones:** A series of uneven stone platforms, seemingly ancient and weathered, form a precarious path leading up to the bubble. Some stones might be loose or crumbling.
- **The Dead Oasis:** The once-fertile ground is now barren and cracked. Twisted, dead trees and the skeletons of desert plants litter the area. The air is still and heavy, with a faint metallic tang characteristic of [[the Mournland]].
- **Light and Shadow:** [[the Mournland]]'s hazy atmosphere creates an ethereal play of light and shadow. The bubble itself might cast strange reflections or refractions, adding to the otherworldly feel.

**What's Inside the Bubble?**

Here are a few possibilities, each with its own challenges and rewards:

**Option 1: The Preserved Garden**

- **Content:** The bubble contains a pocket of vibrant life, a perfectly preserved fragment of a pre-Mourning oasis. Lush plants, colorful flowers, and small, harmless creatures thrive within. The air is sweet and clean. A small, ornate fountain stands at the center.
- **Challenge:**
    - **Getting In:** The surface of the bubble is resistant to entry. A creature must make a DC 10 Strength or Dexterity check to push through the surface tension without bouncing off. The check is automatically failed if they do not land on the bubble from a stepping stone.
    - **The Guardian:** A **Water Weird** (Monster Manual, p. 299) made of pure, fresh water inhabits the bubble, acting as a guardian of the garden. It is initially passive but will attack if the plants are harmed or if someone tries to take water from the fountain.
- **Reward:**
    - **Knowledge:** The garden could contain clues about the Mourning or the history of the region.
    - **Rare Plants:** The plants could have unique properties, useful for potion-making or other crafting.
    - **Healing Waters:** The water from the fountain could have restorative properties, granting temporary hit points or removing a condition.
    - **Tranquility:** The garden offers a moment of peace and respite from the horrors of [[the Mournland]].

**Option 2: The Aberrant Aquarium**

- **Content:** The bubble is a strange, alien ecosystem. Bizarre, bioluminescent plants sway in the water, and odd, fish-like creatures with too many eyes or limbs dart among them. The water has a slightly oily sheen.
- **Challenge:**
    - **Psychic Resonance:** The environment within the bubble has a strange psychic resonance. Characters inside must make a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw each hour or suffer the effects of a _confusion_ spell for 1 minute.
    - **Aberrant Contamination:** Spending too long in the bubble (e.g., more than an hour) or drinking the water could have strange side effects (e.g., temporary mutations, psychic sensitivity, or a connection to an alien entity).
    - **Hostile Inhabitants:** The seemingly harmless creatures might become aggressive if disturbed, attacking with strange abilities (e.g., psychic blasts, poisonous barbs, or the ability to teleport short distances). Use reskinned **Quippers** (Monster Manual, p. 335) that deal psychic damage.
- **Reward:**
    - **Unique Materials:** The plants or creatures could contain rare and valuable components for crafting or spellcasting.
    - **Psionic Artifact:** A strange, otherworldly object at the bottom of the bubble could grant psionic abilities or insights into the nature of the Far Realm.
    - **Aberrant Knowledge:** Studying the ecosystem could reveal secrets about the Far Realm or other aberrations.

**Option 3: The Trapped Entity**

- **Content:** The bubble holds a single, large creature suspended in a state of stasis. It could be a beautiful, celestial being, a powerful elemental, or a horrific aberration. The creature is clearly alive but unable to move.
- **Challenge:**
    - **Moral Dilemma:** The party must decide whether to free the creature or leave it trapped.
    - **Unknown Consequences:** Freeing the creature could have unpredictable and potentially dangerous consequences.
    - **The Trap:** The bubble itself might be a magical trap designed to contain the creature. Disabling the trap could require solving a puzzle, finding a specific key, or performing a ritual.
- **Reward:**
    - **Boon from the Entity:** If the creature is benevolent, it could offer the party a gift or its assistance in a future endeavor.
    - **Powerful Artifact:** The creature's prison might contain a powerful magical item that was used to trap it.
    - **Knowledge and Power:** Learning about the creature and its imprisonment could provide valuable knowledge or unlock new abilities for the party.

**Option 4: The Illusory Oasis**

- **Content:** The bubble is an illusion, a cruel trick of [[the Mournland]]. What appears to be water is actually empty air, or worse, a shimmering, acidic gas.
- **Challenge:**
    - **Discerning the Illusion:** A successful DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check reveals the illusory nature of the bubble.
    - **The Trap:** Stepping into the bubble could cause a character to fall to the ground below, taking falling damage. Alternatively, the gas could inflict acid damage and potentially blind or poison those who inhale it.
    - **Guardian of the Illusion:** A creature that created the illusion, such as a **Mournland Mirage** (use the **Will-o'-Wisp** stat block from the Monster Manual, p. 301, but give it the ability to cast _major image_ at will) might lurk nearby, attacking those who get too close.
- **Reward:**
    - **Survival:** Avoiding the trap is a reward in itself.
    - **Understanding [[the Mournland]]:** The encounter teaches the party that not everything in [[the Mournland]] is what it seems.
    - **Treasure:** The creature that created the illusion might have a small hoard of treasure nearby.

**Adding Depth to the Encounter:**

- **Environmental Storytelling:** Use the dead oasis to hint at what happened during the Mourning. Perhaps there are remnants of a village, signs of a battle, or strange, mutated plants.
- **Sensory Details:** Describe the sounds (or lack thereof), smells, and feel of the air to create a sense of unease and mystery.
- **Player Choice:** Allow the players to decide how they want to approach the bubble. Do they try to enter it? Do they study it from afar? Do they try to destroy it?
- **Consequences:** The party's actions should have consequences. For example, disturbing the bubble could attract unwanted attention from other creatures in [[the Mournland]].

**Scaling the Encounter:**

- **Easier:** Lower the DCs of the checks, reduce the damage dealt by traps or creatures, or provide more clues about the nature of the bubble.
- **Harder:** Increase the DCs, add more dangerous creatures or traps, or make the consequences of failure more severe.

This exploration encounter offers a unique and memorable experience for your players. It encourages them to think creatively, assess risks, and make choices that could have significant consequences. Remember to adapt the encounter to fit your specific campaign and the choices your players have made. Have fun bringing the Oasis Above to life!
