# **Khorvaire: A Continent Scarred by War**

- **The Last War:** Khorvaire was ravaged by a century-long conflict known as the Last War, fought between the Five Nations that once made up the Kingdom of Galifar. The war ended with the Treaty of Thronehold and the creation of the Mournland, a devastated wasteland that was once the nation of Cyre.
- ** [[dragonmarked houses]] :** These powerful, extended families hold a unique and influential position in Khorvaire. Each house controls a specific industry or area of expertise, granted to them by their hereditary dragonmarks. They often operate independently of national borders and can wield significant political and economic power. They were instrumental in the Last War, providing magical and mundane services to the warring nations.
- **Legacy of the Mourning:** The Mourning brought the Last War to an abrupt end, but it left a lasting scar on the continent. The destruction of Cyre created a power vacuum, heightened tensions between nations, and sparked fears of another devastating war. It also created a humanitarian crisis, as many refugees fled the Mournland.

**Questline Connections:**

- **The Search for the Truth:** The mystery of [[the Mourning]], [[the Final Sanction]], and [[the Disciples of the Unwritten Name]] could be a central questline, with clues and leads scattered throughout the different regions of Khorvaire. Each nation, as well as the  [[dragonmarked houses]] , might hold a piece of the puzzle.
- **The Croydonite Trade:** The trade and exploitation of [[Croydonite]] could be a major source of conflict, with different nations and factions vying for control of this valuable and dangerous resource. The party might be hired to protect shipments, investigate smuggling operations, or shut down illegal mining operations.
- **The Refugee Crisis:** The influx of Cyran refugees into neighboring nations could create social and economic tensions, leading to quests involving humanitarian aid, conflict resolution, or uncovering conspiracies related to the refugees.
- **The Rise of Cults:** The chaos and uncertainty following the Mourning could lead to the rise of new cults, some of which might be centered around the Mournland or the entities trapped within it. The party might be tasked with investigating these cults and stopping their activities.
- **The Threat of War:** The unresolved tensions between the nations of Khorvaire could erupt into a new conflict, with the Mournland as a potential battleground or source of powerful weapons. The party might be drawn into this conflict, forced to choose sides or find a way to prevent another devastating war.

**In summary, the wider geographies of Khorvaire provide a rich backdrop for your West Marches campaign. Each nation has its own unique history, culture, and motivations, and their proximity to the Mournland creates opportunities for conflict, intrigue, and adventure. By weaving these nations and their interests into the story, you can create a dynamic and engaging world where the players' choices have far-reaching consequences, not just for Salvation and the Mournland, but for the entire continent.**
