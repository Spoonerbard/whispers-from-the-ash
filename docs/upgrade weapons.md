


**I. General Principles:**

- **Modularity:** Each item will be broken down into a **base component** and several **upgrade components**.
- **Rarity:** Components will have different rarities, with basic components being more common and upgrade components being rarer and more powerful.
- **Blueprints/Recipes:** Players will need to find blueprints or recipes to learn how to combine components into specific items. These can be found in ruins, obtained from NPCs (like Troxler's research), or learned through experimentation.
- **Tiers:** The crafted items will have tiers, just like Troxler's crafting abilities, reflecting the increasing power and complexity of the creations.
- **Mournland Materials:** The items will incorporate materials unique to the Mournland, such as Croydonite, mutated creature parts, and salvaged warforged components.
- **Customization:** Players will have choices in how they combine components, allowing them to tailor the items to their specific needs and playstyles.

**II. Item Examples:**

**1. The Mourning Star (Repeating Crossbow)**

- **Concept:** A powerful repeating crossbow, reflecting the advanced technology of pre-Mourning Cyre, but infused with the Mournland's strange magic. This is a powerful weapon, capable of firing multiple bolts in quick succession, though it's difficult to make, requiring some skill to use effectively.
- **Base Component:**
    - **Intact Crossbow Frame:** Found in armories or military outposts. A sturdy, well-crafted crossbow frame, often made of metal and high-quality wood. It is clearly of superior craftsmanship. Requires a DC 10 Dexterity check to string the bow, or a DC 15 Strength check. On a failure the crossbow takes 1d4 damage.
- **Upgrade Components:**
    - **Tier 1:**
        - **Clockwork Repeater Mechanism:** Adds an additional shot to the crossbow's attack. Found in clockwork devices or warforged remains. Requires a DC 10 Intelligence (Investigation) check to find a suitable part.
        - **Blueprint: Accurate Repeater:** Provides a +1 bonus to attack rolls. Found in hidden caches or গবেষণাগার notes. Requires a DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) check to decipher the blueprint.
    - **Tier 2:**
        - **Fey-Touched Silk String:** Adds 1d4 psychic damage to each bolt. Found in areas with strong connections to the Feywild or from corrupted fey creatures. Requires a DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) check to harvest the silk without damaging it.
        - **Blueprint: Reinforced Frame:** Increases the crossbow's damage by one die size (e.g. from 1d6 to 1d8). Found in গবেষণাগার notes or hidden caches. Requires a DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) check to decipher the blueprint.
    - **Tier 3:**
        - **Beholder Eye Lens:** Adds a random beholder eye ray effect to the crossbow's attack (scaled down for balance). Found from mutated beholders or in areas with strong connections to Xoriat. Requires a DC 20 Wisdom (Perception) to find a suitable specimen.
        - **Blueprint: Rapid Fire Mechanism:** Allows the user to make two attacks with the bonus action. Found in গবেষণাগার notes or hidden caches. Requires a DC 16 Intelligence (Arcana) check to decipher the blueprint.
    - **Tier 4:**
        - **Singing Stone Core:** A Singing Stone from the Sky Song Tavern (or another source) that allows the crossbow to channel sonic energy. The crossbow now deals thunder damage and has a chance to stun or deafen targets. Requires a DC 20 Intelligence (Arcana) check to attune the singing stone to the crossbow.
        - **Blueprint: The Mourning Star:** A legendary blueprint that unlocks the crossbow's full potential, allowing it to fire a volley of explosive bolts or a single, devastating shot that deals massive damage. Requires a DC 20 Intelligence (Arcana) check to decipher the blueprint, as well as a DC 20 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check to make the necessary adjustments to the crossbow.
- **Crafting:** Troxler (or another skilled artisan) can combine the components based on the blueprints. Each tier of upgrade requires a higher level of skill and more expensive materials.

**2. Shadow's Embrace (Rapier - Warlock Weapon)**

- **Concept:** A rapier that embodies Gyda's connection to the Shadowfell and her internal conflict. The blade is partially made from Croydonite, reflecting the darkness in her past.
- **Base Component:**
    - **Shadow-Touched Blade:** The top third of the blade is made of a dark, matte metal that seems to absorb light, while the lower two thirds are made from a strange, black material that is as hard and sharp as steel, but has a slightly porous, almost bone like texture. Requires attunement by a Warlock.
- **Upgrade Components:**
    - **Tier 1:**
        - **Shadowfell Shard:** A small, jagged piece of obsidian found in areas with a strong connection to the Shadowfell. It can be integrated into the hilt, granting the **Shadowfell's Chill** ability once per day. The next successful attack with the weapon deals an additional 1d4 cold damage, and the target must make a Constitution saving throw (DC equal to Gyda's spell save DC). On a failed save, the target's speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of their next turn. Requires a DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) check to find.
        - **Tattered Silk Wrap:** A piece of silk from an assassin's outfit, used to provide a better grip. This can be used to replace the leather on the hilt, giving the rapier the properties of a **+1 weapon**. Requires a DC 10 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check to apply to the weapon without damaging it.
    - **Tier 2:**
        - **Whisper of the Dead:** A vial containing the ectoplasm of a powerful spirit. When applied to the blade, it allows the weapon to cast the _speak with dead_ spell once per day without using a spell slot. Requires a DC 15 Intelligence (Investigation) check to find such a concentration of ectoplasm.
        - **Essence of Loss:** A vial containing the distilled essence of sorrow, harvested from a place of great tragedy in the Mournland. When applied to the blade, it adds 1d4 psychic damage to each successful attack. Requires a DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) check to harvest without being overcome by the sorrow.
    - **Tier 3:**
        - **Soul স্টোন:** A small, polished stone that can absorb a fragment of a creature's soul. When embedded in the pommel, it grants the weapon the ability to cast the _inflict wounds_ spell once per day, at first level, without expending a spell slot. Requires the user to kill a creature with the blade to charge the stone. The spell slot level increases by 1 for each tier of the weapon.
        - **Shadowfell Infusion:** The blade can be further infused with Shadowfell energy, increasing the damage of **Shadowfell's Chill** to 2d4 and allowing it to be used twice per day. This requires a special ritual and a DC 18 Arcana check. On a failure, the weapon is damaged, and the user takes 2d4 necrotic damage.
    - **Tier 4:**
        - **The Raven Queen's Blessing:** A rare and powerful enchantment that can be applied to the blade, granting it the ability to cast the _finger of death_ spell once per week. This requires a special ritual and a DC 20 Arcana check. On a failure, the weapon is destroyed, and the user takes 8d8 necrotic damage.
        - **Severance:** The weapon's connection to the Shadowfell weakens. [[Gyda]]no longer suffers disadvantage on saving throws against banishment, and the exhaustion effect is removed. The weapon's Shadowfell's Chill now deals radiant damage instead of cold damage and the weapon now deals an additional 1d4 radiant damage to fiends and undead. Requires a special ritual and a DC 20 Arcana check. On a failure, the weapon is damaged, and the user takes 4d8 radiant damage.

**3. Warden's Regrowth (Druid Item)**

- **Concept:** An item that embodies the druid's connection to nature and the cycle of life and death, reflecting their backstory and their desire to protect the natural world. This could be a staff, a shield, or a suit of armor.
- **Base Component:**
    - **Seed of the Mourning Treant:** The item we designed earlier. Once per day, the user can plant the seed in any soil, dirt, sand, ash, or other porous material. Within one minute, a twenty-foot-tall tree rapidly sprouts from the seed. The tree's species is determined by the type of soil it is planted in, but it will always be a hardy, resilient variety. While it is a mundane tree in most senses, it carries the faint magical aura of the seed that birthed it. The moment the tree begins to grow, it emits a powerful psychic scream. This scream is not audible in the traditional sense but is heard directly in the minds of all creatures within a one-mile radius. The scream is a wordless আওয় of pain, loss, and rage, echoing the trauma of the Mourning. It's not necessarily harmful but is extremely unsettling and disorienting. The scream reveals the exact location of the tree to all who hear it. They instinctively know the direction and distance to the tree's location. The intensity of the scream might vary depending on the environment. In areas heavily affected by the Mourning, the scream might be amplified or distorted. The tree, despite its rapid growth, is exceptionally sturdy. It can support the weight of up to three Medium creatures at a time, even on its highest branches (up to twenty feet). The branches are thick and strong, providing secure handholds and footholds. The seed recharges its magic at each dawn, becoming dormant and inert until then. The tree, once grown, remains until it is destroyed or withers naturally, which in the Mournland, could take days or weeks depending on the specific location it was grown.
- **Upgrade Components:**
    - **Tier 1:**
        - **Living Bark:** A piece of bark from a rare, resilient tree found in the Mournland. When incorporated into the item, it grants the user resistance to bludgeoning damage. Requires a DC 10 Wisdom (Nature) check to identify and harvest properly.
        - **Whispering Root:** A root from a tree that grows near areas with strong psychic residue. When incorporated into the item, it allows the user to cast the _speak with plants_ spell once per day. Requires a DC 10 Intelligence (Nature) check to find.
    - **Tier 2:**
        - **Heartwood of the Grove:** A piece of wood from the heart of a corrupted grove, such as the one Roric might have created. When incorporated into the item, it grants the user advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. Requires defeating a powerful plant based creature.
        - **Tears of the Weeping Oasis:** Water collected from the Weeping Oasis. When used in the crafting process, it allows the user to cast the _lesser restoration_ spell once per day, using the item as a focus. Requires a DC 15 Constitution saving throw to collect the water without suffering any ill effects.
    - **Tier 3:**
        - **Seed of Evergrowth:** A rare seed found in a hidden part of the Mournland, said to possess the power of rapid regeneration. When incorporated into the item, it allows the user to cast the _regenerate_ spell once per week. Requires a DC 20 Wisdom (Survival) check to find.
        - **Roric's Research Notes:** If the party defeats Roric and recovers his notes, they might find instructions on how to further enhance the item with corrupted nature magic, granting it additional powers but potentially at a cost to the user. Requires a DC 18 Intelligence (Arcana) check to decipher.
    - **Tier 4:**
        - **Blessing of the Warden:** A powerful blessing bestowed upon the item by a high-ranking member of the Wardens of the Wood or a powerful nature spirit. This could grant the item the ability to cast high-level druid spells, such as _mass cure wounds_ or _wall of thorns_, once per day. Requires the completion of a major quest for the Wardens of the Wood.
        - **Heart of the Mourning Tree:** If the party can find and purify the source of the corruption in a specific area of the Mournland (such as the Twisted Forest), they might be able to harvest the "Heart" of the corrupted area - a piece of the original tree or a powerful magical object that has been cleansed of the Mourning's taint. This could be used to upgrade the item, granting it immense power but potentially requiring the user to sacrifice something in return. Requires a major quest to complete.

**4. Sentinel's Panoply (Heavy Armour)**

- **Concept:** A suit of heavy armor, designed for a member of House Deneith, but infused with the wild magic of the Mournland. This armor is ideal for a warrior who wants to stand their ground and protect their allies. This is ideal for the paladin/cleric, or potentially a fighter.
- **Base Component:**
    - **Sentinel's Plate:** A suit of plate armor, bearing the insignia of House Deneith. It is clearly well made, and has a number of scratches and dents, suggesting it has seen a lot of use. The wearer has disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks, and it takes 5 minutes to don or doff this armor. A creature must have a Strength score of 15 or higher to wear this armor, or their speed is reduced by 10 feet. This armor provides an AC of 18.
- **Upgrade Components:**
    - **Tier 1:**
        - **Pauldrons of the Watchful:** These shoulder guards are etched with the image of an eye. While wearing the armor, the user gains a +1 bonus to initiative rolls. Requires a DC 10 Intelligence (Investigation) check to find a suitable set.
        - **Gauntlets of the Defender:** These gauntlets are reinforced with steel bands. While wearing the armor, the user can use their reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll of a creature within 5 feet that is attacking a target other than the wearer. Requires a DC 10 Strength (Athletics) check to find a suitable pair.
    - **Tier 2:**
        - **Breastplate of the Stalwart:** This breastplate is emblazoned with the image of a shield. While wearing the armor, the user gains a +1 bonus to their AC. Requires a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check to find.
        - **Greaves of the Steadfast:** These leg guards are made from a বিশেষ type of metal that is resistant to magic. While wearing the armor, the user has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects that would forcibly move them. Requires a DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth) check to find a suitable pair, as they are often hidden away.
    - **Tier 3:**
        - **Helm of the Vigilant:** This helmet is fitted with a visor that glows with a faint light. While wearing the armor, the user gains darkvision out to a range of 60 feet and cannot be surprised. Requires a DC 20 Intelligence (Investigation) check to find.
        - **Shield of the Protector:** This shield is made from a special type of wood that is resistant to fire. While wielding the shield, the user gains resistance to fire damage. Requires a DC 20 Strength (Athletics) check to find a suitable specimen, as they are often integrated into the walls of buildings.
    - **Tier 4:**
        - **Mark of the Sentinel:** This magical mark can be applied to the armor, granting it the ability to cast the _warding bond_ spell once per day without expending a spell slot. This requires a special ritual and a DC 20 Arcana check. On a failure, the armor is damaged, and the user takes 4d6 force damage.
        - **The Sentinel's Panoply:** A legendary upgrade that unlocks the armor's full potential. The armor becomes a +3 suit of magical armor and grants the wearer immunity to the frightened condition. Requires a special ritual and a DC 25 Arcana check. On a failure, the armor is destroyed, and the user takes 10d6 force damage.

**Materials and Acquisition:**

- **Croydonite:** Different types of Croydonite could add unique properties to the items, but also risks.
- **Creature Parts:** Scales, hides, fangs, and other parts from Mournland creatures could be used to enhance the items' effectiveness.
- **Lost Technology:** Components from warforged, ruined constructs, or pre-Mourning magical devices could be salvaged and repurposed.
- **Blueprints:** Blueprints could be found in ruined workshops, libraries, or even in the possession of certain NPCs.
- **Experimentation:** Troxler might be able to experiment with different combinations of materials, potentially leading to new discoveries (or dangerous failures).

**Crafting and Troxler:**

- **Expertise:** Troxler is the primary NPC for crafting these items, but as the campaign progresses, the party might encounter other skilled artisans who can assist them or offer specialized services.
- **Costs:** Crafting should involve significant costs, both in terms of gold and rare materials.
- **Time:** Crafting complex items should take time, potentially days or even weeks for higher-tier upgrades.
- **Challenges:** The crafting process itself could involve challenges, such as skill checks, dealing with unstable materials, or even attracting unwanted attention from creatures
