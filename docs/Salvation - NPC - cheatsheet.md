**Salvation NPC Cheat Sheet**

**Law Enforcement ([[House Tharashk]]):**

- **Sheriff Hardin "Hard-Eye" [[Sheriff Karr|Karr]]:**
    
    - **Role:** Sheriff of Salvation, enforces the law and protects House Tharashk's interests.
    - **Personality:** Stern, gruff, no-nonsense, loyal to House Tharashk but primarily concerned with the town's safety. Stickler for rules. Dislikes music.
    - **Secret:** Lost his little finger in a card game gone wrong, ashamed of it.
    - **Plot Connection:** Represents House Tharashk's authority and is a source of information and quests.
    - **Appearance:** Grizzled, imposing human, handlebar mustache, wide-brimmed hat, leather duster, custom repeating crossbow, House Tharashk badge, missing little finger.
- **Deputy "Deadeye" [[Deputy Dal|Dal]]:**
    
    - **Role:** Deputy Sheriff, eager to prove himself.
    - **Personality:** Young, lanky half-elf, nervous, twitchy, skilled marksman but lacks confidence. Idolizes Sheriff Karr.
    - **Secret:** Infatuated with Grelga, the half-orc barkeep.
    - **Plot Connection:** His brother is a Cannith agent, monitoring him for signs of magical aptitude.
    - **Appearance:** Lanky half-elf, constantly cleaning his twin hand crossbows, nervous twitch, bandana.
- **Deputy "Whisper" [[Deputy Elara|Elara]]:**
    
    - **Role:** Deputy Sheriff, most competent and enigmatic.
    - **Personality:** Quiet, observant, deadly efficient, former assassin, deaf, expert lip-reader.
    - **Secret:** Secretly a double agent for House Cannith, monitoring Tharashk and gathering intel.
    - **Plot Connection:** Represents House Cannith's clandestine interests in the Mournland.
    - **Appearance:** Slight, hooded human woman, throwing knives, whip, speaks in whispers, rarely speaks.
- **Deputy "Bruiser" [[Deputy Bron|Bron]]:**
    
    - **Role:** Deputy Sheriff, muscle of the group.
    - **Personality:** **Hulking half-orc**, simple, loyal, enjoys drinking and brawling. Follows orders without question.
    - **Secret:** Terrified of spiders. Has a surprisingly good singing voice.
    - **Plot Connection:** Provides comic relief but can also be surprisingly insightful.
    - **Appearance:** Hulking half-orc, scarred face, dented breastplate, warhammer, often carries a keg of beer.

**Iron Defenders:**

- **Lemon:** Sheriff Karr's Iron Defender. Acid-themed, sickly yellow-green, aggressive, leaks acid.
- **Apple:** Deputy Dal's Iron Defender. Fire-themed, shiny red, by-the-book, eager to please.
- **Plum:** Deputy Elara's Iron Defender. Necrotic/shadow-themed, deep purple-black, stealthy, independent.
- **Orange:** Deputy Bron's Iron Defender. Lightning-themed, bright orange, loud, boisterous, fiercely loyal.

**Traders and Merchants:**

- **"Old Man" [[Silas]] (Edgar ir'Wynarn):**
    
    - **Role:** General Store Owner.
    - **Personality:** Cranky, suspicious, shrewd bargainer, secretly a disgraced Cyran noble and scholar.
    - **Secret:** Hiding his true identity and his knowledge of pre-Mourning history and magic, he was
    - **Plot Connection:** Interested in [[the Final Sanction]], potential source of historical information.
    - **Appearance:** Wizened, elderly human, hunched back, scraggly white beard, suspicious squint, patched-up clothes.
- **[[Mara Fingers Keen]]:**
    
    - **Role:** Black Market Trader.
    - **Personality:** Nimble, young halfling, quick hands, mischievous, charming, opportunistic, secretly lonely.
    - **Secret:** Working for House Phiarlan/Thuranni, gathering information. Also secretly cares for orphans.
    - **Plot Connection:** Tied to the underworld, potential source of rare items and information.
    - **Appearance:** Young halfling, nimble, mischievous grin, dark clothing, many rings with hidden compartments, pet ferret.
- **[[Dr. Alaric Vex (The Alchemist)]]:**
    
    - **Role:** Alchemist, researcher.
    - **Personality:** Young tiefling, brilliant, eccentric, obsessed with the Mournland, morally ambiguous, struggles with social cues.
    - **Secret:** Funded by a shadowy organization, addicted to his own concoction.
    - **Plot Connection:** His research into Croydonite and Mournland materials could have major consequences.
    - **Appearance:** Young tiefling, crimson skin, swept-back horns, piercing yellow eyes, stained lab coat, silver tongue piercing, multitude of vials and tools.

**Other NPCs:**

- **"Mama" Elara Thorne:**
    
    - **Role:** Landlady of the party's base (Shack E).
    - **Personality:** Middle-aged human woman, sturdy, kind, no-nonsense, secretly a retired adventurer.
    - **Secret:** Skilled with a crossbow, saving to leave Salvation, has a hidden past.
    - **Plot Connection:** Potential mentor figure, might be drawn back into adventuring.
    - **Appearance:** Middle-aged human woman, sturdy build, kind face, no-nonsense attitude, always has her sleeves rolled up, a large scar on her right arm.
    
- **Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight:**
    
    - **Role:** Town cleric, healer.
    - **Personality:** Young centaur, gentle, compassionate, timid, inexperienced, soft-spoken, good listener.
    - **Secret:** Fled from the Feywild through a manifest zone, traumatized, fears the Mournland and his own powers.
    - **Plot Connection:** Tied to the Feywild, potential source of healing and nature-related information.
    - **Appearance:** Young centaur, chestnut brown equine body, lean, muscular human torso, warm brown skin, braided, light brown hair adorned with feathers and beads, a limp.
- **Grelga:**
    
    - **Role:** Barkeep at the Crooked Tankard.
    - **Personality:** Gruff but fair half-orc, shrewd businesswoman, skilled fighter, secretly lonely.
    - **Secret:** Former mercenary, secretly in love with Sheriff Karr.
    - **Plot Connection:** Source of rumors and information, potential contact for the underworld.
    - **Appearance:** Strong, female half-orc.

**The Steel Stoners (Dwarven Miners):**

- **Borin "Stonebeard" Grimcleaver:** Leader, gruff, pragmatic, ruthless, resents House Kundarak, being blackmailed.
- **Gretta "Gem-Eye" Stonehand:** Second-in-command, skilled miner, fiercely loyal, secretly concerned about Croydonite, blackmailer.
- **Dwalin "The Mole" Kettlebottom:** Nervous scout, secretly informing for House Phiarlan/Thuranni, deathly afraid of birds.

**The Order of the Broken Sky:**

- **Vaelia Moonwhisper:** Leader, elderly human archmage, wise, patient, ruthless, secretive, may have ties to Gyda's past.
- **Kaelen "The Raven" Rhen:** Field agent, young half-elf, cynical, distrustful, haunted by the Mournland, skilled scout and infiltrator.
- **Archivist Theron:** Gnome scholar, brilliant, eccentric, obsessed with pre-Mourning magic, secretly experimenting with Croydonite.

**This cheat sheet should provide you with a quick reference for all the important NPCs in Salvation. Remember that their personalities, motivations, and secrets can evolve over time based on the players' actions and the unfolding story. Use these NPCs to create engaging interactions, offer quests, provide information, and drive the narrative forward. They are the lifeblood of Salvation, and their stories are intertwined with the fate of the town and the mysteries of the Mournland.**

**Crafting and Trade:**

- **[[Troxler, the Rookie Trader]]:** A rookie female, gnome, artificer specializing in reverse-engineering and crafting magic items. They are eager to learn and improve, and their skills grow alongside the party's. They are the primary contact for the modular crafting system.

**Merchants and Tavern Owners:**

- ** [[Grelga]] :** A female half-orc, owner of  [[the crooked tankard]]  tavern. Deputy Dal has a crush on her. (We haven't fleshed her out much yet, so there's room to expand on her personality and role.)

**The Steel Stoners (Dwarven Miners):**

- **Borin "Stonebeard" Grimcleaver:** The leader of the Steel Stoners, a gruff, pragmatic dwarf with a missing eye and a resentment towards House Kundarak. He's secretly being blackmailed.
- **Gretta "Gem-Eye" Stonehand:** Borin's second-in-command, a skilled miner with a gemstone eye, and a secret সন্দেহ about the safety of Croydonite. She is the one blackmailing Borin.
- **Dwalin "The Mole" Kettlebottom:** The clan's nervous scout, secretly providing information to House Phiarlan/Thuranni and is deathly afraid of birds.

**The Order of the Broken Sky:**

While these NPCs are not residents of [[npcs]], they have a strong connection to the town and the events unfolding in [[the Mournland]].

- **Vaelia Moonwhisper:** The leader of the Order, an elderly human archmage and diviner. She is seeking the truth behind the Mourning and believes the Sky Song Tavern holds a key. She may have connections to Gyda's past.
- **Kaelen "The Raven" Rhen:** A half-elf field agent and scout for the Order, haunted by a past experience in [[the Mournland]].
- **Archivist Theron:** A gnome scholar and archivist for the Order, obsessed with pre-Mourning magic and secretly experimenting with Croydonite.
