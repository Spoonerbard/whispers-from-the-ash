**Marrow Leech (formerly "Moopsey the Bone Drinker")**

**Description:**

- **Appearance:** The Marrow Leech is a bizarre, unsettling creature resembling a large, flightless bird, similar in size and build to an ostrich. However, its body is covered in thick, overlapping scales of a mottled grey-brown color, providing excellent camouflage in the desolate landscape. Its legs are powerful and end in sharp, three-toed claws.
- **Head and Neck:** Its head is relatively small and bird-like, with beady, black eyes that constantly swivel, mounted on a long, flexible, arm-like neck that can rotate a full 360 degrees. This neck is surprisingly strong and can lash out with surprising speed.
- **The Stalk:** The most disturbing feature is a thick, scaly, retractable stalk that emerges from the creature's midriff. This stalk is tipped with a lamprey-like maw ringed with sharp, bony plates and a long, hollow tongue used for sucking up water, nutrients, and, most horrifyingly, bone marrow from its prey.
- **Sound:** The Marrow Leech emits a low, guttural croaking sound when it is hunting or when it prepares it's sonic attack.

**Habitat:**

- Marrow Leeches are found throughout the Mournland, but they prefer areas with some water sources, such as the Stillwater, or areas with occasional rainfall, and are drawn to locations with fresh kills, that still have intact bones.

**Behavior and Tactics:**

- **Ambush Predators:** Marrow Leeches are primarily ambush predators, using their camouflage and flexible necks to strike from unexpected angles.
- **Charge and Leap:** They can charge at enemies with surprising speed, using their powerful legs to leap across obstacles or close the distance to their prey.
- **Deafening Croak:** They emit a deafening, low-frequency croak that can disorient and even damage their foes, especially effective against undead.
- **Grapple and Drain:** Their primary goal is to grapple a target with their powerful legs and neck, then use their feeding stalk to pierce the victim's body and drain their bone marrow. This is a gruesome and debilitating attack.
- **Pack Hunters:** While they can hunt alone, they are often found in small packs of 3-5 individuals, coordinating their attacks to isolate and overwhelm larger prey.
- **Territorial:** They are fiercely territorial and will defend their hunting grounds from intruders.

- **Claws:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage.
- **Deafening Croak (Recharge 5-6):** The Marrow Leech emits a powerful, low-frequency croak. Each creature within 30 feet of the Marrow Leech must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 10 (3d6) thunder damage and is deafened for 1 minute. **Reactions:** **Latch and Grapple:** When the Marrow Leech hits a Large or smaller creature with it's bite attack, it can immediately grapple the target (escape DC 13).

**Tactics:**

- **Hit and Run:** Marrow Leeches will use their speed and Deafening Croak to disorient their prey, then quickly retreat.
- **Focus Fire:** If in a pack, they will focus their attacks on a single target, trying to grapple it and use their Bone Drain ability.
- **Use the Environment:** They will use their surroundings to their advantage, hiding behind rocks or ruins before ambushing their prey. They might also try to lure their victims into areas where their croak attack will be more effective, such as narrow canyons or enclosed spaces.

**Possible Encounters:**

- **The Watering Hole:** The party discovers a small, muddy watering hole where a pack of Marrow Leeches is lying in wait for thirsty creatures.
- **The Ambush:** As the party travels through a narrow canyon, they are ambushed by Marrow Leeches that leap down from the cliffs above.
- **The Nest:** The party stumbles upon a Marrow Leech nest, filled with eggs and guarded by several protective adults.
- **The Scavengers:** The party finds the remains of a recent battle, with a group of Marrow Leeches fighting over the carcass of a larger creature.

**Rewards:**

- **Marrow Leech Hide:** The creature's tough, scaly hide could be used to craft armor or other items.
- **Feeding Stalk:** The feeding stalk might be valuable to alchemists or researchers interested in the creature's unique biology, such as Troxler.
- **Bone Marrow:** While disturbing, the bone marrow extracted by the Marrow Leeches might have unique alchemical properties, but could also be dangerous or addictive.

**The Marrow Leech is a formidable and disturbing addition to the Mournland's ecosystem. Remember to emphasize the creature's unsettling appearance, its predatory nature, and the horrifying implications of its Bone Drain attack.
