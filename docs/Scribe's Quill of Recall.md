**Revised Scribe's Quill of Recall (Tier 1)**

**Description:** An ordinary-looking quill pen crafted from raven feather and bone. It feels strangely warm to the touch, and when used to write, the ink seems to flow effortlessly onto the page.

**Tier 1 Ability:**

- **Diligent Scribe:** Once per day, the wizard can use the quill to reduce the time and cost required to transcribe a spell into their spellbook. The time required is halved, and the gold cost is reduced by 25%. The spell must be of a level they can normally cast.
- **Frugal উপায়:** In addition to this, the wizard can make a DC 10 Intelligence (Arcana) check when they finish copying a spell into their spellbook. On a success, they do not expend the gp worth of materials normally used to copy the spell.

**Upgrade Paths:**

We'll keep the focus on enhancing the quill's ability to transcribe spells, making it more efficient and eventually allowing for the copying of higher-level spells. Here are three paths: **Accuracy, Efficiency, and Mastery.**

**1. Path of Accuracy (Focus: Reducing Failure Chance and Enhancing Understanding):**

- **Theme:** This path emphasizes the meticulous and precise nature of spell transcription, reducing the chance of errors and deepening the wizard's understanding of the spells they copy.
- **Tier 2 Upgrade:**
    - **Insightful Script:** When using the quill to transcribe a spell, the wizard can make an Intelligence (Arcana) check. The DC is equal to 10 + the spell's level. On a success, they gain a temporary insight into the spell's workings, granting them advantage on the next ability check or saving throw they make related to that spell within the next 24 hours.
    - **Requirement:** The wizard must successfully transcribe a certain number of spells using the quill, demonstrating their dedication to the craft. They might also need to study a rare text on spell theory or decipher a complex magical diagram.
- **Tier 3 Upgrade:**
    - **Flawless Replication:** When using the quill to transcribe a spell, the wizard automatically succeeds on any ability checks required to copy the spell perfectly, even if they don't expend the gold.
    - **Requirement:** The wizard must transcribe a spell of 5th level or higher using the quill. They might also need to acquire a special type of ink or parchment, such as ink infused with dragon's blood or parchment made from the skin of a magical creature.
- **Tier 4 Upgrade:**
    - **Master's Insight:** When the wizard uses their **Insightful Script** ability, the insight they gain into the spell's workings is permanent. The first time they cast the spell each day, they have advantage on their first attack roll with the spell, or their target has disadvantage on their first saving throw against the spell. The wizard can also now use their **Frugal উপায়** ability twice per day.
    - **Requirement:** The wizard must transcribe a spell of 7th level or higher using the quill. They must also have transcribed at least 10 spells without ever failing an ability check.

**2. Path of Efficiency (Focus: Reducing Time and Cost):**

- **Theme:** This path emphasizes speed and resourcefulness, allowing the wizard to copy spells more quickly and with less expenditure of valuable materials.
- **Tier 2 Upgrade:**
    - **Swift Script:** The time required to transcribe a spell using the quill is reduced to one-quarter of the normal time (e.g., 30 minutes for a 2nd-level spell).
    - **Requirement:** The wizard must transcribe a certain number of spells within a limited timeframe, demonstrating their ability to work quickly and efficiently. They might also need to acquire a special type of ink that dries instantly or a quill made from a creature known for its speed.
- **Tier 3 Upgrade:**
    - **Economical Replication:** The gold cost to transcribe a spell is reduced by 50%. The wizard can use their **Frugal উপায়** ability twice per day.
    - **Requirement:** The wizard must successfully transcribe a spell using only half the usual amount of ink or parchment, demonstrating their mastery over resource management. They might also need to study with a master scribe or learn a technique for conserving magical materials.
- **Tier 4 Upgrade:**
    - **Effortless Transcription:** The wizard can now transcribe spells of up to 5th level into their spellbook using the quill, without expending any time or gold, once per day. They can also use their **Frugal উপায়** ability three times per day.
    - **Requirement:** The wizard must transcribe a spell of 6th level or higher using the quill, using only half the normal amount of time and resources.

**3. Path of Mastery (Focus: Unlocking Hidden Potential):**

- **Theme:** This path delves into the deeper magic of the quill, unlocking abilities that go beyond mere transcription and allow the wizard to manipulate spells in new ways.
- **Tier 2 Upgrade:**
    - **Echoing Script:** Once per day, when the wizard casts a spell they have transcribed using the quill, they can choose to have the spell's verbal and/or somatic components originate from the location where they transcribed the spell, instead of from their own location. The location must be within the same plane of existence, and within 120 feet of the wizard.
    - **Requirement:** The wizard must successfully decipher a complex magical text or diagram related to the manipulation of spell energy. They might also need to infuse the quill with a rare material, such as a piece of a spell scroll or a gem that resonates with magical energy.
- **Tier 3 Upgrade:**
    - **Glyphic Infusion:** When the wizard uses the quill to transcribe a spell, they can infuse it with a special glyph. The next time they cast that spell, they can activate the glyph as a bonus action. The glyph's effect depends on the spell's school of magic (e.g., an abjuration glyph might grant temporary hit points, an evocation glyph might deal extra damage, a transmutation glyph might alter the spell's target).
    - **Requirement:** The wizard must study with a master enchanter or a creature with innate magical abilities, learning how to infuse spells with additional properties.
- **Tier 4 Upgrade:**
    - **Arcane Synthesis:** Once per day, the wizard can use the quill to combine two spells they have transcribed into their spellbook into a single, more powerful effect. The two spells must be of the same level, and the resulting effect is determined by the combination of schools of magic involved (e.g., combining a fire spell with an ice spell might create a blast of freezing fire). The new spell uses both of the original spell slots. The wizard can also now use their **Frugal উপায়** ability three times per day.
    - **Requirement:** The wizard must successfully combine two different spells into a single effect, demonstrating their mastery over spell manipulation. They might also need to acquire a unique and powerful magical artifact, such as a fragment of a broken staff of power or a gem that resonates with multiple schools of magic.
