#npcs/factions/Salvation 


**I. General Principles**

- **Town Tier:** The availability and quality of goods and services are directly tied to Salvation's current tier (Outpost, Frontier Town, Thriving Hub, Regional Power). As the town grows, more merchants arrive, existing shops expand their offerings, and the overall quality of goods improves.
- **Player Actions:** The party's actions, such as completing quests, discovering resources, and attracting new settlers, will directly contribute to the town's growth and the evolution of its merchants' wares.
- **Scarcity and Value:** In the early stages, basic goods might be expensive and of poor quality due to the difficulty of obtaining them in the Mournland. As the town develops, prices might stabilize, and higher-quality items become more readily available.
- **Uniqueness:** Salvation should offer some unique goods and services not readily available elsewhere, reflecting its proximity to the Mournland and the specialized skills of its inhabitants (e.g., Troxler's crafting, Dr. Vex's alchemy).

**II. Merchant Wares and Services Progression**

Here's a breakdown of how specific merchants and their offerings might evolve through the tiers:

**A. "Old Man" [[Silas]] (General Store)**

- **Tier 1: Outpost**
    - **Goods:**
        - **Basic Tools:** Crowbars, hammers, shovels, ropes, etc. (poor quality, prone to breaking).
        - **Rations:** Bland, unappetizing, and barely enough to sustain a person (short shelf life).
        - **Torches and Lanterns:** Dim, unreliable, and requiring frequent refueling.
        - **Basic Weapons and Armor:** Clubs, daggers, spears, leather armor (poor quality, offering minimal protection).
        - **Adventuring Gear:** Bedrolls (one-use), backpacks (small capacity), waterskins (leaky), etc.
        - **Other:** Candles, chalk, ink, parchment, quills, basic clothing.
    - **Services:**
        - Buys salvage and trinkets from the Mournland (at low prices).
        - Offers rumors and information (often unreliable or exaggerated).
- **Tier 2: Frontier Town**
    - **Goods:**
        - **Improved Tools:** More durable tools, less likely to break.
        - **Better Rations:** Slightly more palatable and nutritious.
        - **Improved Lighting:** Brighter, longer-lasting torches and lanterns.
        - **Better Weapons and Armor:** Swords, axes, bows, studded leather, chain shirts (standard quality).
        - **More Adventuring Gear:** Larger backpacks, more durable bedrolls, improved waterskins.
        - **Other:** Basic crafting materials (wood, leather, metal), simple medicines.
    - **Services:**
        - Starts offering a wider range of goods.
        - Might start offering credit to adventurers he trusts.
- **Tier 3: Thriving Hub**
    - **Goods:**
        - **High-Quality Tools:** Masterwork tools that provide bonuses to skill checks.
        - **Preserved Rations:** More nutritious and long-lasting rations, specially prepared for expeditions into the Mournland.
        - **Specialized Lighting:** Everburning torches, sunrods.
        - **High-Quality Weapons and Armor:** Masterwork weapons and armor, potentially including heavier armor types like breastplates and half plate.
        - **Mournland-Adapted Gear:** Specialized clothing, goggles, gas masks, and other gear designed to mitigate the hazards of the Mournland.
    - **Services:**
        - Can acquire specific mundane items on request (with a markup).
        - Starts stocking rare materials found by adventurers.
- **Tier 4: Regional Power**
    - **Goods:**
        - **Exotic Goods:** Items from distant lands, brought in by trade caravans.
        - **Rare Materials:** High-quality crafting materials, potentially including rare metals and gems.
    - **Services:**
        - Offers a wide range of specialized services, such as repairs, appraisals, and identification of mundane items.

**B. Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight (Cleric - Healing Services)**

- **Tier 1: Outpost**
    - **Services:**
        - _Cure Wounds_ (1st level, limited to 1d8 + Wisdom modifier healing).
        - _Lesser Restoration_ (can only cure basic poisons and diseases).
        - _Identify_ (to determine magical effects and curses).
        - Herbal remedies for minor ailments (non-magical healing).
        - Can mend clothing, and other cloth items.
    - **Limitations:**
        - Can only cast a limited number of spells per day.
        - Lacks the power to deal with serious injuries or magical afflictions.
        - His fear restricts his spellcasting to 1st level spells.
    - **Pricing:**
        - _Cure Wounds_: 10 gp
        - _Lesser Restoration_: 50 gp
        - _Identify_: 20 gp
- **Tier 2: Frontier Town**
    - **Services:**
        - _Cure Wounds_ (up to 2nd level, 2d8 + Wisdom modifier healing).
        - _Lesser Restoration_ (can cure more potent poisons and diseases).
        - _Prayer of Healing_
        - _Gentle Repose_
        - Basic potion brewing (e.g., _potion of healing_).
        - Can start to use 2nd level spells.
    - **Limitations:**
        - Still limited by his fear and inexperience.
        - May need time to prepare more complex remedies.
    - **Pricing:**
        - _Cure Wounds_ (2nd level): 25 gp
        - _Prayer of Healing_: 40 gp
        - _Gentle Repose_: 50 gp
        - _Potion of Healing_: 50 gp (takes 1 day to brew)
- **Tier 3: Thriving Hub**
    - **Services:**
        - _Cure Wounds_ (up to 3rd level, 3d8 + Wisdom modifier healing).
        - _Remove Curse_
        - _Revivify_ (Material components must be provided, with an additional 50gp fee)
        - _Protection from Poison_
        - More advanced potion brewing (e.g., _potion of greater healing_).
        - Can start to use 3rd level spells.
    - **Limitations:**
        - Still restricted by his fear from using higher-level magic.
        - May need rare ingredients for certain spells or potions.
    - **Pricing:**
        - _Cure Wounds_ (3rd level): 40 gp
        - _Remove Curse_: 90 gp
        - _Revivify_: 350 gp (300 gp for the diamond, 50 gp for the service)
        - _Protection from Poison_: 40 gp
        - _Potion of Greater Healing_: 150 gp (takes 3 days to brew)
- **Tier 4: Regional Power**
    - **Services:**
        - _Heal_
        - _Greater Restoration_
        - Advanced potion brewing (e.g., _potion of superior healing_, _potion of vitality_).
    - **Limitations:**
        - His fear prevents him from using spells beyond 3rd level.
    - **Pricing:**
        - _Heal_: 150 gp
        - _Greater Restoration_: 450 gp
        - _Potion of Superior Healing_: 500 gp (takes 1 week to brew)
        - _Potion of Vitality_: 500 gp (takes 1 week to brew)

**C. Dr. Alaric Vex (Alchemist)**

- **Tier 1: Outpost**
    - **Services:**
        - Offers to identify strange materials found in the Mournland (for a fee).
        - Can create basic alchemical items (e.g., acid, alchemist's fire, antitoxin) but of questionable quality.
        - Will buy unusual items, and monster parts from the party.
    - **Quest Related:**
        - Asks the party to retrieve a sample of water from the Stillwater.
- **Tier 2: Frontier Town**
    - **Services:**
        - Starts offering experimental potions and elixirs with unpredictable effects.
        - Can create more potent versions of basic alchemical items.
        - Can start to identify basic magical items.
    - **Quest Related:**
        - Begins to ask for specific creatures to be captured alive.
- **Tier 3: Thriving Hub**
    - **Services:**
        - Offers a wider range of alchemical items, including some that are unique to the Mournland.
        - Can create custom concoctions based on the party's needs (if they provide the right ingredients).
        - Can identify most magical items, and their properties.
    - **Quest Related:**
        - Asks the party to retrieve rare items from specific locations.
- **Tier 4: Regional Power**
    - **Services:**
        - His creations become highly sought after, though some are still dangerous or unstable.
        - Might start offering services related to modifying or enhancing existing magic items.
        - Can identify all magical items.
    - **Quest Related:**
        - Will only deal with the party if they have earned his trust, otherwise he will refuse to deal with them.

**D. Mara "Fingers" Keen (Black Market Trader)**

- **Tier 1: Outpost**
    - **Goods:**
        - Stolen goods, basic weapons and armor, low-quality tools, and other illicit items.
    - **Services:**
        - Can acquire specific items on request (with a high markup and no guarantees).
        - Fencing stolen goods.
        - Providing information and rumors (for a price).
- **Tier 2: Frontier Town**
    - **Goods:**
        - Starts offering some salvaged Mournland artifacts, potentially including minor magical items.
        - More variety of stolen goods, including some higher-quality items.
    - **Services:**
        - Her network of contacts expands, allowing her to acquire more specific or unusual items.
        - Starts offering smuggling services in and out of Salvation.
- **Tier 3: Thriving Hub**
    - **Goods:**
        - Offers a wider selection of Mournland artifacts, including some with potent (and potentially dangerous) effects.
        - Starts dealing in rare and valuable materials, such as refined Croydonite.
    - **Services:**
        - Can arrange for the transport of illegal goods or passengers.
        - Her information network extends further into the Mournland and beyond.
- **Tier 4: Regional Power**
    - **Goods:**
        - Becomes a major source of rare and powerful magical items, some of which might be cursed or have dangerous origins.
    - **Services:**
        - Her influence extends to other settlements, and she might even be able to arrange meetings with influential figures.
