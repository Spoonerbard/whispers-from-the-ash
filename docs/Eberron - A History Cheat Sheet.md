#lore/history


Eberron's history is a tapestry woven with dragons, prophecies, and powerful magic. Here's a glimpse into the key events and lore:

**The Age of Dragons:**

- **The Progenitor Dragons:** Eberron began with three powerful dragons: [[Siberys]], [[Eberron]], and [[Khyber]]. They represent creation, preservation, and destruction, respectively.
- **The Dragon War:** Siberys and Khyber clashed over the Draconic Prophecy, a mystical foretelling of the future. Eberron intervened, binding Khyber within her coils to prevent universal destruction.
- **The World is Born:** [[Siberys]] became the ring of starlight around the world, Eberron became the world itself, and [[Khyber]] became the underworld.

**The Age of Giants:**

- **Giant Civilization:** Giants ruled Eberron, building vast empires and wielding powerful magic.
- **The Dhakaani Empire:** Goblinoid races, led by the [[Dhakaani]], rose to challenge [[the giants]]. This conflict ultimately led to the decline of both civilizations.

**The Age of Monsters:**

- **The Daelkyr War:** Aberrant creatures from [[Xoriat]], the plane of madness, invaded Eberron. These [[Daelkyr]] warped reality and created monstrous creatures.
- **The Age of Demons:** Demons poured forth from Khyber, wreaking havoc across the land.
- **The Couatl and the Draconic Prophecy:** [[Couatls]], celestial beings, guided heroes to fight back the demons and seal the gates to [[Khyber]].

**The Age of Humans:**

- **The Rise of Humanity:** Humans arrived on Eberron and established their own nations.
- **The Galifar Empire:** King Galifar I united the human kingdoms, creating a vast and powerful empire that lasted for centuries.
- **The Last War:** After Galifar's death, his heirs plunged the empire into a devastating civil war that lasted for a century.

**The Present Age:**

- **The Treaty of Thronehold:**  the last war ended in a fragile peace, with Khorvaire divided into five nations.
- **[[the Mournland]]:** A magical catastrophe during [[the last war]] created a blighted wasteland filled with danger.
- **The  [[dragonmarked houses]] :** Powerful families with hereditary magic control much of Eberron's economy and society.

**Important Lore:**

- **The Draconic Prophecy:** A mysterious prophecy that foretells the future of Eberron. Many factions seek to interpret and manipulate it for their own ends.
- **The Mark of Death:** A curse that prevents resurrection, adding a sense of finality to death in Eberron.
- **Magic as Technology:** Magic is integrated into everyday life, powering airships, trains, and even creating artificial life like the [[warforged]].
