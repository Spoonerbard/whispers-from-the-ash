#lore/history 


- **Geography:** A large and diverse nation in southwestern [[Khorvaire]], bordering [[the Mournland]] to the east. It encompasses vast forests, fertile plains, and a long coastline. It's capital is [[Wroat]].
    - **Culture:** Breland is known for its pragmatism, its industrial might, and its relatively liberal and democratic ideals. It's a melting pot of different cultures and races. They are known for their strong military and their intelligence agency, [[the King's Citadel]].
    - **Post-War:** Breland suffered heavy losses during the Last War but has recovered relatively quickly, thanks to its strong economy and industrial base. They are focused on maintaining their independence and expanding their influence in the region. They are ruled by [[King Boranel ir'Wynarn]], a popular and charismatic leader.
    - **Connection to the Campaign:**
        - **Sharn:** [[The city of Sharn]], a major metropolis built on a cliff overlooking the Dagger River, is located in Breland and could serve as a hub for trade and intrigue connected to the Mournland.
        - **House Tharashk:** [[House Tharashk]] has a strong presence in Breland, and their activities in the Mournland are likely to be based out of Brelish cities.
        - **Refugees:** Breland might have absorbed a large number of Cyran refugees, creating social and economic tensions that could be relevant to the campaign. Many of these refugees could have settled in Salvation.
        - **Questlines:** The party might be hired to transport goods or passengers between Sharn and Salvation, investigate smuggling operations related to Croydonite, or deal with social unrest caused by the influx of refugees. Breland might also be interested in securing the border with the Mournland, leading to opportunities for the party to work with the Brelish military.
