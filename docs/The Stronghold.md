**Tier 4: The Stronghold**

- **Description:** A fortified and imposing structure, possibly a small keep or a heavily modified building, that serves as a symbol of the party's power and influence.
- **Requirements:**
    - Town must reach Tier 4 (Regional Power).
    - Party must be renowned throughout the region.
    - Cost: 10,000 gp.
- **Starting Features:**
    - **Fortified Walls & Gate:** Offers significant protection against attacks.
    - **Barracks:** Quarters for a small contingent of guards or soldiers (if the party chooses to hire them).
    - **War Room:** A strategic planning center with detailed maps and communication capabilities.
    - **Vault:** A heavily fortified and magically protected area for storing the most valuable treasures and artifacts.
    - **Private Quarters:** Luxurious and personalized living spaces for each party member.
- **Upgrades:**
    - **Magical Defenses (Cost: 5,000+ gp, requires high-level spellcasters and rare components):** Wards, alarms, or other magical protections.
    - **Siege Weapon Emplacements (Cost: 2,000+ gp, requires specialized engineering):** Ballistae, catapults, or other defenses against large-scale attacks.
    - **Dungeon/Prison (Cost: 1,000 gp, requires significant construction):** A secure area for holding prisoners or dangerous creatures.
    - **Hidden Escape Tunnel (Cost: 1,500 gp, requires skilled miners):** Provides a secret way to escape in case of a siege or attack.
