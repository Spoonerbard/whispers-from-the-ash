[[Eberron - A History Cheat Sheet]] 

**The Galifar Empire:**

- **Unification and Prosperity:** For centuries, the human kingdoms of Khorvaire were united under the Galifar Empire. King Galifar I, a legendary figure, forged this empire through diplomacy and strategic alliances. This era saw relative peace and prosperity, with advancements in magic and technology.
- **Seeds of Conflict:** However, the empire's stability was dependent on the strong leadership of the king. As Galifar I aged, the question of succession loomed large. He had five heirs, each ruling a separate kingdom within the empire: Aundair, Breland, Karrnath, Thrane, and Cyre.

**The King's Death and the War of Succession:**

- **The Spark:** When King Galifar I died, no clear successor was named. Each heir believed they had the right to rule, and tensions quickly escalated into open warfare. This marked the beginning of the Last War.
- **A Century of Conflict:** The war raged for a hundred years, devastating Khorvaire. Each nation had its own strengths and strategies: Aundair with its powerful mages, Breland with its industry and innovation, Karrnath with its disciplined armies and necromancy, Thrane with its fervent faith and paladins, and [[Cyre]]e]]with its cunning tactics and espionage.
- **Shifting Alliances:** Alliances shifted throughout the war, with nations forming and breaking pacts as their interests changed. This created a complex and unpredictable political landscape.

**The Rise of New Powers:**

- ** [[dragonmarked houses]] :** The war also saw the rise in influence of the Dragonmarked Houses. These families, with their hereditary magical abilities, profited from the war by providing essential services like transportation, weapons manufacturing, and healing.
- **The Lord of Blades:** In the chaos of war, a warforged leader known as the Lord of Blades emerged, seeking to create a nation for his kind. This added another layer of complexity to the conflict.

**The Mourning:**

- **The Day of Mourning:** In the year 994 YK, a catastrophic magical event occurred in Cyre, transforming the nation into the blighted wasteland known as [[the Mournland]]. The exact cause of the Mourning remains a mystery, but it effectively ended the Last War.
- **A Shattered Continent:** The Mourning left deep scars on Khorvaire, both physically and emotionally. Millions perished, and the land itself was irrevocably changed. The war ended not with a decisive victory, but with exhaustion and a profound sense of loss.

**The Aftermath:**

- **The Treaty of Thronehold:** In the wake of the Mourning, the surviving nations signed the Treaty of Thronehold, establishing a fragile peace. However, tensions remain, and the wounds of the war are far from healed.
- **Unanswered Questions:** The Mourning left many unanswered questions. What caused it? Was it an accident, or a deliberate act? These mysteries continue to haunt Eberron, shaping its present and future.

This period leading up to the Mourning is crucial for understanding the current state of Eberron. It's a time of great upheaval and change, where the old order crumbled, and a new, uncertain future emerged.

# [[Cyre]]Before the Mourning

- **The Jewel of Galifar:** [[Cyre]]was considered the heart of the Galifar Empire, known for its beauty, artistry, and vibrant culture. It was a land of rolling hills, fertile plains, and bustling cities.
- **Metropolis of Making:** House Cannith had a strong presence in Cyre, with their capital of Eston located within its borders. This led to [[Cyre]] being at the forefront of magical innovation and technology.
- **A Diverse People:** Cyrans were known for their adaptability and open-mindedness. They welcomed people from all walks of life, fostering a diverse and cosmopolitan society.
- **The Royal Family:** King Jarot ir'Wynarn ruled [[Cyre]]with [[Queen Mishann]]. They were known for their charisma and dedication to their people. Prince Oargev was their heir, a promising young leader.
- **Military Prowess:** Cyre's military was renowned for its cunning strategies and skilled spies. They were masters of unconventional warfare, using their intelligence network to their advantage.
- **Cultural Hub:** [[Cyre]] was a center of art, music, and theater. Its cities were known for their grand architecture and vibrant cultural scene.

**Key Cities:**

- **[[Metrol]]:** The capital city, a bustling metropolis known for its beautiful gardens and impressive architecture.
- **[[Eston]]:** A major industrial center, home to House Cannith and a hub of magical innovation.
- **[[Vralkek]]:** A city known for its military academies and training grounds.

**Cyran Values:**

- **Idealism:** Cyrans were known for their optimism and belief in a better future. They were driven by a strong sense of hope and a desire to create a more just and equitable society.
- **Innovation:** [[Cyre]] embraced progress and change, constantly seeking new ways to improve their lives and their nation.
- **Unity:** Despite their diversity, Cyrans were united by a strong sense of national pride and a shared vision for the future.

**Legacy:**

- **Lost to the Mourning:** The Mourning tragically ended Cyre's story, leaving behind a desolate wasteland and a legacy of unanswered questions.
- **The Ghost of Cyre:** The memory of [[Cyre]]lives on in the hearts of its survivors, who cling to the hope of one day reclaiming their homeland.
- **A Warning:** Cyre's fate serves as a reminder of the fragility of peace and the devastating consequences of unchecked conflict.

Though [[Cyre]]is gone, its impact on Eberron remains. Its legacy can be found in the scattered survivors, the echoes of its culture, and the enduring mystery of the Mourning.
