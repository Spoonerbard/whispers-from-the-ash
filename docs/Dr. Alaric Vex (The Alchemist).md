He carries a multitude of vials, pouches, and tools on his belt and bandoliers, and he's rarely seen without his thick, leather-bound notebook. He has a tail, that will often twitch when he is excited.

He's driven by a burning curiosity to understand the Mournland's secrets, particularly the properties of its mutated flora and fauna, and the strange substances found within it, like the water from the Stillwater, and especially Croydonite. He can be charming and persuasive when he needs to be, but he often becomes lost in his own thoughts, muttering to himself and scribbling furiously in his notebook. He's not inherently malicious, but his নৈতিক compass is flexible, and he's willing to take risks and make difficult choices in the pursuit of knowledge. He struggles with social cues.
- **Motivation:** To unlock the secrets of the Mournland through alchemical experimentation. He believes that the mutated environment holds the key to powerful new discoveries, potentially even a way to heal the land or to create substances with incredible properties. He is also driven by a desire for recognition and respect, wanting to prove his genius to the world.
- **Secret:** Dr. Vex is not just interested in the Mournland for scientific reasons. He's being secretly funded by a shadowy organization (you can choose which one – the Order of the Broken Sky, a গোপন branch of House Cannith, or even a Lord of Dust) that wants him to develop a specific alchemical concoction using Mournland materials. This could be a powerful weapon, a mutagen, or a substance that can manipulate the trapped souls within Croydonite. He is also addicted to a substance of his own creation.
- **Laboratory:** Dr. Vex has set up a small, cluttered laboratory in a run-down building in Salvation. The lab is filled with strange যন্ত্রপাতি, bubbling concoctions, and shelves overflowing with bizarre ingredients. It's a place of both wonder and danger, where the line between genius and madness is blurred. There is often a strange smell coming from the lab.
- **Relationship with the Party:** Dr. Vex sees the party as valuable assets, a means to acquire the rare materials he needs for his experiments. He'll offer them quests, rewards, and potentially even unique alchemical items in exchange for their help. However, he's also likely to manipulate them, withholding information or downplaying the risks involved in his requests. Whether he becomes a friend or a foe will depend on how the party interacts with him and the choices they make regarding his research.

# **Questline: The Stillwater Sample**

- **The Request:** Dr. Vex approaches the party with a proposition. He needs a sample of water from the Stillwater (the lake to the northeast), specifically from the deepest part, where the contamination is most concentrated. He claims he needs it for his research into the Mournland's effects on the environment. He provides them with a specially designed container – a **Mournland-Proof Flask** – that he says is capable of safely holding the water.
- **The Container:** The Mournland-Proof Flask is a sturdy, lead-lined container with intricate runes etched on its surface. It's designed to contain hazardous substances and prevent them from leaking or interacting with the outside environment. It might also have a built-in mechanism for extracting a small, controlled amount of the substance inside without opening the container. It is clearly very expensive.
- **The Dangers:**
    - **Wildlife:** The journey to the lake and the retrieval of the sample will be fraught with danger. The party might encounter the Stillwater Lurker, packs of Mournland Leeches, or other mutated creatures that inhabit the area.
    - **The Water:** The water itself is hazardous. Direct contact could cause mutations, illness, or other unpredictable effects (refer to the Mournland Water table).
    - **The Deep:** The deepest part of the lake might be home to unknown dangers, such as underwater caves, strong currents, or a sinkhole that could drag the party down into the depths. The party will need to find a way to reach the bottom and collect a sample without getting themselves killed.
    - **The Stillwater Lurker:** As described before, this creature is a formidable foe, and it might be drawn to the party's attempts to collect the water sample. It could be guarding the deepest part of the lake or might be attracted by the disturbance.
- **Possible Complications:**
    - **The Flask is Flawed:** The Mournland-Proof Flask might not be as foolproof as Dr. Vex claims. It could leak, break, or have some other unforeseen side effect when exposed to the Stillwater's corrupted water.
    - **Competition:** Another group (e.g., the Steel Stoners, agents of the Order of the Broken Sky, or another adventuring party) might also be after a sample of the water, leading to a race or a confrontation.
    - **The Lure:** The deepest part of the lake could contain something else that draws the party's attention – a lost artifact, a hidden cave, or a strange energy signature that interferes with their equipment.
- **Rewards:**
    - **Payment:** Dr. Vex will reward the party with gold or other valuables upon successful retrieval of the water sample.
    - **Alchemical Items:** He might offer them some of his own creations, such as potions, elixirs, or even experimental weapons or tools. These items could be powerful but might have unpredictable side effects.
    - **Information:** Dr. Vex might share some of his knowledge about the Mournland, Croydonite, or the specific properties of the Stillwater. However, he's likely to be selective with what he reveals, keeping his true motives hidden.
    - **Reputation:** Completing this quest will increase the party's reputation in Salvation, potentially opening up new opportunities or attracting the attention of other factions.

**Moral Choices:**

- **Trusting Dr. Vex:** The party will have to decide whether they trust Dr. Vex and his intentions. Is he truly trying to understand the Mournland, or does he have a more sinister agenda? They might investigate him, or try to find out more about his past.
- **The Purpose of the Sample:** What will Dr. Vex do with the water sample? Will he use it for research, to create weapons, or for something even more dangerous? The party might have to choose whether to help him with his experiments or try to stop him.
- **The Fate of the Stillwater:** The party's actions could have consequences for the Stillwater and its ecosystem. Will they try to find a way to cleanse the lake, or will they simply exploit its resources for their own gain?

**Dr. Vex's Potential Alignment Shift:**

- **Path of Good:** If the party consistently demonstrates concern for the well-being of others, helps Dr. Vex see the error of his ways, and encourages him to use his knowledge for good, he might eventually abandon his more dangerous experiments and become a valuable ally. He could even help the party find a way to heal the Mournland or free the souls trapped in Croydonite. This will likely require the party to destroy his current research and convince him to abandon his ties to the shadowy organization.
- **Path of Evil:** If the party encourages Dr. Vex's darker impulses, helps him acquire dangerous materials, and turns a blind eye to the consequences of his experiments, he might become a dangerous villain, unleashing alchemical horrors upon the Mournland or using his creations to seize power in Salvation. He might even become a recurring antagonist, forcing the party to confront the consequences of their choices. This will likely involve the party defeating him in combat, and destroying his lab.
- **Path of Neutrality:** Dr. Vex could remain morally ambiguous, continuing his research but becoming more cautious and perhaps even sharing some of his discoveries with the party if they prove themselves trustworthy. He would likely still pursue his own goals, but might do so in a way that minimizes harm to others. He would likely remain in Salvation, continuing his research, and would become more reclusive.

This questline provides a strong introduction to Dr. Vex and his morally complex nature. It allows the players to explore the dangers of the Stillwater, make difficult choices about who to trust, and begin to understand the potential consequences of unchecked ambition in the Mournland. The choices they make will shape Dr. Vex's future and potentially determine whether he becomes a powerful ally or a formidable enemy.
