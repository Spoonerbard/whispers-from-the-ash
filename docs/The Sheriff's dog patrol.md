Here's how we can implement this, combining the Iron Defender stat block with elemental variations:


# **[[Sheriff Karr]]'s Iron Defender: [[Lemon]]**

- **Name:** Lemon
- **Appearance:** Lemon is a standard Iron Defender, but its metal body has a yellowish hue, and its eyes glow with a sickly green light. It is slightly smaller than the other Iron Defenders, and moves with an uncomfortable gait. Acidic fumes occasionally leak from its joints, and it leaves a faint trail of acidic residue wherever it walks. Its body is covered in scratches and dents, suggesting it has seen a lot of use. It's bite leaves a lingering burning sensation.
- **Elemental Theme:** Acid
- **Stat Block Modifications:**
    - **Bite Attack:** Lemon's bite attack deals an additional 1d4 acid damage on a hit.
    - **Acidic Bite:** When Lemon hits with its bite attack, the target must make a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or their equipment takes damage. If the target is wearing metal armor, the armor takes a permanent and cumulative -1 penalty to the AC it offers. The armor is destroyed if the penalty reduces its AC to 10. If the target is1 holding a metal weapon, the weapon takes a permanent and cumulative -1 penalty to damage rolls. The weapon is destroyed if the penalty reduces its damage to 0.
    - **Acid Resistance:** Lemon has resistance to acid damage.
    - **Citrus Scent:** Lemon gives off a strong citrus scent that some find pleasant and others find overwhelming.
- **Personality:** Lemon is fiercely loyal to Sheriff Karr and follows his commands without question. It's the most aggressive of the Iron Defenders and seems to relish in causing pain. It often startles people with it's acidic leaks.

# **[[Deputy Dal]]'s Iron Defender: [[Apple]]**

- **Name:** Apple
- **Appearance:** Apple is a sleek, well-maintained Iron Defender, its metal plates painted a bright, shiny red. Its movements are precise and efficient. It has several notches on it's torso, each one carefully carved. It's eyes glow with a warm, comforting light.
- **Elemental Theme:** Fire
- **Stat Block Modifications:**
    - **Heated Body:** Apple's body radiates intense heat. Any creature that touches Apple or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 1d4 fire damage.
    - **Fire Trail:** When Apple moves, it leaves behind a trail of harmless, flickering flames that dissipate after a few seconds. This trail will ignite flammable objects.
    - **Fire Resistance:** Apple has resistance to fire damage.
    - **Shiny:** Apple is constantly polishing itself, and its body gleams in the light.
- **Personality:** Apple is eager to please and always tries to do its best, reflecting Dal's personality. It's the most "by-the-book" of the Iron Defenders and takes its duties very seriously.

# **[[Deputy Elara]]'s Iron Defender: Plum**

- **Name:** Plum
- **Appearance:** Plum is a stealthy Iron Defender, its metal body a deep, purplish-black. It moves silently, almost like a shadow. It has a pair of retractable blades hidden in its forearms. It's eyes glow with a faint, purple light. It is often seen hiding in the shadows, watching.
- **Elemental Theme:** Necrotic/Shadow
- **Stat Block Modifications:**
    - **Shadow Strike:** Once per turn, when Plum hits a creature with its bite attack, it can deal an extra 1d6 necrotic damage if it has advantage on the attack roll or if the target is within 5 feet of an ally of Plum's that isn't incapacitated.
    - **Shadow Blend:** While in dim light or darkness, Plum can use a bonus action to become heavily obscured until it moves or takes an action or reaction. It will look like a part of the shadows, and will be very hard to spot.
    - **Vulnerability to Radiant Damage:** Plum has vulnerability to radiant damage.
- **Personality:** Plum is quiet and mysterious, just like Elara. It's the most independent of the Iron Defenders and often operates autonomously, carrying out Elara's orders with ruthless efficiency. It seems to enjoy stalking its prey.

# **[[Deputy Bron]]'s Iron Defender: Orange**

- **Name:** Orange
- **Appearance:** Orange is a heavily armored, bulky Iron Defender, its metal plates painted a bright, almost garish orange. It's the largest of the group and moves with surprising speed. It has a large, blunt head that it uses to bash its enemies. It's eyes glow with a fierce, orange light.
- **Elemental Theme:** Lightning
- **Stat Block Modifications:**
    - **Lightning Charge:** Once per turn, when Orange moves at least 20 feet straight toward a target and hits it with its bite attack on the same turn, the target takes an additional 1d6 lightning damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 11 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.
    - **Lightning Resistance:** Orange has resistance to lightning damage.
    - **Loud:** Orange's movements are accompanied by loud clanking and whirring sounds, and sparks of electricity often arc from its body, it also enjoys making loud noises with it's voice box.
- **Personality:** Orange is loud, boisterous, and always eager for a fight, much like Bron. It's the least intelligent of the Iron Defenders but also the most fiercely loyal. It's always the first to jump into a brawl, much to the dismay of the other Iron Defenders. It will often try to scare it's enemies by roaring at them.

**Iron Defender Tactics:**

- **Sheriff Karr and Lemon:** Will use Lemon to harass and weaken enemies, taking advantage of its acidic bite to damage weapons and armor.
- **Deputy Dal and Apple:** Will use Apple as a frontline fighter, using its heated body to deal damage to anyone who gets too close.
- **Deputy Elara and Plum:** Will use Plum for stealth and ambush tactics, taking advantage of its Shadow Strike and Shadow Blend abilities.
- **Deputy Bron and Orange:** Will use Orange as a battering ram, charging into enemies and knocking them prone with its Lightning Charge.

**Important Considerations:**

- **Power Level:** While these Iron Defenders are enhanced, they should still be manageable for a lower-level party. They are meant to be a challenge, not an insurmountable obstacle. Remember, they use the standard Iron Defender stat block as a base.
- **Personality:** Give each Iron Defender a distinct personality, reflecting their elemental theme and their owner's personality. This will make them more memorable and engaging.
- **Weaknesses:** Consider giving each Iron Defender a specific weakness related to their element (e.g., Lemon is vulnerable to cold, Apple is vulnerable to water, Plum is vulnerable to radiant damage, Orange is vulnerable to ground-based attacks).
- **Visual Cues:** Make sure the Iron Defenders' appearances clearly reflect their elemental themes. This will help players identify their abilities and weaknesses.

**Plot Hooks:**

- **The Iron Defenders Malfunction:** One or more of the Iron Defenders malfunctions, potentially due to Croydonite exposure or sabotage. The party might have to subdue the malfunctioning construct(s) before they cause too much damage.
- **The Stolen Schematics:** Someone steals the schematics for the Iron Defenders (perhaps a rival faction or a thief working for House Cannith). The party is hired to recover them.
- **The Upgrade Opportunity:** Troxler might be able to upgrade the Iron Defenders, enhancing their elemental abilities or giving them new features. This could require the party to find rare materials or components in the Mournland.
- **The Investigation:** The party might be tasked with investigating a crime in which the Iron Defenders were involved, perhaps a situation where they used excessive force. This could lead to a moral dilemma about the use of such constructs in law enforcement.
