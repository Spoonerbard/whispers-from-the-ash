[[The Cults of the Dragon Below]]

Imagine a collection of insane, fanatical cults obsessed with the darkness beneath Eberron. They don't share a single leader or even a unified goal, but they're all drawn to the power and madness that seeps up from Khyber, the world below.

**Who do they worship?**

- **Daelkyr:** Aberrant beings from Xoriat, the plane of madness. They're known for their bizarre forms, reality-warping powers, and desire to reshape Eberron in their own twisted image. Think mind flayers and beholders on steroids.
- **Overlords:** Powerful fiends trapped within Khyber. They crave freedom and dominion over Eberron. Each Overlord has a unique agenda and influence, but they're all incredibly dangerous.
- **Khyber itself:** Some cults worship the darkness and chaos of Khyber as a whole, embracing its destructive potential.
- **Madness:** Some cults are driven by pure insanity, their beliefs and rituals defying all logic and reason.

**What are their goals?**

The goals of individual cults vary wildly, but here are some common themes:

- **Release the Daelkyr or Overlords:** Some cults seek to free these imprisoned entities, believing they'll bring about a new age of power or chaos.
- **Transform Eberron:** Some cults want to reshape the world in the image of Khyber, unleashing its darkness and warping reality.
- **Gain power:** Some cultists simply crave the power offered by the entities they worship, using dark rituals and forbidden knowledge to achieve their goals.
- **Embrace madness:** Some cults see insanity as a form of enlightenment, seeking to break free from the constraints of reason and embrace the chaos of Khyber.

**Why are they important in Eberron?**

The Cults of the Dragon Below represent a constant threat to the stability and sanity of Eberron. Their actions can range from small-scale disturbances to world-altering events. They're a source of intrigue and danger, and they can be used to create compelling stories and challenges for players.

**Examples of Cults:**

- **The Whisperers:** A cult devoted to the Daelkyr Dyrrn, known for their insidious whispers that drive people to madness.
- **The Inner Sun:** A cult worshipping the Overlord Belashyrra, seeking to unleash her fiery power upon the world.
- **The Dreaming Dark:** A cult connected to the plane of Dal Quor, the realm of dreams, and its monstrous inhabitants, the quori.
