#npcs/factions/Salvation 




- **Appearance:** A lanky, young half-elf with sharp eyes and a nervous twitch. He's always cleaning his twin hand crossbows, even when they're spotless. He wears a worn leather vest over a faded blue shirt, and a bandana around his neck.
- **Personality:** Eager to prove himself, Dal is a skilled marksman but lacks experience and confidence. He idolizes Sheriff Karr and tries to emulate his 
- calm demeanor, but often comes across as awkward or unsure of himself. He's quick to draw his weapons but hesitant to fire.
- **Motivation:** To earn the respect of Sheriff Karr and become a respected lawman in his own right, and secretly, to impress a certain someone in town.
- **Secret:** Dal is secretly infatuated with the owner of [[ [[the crooked tankard]] ]], a female half orc named [[Grelga]]. He's too shy to express his feelings directly, so he tries to impress her by showing off his (somewhat exaggerated) exploits as a deputy.
- **Cannith Connection:** Unknown to Dal, his nervous twitch and his habit of constantly cleaning his weapons are being monitored by his older brother, a Cannith agent embedded among the deputies. His brother sends regular reports on Dal's behavior, hoping to identify any signs of magical aptitude or a connection to the Mourning.
- **Quote:** "Hold it right there! I... I mean, uh, you're under arrest! Or, you know, just stop what you're doing."
