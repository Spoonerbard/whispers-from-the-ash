**Tinker's Multitool: The Artificer's Companion**

**Description:** This ingenious device is a marvel of miniaturization and adaptable engineering. It's a compact collection of tools, small enough to fit in the palm of a hand, crafted from polished metal and adorned with intricate gears, levers, and ক্ষুদ্র lenses. The Multitool can reconfigure itself into a variety of tools with a flick of the wrist, making it an invaluable asset for any Artificer.

**Current Tier 1 Ability:**

- **Masterful Precision:** Once per day, the artificer can use the multitool to gain proficiency in any one tool for 1 hour.

**Upgrade Paths:**

We'll focus on expanding the multitool's versatility, allowing it to replicate more tools, perform more complex tasks, and even incorporate magical effects. We'll offer three paths: **Adaptation, Innovation, and Miniaturization.**

**1. Path of Adaptation (Focus: Expanding Tool Proficiency and Versatility):**

- **Theme:** This path emphasizes the artificer's ability to adapt to any situation, using the multitool to replicate a wide array of tools and overcome diverse challenges.
- **Tier 2 Upgrade:**
    - **Expanded Repertoire:** The artificer can now use the multitool to gain proficiency in any two tools at a time, instead of one. The duration remains 1 hour. They can now use this ability twice per day.
    - **Requirement:** The artificer must successfully use the multitool to craft or repair an item using a variety of different tools. They might also need to study the schematics of different tools or disassemble and reassemble a complex mechanical device.
- **Tier 3 Upgrade:**
    - **Adaptive Expertise:** When using the multitool to gain proficiency in a tool, the artificer can choose to gain expertise in that tool instead, doubling their proficiency bonus for any ability checks made with it. They can only use this on one tool at a time, and for a duration of ten minutes.
    - **Requirement:** The artificer must use the multitool to overcome a particularly challenging obstacle that requires a specific tool proficiency, demonstrating their mastery over that tool. They might also need to acquire a rare or specialized tool to study its design.
- **Tier 4 Upgrade:**
    - **Universal Aptitude:** The artificer can now use the multitool to gain proficiency in any three tools at a time, instead of two. They can use their **Adaptive Expertise** on two tools at a time. They can now use their **Masterful Precision** ability three times per day, but the duration for this is reduced to 10 minutes.
    - **Requirement:** The artificer must use the multitool to create a complex item that requires proficiency in multiple tools, showcasing their ability to adapt and improvise under pressure. They might also need to study with a master artisan or decipher an ancient text on tool usage.

**2. Path of Innovation (Focus: Enhancing Crafting and Creating Unique Effects):**

- **Theme:** This path focuses on the artificer's ingenuity, allowing them to use the multitool to enhance their crafting abilities and create unique, customized effects.
- **Tier 2 Upgrade:**
    - **Inspired Crafting:** When using the multitool to craft an item, the artificer can add their Intelligence modifier to the roll, in addition to any other applicable modifiers. They can also expend a spell slot of level 1 or higher to reduce the crafting time by half.
    - **Requirement:** The artificer must successfully craft a particularly complex or innovative item using the multitool. They might also need to study with a master craftsman or decipher a blueprint for a new invention.
- **Tier 3 Upgrade:**
    - **Modular Infusions:** When the artificer creates an infusion using their Infuse Item feature, they can use the multitool to modify the infusion, adding a minor additional effect or altering its properties. This could involve changing the damage type of a weapon infusion, increasing the duration of an effect, or adding a unique rider effect. The artificer can make one additional infusion per day.
    - **Requirement:** The artificer must successfully infuse an item with a particularly complex or challenging infusion. They might also need to acquire a rare material or study the properties of a specific type of magic.
- **Tier 4 Upgrade:**
    - **Masterwork Innovation:** The artificer can now create temporary magical items using the multitool. They can expend a spell slot of 2nd level or higher and use the multitool to create a temporary magic item that replicates the effects of a common or uncommon magic item. The item lasts for 24 hours before losing its magic. The artificer can use this ability once per day, and can only have one such item in existence at a time. The level of the spell slot used determines the rarity of the magic item that can be created. A 2nd level slot can create a common item, while a 3rd level or higher slot can create an uncommon item.
    - **Requirement:** The artificer must create a truly innovative and groundbreaking item using their crafting skills, demonstrating their mastery over magical and mechanical principles. They might also need to acquire a legendary crafting material or decipher a lost text on advanced artificing techniques.

**3. Path of Miniaturization (Focus: Enhancing the Multitool's Form and Efficiency):**

- **Theme:** This path focuses on making the multitool itself more compact, efficient, and powerful, turning it into a truly remarkable device.
- **Tier 2 Upgrade:**
    - **Compact Design:** The multitool becomes even more compact and easier to use. The artificer can now use the multitool as a spellcasting focus for their artificer spells. The range of spells cast in this way is reduced by half.
    - **Requirement:** The artificer must spend time refining the multitool's design, making it smaller and more efficient. They might need to acquire rare materials, such as mithral or miniaturized gears.
- **Tier 3 Upgrade:**
    - **Integrated Components:** The multitool can now integrate small magical components, such as a gem that glows in the dark or a compass that always points north. This allows the artificer to add minor magical effects to the multitool itself, enhancing its functionality. The multitool can hold a maximum of two such components.
    - **Requirement:** The artificer must successfully integrate a magical component into the multitool without damaging it. They might also need to study the principles of magical item construction or learn a new technique for miniaturizing magical effects.
- **Tier 4 Upgrade:**
    - **Self-Replicating Tool:** Once per day, the artificer can use the multitool to create a temporary duplicate of itself. This duplicate lasts for 1 hour and has all the abilities of the original multitool, but any abilities used by the duplicate count towards the daily uses of the original. The artificer can also, as an action, transform the multitool into any tool they are proficient with. This transformation lasts until the artificer uses an action to transform it back. The artificer can now use their **Masterful Precision** ability at will.
    - **Requirement:** The artificer must create a truly remarkable invention using the multitool, demonstrating their mastery over miniaturization and magical engineering. They might also need to acquire a legendary tool or a piece of technology from a lost civilization.
