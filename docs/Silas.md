[[Salvation NPCs]]

**1. "Old Man" Silas (General Store Owner)**

- **Services:** Sells basic adventuring gear, tools, rations, and other mundane supplies. His goods are generally of low quality (Tier 1) but are readily available. He also buys salvage and trinkets from the Mournland, but he drives a hard bargain.
- **Personality:** Cranky, suspicious, and tight-fisted. He's seen a lot of adventurers come and go and doesn't get attached easily. He is secretly a scholar, and might let slip some of that knowledge when dealing with those he deems intelligent.
- **Rumors & Information:**
    - He's heard stories about all the locations near Salvation and might share them for a price, though he tends to exaggerate the dangers to discourage competition.
    - He might know something about the previous adventuring party that went into the Ashen Wastes, as he likely sold them supplies.
    - He's heard whispers about the Steel Stoners and their mining operation but pretends not to know anything. He might be persuaded to reveal more if the party gains his trust or offers him something he wants.
- **Quest Hooks:**
    - **The Lost Ledger:** Silas misplaces his ledger (or claims it was stolen) and asks the party to retrieve it. The ledger contains records of his dealings, including some potentially incriminating information. He will reward the party for it's return. He will accept it's destruction as a suitable alternative.
    - **The Rare Ingredient:** Silas needs a specific rare ingredient from the Mournland for a "special order." He's willing to pay well for it, but he won't reveal who the order is for. This could be a seemingly innocuous item that is actually used in a dangerous ritual or experiment.
    - **Competition:** A new merchant arrives in town, undercutting Silas's prices and threatening his business. Silas might ask the party to "discourage" the competition, potentially leading to a moral dilemma.
- **Secret:** He is **Edgar ir'Wynarn**, a disgraced Cyran noble and scholar, who fled to Salvation after the Mourning. He's hiding his true identity and his knowledge of pre-Mourning history and magic. He's particularly interested in any information about the Final Sanction. He might reveal his true identity to the party if they earn his trust and prove themselves to be both intelligent and discreet.
