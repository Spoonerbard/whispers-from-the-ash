[[conductor husks]] : The Mourned Commuters

**Lore:**

- **Origin:** These horrific creatures were once ordinary citizens of Cyre, waiting to board the lightning rail on the Day of Mourning. Caught in the time-accelerated cataclysm, their bodies were petrified and infused with a volatile mix of magical energy, Croydonite, and a sickly yellow, acidic substance that now constantly weeps from their cracked chests. They are a horrifying reminder of the once-great nation, and a common sight near any of the old lightning rail lines.
- **Nature:** Conductor Husks are animated by a twisted form of necromantic energy, fueled by the lingering trauma of the Mourning. They are driven by a fragmented memory of their last moments, endlessly repeating the motions of waiting for a train that will never come. They are not particularly intelligent but are highly aggressive towards anything that disrupts their routine.
- **Appearance:** Conductor Husks are gaunt, desiccated figures, their skin hardened and cracked like ancient stone, with a sickly yellow hue. Their eyes are hollow sockets, but they seem to track movement nonetheless. Their chests are cracked open, revealing a hollow cavity from which a viscous, yellow acid constantly drips. Shards of Croydonite protrude from their bodies, particularly around their mouths and hands. They are often found clutching tickets for the lightning rail, or other mundane items they might have been holding when the Mourning occurred. They often smell strongly of acid.
- **Habitat:** They are commonly found near the remnants of lightning rail stations and tracks within the Mournland, especially in areas like the dead forest, where the time distortion was particularly intense. They often wander aimlessly, as if waiting for a train, but will become aggressive if disturbed.

**Stat Block:**

**Conductor Husk** _Medium undead, neutral evil_

**Armor Class:** 11 (natural armor) **Hit Points:** 13 (3d8) **Speed:** 20 ft.

**STR** 10 (+0) **DEX** 12 (+1) **CON** 11 (+0) **INT** 3 (-4) **WIS** 6 (-2) **CHA** 5 (-3)

**Damage Immunities:** Acid, Poison **Condition Immunities:** Poisoned **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 8 **Languages:** Understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak **Challenge:**1 1/2 (100 XP)

**Traits:**

- **Acidic Weep:** The Conductor Husk's chest cavity constantly drips with acid. Any creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of the Husk takes 2 (1d4) acid damage. The area within 5 feet of the husk is considered difficult terrain.
- **Turn Resistance:** The Conductor Husk has advantage on saving throws against effects that turn undead.
- **Brittle:** If the Conductor Husk takes bludgeoning damage, then it's AC is reduced by 1 until the start of it's next turn.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity:** While in sunlight, the Conductor Husk has disadvantage on attack rolls, as well as on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

**Actions:**

- **Slam:** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_2 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage.
- **Croydonite Dart:** _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) acid damage. The target must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of their next turn.

**Tactics:**

- **Swarm:** Conductor Husks are most dangerous in large numbers, using their Acidic Weep to create hazardous areas and overwhelming their enemies with sheer numbers.
- **Croydonite Darts:** They will use their Croydonite Darts to attack from a distance, trying to poison their targets and soften them up for close combat.
- **Mindless Aggression:** They will relentlessly pursue any living creature that enters their territory, attacking until they are destroyed.
- **Environmental Awareness:** While not intelligent, they are instinctively aware of their surroundings and will use the environment to their advantage, such as herding enemies into areas with their Acidic Weep or taking cover behind obstacles.

**Variations:**

- **Conductor Captain:** A slightly stronger version of the Conductor Husk, perhaps a former lightning rail conductor. It could have more hit points, a higher AC, a more powerful Croydonite Dart attack (e.g., dealing extra damage or having a longer range), and the ability to command other Conductor Husks within a certain radius. It could also make a slam attack.
- **Exploding Husks:** Some Conductor Husks might be filled with an unstable mixture of acid and Croydonite, causing them to explode upon death. This could deal acid or fire damage to creatures within a small radius.

**Encounter Design:**

- **Numbers:** Conductor Husks should usually be encountered in groups of 5-10, or even more in heavily infested areas. Their low individual hit points are offset by their numbers and their Acidic Weep ability, which can quickly wear down a party's health.
- **Terrain:** Encounters should take place near remnants of the lightning rail, such as ruined stations, tracks, or overturned rail cars. These locations can provide cover for the husks and create difficult terrain for the players.
- **Objectives:** Encounters could involve simply surviving the onslaught of husks, reaching a specific location within the infested area, retrieving an item from a ruined station, or even trying to find a way to lay the husks to rest.
- **Environmental Hazards:** The area might contain other hazards, such as unstable structures, pools of acid, or areas where the Croydonite concentration is particularly high, causing mutations or other harmful effects.

**Rewards:**

- **Croydonite Shards:** Conductor Husks might drop small Croydonite shards that can be used for crafting or sold to Troxler.
- **Salvage:** The husks might be carrying remnants of their former lives, such as coins, jewelry, or other personal items.
- **Clues:** Examining the remains of the lightning rail station or the belongings of the husks might provide clues about the events of the Mourning or the layout of the surrounding area.
- **Experience:** Defeating large numbers of Conductor Husks can be a good source of experience points.

**Flavor and Storytelling:**

- **Emphasize the Tragedy:** Remind the players that these creatures were once ordinary people, caught in a horrific event. Describe the tattered remnants of their clothing, the personal items they carry, or the expressions of terror frozen on their faces.
- **Use the Environment:** Describe the ruined lightning rail station in detail, highlighting the once-advanced technology now twisted and broken.
- **Sound Design:** Use sound effects to create an atmosphere of unease and dread. The dripping of acid, the shuffling of feet, the faint clanging of the rail, and the distant echoes of a train whistle can all add to the experience.
- **Moral Choices:** The players might be faced with moral choices related to the Conductor Husks. Is it better to destroy them, even though they were once innocent victims? Or is there a way to release them from their torment and allow their souls to find peace?

By using the Conductor Husks effectively, you can create challenging and memorable encounters that reinforce the themes of loss, horror, and the lingering consequences of the Mourning. They are a visual reminder of the human cost of the catastrophe and a tangible threat that embodies the dangers of the Mournland.
