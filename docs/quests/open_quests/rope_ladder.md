Troxler's Troublesome Talking Rope Ladder (Prototype)

Item Type: Wondrous Item, Requires Testing

Rarity:  Uncommon (for now, if it doesn't explode)

Description:

Troxler's latest invention, this seemingly ordinary rope ladder is anything but. Crafted from specially treated fibers infused with a dash of experimental alchemical goo (Troxler is pretty sure it's stable), this ladder has the remarkable ability to repair itself and, much to the potential annoyance of its users, talk.

Properties:

    Self-Repairing: When damaged, the rope ladder will slowly mend itself. Cuts and frays disappear over the course of 1d4 hours. If completely severed, the two ends will, if placed near each other, slowly grow back together over the course of 2d6 hours. Larger sections can grow back given enough time.
    Sentient & Chatty: The ladder is sentient, possessing a somewhat quirky and often overly-talkative personality. It tends to offer unsolicited advice, complain about being stepped on, and make observations about its surroundings. Its intelligence is roughly equivalent to that of a precocious child.
    Limited Durability (for now): As a prototype, the ladder's durability is still being tested. While it can hold a reasonable amount of weight (up to 500 lbs), it might complain a lot under heavy loads. There's also a small chance (5% on a d100 roll, each time it takes damage) that it might temporarily lose its ability to speak or repair itself, reverting to a normal, if slightly damp, rope ladder for 1d6 hours.

Troxler's Notes (scrawled on a piece of parchment attached to the ladder):

    "Right then, adventurers!  Need you lot to give this beauty a proper test run.  Claims it can regrow itself, see?  Also, it talks.  A lot.  Don't let it boss you around too much.  If it starts singing, well, you've been warned.  Bring back data! And the ladder, preferably. Unless it runs off.  Or explodes.  Then just bring back data."
