#npcs/factions/world 

Okay, here's a cheat sheet for the major factions and organizations in Eberron that would be interested in the Mournland, along with their motivations, methods, and potential connections to your campaign. This will help you keep track of the larger forces at play and create a more dynamic and interconnected world.

**Factions & Organizations with Interest in the Mournland:**

**1. The Order of the Broken Sky:**

- **Motivations:**
    - Uncover the truth behind the Mourning.
    - Study pre-Mourning magic and artifacts.
    - Prevent another magical catastrophe.
    - Potentially heal or reverse the effects of the Mournland.
- **Methods:** Scholarly research, গোপন expeditions into the Mournland, information gathering, গোপনে manipulating events from behind the scenes.
- **Leadership:** Led by the enigmatic archmage Vaelia Moonwhisper, who has foreseen the importance of the Sky Song Tavern.
- **Presence in Salvation:** Currently operating in secret, with agents like Kaelen "The Raven" Rhen gathering intelligence.
- **Relationship with the Party:** Potential allies or rivals, depending on the party's actions and goals.
- **Secrets:** Vaelia's past connection to the events of the Mourning, the true extent of their knowledge, their ultimate goal.
- **Interest in:** The Sky Song Tavern, the Displaced Depot, any location with strong magical residue or connections to the past. They are also interested in the true nature of Croydonite.

**2. House Cannith:**

- **Motivations:**
    - Recover lost technology and research from Cyre.
    - Reassert their dominance in the field of magical innovation.
    - Develop new weapons and constructs using Mournland resources.
    - Potentially weaponize Croydonite.
    - Find their lost creation forge.
- **Methods:** Secret expeditions into the Mournland, espionage, manipulation of other factions, গোপনে funding research and development. They are also interested in controlling the narrative surrounding the Mourning.
- **Leadership:** Divided into three branches: Cannith South (Merrix), Cannith West (Zorlan), and Cannith East (Jorlanna). Each branch has its own agenda.
- **Presence in Salvation:** Deputy "Whisper" Elara is a Cannith agent (likely Cannith East), and the arrival of agents in Salvation is imminent. Jorun d'Cannith is in league with the Order of the Broken Sky.
- **Relationship with the Party:** Potential employers, rivals, or targets of espionage, depending on the party's actions and allegiances.
- **Secrets:** The extent of their pre-Mourning research, the true purpose of the warforged, their involvement in the creation of the Mourning (rumored).
- **Interest in:** The city of Making, any surviving Cannith facilities, warforged technology, and Croydonite. They are also interested in the Lord of Blades.

**3. House Tharashk:**

- **Motivations:**
    - Profit from the Mournland's resources (Croydonite, minerals, salvage).
    - Expand their influence and control in the region.
    - Establish a strong presence near the Mournland to deter rivals.
    - Find and exploit new sources of dragonshards.
- **Methods:** Establishing mining operations, employing prospectors and bounty hunters, controlling trade routes, and using their influence to manipulate local governments. They are not afraid to use force to protect their interests.
- **Leadership:** The current head of House Tharashk is Khundar'k, a shrewd and ruthless orc.
- **Presence in Salvation:** They employ the Sheriff and his deputies, effectively controlling law and order in the town. They are also involved in the Croydonite trade through the Steel Stoners.
- **Relationship with the Party:** Employers, potential allies, or rivals depending on whether the party's actions align with or oppose Tharashk's interests.
- **Secrets:** The true extent of their involvement in the Croydonite trade, their dealings with other factions, their long-term plans for the Mournland.
- **Interest in:** Croydonite mining, controlling trade routes, suppressing opposition, and finding new sources of wealth. They are also interested in anything that could give them an edge over their rivals.

**4. House Phiarlan/Thuranni (The Shadow Network):**

- **Motivations:**
    - Gather information about the Mourning and its aftermath.
    - Exploit the chaos of the Mournland for their own gain.
    - Recover lost artifacts or secrets from Cyre.
    - Establish a foothold in the region to expand their influence.
- **Methods:** Espionage, infiltration, sabotage, assassination, manipulating events from the shadows. They prefer to work behind the scenes, using others to achieve their goals.
- **Leadership:** House Phiarlan is led by Baron Elvinor Elorrenthi d'Phiarlan, while House Thuranni is led by Baron Elar d'Thuranni. The two houses are bitter rivals, but they share similar methods and motivations.
- **Presence in Salvation:** Mara "Fingers" Keen is secretly working for one of the houses. Dwalin "The Mole" Kettlebottom is also secretly sending information to one of the houses. They may have other agents in the town or the surrounding area.
- **Relationship with the Party:** Potential employers, sources of information, or dangerous adversaries, depending on the party's choices and allegiances.
- **Secrets:** Their true goals in the Mournland, the extent of their network, their involvement in past events.
- **Interest in:** The Sky Song Tavern, the Displaced Depot, the activities of other factions, any location with a strong connection to illusion or divination magic. They are also interested in the Order of the Broken Sky, and any secrets they might be hiding.

**5. The Steel Stoners:**

- **Motivations:**
    - Mine Croydonite and make a profit.
    - Evade House Kundarak justice.
    - Establish their independence.
    - Get revenge on House Kundarak.
- **Methods:** Operating a গোপন mining operation, smuggling Croydonite out of the Mournland, defending themselves against rivals and law enforcement.
- **Leadership:** Led by Borin "Stonebeard" Grimcleaver, with Gretta "Gem-Eye" Stonehand as his second-in-command.
- **Presence in Salvation:** They have a base camp in Salvation and are known to frequent the Crooked Tankard. They are generally tolerated but viewed with suspicion.
- **Relationship with the Party:** Potential allies, trading partners, or rivals, depending on the party's actions.
- **Secrets:** What they stole from House Kundarak, the extent of their Croydonite operation, their connection to other factions.
- **Interest in:** Croydonite, new mining locations, evading capture. They are also interested in acquiring new weapons and equipment.

**6. The Lord of Blades:**

- **Motivations:**
    - Establish a warforged nation in the Mournland.
    - Free warforged from servitude to other races.
    - Potentially create a warforged god.
    - Take revenge on those who wronged him or his people.
- **Methods:** Gathering followers, raiding settlements for supplies and technology, establishing hidden bases, potentially experimenting with creating new warforged or modifying existing ones. They are also searching for something in the Mournland, something that will help them achieve their goals.
- **Leadership:** The Lord of Blades, a charismatic and powerful warforged.
- **Presence in the Mournland:** Currently operating in secret, but his influence is growing. His forces are likely to be encountered in the deeper parts of the Mournland. The arrival of the Steel Sentinels suggests that he is becoming more active.
- **Relationship with the Party:** Likely enemies, as the party represents a threat to his plans. However, he might try to recruit warforged members to his cause or manipulate the party into serving his interests.
- **Secrets:** His ultimate goal, the nature of his connection to the Mourning, the source of his power.
- **Interest in:** The city of Making, warforged technology, the Progenitor Dragon Bone, the creation forge, and any artifacts related to his goals. He is also interested in the activities of House Cannith, and any potential rivals.

**7. The Church of the Silver Flame (Thrane):**

- **Motivations:**
    - Combat the spread of evil and corruption.
    - Protect the innocent from the dangers of the Mournland.
    - Potentially cleanse or reclaim the Mournland in the name of the Silver Flame.
    - Establish a presence near the Mournland to monitor the situation and offer aid to those in need.
- **Methods:** Sending missionaries and paladins to the region, establishing outposts and temples, providing healing and protection, combating undead and other evil creatures. They are also likely to be investigating the causes of the Mourning.
- **Leadership:** The Keeper of the Flame, Jaela Daran, a young girl believed to be the voice of the Silver Flame.
- **Presence in Salvation:** Sister Arya represents a small presence of the Church in Salvation. They might establish a larger temple or outpost as the town grows.
- **Relationship with the Party:** Potential allies, offering healing, support, and quests related to combating evil. However, their zealotry might also bring them into conflict with the party if they perceive their actions as heretical or dangerous.
- **Secrets:** The true extent of their knowledge about the Mourning, their plans for the region, any internal conflicts or schisms within the Church.
- **Interest in:** The Glass Plateau, any locations with strong necromantic or fiendish influence, reports of demonic activity. They are also interested in the activities of the Order of the Broken Sky, and any other groups that they deem to be heretical.

**8. The Becoming God:**

- **Motivations:**
    - To understand the nature of the divine spark within warforged.
    - To help warforged achieve their full potential.
    - To create a god made in their own image.
- **Methods:** The main sect of the Becoming God is a peaceful organization, focused on philosophical debate and spiritual exploration. However, the Disciples of the Unwritten Name, the radical sect responsible for the creation of the portal, sought to create a warforged god through any means necessary. Their methods involved dark rituals, forbidden technology, and possibly even blood sacrifices.
- **Leadership:** The main sect is led by a council of elders, while the Disciples of the Unwritten Name were likely led by a charismatic and powerful individual.
- **Presence in the Mournland:** The main sect might have a hidden temple or monastery somewhere in the Mournland, where they study the nature of warforged and the divine. The Disciples of the Unwritten Name were likely wiped out during the Mourning, but their influence might still be felt.
- **Relationship with the Party:** The main sect might be wary of the party, but they could potentially become allies if the party proves themselves to be sympathetic to their cause. The Disciples of the Unwritten Name, if any remain, would likely be enemies.
- **Secrets:** The true nature of the warforged soul, the location of their hidden temple, the extent of their knowledge about the Mourning.
- **Interest in:** The warforged that was part of Anya's expedition, any warforged encountered in the Mournland, particularly those that display unusual abilities or memories. They are also interested in the activities of the Lord of Blades, and any attempts to create a warforged god.
