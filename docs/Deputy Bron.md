**Deputy "Bruiser" Bron**

- **Appearance:** A hulking half-orc with a scarred face, a thick neck, and fists the size of ham hocks. He wears a dented breastplate over a stained tunic, and carries a large warhammer slung across his back. He is often seen ক্যারিং a large keg of beer on one shoulder.
- **Personality:** Bron is a simple, straightforward man who enjoys drinking, brawling, and enforcing the law (in that order). He's not particularly bright, but he's fiercely loyal to Sheriff Karr and has a surprisingly strong sense of justice. He follows orders without question.
- **Motivation:** To keep his belly full, his mug filled, and to avoid the wrath of the Sheriff. He secretly wants to find a nice orc woman to settle down with.
- **Secret:** Bron is terrified of spiders, even small ones. He will go to great lengths to avoid them, and the other deputies sometimes use this fear to play pranks on him. He can often be heard singing to himself, and has a surprisingly good singing voice.
- **Quote:** "Sheriff says no trouble. So no trouble. Or Bruiser smash."
