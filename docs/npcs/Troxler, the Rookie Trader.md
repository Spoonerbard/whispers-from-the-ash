#npcs/factions/Salvation 



**Appearance:** Troxler is a young, eager female, gnome (quark) with a nervous but ambitious glint in their eyes. They wear practical, slightly worn clothing, and are often seen fiddling with tools or examining strange objects. They might have burn marks, minor cuts, or singed hair from their experiments.

**Personality:** Troxler is enthusiastic about their craft but lacks experience and confidence. They are easily impressed by the adventurers and their tales of daring exploits. They are honest, perhaps to a fault, and will openly admit their limitations. They are driven by a desire to learn and improve, and they see the adventurers as a path to achieving that goal.

**Role:** Troxler serves as a unique merchant and craftsman in [[npcs]]. They specialize in reverse-engineering magical items, unlocking their secrets and crafting new items based on those discoveries. They are especially interested in unusual materials harvested from monsters or strange items found in ruins.

**Troxler's Crafting Progression**

Troxler's crafting abilities improve alongside the party's, creating a symbiotic relationship. Here's how it works:

**1. Leveling Up:**

- **Level Equivalency:** Troxler's "level" doesn't directly correspond to character levels but represents their crafting proficiency. You can create a separate progression table for Troxler (see example below).

**2. Crafting Tiers:**

- Troxler's crafting abilities are divided into tiers (e.g., Apprentice, Journeyman, Expert, Master). Each tier unlocks new crafting options and the ability to work with more powerful magical items.
- **Tier Requirements:** Troxler must reach a certain level to unlock each tier.

**3. Material Requirements:**

- **Mundane Components:** Troxler needs basic crafting materials (e.g., leather, metal, wood) which they can purchase or the party can provide.
- **Magical Essence:** This is the key ingredient. Troxler can extract Magical Essence from:
    - **Unidentified Magic Items:** The party can bring unidentified magic items to Troxler. The rarity of the item determines the amount of Magical Essence extracted. The item is destroyed in the process.
    - **Monster Parts:** Certain monsters might have body parts that contain Magical Essence (e.g., a beholder's eyestalk, a dragon's scale, a fire elemental's core). You'll need to determine which monsters in your setting have these special parts.
    - **Rare Materials:** The party might find rare materials in their explorations (e.g., a strange metal from a meteor, a crystal from a magical cave) that contain Magical Essence.
- **Magical Essence Storage:** Troxler has a limited capacity for storing Magical Essence. The party may need to help them expand their storage (a side quest, perhaps?).

**4. Crafting Options:**

- **Reverse Engineering:** This is Troxler's specialty. By studying an unidentified magic item (and extracting its Magical Essence), Troxler can learn to craft a limited number of items of that type or rarity.
- **Custom Orders:** Once Troxler has learned to craft an item, the party can commission him to make more, provided they supply the necessary materials and pay a fee.
- **Repairs:** At higher tiers, Troxler can repair damaged magic items, potentially for a lower cost than buying a new one.
- **Experimentation:** As Troxler gains experience, they might start experimenting with new crafting techniques, potentially leading to unique or even unpredictable results. The players could provide ideas for these experiments.

**Roleplaying [[Troxler]]:**

- **Eager Apprentice:** At low levels, Troxler is enthusiastic but inexperienced. They might make mistakes, and their creations might not always be perfect.
- **Growing Confidence:** As Troxler levels up, they become more confident in their abilities. They might start offering advice to the party and taking on more ambitious projects.
- **Grateful for the Opportunity:** Troxler sees the adventurers as their mentors and benefactors. They are always willing to help the party, even if it means taking risks.
- **Driven by Curiosity:** Troxler is fascinated by magic and always eager to learn more. They might ask the party endless questions about their adventures and the items they find.

**Possible Quests Involving Troxler:**

- **Retrieve a rare material:** The party could be tasked with finding a specific material that Troxler needs for a special project.
- **Recover stolen tools or notes:** Troxler's workshop could be robbed, and the party needs to recover their stolen tools or research notes.
- **Test a new invention:** Troxler could ask the party to test out a new, experimental item.
- **Expand Troxler's Workshop:** The party could help Troxler expand his workshop to increase their magical essence storage, or build new crafting stations.
