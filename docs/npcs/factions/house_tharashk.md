House Tharashk
================

![[tharashk_embelm.webp]]

House Tharashk, with its roots in the dwarven clans of the Ironroot Mountains, holds a unique and powerful position in Eberron.  They are renowned for their expertise in mining, metallurgy, and the Mark of Finding, which grants them exceptional tracking and locating abilities.  This expertise, combined with their shrewd business acumen, has allowed them to amass significant wealth and influence.

Their role as peacekeepers around the Mournland stems from a combination of factors.  First, their Mark of Finding makes them uniquely suited to navigating the treacherous and unpredictable terrain of the Mournland.  They are often sought after to locate lost individuals, recover valuable resources, and track down those who dare to venture into the blighted territory.

Second, House Tharashk has a vested interest in maintaining peace in the region.  The Mournland, while dangerous, also holds the potential for immense profit.  Recovering resources from the ruined nation of Cyre, securing salvage rights, and controlling trade routes are all lucrative endeavors.  Open warfare disrupts these operations, making peace a more profitable option.

Third, the Last War left a deep scar on Eberron.  The destruction of Cyre and the creation of the Mournland serve as a stark reminder of the devastating consequences of conflict.  House Tharashk, like many other factions, recognizes the importance of preventing another such catastrophe.  Their role as peacekeepers, therefore, is not entirely altruistic, but rather a pragmatic approach to safeguarding their own interests and the stability of the region.

However, House Tharashk's peacekeeping efforts are not without their complexities.  Their focus on profit can sometimes clash with the needs of the local communities.  They are known for their hard bargaining and their willingness to exploit resources, which can lead to resentment and conflict.  Furthermore, the delicate political balance around the Mournland means that House Tharashk must constantly navigate the competing interests of other factions, including those who might seek to exploit the Mournland for their own purposes.  This tightrope walk requires them to be both powerful and subtle, using diplomacy and economic pressure as often as they use force.  Their reputation is one of shrewd dealers, not necessarily benevolent protectors.


Treaties and International Agreements
--------------------------------------

According to treaties and international agreements, House Tharashk is responsible for the guardianship and custodianship of the area known as the Mournland, formerly the kingdom of Cyre. This responsibility was given to them in the aftermath of the Last War, when the kingdom of Cyre was devastated by the Day of Mourning.

The Mournland is a vast and dangerous region, filled with twisted and corrupted creatures, as well as powerful magical energies. House Tharashk is responsible for ensuring that the Mournland is not exploited or desecrated by outsiders, and that any valuable resources that are found within its borders are extracted and used for the greater good.

Despite the challenges and dangers of the Mournland, House Tharashk sees this responsibility as a great opportunity to prove their worth and to demonstrate their value to the people of Eberron. They are fiercely protective of the Mournland and will stop at nothing to defend it against any threats.
