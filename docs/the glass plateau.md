**The Glass Plateau: A Mirror of Horror**

**Appearance and Location:**

- **Gleaming Expanse:** The Glass Plateau is a vast, flat expanse of smooth, black glass, stretching for miles across the western[[mournland]]. It reflects the sky above like a dark mirror, creating an unsettling and disorienting landscape. It is almost perfectly flat, with no hills or valleys to break up the monotony.
- **Jagged Edges:** The edges of the plateau are sharp and jagged, as if the land itself shattered and was then replaced with this unnatural glass.
- **Unnatural Stillness:** The Plateau is eerily still. No wind blows across its surface, and no sound can be heard, not even the crunch of footsteps on the glass. This creates a sense of profound isolation and unease.
- **Disturbing Reflections:** The glass reflects not only the sky but also distorted, shadowy images that seem to move on their own, just at the edge of perception. These could be glimpses into the true nature of the Plateau, or perhaps echoes of the horrors trapped within it.

**Rumors and Misconceptions:**

- **A Second Source:** The prevailing rumor is that the Glass Plateau was a secondary source of the Mourning, another location where a catastrophic magical event occurred. This is partly true, but the reality is far worse.
- **Crystalline Corruption:** Some believe the Plateau is made of a unique form of Croydonite, or that it's the result of a massive explosion of the material. This is a misconception, though [[Croydonite]] might be found in the surrounding areas, drawn to the Plateau's dark energy.
- **A Place of Power:** There are whispers that the Plateau holds a source of great power, and some foolhardy individuals might venture there seeking to harness its energy.

**The True Nature of the Glass Plateau:**

- **A Prison, Not a Source:** The Glass Plateau is not a source of the Mourning, but rather a **prison**, created by the cabal during the Final Sanction. It was designed to contain something far too dangerous to be destroyed, something that predates even the Mourning itself.
- **The Obsidian Prison:** The "glass" is, in fact, a magically created form of obsidian, infused with powerful binding and নিষেধাজ্ঞা spells. It's a containment field, designed to hold something within it, buried deep beneath the surface.
- **The Thing Below:** Imprisoned beneath the Plateau is an entity of immense power and ancient evil, let's call it **"The Devourer of Dreams"** for now. This being is not from Eberron, nor is it from any of the known planes. It's something far older, a remnant of a previous reality or a being from the darkness between the stars. It could be a powerful being from the far realm, or even a being that predates the far realm, and is only able to interact with the world through dreams.
- **The Connection to the Mourning:** The Disciples of the Unwritten Name, in their reckless pursuit of power, somehow became aware of the Devourer of Dreams. They believed they could control it, that they could use its power to fuel their creation of a new god. Their ritual, the one that created the portal, was not only an attempt to make their warforged god, it was also an attempt to _breach the Obsidian Prison and draw upon the Devourer's power_. This is why the portal manifested a connection to a place of madness, the magic of the ritual coming into contact with the Devourer.
- **The Cabal's Choice:** The cabal, in enacting the Final Sanction, realized the true nature of the Plateau and the entity trapped beneath it. They chose not to destroy it, for fear of what might be unleashed. Instead, they reinforced the prison, channeling the energy of the closing portal to strengthen the Obsidian Prison's bindings, ensuring the Devourer of Dreams remained trapped. They also manipulated the flow of time magic, so that any creature trapped within the prison would be trapped for an eternity.
- **The Threat Remains:** The prison is weakening. The Devourer of Dreams is not truly sentient in a way that mortals can comprehend, but it is aware, and it is constantly testing its bonds. Its influence seeps out, causing strange dreams, reality distortions, and attracting creatures of darkness to the Plateau.

**Possible Encounters on the Glass Plateau:**

- **Distorted Reflections:** The party might encounter their own twisted reflections in the glass, animated by the Devourer's power. These reflections could offer cryptic warnings, taunt the characters, or even attack them. Use the **Doppelganger** statistics, but make them immune to non-magical attacks.
- **Whispers of Madness:** The closer one gets to the center of the Plateau, the stronger the Devourer's psychic influence becomes. Characters might hear whispers in their minds, urging them to break the prison or offering them power in exchange for their service. These could be represented by Wisdom saving throws, with failures leading to temporary madness or the planting of insidious suggestions.
- **Guardians of the Prison:** The cabal might have left behind guardians to protect the prison – constructs, elementals, or even celestial beings, bound to their duty for eternity. They might be corrupted by the Devourer's influence, or they might be desperately trying to maintain the weakening seals. Use **Animated Armor**, **Invisible Stalker** or **Shield Guardian** stats.
- **The Shadow of the Devourer:** A manifestation of the Devourer's power, a creature of pure shadow and madness. This could be a powerful boss encounter, a being that can manipulate dreams, teleport through shadows, and drain the life force of its victims. Use **Shadow Demon** or **Nightwalker** statistics.

**Clues and Discoveries:**

- **Fragmented Memories:** Characters experiencing the Devourer's psychic influence might have fragmented visions or dreams that reveal the true nature of the Plateau and the entity trapped beneath it.
- **The Cabal's Writings:** The party might discover hidden records left behind by the cabal, detailing their creation of the Obsidian Prison and their fears about the Devourer of Dreams. These records might be incomplete or written in a code that needs to be deciphered.
- **The Order's Knowledge:** The Order of the Broken Sky might have some knowledge about the true nature of the Glass Plateau, though their understanding is likely incomplete and shrouded in secrecy. They might consider it to be too dangerous to explore.
- **The Nature of the "Glass":** Through experimentation or research, the party might discover that the "glass" is not ordinary glass, but a magically reinforced material with unique properties. They might even find a way to shatter a small piece, revealing the darkness that lies beneath.
- **The Warrior Who Spared Gyda:** This NPC might have encountered the Glass Plateau during his travels and could have valuable information about its dangers. He might even know a way to temporarily weaken the prison's defenses, though he would warn against doing so.

**Plot Hooks:**

- **The Nightmares Begin:** People in Salvation start experiencing vivid, shared nightmares, all centered around a vast, glassy expanse and a feeling of being watched by something ancient and malevolent.
- **The Order's Expedition:** The Order of the Broken Sky launches an expedition to the Glass Plateau, seeking to study the prison and assess the threat posed by the Devourer of Dreams. The party might be hired to assist them, or they might be competing with the Order to reach the Plateau first.
- **The Mad Prophet:** A crazed prophet emerges from [[the Mournland]], ranting about the "Devourer of Dreams" and the "end of all things." He claims that the prison is weakening and that the Devourer will soon be free.
- **The Artifact of Binding:** The party discovers an artifact that is somehow connected to the Obsidian Prison, perhaps a key to strengthening its defenses or a tool that can be used to communicate with the entity trapped within.

**The Glass Plateau is a location of immense danger and profound mystery. It's a place where the players will confront not only terrifying creatures but also the horrifying truth about the Mourning and the ancient evil that lies buried beneath the surface. Their choices there could determine whether the Devourer of Dreams remains imprisoned or breaks free to consume all of reality.**
