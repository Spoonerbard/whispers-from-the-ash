

**1. [[The Ashen Wastes]] (Desert with Black Sand)**

- **Overall Layout:** A relatively open area with rolling sand dunes of varying heights. The black sand creates an eerie, almost alien landscape.
- **Key Features:**
    - **Sand Dunes:** Provide cover and varying elevations. Some dunes might be unstable, collapsing when climbed or causing characters to slide down.
    - **Sinkholes:** Hidden beneath the sand, these could be small pitfalls or large areas that collapse, dropping characters into underground tunnels or Ashworm lairs. They could be represented by a slightly darker shade of black on the map.
    - **Ashworm/Sand Stalker Tunnels:** Entrances to underground tunnels used by the creatures. These could be marked on the map or hidden, requiring successful checks to locate.
    - **Remains of the Failed Expedition:** A partially buried campsite, scattered equipment, and skeletal remains provide a grim reminder of the dangers. This area could offer some cover or difficult terrain.
    - **Elevated Vantage Points:** A few rocky outcroppings or the tops of the highest dunes could provide vantage points for ranged attacks or reconnaissance.
    - **Black Sand:** The sand is infused with necromantic energy, represented by a faint yellow glow.
- **Map Elements:**
    - Contour lines to represent the varying heights of the dunes.
    - Markers for sinkholes (hidden or visible).
    - Symbols for [[Ashworm]]/Sand Stalker tunnels.
    - Icons for the remains of the expedition.
    - Shading to indicate areas of bright sunlight and deep shadow.

**4. [[The Illusory Outpost]] (Ruined City/Ruins)**

- **Overall Layout:** The ruins of a small city or outpost, now warped by illusion magic. Buildings might appear intact from a distance but crumble to dust upon closer inspection. Streets might lead to dead ends or loop back on themselves. The area is shrouded in a green mist, and a faint purple glow emanates from certain objects.
- **Key Features:**
    - **Illusory Walls and Structures:** Buildings, walls, or other structures that appear real but are actually illusions. These could be marked on the map with dotted lines or a special symbol.
    - **Phantom Guardians:** Illusory creatures that patrol the area, potentially attacking the party if they get too close.
    - **The Source of the Illusions:** A central location where a damaged artifact or malfunctioning device is creating the illusions. This could be a tower, a temple, or a hidden chamber within a building.
    - **Areas of Real Terrain:** Not all of the area is illusory. Some buildings or structures might be real, offering solid ground and potential shelter.
    - **Divination Zones:** Areas where the purple glow is strongest, indicating a concentration of divination magic. These areas might offer glimpses into the past or future.
- **Map Elements:**
    - A mix of solid and dotted lines to represent real and illusory structures.
    - Symbols for phantom guardians and their patrol routes.

    - Shading to indicate areas shrouded in green mist.
