**I. The Party Base: From Shack to Fort**

The party's base will evolve over time, reflecting their growing resources and influence. We'll tie its development to both the **Town Tiers** and the party's own accomplishments.

**Base Tiers:**

- **Tier 1: [[The Shack]] (Levels 1-4):** A humble, run-down structure that barely provides shelter. it has some births, a latrine
- **Tier 2: [[The Safehouse]] (Levels 5-10):** A more secure and comfortable dwelling with basic amenities. #rewards/base
- **Tier 3: [[The Headquarters]] (Levels 11-16):** A well-equipped base with specialized areas for the party's needs. #rewards/base
- **Tier 4: [[The Stronghold]] (Levels 17-20):** A fortified and prestigious base that reflects the party's power and influence. #rewards/base


**III. Making it Dynamic**

- **Player Choice:** Allow players to choose which upgrades they want to prioritize, reflecting their playstyle and goals.
- **Quests for Upgrades:** Tie certain upgrades to specific quests or challenges in [[The Mournland]]. For example, the party might need to recover a rare component from a dangerous ruin to build an enchanting chamber.
- **Repairs and Maintenance:** The base should require occasional repairs and maintenance, especially after attacks or due to the harsh environment. This creates ongoing costs and encourages players to invest in the base's upkeep.
- **Base Defense:** As the party's base becomes more prominent, it could become a target for enemies. Prepare scenarios where the party must defend their base from attacks. These could be random events or tied to the campaign's plot.
- **NPC Staff:** The party might hire NPCs to help maintain the base, craft items, or provide other services. These NPCs could have their own personalities, skills, and side quests.

**IV. Tying the Base to Exploration**

- **Trophies and Mementos:** Encourage players to decorate their base with trophies and mementos from their adventures. This creates a visual record of their accomplishments and makes the base feel more personalized.
- **Information Hub:** The base can become a central repository for information gathered during explorations. The library, map room, and scrying chamber can all contribute to this.
- **Crafting Resources:** Materials gathered in [[the Mournland]] can be used to upgrade the base or craft unique items within its workshops.
- **Base as a Launchpad:** The base's location within [[npcs]] and its growing amenities will make it an increasingly useful launchpad for expeditions into [[the Mournland]].
