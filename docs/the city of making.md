**Making** is a city of [Cyre](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Cyre "Cyre"), once one of its major cities lying near the heart of the kingdom, and now a ruin in the dead center of the [Mournland](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Mournland "Mournland"), in the middle of the [Glass Plateau](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Glass_Plateau "Glass Plateau").[[1]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-FN-p78-1)[[2]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-EPG-p130-2)[[3]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-ECG-p92-3)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-RftLW-p221-4)

## History[](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making?veaction=edit&section=1 "Edit section: History")

Some theorize that the [Mourning](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Mourning "Mourning") itself began in Making. On the [Day of Mourning](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Day_of_Mourning "Day of Mourning"), 20 [Olarune](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Olarune "Olarune") in [994 YK](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/994_YK "994 YK"), it appears that something horrible happened within, nearby, or around the city, and from there it spread out to overwhelm and destroy the entire kingdom of Cyre.[[5]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-FN-p77-5)[[6]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-FN-p83-6)[[3]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-ECG-p92-3)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-RftLW-p221-4)

All around Making, the land of southern [[Cyre]]was hugely reshaped by the upwelling of the Glass Plateau, and weeks of earthquakes followed.[[6]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-FN-p83-6)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-RftLW-p221-4) In 997 YK, the tremors of the Glass Plateau were repeated.[[6]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-FN-p83-6)

## Geography[](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making?veaction=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Geography")

The city is now surrounded by a flat tableland of obsidian and jagged glass.[[2]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-EPG-p130-2)[[3]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-ECG-p92-3) Making, and everything atop the Glass Plateau, are completely inaccessible from the ground.[[6]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-FN-p83-6) The Saerun Road once ran to Making, before it was cut by the plateau.[[3]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-ECG-p92-3)

## Description[](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making?veaction=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Description")

It's been described as a dead city[[2]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-EPG-p130-2) with its ruins still rising above the flat expanse of the Glass Plateau.[[3]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-ECG-p92-3)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-RftLW-p221-4)

## Inhabitants[](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making?veaction=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Inhabitants")

The ruins of Making are home to [living spells](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Living_spell "Living spell") and weird creatures.[[3]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-ECG-p92-3)

## Organizations[](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making?veaction=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Organizations")

Making was the base of [House Phiarlan](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/House_Phiarlan "House Phiarlan") until the Day of Mourning. Phiarlan's baron was conveniently absent from the city on that day, which prompts conspiracy theories about a connection with the catastrophe.[[7]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-7)

It was also a stronghold of [House Cannith](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/House_Cannith "House Cannith").[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-RftLW-p221-4) Wild theories claim that Cannith maintained a secret facility deep beneath Making.[[3]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-ECG-p92-3)[[4]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-RftLW-p221-4)

Making is one proposed site for the base of operations of [the Blades](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/The_Blades "The Blades"), those [warforged](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Warforged "Warforged") who follow the [Lord of Blades](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Blades "Lord of Blades").[[8]](https://eberron.fandom.com/wiki/Making#cite_note-ECG-p99-8)
