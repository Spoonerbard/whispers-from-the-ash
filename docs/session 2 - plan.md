**II. Session Content**

- **Opening Scene (Salvation):**
    - **Description:** The party awakens in Salvation after their night in the Sky Song Tavern. The town is slowly coming to life. The arrival of the House emissaries has caused a noticeable change in the town's atmosphere. People are more on edge, and there's a sense of anticipation in the air.
    - **Anya's Companions:** Anya is still weak but can now talk. She is confused and distressed by the state of her companions. She can confirm their identities: **Borin Stonebeard** (the dwarf) is her cousin, and **Unit 734** (the warforged) is a close friend and comrade. She can also confirm that they are much younger than they should be and that they are speaking an ancient dialect of Dwarvish and a strange form of common. She will also be able to identify the symbol on Borin's arm as an ancient symbol of his god. She begs the party to help her find a way to restore them to their proper ages. She does not know what happened to the last member of her party.
    - **The Journal:** The party might try to decipher the tavern keeper's journal. They could seek help from Silas (who might reveal a bit more of his scholarly background if approached correctly), or they might try to find a translator in town (perhaps Mara can put them in touch with someone, for a price). At this stage, they should be able to decipher only portions of it, enough to get some clues about the Sky Song Tavern's past, Fizzwick's experiments with Sky Song Ale, and perhaps a hint about the Singing Stones. The party will likely need to find a way to translate the rest of the journal later.
    - **Hook:** The party is approached by either Deputy Dal or Sheriff Karr. They are asked to investigate the Ashen Wastes further and bring back any information they can about the missing members of Anya's party, and, if possible, to retrieve their bodies. They are also asked to keep an eye out for any further disturbances.
- **Exploration & Travel:**
    - **Destination:** The Ashen Wastes.
    - **Travel Time:** Half a day's journey to reach the edge of the wastes.
    - **Planned Encounters:** None planned for the journey.
    - **Random Encounters:** Roll for random encounters, but make them relatively low-stakes for this session. This is more about investigation and setting the scene for future encounters.
    - **Environmental Hazards:** Heat, dehydration, potential sinkholes.
    - **Resource Management:** The party should be reminded to bring sufficient water and to consider the effects of the heat.
- **Location/Encounter Details:**
    - **The Ashen Wastes (Revisited):**
        - **Description:** The black sand desert is even more unsettling in the daylight. The heat is oppressive, and the air shimmers with distortion. The faint yellow glow of necromantic energy is more visible during the day.
        - **Inhabitants/Creatures:** Ashworms or Sand Stalkers (use the descriptions from previous responses). The party might encounter a small group of them, or they might find evidence of their presence (tracks, tunnels, etc.).
        - **Challenges:** Navigating the dunes, dealing with the heat and dehydration, avoiding sinkholes, and potentially encountering hostile creatures. The party might also have to deal with the psychological effects of the wastes, such as the faint whispers or the unsettling feeling of being watched.
        - **Rewards:** Further clues about the missing party, potentially some salvage, information about the creatures' behavior, and a better understanding of the Ashen Wastes' layout.
        - **Secrets:** The party might discover that the creatures are drawn to movement or sound, providing a way to avoid or manipulate them. They might also find evidence that the black sand is infused with Croydonite.
        - **Possible Complications:** A sudden sandstorm could reduce visibility and create dangerous conditions. The party might stumble upon a hidden Ashworm nest or a particularly large and aggressive Sand Stalker.
    - **The Search for the Missing Party:**
        - **Tracking:** The party can use their skills (Survival, Investigation, Perception) to track the movements of Anya's party. They might find footprints, discarded equipment, or signs of a struggle.
        - **Clues:** The party might find:
            - **A hastily abandoned campsite:** Suggesting the party was ambushed or forced to flee.
            - **A damaged weapon or piece of armor:** Bearing the insignia of Anya's party.
            - **A hastily written note or a map:** Providing clues about the party's intended destination or their next move.
            - **A personal item belonging to one of the missing members:** A locket, a journal, or a small, carved figurine.
        - **The Trail Leads On:** The trail eventually leads the party towards the **Displaced Depot** in the Silent Grove, setting up the next major encounter. The trail might seem to disappear or reappear in odd places, hinting at the involvement of teleportation magic or a creature that can move through the earth.
- **Social Interactions:**
    - **Anya (if she accompanies them):** Anya can provide some insights into her party members and their skills, but her memories of the events in the Ashen Wastes are still fragmented.
    - **Silas:** Before leaving, the party might seek further information from Silas, who could reveal more about his past as an adventurer or offer advice on surviving in the Mournland.
    - **Dr. Vex:** The party might encounter Dr. Vex, who is also exploring the Ashen Wastes, searching for rare ingredients. He might offer them a trade or a quest in exchange for their help.
- **The Displaced Depot (Cliffhanger):**
    - **Description:** As the party follows the trail, they emerge from the Ashen Wastes and enter the edge of the Silent Grove. Before them, they see the out-of-place and overgrown ruins of the Displaced Depot.
    - **Atmosphere:** The transition from the desert to the forest should be jarring. The silence of the grove is oppressive, and the air feels heavy with a different kind of magic.
    - **Hook:** The party spots signs of recent activity around the station – footprints, disturbed vegetation, and perhaps the lingering smell of smoke or ozone. They might also hear the faint sounds of machinery or movement coming from within the station. The barrels are nowhere to be seen.

**III. Session Wrap-Up**

- **Experience Points:** Award XP for exploration, overcoming challenges, roleplaying, and making discoveries.
- **Treasure & Rewards:** The party might find some salvage, components, or information in the Ashen Wastes. They might also receive a reward from Anya for finding her companions, even in their altered state.
- **Consequences:** The party's actions in the Ashen Wastes might attract the attention of the creatures that inhabit the area, making future travel more dangerous.
- **Update Town Status:** The party's discoveries might generate new rumors or attract the interest of other NPCs in Salvation.
- **Update Faction Status:** The party's actions might influence their relationship with the Order of the Broken Sky, House Cannith, House Tharashk, or other factions.
- **Cliffhanger/Preview:** End the session with the party approaching the Displaced Depot, setting the stage for a more in-depth exploration of the station in the next session.
- **Session Feedback:** Get feedback from your players on what they enjoyed, what they found challenging, and what they'd like to see more of in future sessions.
- **Log:** Briefly log what happened, any significant discoveries, and the choices the players made.

**This session plan provides a framework for a dynamic and engaging session that builds upon the events of the first part of session one and sets the stage for future adventures. It incorporates exploration, investigation, social interaction, and the potential for combat, while also advancing the overarching plot and revealing more about the mysteries of the Mournland. Remember to be flexible and adapt to your players' choices, allowing them to shape the story and explore the world at their own pace.**
