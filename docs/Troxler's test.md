Those are excellent ideas! Focusing on survival gear and tools that interact with the environment in unique ways is perfect for a Mournland setting. It gives Troxler a more distinct niche, and it provides the party with tangible benefits for interacting with her.

Here's how we can develop these ideas, incorporating your concepts and adding some details to make them fit with Troxler's character and the overall campaign:

**Troxler's Experimental Gear (Tier 2):**

**1. The Mourning-Resistant Tent (Prototype):**

- **Description:** A single-person tent made from a strange, rubbery material interwoven with thin strands of metal. It's lightweight but surprisingly sturdy. It packs down to a surprisingly small size. The tent is a dull grey color, and it hums faintly when touched. It is designed to withstand the harsh conditions of the Mournland.
- **Abilities:**
    - When fully set up, the tent provides resistance to the effects of the weather inside.
    - When inside the tent, the user is immune to the effects of the **Screaming Silence**.
    - The tent provides advantage to saving throws to resist the effects of environmental hazards while the user is inside.
    - The tent provides half cover.
- **Drawbacks:**
    - **Unstable Prototype:** The tent is a prototype and might malfunction. Each time it's used, roll a d20. On a 1-5, the tent provides minimal protection, only negating the effects of environmental hazards, but not granting any other benefits. On a roll of 1, the tent malfunctions spectacularly, potentially exposing the occupants to the full brunt of the hazard, or even creating a localized hazard of its own (e.g., a small, contained explosion, a brief burst of wild magic, or a temporary rift to another plane).
    - **Limited Space:** The tent is designed for one person and is cramped at best.
    - **Setup Time:** The tent takes 10 minutes to set up and requires a DC 10 Intelligence check to assemble correctly. On a failed check, the tent is not properly assembled and provides no benefits.
- **Quest Tie-In:** Troxler needs the party to field-test the tent in the Mournland. She might offer them a discount or even give them the prototype for free in exchange for detailed feedback on its performance. She might also ask them to collect data on specific environmental hazards while using the tent. She might also ask the party to retrieve materials needed to improve the tent's design, such as the hide of a creature that is naturally resistant to the Mournland's effects.
- **Upgrade Potential:**
    - **Tier 3:** The tent becomes more reliable (malfunctions on a roll of 1-2 on a d20), can accommodate two people, and grants resistance to the damage type of the hazard encountered while inside. The DC to assemble the tent is removed. It now provides 3/4 cover.
    - **Tier 4:** The tent is almost completely reliable (malfunctions on a 1), can accommodate four people, provides immunity to the damage type of the hazard encountered while inside, and can be set up as an action. It now provides full cover.

**2. Croydonite-Climbing Gear:**

- **Description:** A set of specialized climbing gear that incorporates small, carefully treated shards of Croydonite. The gear includes gloves, boots, and a harness, all made from tough, Mournland-resistant material. The Croydonite shards are embedded in the palms of the gloves and the soles of the boots, as well as in specially designed pitons.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Croydonite Grip:** The gear allows for climbing on surfaces with Croydonite veins, even those that are too smooth or unstable for normal climbing. The Croydonite shards in the gear grip onto the Croydonite veins, providing a secure hold.
    - **Enhanced Climbing:** The gear grants advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks made to climb any surface.
- **Drawbacks:**
    - **Croydonite Exposure:** Using the gear exposes the climber to the risk of Croydonite contamination. Each hour spent climbing with the gear requires a DC 12 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the character suffers a minor mutation or other effect related to the type of Croydonite they are in contact with.
    - **Limited Purchase:** The Croydonite shards have a limited "grip" and need to be replaced or recharged after each use. Troxler can provide replacements, but they are expensive.
    - **Unpredictable:** In areas with high concentrations of Croydonite, the gear might become unpredictable, either providing an extraordinary boost to climbing ability or suddenly losing its grip.
- **Quest Tie-In:** Troxler might need a specific type of Croydonite, found only in a particularly dangerous location, to perfect the climbing gear. She might also ask the party to test the gear on different types of Croydonite veins, gathering data on its effectiveness and potential risks.
- **Upgrade Potential:**
    - **Tier 3:** The gear no longer requires replacement Croydonite shards. The DC for the Constitution saving throw is reduced to 10. The gear also grants a +5 bonus to climbing checks on non-Croydonite surfaces.
    - **Tier 4:** The gear grants immunity to the negative effects of Croydonite exposure while climbing. The climber can also choose to "overcharge" the gear, granting them a climb speed equal to their walking speed for 1 minute. This can be done once per day.

**3. Rocketspear Grappling Hook:**

- **Description:** A modified grappling hook that incorporates a small, single-use, rocket-like device powered by refined Croydonite. The hook is attached to a length of sturdy rope or chain. The device is launched from a handheld launcher, and is surprisingly accurate.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Extended Range:** The grappling hook can be fired up to 120 feet, allowing the user to reach distant or elevated locations.
    - **Powerful Propulsion:** The rocket provides a powerful initial burst of speed, allowing the user to quickly ascend or descend.
    - **Magic Missile Dart:** In addition to the grappling hook, the launcher fires a dart that deals 1d4+1 force damage, as per the _magic missile_ spell. The user must make a ranged weapon attack to grapple a target, rather than firing it at a point.
- **Drawbacks:**
    - **Single-Use Rocket:** The rocket device is single-use and must be replaced after each firing. Troxler can craft replacements, but they require rare and expensive materials.
    - **Risk of Malfunction:** Due to the volatile nature of Croydonite, there's a small chance (e.g., 5% or a 1 on a d20) that the rocket will malfunction, potentially exploding in the user's hand or veering off course.
    - **Limited Control:** Once fired, the grappling hook is difficult to control, and the user might be pulled along a path they didn't intend.
- **Quest Tie-In:** Troxler might need the party to recover a specific type of metal or a rare component from a ruined Cannith forge to improve the rocket's design. She might also ask them to test the grappling hook in different environments, gathering data on its range, accuracy, and potential for malfunction. She might also ask for the party to test it on different creatures.
- **Upgrade Potential:**
    - **Tier 3:** The grappling hook's range is increased to 240 feet, and the damage of the dart increases to 3d4+3. The chance of malfunction is reduced (e.g., a 1 on a d100). The grappling hook can now be used to pull creatures towards the user.
    - **Tier 4:** The grappling hook can now be fired twice before needing to be reloaded. The damage of the dart increases to 5d4+5. The grappling hook can be used as a melee weapon, dealing 1d6 piercing damage on a hit. The grappling hook can now be used to pull objects towards the user.

**Roleplaying Troxler:**

- **Mad Inventor:** Emphasize Troxler's eccentric genius and their obsession with pushing the boundaries of artifice. They should be constantly tinkering, experimenting, and coming up with new (and potentially dangerous) ideas.
- **Testing on the Party:** Troxler sees the party as a valuable source of field testing for their inventions. They might offer them discounts or even free prototypes in exchange for their willingness to try out the new gear and report back on its performance.
- **Ethical Ambiguity:** Troxler's inventions often involve ethically questionable materials (like Croydonite) and carry significant risks. This creates opportunities for moral dilemmas and roleplaying challenges for the party. Will they accept the risks in exchange for the potential rewards? Will they challenge Troxler on the ethics of their work?
- **Secret Patron:** Remember that Troxler is secretly being funded by a shadowy organization. This connection could be gradually revealed through their inventions, their requests for specific materials, or their interactions with other NPCs.

**By offering these unique and experimental items, Troxler becomes a more engaging and dynamic NPC. They provide the party with new tools and abilities, encourage them to explore the Mournland in different ways, and create opportunities for both exciting successes and spectacular failures. The element of risk and the potential for unexpected consequences will keep players on their toes and make each interaction with Troxler a memorable one.**
