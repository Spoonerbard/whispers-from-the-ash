**2. Mara "Fingers" Keen (Black Market Trader)**

- **Services:** Deals in rare and often illegal goods, including stolen items, salvaged artifacts, and restricted substances. She can also procure specific items on request, but her prices are steep, and her methods are questionable. She is an expert at smuggling, lockpicking and pickpocketing. She can get access to almost anything, given enough time.
- **Personality:** Charming, cunning, and opportunistic. She's always looking for an angle and is not afraid to take risks. She has a network of contacts and informants throughout the region. She is secretly very lonely.
- **Rumors & Information:**
    - She has connections to various factions operating in the Mournland, including smugglers, bandits, and potentially even more dangerous groups.
    - She might know something about the Steel Stoners' activities or the whereabouts of valuable artifacts.
    - She's heard whispers about the Order of the Broken Sky and their interest in the Mournland.
- **Quest Hooks:**
    - **The Special Delivery:** Mara asks the party to deliver a package to a contact in a dangerous part of the Mournland. The contents of the package are unknown, and the recipient might be someone unexpected.
    - **The Missing Shipment:** A shipment of goods Mara was expecting has gone missing. She hires the party to track it down and recover it. The shipment might have been stolen by bandits, seized by House Tharashk, or lost in a hazardous area of the Mournland.
    - **The Competition:** A rival black market trader arrives in town, threatening Mara's business. She might ask the party to help her eliminate the competition, either through sabotage, intimidation, or framing them for a crime. She will not kill her competition.
- **Secret:** She's working for House Phiarlan/Thuranni, gathering intelligence and manipulating events in Salvation. She's also searching for a specific artifact, **The Serpent's Eye**, a powerful gem said to have been lost in the Mournland. She is also secretly looking after a small group of orphans in Salvation, using her ill-gotten gains to provide for them. She hides this from everyone.
