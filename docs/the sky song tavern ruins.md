**The Sky Song Tavern: A Ruined Oasis**

**Location:** Situated in a square adjacent to [[npcs]], making it an ideal early-game exploration site. Perhaps it's nestled in a small canyon or at the foot of a rocky outcrop, offering some natural protection from the elements.

**Exterior:**

- **Rundown Structure:** The tavern is a two-story building made of stone and what was once sturdy timber. Now, it's in a state of disrepair. The roof has partially collapsed, windows are shattered, and the walls are covered in scorch marks and strange, crystalline growths (hints of Croydonite).
- **Faded Grandeur:** Despite its dilapidated state, hints of its former glory remain. Faint, magical runes are etched into the doorframe, and remnants of colorful murals depicting musical scenes can be seen on the walls.
- **Eerie Silence:** Unlike the surrounding wasteland, the immediate area around the tavern is strangely silent, as if the building itself is absorbing sound. This could be a residual effect of its magical past.
- **The Sign:** A weathered wooden sign hangs precariously above the entrance, depicting a lyre with wings. Beneath it, barely legible, are the words "The Sky Song Tavern."

**Interior:**

- **Ground Floor:**
    - **Common Room:** A large, open space that once served as the tavern's main hall. Tables and chairs are overturned and broken. A large, stone fireplace dominates one wall, now filled with ash and debris.
    - **Bar Area:** A long, কাঠের counter, partially collapsed, still has remnants of shelves that once held bottles and glasses. A faint, sweet smell lingers in the air.
    - **Stage:** A raised platform at one end of the room suggests the tavern once hosted performances.
    - **Enchanted Murals:** Faded murals on the walls depict musicians playing instruments. These murals have a faint magical aura and might react to the presence of magic or music (e.g., subtly shifting, faintly glowing, or emitting musical notes when a spell is cast or a song is sung nearby).
- **Second Floor:**
    - **Collapsed Rooms:** The second floor is mostly collapsed, with only a few partially intact rooms that once served as guest quarters. These rooms are dangerous to enter due to the unstable structure.
    - **Hidden Chamber:** A successful DC 15 Investigation check in one of the rooms reveals a hidden chamber behind a loose wall panel. This chamber contains the tavern keeper's private belongings, including a journal (see "Secrets of the Sky Song" below).
- **Basement:**
    - **Sealed Door:** A sturdy, iron-bound door in the common room leads to the basement. It's magically sealed and requires a DC 17 Arcana check or a specific command phrase (found in the tavern keeper's journal) to open.
    - **Enchanted Brewery:** The basement was once a magical brewery where the tavern's signature drink, "Sky Song Ale," was made. Remnants of brewing equipment, strangely preserved barrels, and glowing runes on the walls hint at the advanced magical techniques used here.

**Secrets of the Sky Song:**

- **The Tavern Keeper's Journal:** This journal, hidden in the secret chamber on the second floor, reveals that the tavern keeper, a gnome named **Fizzwick Gearwhistle**, was a skilled artificer and enchanter. He used his knowledge to create a tavern that was a marvel of its time.
- **Sky Song Ale:** Fizzwick's signature creation was a magical ale called "Sky Song." It was brewed using a unique process involving enchanted fermentation and rare herbs found in the region before the Mourning. The ale had a variety of effects, depending on the specific recipe:
    - **Chorus of Vigor:** Granted temporary hit points and advantage on Strength checks.
    - **Ballad of Agility:** Increased movement speed and granted advantage on Dexterity checks.
    - **Sonata of Warding:** Provided resistance to a specific type of damage.
    - **Requiem of Slumber:** A powerful sleeping draught.
- **The Singing Stones:** Fizzwick used a set of enchanted stones, called "Singing Stones," to control the tavern's magical features. These stones were activated by specific musical notes or phrases. The journal hints at their location within the tavern.
- **The Mourning:** The journal entries leading up to the Mourning express Fizzwick's growing concern about the escalating tensions and his fear of a coming catastrophe. His last entry is dated the day of the Mourning and simply reads, "May the music play on, even in silence."

**Magical Features (Remnants):**

- **The Singing Stones:** These stones, if found and reactivated, could restore some of the tavern's magical functions or be used to craft new magical items. They might be hidden in the murals, under the stage, or even embedded in the walls.

**Rewards:**

- **Knowledge:** The tavern keeper's journal, the Singing Stones, and the magical brewery offer valuable insights into pre-Mourning magic and the events leading up to the catastrophe.
- **Magical Items:** The Singing Stones themselves could be powerful magical items. Remnants of Sky Song Ale might still be potent, and other magical trinkets could be found hidden throughout the tavern.
- **Safe Haven:** If restored to some of its former glory, the Sky Song Tavern could serve as a valuable base of operations for the party in [[the Mournland]].
- **Alliance with the Order:** Gaining the favor of the Order of the Broken Sky could provide the party with access to powerful magic, valuable information, and influential allies. However, it could also draw them into a web of intrigue and danger.
