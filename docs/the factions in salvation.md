**1. House Cannith:**

- **Motivation:** House Cannith was deeply involved in the Last War, creating magical weapons, warforged, and other arcane technologies. The Mourning likely destroyed many of their creations and research facilities within Cyre. They would be driven by a desire to:
    - **Recover Lost Knowledge:** Salvage lost Cannith technology, blueprints, and research notes from the ruins of their former strongholds.
    - **Study the Mourning:** Understand the magical catastrophe that created [[the Mournland]]], both to prevent a similar event and to potentially harness its power.
    - **Rebuild their Supremacy:** Re-establish their dominance in the magical industry by being the first to unlock the secrets of [[the Mournland]].
    - **Monopolize Croydonite:** They would see it as an incredibly powerful resource, perfect for creating new and terrible weapons, as well as potentially improving the functionality of warforged.
- **Expertise:**
    - **Artifice and Crafting:** Cannith's expertise in artifice and crafting makes them perfectly suited to analyze and potentially utilize [[Croydonite]] and other[[mournland]] resources.
    - **Magical Research:** They have the resources and expertise to conduct large-scale magical research into the nature of the Mourning.
    - **Warfare and Weaponry:** Their history of weapon development makes them likely to see the military applications of[[mournland]] discoveries.

**2. House Tharashk:**

- **Motivation:** House Tharashk specializes in prospecting, finding, and extracting natural resources. [[the Mournland]], despite its dangers, represents a vast, untapped source of potentially valuable materials, including:
    - **Croydonite:** They would see its potential as a valuable commodity, even if dangerous.
    - **Rare Minerals:** The geological upheaval caused by the Mourning could have exposed rare and valuable minerals.
    - **Dragonshards:** Although more common in other parts of Eberron, it's possible that the Mourning uncovered new Dragonshard deposits within the former Cyre.
    - **Salvage:** Tharashk wouldn't shy away from salvaging valuable materials, no matter how dangerous their source.
- **Expertise:**
    - **Prospecting and Mining:** Tharashk has the skills and resources to locate and extract resources, even in hazardous environments. They also have experience with monstrous labour, that would be particularly helpful for this environment.
    - **Dealing with Dangerous Environments:** Their work often takes them to dangerous and remote locations, giving them experience that would be valuable in [[the Mournland]].
    - **Finding the Unfindable:** Their mark of finding makes them uniquely qualified to locate resources and navigate the warped landscape.
    - **Monstrous Labor:** They are used to working with monstrous creatures and could likely adapt to using creatures found in [[the Mournland]] for labor.

**3. House Phiarlan (or Thuranni):**

- **Motivation:** Both House Phiarlan and House Thuranni deal in secrets, information, and espionage. [[the Mournland]] is a source of great mystery and potential power, making it a prime target for their interests:
    - **Uncover the Truth:** They would be driven to uncover the truth behind the Mourning, seeking to understand what happened and who was responsible. This information could be incredibly valuable.
    - **Exploit the Chaos:** [[the Mournland]] is a lawless place, perfect for গোপন operations and লুকিয়ে dealings.
    - **Recover Lost Artifacts:** They might be seeking specific artifacts or pieces of information lost during the Mourning, either for their own purposes or for a client.
    - **Establish a Foothold:** The lack of established powers in [[the Mournland]] offers an opportunity to gain influence.
- **Expertise:**
    - **Espionage and Infiltration:** Both houses are masters of stealth, disguise, and information gathering, skills that would be invaluable in navigating the dangers of [[the Mournland]].
    - **Information Brokering:** They could use information gleaned from [[the Mournland]] to their advantage, selling it to the highest bidder or using it to manipulate events.
    - **Shadow Network:** Their extensive network of contacts and informants could provide them with valuable intelligence about [[the Mournland]] and its inhabitants.

**Why not others?**

While other houses might have some interest in [[the Mournland]], their focus lies elsewhere:

- **House Deneith:** Primarily mercenaries and bodyguards; while they might be hired to protect expeditions, they wouldn't likely launch their own. However, there is a compelling case to suggest that House Deneith could be contracted to protect a joint operation of multiple houses.
- **House Ghallanda:** Focused on hospitality; they might be involved in supporting settlements like Sanctuary but not in direct exploration.
- **House Jorasco:** Healers; they might be interested in studying the effects of the Mourning on living creatures but are less likely to be involved in large-scale exploration.
- **House Kundarak:** While they deal in security and banking, their focus is on established settlements rather than wastelands.
- **House Lyrandar:** Primarily focused on air and sea travel; their abilities are less relevant to the land-based exploration of [[the Mournland]].
- **House Medani:** While they deal in warnings and investigation they are a relatively small house and would not have the resources for a large scale operation.
- **House Orien:** Focused on land-based transportation; their established routes are likely destroyed, and [[the Mournland]] is too dangerous for regular trade.
- **House Sivis:** Primarily concerned with communication; they might be interested in restoring communication networks but not in direct exploration.
- **House Vadalis:** While they deal with animals, the mutated creatures of [[the Mournland]] are likely too dangerous and unpredictable for their usual methods.

**Potential Conflicts:**

The interests of these three houses in [[the Mournland]] could easily lead to conflict:

- **Cannith vs. Tharashk:** Both houses might clash over the control of resources, particularly Croydonite. Cannith might want it for research and development, while Tharashk might see it as a commodity to be exploited.
- **Cannith vs. Phiarlan/Thuranni:** Cannith might view the Shadow Network as a threat to their গোপন research, while Phiarlan/Thuranni might see Cannith's activities in [[the Mournland]] as a potential source of valuable information or a dangerous development to be monitored.
- **Tharashk vs. Phiarlan/Thuranni:** Tharashk's mining operations could uncover secrets that Phiarlan/Thuranni wants to remain buried, or Phiarlan/Thuranni might try to manipulate Tharashk into doing their dirty work.
