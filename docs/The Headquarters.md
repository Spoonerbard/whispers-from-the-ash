**Tier 3: The Headquarters**

- **Description:** A spacious building, possibly a former merchant's house or a small guildhall, in a prominent location. It's well-equipped and customized to the party's needs.
- **Requirements:**
    - Town must reach Tier 3 (Thriving Hub).
    - Party must have made significant contributions to [[npcs]]'s growth.
    - Cost: 2,000 gp.
- **Starting Features:**
    - **Expanded Workshop:** With specialized areas for different types of crafting (e.g., blacksmithing, woodworking, leatherworking).
    - **Advanced Alchemy Lab:** Capable of producing more potent potions and alchemical substances.
    - **Map Room:** A dedicated space for displaying maps, plotting expeditions, and storing geographical knowledge.
    - **Training Area:** A space for practicing combat skills or other abilities.
    - **Meeting Hall:** A larger room for hosting guests or holding discussions with allies.
- **Upgrades:**
    - **Enchanting Chamber (Cost: 1,000 gp, requires rare materials and specialized tools):** Allows for the creation or modification of magic items (requires [[Troxler]]'s assistance or another skilled enchanter).
    - **Scrying Pool/Chamber (Cost: 750 gp, requires specific components):** Enables the party to gather information or communicate over long distances.
    - **Teleportation Circle (Cost: 5,000 gp, requires a high-level spellcaster and rare components):** Allows for instantaneous travel to other established circles (if any exist in your setting).
    - **Armory (Cost: 500 gp, requires weapon racks and armor stands):** A secure and organized storage area for weapons and armor.
    - **Garden (Cost: 300 gp, requires seeds and gardening tools):** An area for growing herbs, food, or other useful plants. Could be inside a greenhouse for protection from the environment.
