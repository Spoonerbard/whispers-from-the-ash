# **1. The Ashen Wastes (Small Desert - Black Sand)**

- **Description:** A small desert area to the [East/West] of Salvation, where the sand is an unnatural black, like finely ground ash. The air shimmers with heat, and the silence is broken only by the occasional whisper of wind. The area gives off a faint aura of necromantic magic. This desert used to be a lush, green, field, where most of the food for the area was grown.
- **The Failed Expedition:** A heavily armed and well-equipped adventuring party ventured into [[the Ashen Wastes]] last week. Only one member, a half-elf ranger named **[[Anya Swiftbow]]** returned, badly injured and driven to the edge of madness. She's currently recovering in Salvation's inn (or makeshift infirmary) but is mostly incoherent, muttering about "they swim, they breathe".
- **Danger:**
    - **"They Swim, They Breathe":** The primary threat in the Ashen Wastes is a species of creature that burrows beneath the black sand. These could be:
        - **Ashworms:** Giant, worm-like creatures that "swim" through the sand, erupting from beneath to attack their prey. They are blind but can sense vibrations in the sand. They might be attracted to movement or sound, making stealth a crucial element of survival. They might be weak to a certain type of magic or have a vulnerability to a specific element, such as cold or radiant damage.
        - **Sand Stalkers:** These creatures could be more humanoid, able to phase through the sand, becoming intangible and leaving only a faint shimmer in their wake. They might be able to mimic the voices of their victims, luring others to their doom. They are weak to loud noises, which disrupts their ability to phase.
        - **The Black Sands:** The sand itself might be infused with necromantic energy. Prolonged exposure could cause exhaustion, weakness, or even drain the life force of living creatures. The sand could also animate, forming into small, vaguely humanoid shapes that attack and try to suffocate their victims, each round a creature is within 5ft of one of these shapes they must make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or take 1d4 necrotic damage.
    - **Environmental Hazards:**
        - **Heat:** The black sand absorbs heat, making the area incredibly hot during the day.
        - **Dehydration:** The dry air and intense heat increase the rate of dehydration.
        - **Sinkholes:** Pockets of loose sand could give way beneath unwary travelers, trapping them or dropping them into underground tunnels.
- **Possible Encounters:**
    - A pack of [[Ashworms]]/Sand Stalkers ambushing the party.
    - A lone, crazed prospector driven mad by the black sand, warning the party to turn back.
    - The remains of the previous adventuring party, offering clues about the creatures and potentially containing valuable salvage.
- **Rewards:**
    - **Map:** A partial map of the area could be recovered from the previous adventuring party or drawn by a skilled cartographer in Salvation based on Anya's ramblings.
    - **Intact Equipment:** Some of the previous party's equipment might still be usable.
    - **Rare Minerals:** The black sand might contain rare minerals or [[Croydonite]] deposits.
    - **Knowledge:** Discovering the nature of the creatures and their weaknesses could be valuable information for future expeditions.

# **2. The Jagged Peaks & The Silent Grove (Rocky Mountains & Dead Forest)**

- **Description:** To the [North/South] of Salvation lies a region of jagged, rocky mountains that ascend into a dead, petrified forest known as the Silent Grove. This is where the ruins of the Sky Song Tavern can be found. The mountains are treacherous, and the forest is eerily silent, creating a sense of unease.
- **The Sky Song Tavern:** The tavern is located on the edge of the Silent Grove, nestled among the foothills of the mountains. It's a two-story stone and timber structure, partially collapsed but still offering some shelter.
- **Dangers:**
    - **Mountainous Terrain:** Difficult climbs, narrow paths, and the risk of rockfalls.
    - **Predators:** Mountain lions, giant eagles, or other creatures adapted to the rocky terrain (potentially mutated by the Mourning).
    - **The Silence of the Grove:** The unnatural silence of the petrified forest can be disorienting and mentally taxing (as described in our previous environmental hazards).
    - **Guardians of the Tavern:** The tavern itself might be inhabited by creatures attracted to its residual magic or remnants of its past (e.g., the animated stein mimic, restless spirits).
    - **The Order of the Broken Sky:** Agents of the Order might be searching for the tavern, creating potential conflict or uneasy alliances.
- **Possible Encounters:**
    - A rockslide blocking the path, forcing the party to find an alternate route.
    - A nest of mutated giant eagles guarding a high peak.
    - A patrol of Order of the Broken Sky agents, investigating the area.
    - Encounter with the specific hazards of the tavern.
- **Rewards:**
    - **The Sky Song Tavern:** Offers a potential safe haven, clues about the Mourning, and the possibility of recovering lost magical knowledge (Fizzwick's journal, the Singing Stones).
    - **Vantage Point:** The mountain peaks offer a panoramic view of the surrounding area, potentially revealing other points of interest.
    - **Rare Herbs:** Certain rare herbs or fungi might grow in the mountains or the forest, useful for crafting or healing.
    - **Hidden Paths:** Secret trails might lead to hidden valleys, caves, or other locations.

# **3. The Stillwater (Lake)**

- **Description:** A large, still lake to the [East/West] of Salvation. The water is an unnatural, dark green color and has a slightly oily sheen. The area around the lake is strangely devoid of life, and the air feels heavy and oppressive. The lake is fed by a river that runs through the Mournland. It used to be a clear blue.
- **Danger:**
    - **Contaminated Water:** The lake water is contaminated by the Mourning. While not immediately deadly, drinking it could have unpredictable effects (use the Mournland Water table from our earlier ).
    - **Aquatic Threats:**
        - **The Stillwater Lurker:** A large, mutated creature that lurks beneath the surface, attacking anything that comes too close. This could be a giant fish, a mutated, or something more horrific. It has developed a taste for flesh.
        - **Mournland Leeches:** These are not ordinary leeches. They might be larger, faster, or able to drain life force in addition to blood. They could latch onto swimmers or even crawl onto land to attack. They are attracted to blood.
        - **Toxic Algae Blooms:** Certain areas of the lake might be covered in toxic algae blooms that release poisonous spores into the air or cause skin irritation on contact. These could also give off a hallucinogenic effect.
    - **The Deep:** The lake might be deeper than it appears, with underwater caves or tunnels leading to unknown dangers. There could also be a sinkhole in the middle of the lake, that drags creatures and objects down into the depths.
- **Possible Encounters:**
    - The Stillwater Lurker attacking the party as they approach the lake or try to cross it.
    - A swarm of Mournland leeches infesting the shallows.
    - A group of desperate survivors trying to purify the lake water, unaware of the true dangers.
    - **The Wreck of the Riverboat:** The partially submerged wreck of a riverboat that once traveled the river that feeds the lake. It might contain valuable cargo or clues about the river's past.
- **Rewards:**
    - **Rumours:** People might talk about a riverboat that used to travel up and down the river, and that it had a সুরক্ষিত onboard.
    - **Fishing:** Despite the contamination, some mutated fish might still be edible (with proper preparation).
    - **Underwater Caves:** Exploring the lake depths could reveal hidden underwater caves containing lost artifacts or rare resources.
    - **The River's Source:** Following the river that feeds the lake could lead to a less contaminated water source or a point of interest further into the Mournland.

**First Session Structure:**

1. **Arrival in Salvation:** The party arrives in Salvation, meets Sheriff Karr, gets their adventuring licenses, and learns about the town and the surrounding areas. They are told about the three locations, and the dangers and mysteries associated with them.
2. **Gathering Information:** The party can talk to NPCs in town, such as the barkeep  [[Grelga]] , the Steel Stoners (if approached cautiously), or any surviving members of the previous expedition (like Anya, if she's recovered enough to speak).
3. **Choosing a Destination:** The party decides which of the three areas they want to explore first. This choice will affect the challenges they face and the rewards they might find. They might split up, or they might go as one group.
4. **Exploration and Encounters:** The party ventures into their chosen area, dealing with the environmental hazards, encountering creatures, and uncovering clues.
5. **Returning to Salvation:** The party returns to Salvation to rest, resupply, report their findings, and plan their next move. They might interact with Troxler to identify or craft items, deal with the Steel Stoners, or attract the attention of the Order of the Broken Sky.

**Remember to:**

- **Emphasize Player Choice:** Let the players decide where to go and how to approach each situation.
- **Use the Environment:** Make the environment a significant part of the challenge and the story.
- **Connect to the Wider Plot:** Tie the discoveries in these areas to the larger mysteries of the Mourning, Croydonite, the Order of the Broken Sky, and the other factions.
- **Reward Exploration:** Make sure the players are rewarded for their efforts, not just with treasure but also with information, reputation, and a sense of accomplishment.
