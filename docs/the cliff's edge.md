# **The Threshold of the Mournland: The Cliff's Edge**

**Flavor Text:**

"You stand at the precipice, the edge of the world you know. A few hours north of Salvation's meager embrace, the land abruptly falls away, plunging into a chasm that seems to pierce the very heart of Eberron. This is the threshold, the demarcation line where the familiar ends and the Mournland begins. Before you, a swirling sea of grey mist, the infamous dead-grey mist, now merely a swirling miasma, obscures the depths, a ghostly shroud clinging to the unseen scars below. The air grows colder here, carrying a faint metallic tang and a whisper of something akin to a silent scream. Jagged, unnatural formations of rock claw their way out of the mist, like the broken teeth of some colossal beast. The ground beneath your feet trembles faintly, a constant, unsettling reminder of the cataclysm that birthed this wound in the world. Above, the sky is a bruised and sullen grey, mirroring the desolation below. This is it. The point of no return. The Mournland awaits."

**The Challenge: Descending the Cliff**

**Description:**

- **Height:** The cliff face is approximately 200 feet high, a significant but not insurmountable climb for a 3rd-level party.
- **Surface:** The rock face is uneven, with a mix of sheer drops, narrow ledges, and loose scree. The rock itself is a strange, dark grey, almost black in places, and seems to absorb the light, it is cold and clammy to the touch. In places it is slick and glassy, in others it is porous.
- **Visibility:** The swirling grey mist reduces visibility, especially further down.
- **Croydonite Veins:** Thin veins of Croydonite are visible on the cliff face, particularly in the lower sections. These veins emit a faint, unsettling glow (choose a color based on the type of magic prevalent in the area - black for chronomancy is a good choice here).
- **Mournland Corruption:** The area is permeated by the corrupting influence of the Mournland. This could manifest as a sense of unease, strange whispers, or even minor physical effects.

**Mechanics:**

- **Skill Checks:** The primary skill for navigating the cliff will be **Athletics (Strength)** for climbing. However, other skills could be useful:
    - **Acrobatics (Dexterity):** To maintain balance on narrow ledges or make difficult jumps.
    - **Perception (Wisdom):** To spot loose rocks, hidden handholds, or signs of danger.
    - **Nature (Intelligence):** To identify stable handholds or avoid areas with Croydonite contamination.
    - **Survival (Wisdom):** To assess the overall stability of the cliff face and predict potential hazards.
- **DC:** Set the base DC for climbing checks at 13. This can be adjusted based on the specific section of the cliff:
    - **Easier Sections (DC 10):** Areas with plenty of handholds and relatively stable rock.
    - **Difficult Sections (DC 15):** Areas with sheer drops, narrow ledges, or loose scree.
    - **Dangerous Sections (DC 18 or higher):** Areas with Croydonite veins, unstable rock formations, or exposure to strong winds or other hazards.
- **Consequences of Failure:**
    - **Minor Failure (1-4 below DC):** The character makes no progress or slips slightly, losing a few feet of progress.
    - **Major Failure (5 or more below DC):** The character falls. The distance fallen could be determined by the degree of failure, or you could set specific distances based on the section of the cliff they are on.
    - **Catching Yourself:** A character who fails a climbing check might be able to make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 15) to catch themselves on a lower ledge or handhold, reducing the distance fallen.
- **Group Checks:** Consider using group checks for certain sections of the climb. This allows characters with different skills to contribute to the overall success of the group. For example, one character might make an Athletics check to secure a rope, while another makes a Perception check to spot the safest route.
- **Equipment:**
    - **Climber's Kit:** Provides advantage on Athletics checks related to climbing. However, in Salvation, the available climber's kits are of poor quality, and might break with extended use.
    - **Rope:** Essential for securing the party and creating safety lines. However, the rope available in Salvation is of poor quality and might snap under stress.
    - **Mournland-Adapted Gear:** As the party progresses, they might acquire specialized gear that makes climbing easier or safer, such as gloves that provide a better grip on the slick rock or boots that offer protection from Croydonite contamination.
- **Environmental Hazards:**
    - **Rockfalls:** Sections of the cliff face might be unstable, causing rockfalls that the party must avoid or risk taking damage. This could be triggered by failed climbing checks or by specific events.
    - **Wind Gusts:** Strong gusts of wind could make climbing more difficult, imposing disadvantage on checks or even threatening to blow characters off the cliff.
    - **Croydonite Exposure:** Touching or getting too close to Croydonite veins could have negative effects, such as psychic damage, mutations, or levels of exhaustion.
    - **Mist:** The swirling mist could reduce visibility, making it harder to see handholds or spot dangers. It might also have other effects, such as causing hallucinations or disorientation.

**Possible Encounters:**

- **Mutated Creatures:** The cliff face could be home to mutated creatures, such as giant spiders, rock lizards, or flying predators, that have adapted to the harsh environment.
- **Living Spells:** The area might be infested with Living Spells, remnants of the magical energies unleashed during the Mourning. These could be spells like _Living Cloudkill_ that drifts through the mist, or _Living Gust of Wind_ that tries to push climbers off the cliff.
- **Guardians:** The Order of the Broken Sky, or another faction, might have posted guardians near the cliff edge to monitor the area or prevent unauthorized entry into the Mournland.

**Returning to the Top:**

- **The Climb Back Up:** The party will need to climb back up the cliff to return to Salvation. This could be just as challenging as the descent, especially if they are injured, exhausted, or carrying heavy loads.
- **Time Pressure:** Emphasize the need to return to Salvation before nightfall, adding a sense of urgency to the climb.
- **Alternative Routes:** Perhaps there are other, less direct ways to get back to the top, such as a hidden tunnel or a series of ledges that lead to a different part of the cliff. Discovering these routes could be a reward for thorough exploration.

**Future Developments:**

- **Permanent Solutions:** As the party gains levels and resources, they might invest in creating a safer and more permanent way to descend into the Mournland:
    - **Rope Bridge:** Constructing a sturdy rope bridge across a narrower section of the chasm.
    - **Staircase:** Carving a staircase into the cliff face, a major undertaking that would require significant time, effort, and resources.
    - **Elevator:** A magical or mechanical lift system, potentially powered by Croydonite (with all the associated risks) or salvaged from a pre-Mourning structure.
    - **Teleportation Circle:** Establishing a permanent teleportation circle at the bottom of the cliff, linked to another circle in Salvation. This would require high-level magic and rare components.
- **Magical Flight:** As the party gains access to spells like _fly_, _levitate_, or items that grant flight, they will be able to bypass the cliff face altogether. However, the dangers of the Mournland will still await them below.
- **Base at the Bottom:** The party might eventually establish a base of operations at the bottom of the cliff, creating a foothold in the Mournland.

**This initial descent of the cliff should be a memorable and challenging encounter, setting the tone for the dangers of the Mournland and emphasizing the importance of preparation, resourcefulness, and teamwork. It also provides a clear visual and symbolic marker of the party's entry into the unknown, a threshold they will cross many times as they delve deeper into the mysteries of the Mourning.**


# **The Cliff's Edge: Gateway to the Mournland**

**Location:** A few hours north of Salvation, marking the definitive border between the relatively safe areas around the town and the true Mournland.

**Visual Description:**

- **The Precipice:** The ground abruptly ends in a sheer drop, a 200-foot chasm that seems to descend into the very depths of the world. The rock face is jagged and uneven, composed of the strange, dark grey stone characteristic of the Mournland.
- **The Warning Sign:** A single, weathered wooden sign stands precariously close to the edge. Its inscription, crudely carved into the wood, reads: "If you value your lives, be somewhere else." The sign is old and worn, but the message is clear. It is slightly slanted, as if it is about to fall over the edge.
- **The Lone Piton:** About ten feet from the sign, a single, rusty piton is hammered into the rock face near the edge. A frayed and severed rope, about 30 feet long, dangles from it, disappearing into the mist below. The rope shows signs of having been cut cleanly, perhaps by a sharp blade or a powerful magical effect. The rope is made of a strange material, that the players will not recognise.
- **The Abyss:** The chasm is filled with a thick, swirling grey mist that obscures the bottom. The mist seems to writhe and move of its own accord, and faint, unsettling sounds – whispers, moans, or the distant grinding of stone – can be heard emanating from its depths.
- **The Sky:** The sky above is a dull, oppressive grey, mirroring the desolation below. The sun is barely visible, casting a weak, diffused light that does little to penetrate the gloom. There is a faint, but constant, electrical charge in the air.
- **Croydonite Veins:** Thin veins of black Croydonite are visible on the cliff face, especially further down, pulsing with a faint, inner light. They seem to be more prevalent on this side of the cliff.
- **No Signs of Life:** There are no signs of plants or animals near the cliff edge. The area is eerily silent, except for the sounds coming from the mist below.

**Atmosphere and Tone:**

- **Desolation:** The scene should convey a sense of utter desolation and abandonment. This is a place where life has been extinguished, replaced by something alien and unsettling.
- **Foreboding:** The atmosphere is thick with a sense of foreboding and dread. The players should feel like they are standing on the threshold of something terrible and unknown.
- **Mystery:** The severed rope, the warning sign, and the strange sounds from the mist all hint at a deeper mystery. What happened here? Who left the sign and the piton? What awaits those who dare to descend into the abyss?

**Encounter Elements:**

- **The Warning Sign:**
    - **Interaction:** The party can examine the sign, perhaps making a Wisdom (Survival) or Intelligence (Investigation) check to determine its age, origin, or any other details. They might also try to decipher any hidden meanings in the message.
    - **Symbolism:** The sign serves as a clear warning, a last chance to turn back before entering the true Mournland. It also reinforces the idea that the players are venturing into a place where even basic survival is not guaranteed.
- **The Lone Piton and Rope:**
    - **Investigation:** Examining the piton and rope could reveal clues about who placed them and what happened to them. A successful Intelligence (Investigation) check might determine the type of rope, its age, and the manner in which it was cut.
    - **The Rope's Material:** The rope is made from a material not commonly found in Khorvaire. It might be a type of magically treated fiber or a material from another plane. This could be a clue to the involvement of a specific faction, such as the Order of the Broken Sky.
    - **A Test of Resolve:** The dangling rope presents a clear choice to the players: will they dare to descend into the unknown, or will they heed the warning and seek another way into the Mournland?
- **The Mist:**
    - **Sensory Details:** Describe the mist's movement, its coldness, and the unsettling sounds that emanate from it. You can also use the color-coding system to give it a specific hue, hinting at the type of magic prevalent in the area below.
    - **Psychic Effects:** Spending too long near the edge of the cliff, gazing into the mist, could have minor psychic effects on the characters. They might experience fleeting visions, hear whispers, or feel a sense of dread and unease. This could be represented by Wisdom saving throws, with failures resulting in temporary penalties or conditions.
    - **Concealment:** The mist provides concealment, making it difficult to see what lies below or to spot any creatures that might be lurking within it.
- **The Cliff Face:**
    - **Climbing Challenge:** As detailed before, the cliff face presents a significant climbing challenge, with varying DCs depending on the section.
    - **Croydonite Veins:** The presence of Croydonite adds another layer of danger, forcing players to choose their path carefully and potentially exposing them to the material's harmful effects.

**Possible Developments:**

- **A Cry for Help:** As the party examines the cliff edge, they might hear a faint cry for help from the mist below, perhaps from a survivor of a previous expedition or a creature trapped on a ledge.
- **An Attack:** A creature from the Mournland might emerge from the mist and attack the party, testing their defenses and forcing them to make a quick decision about whether to fight or flee. This could be a flying creature, such as a mutated giant eagle, or something more sinister, like a Living Spell that drifts up from the depths.
- **A Vision:** One of the characters might experience a sudden vision, triggered by the psychic residue of the Mourning or the proximity to the entity beneath the Glass Plateau. This vision could offer a clue about the history of the Mournland, the nature of the Final Sanction, or the dangers that await them below.
- **The Order's Arrival:** A group of agents from the Order of the Broken Sky might arrive at the cliff edge, also investigating the area. This could lead to a tense standoff, a negotiation, or even a joint exploration of the depths.

**Rewards:**

- **Information:** Examining the sign, the rope, and the cliff face can provide valuable information about the Mournland and the dangers it holds.
- **Map:** If the party decides to chart the area, they can create a detailed map of the cliff edge, marking the location of the piton, any Croydonite veins, and any other notable features. This map will be valuable for future expeditions.
- **Experience:** Overcoming the challenges of the cliff face, dealing with any encounters, and gathering information should award the party with experience points.
- **Intangible Rewards:** The sheer act of facing their fears and venturing to the edge of the Mournland is a significant accomplishment. It marks a turning point in the campaign, a moment where the players commit to exploring the unknown and confronting the horrors that lie within.

**This encounter at the cliff edge is more than just a physical obstacle; it's a symbolic threshold. It's a test of the party's courage, their resourcefulness, and their willingness to face the unknown. The choices they make here will set the tone for their future explorations of the Mournland and shape their understanding of the dangers and mysteries that await them.**
