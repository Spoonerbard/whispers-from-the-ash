#rulesofplay 

**(Issued under the Authority of House Tharashk, by Order of the Esteemed Sheriff Karr)**

**Official Document - Do Not Fold, Spindle, or Mutilate (Seriously)**

**Article I: Grant of Limited and Probably Temporary Privileges**

By the power vested in House Tharashk (and begrudgingly acknowledged by everyone else within spitting distance of the Mournland), the bearer of this exceptionally important document (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensee") is hereby granted the dubious privilege of traipsing through the desolate, dangerous, and likely deadly wasteland known as the Mournland. This privilege is extended with the understanding that the Licensee is probably not going to find anything of value, but hey, who are we to stand in the way of misplaced optimism?

**Article II: Salvage Operations (aka Looting, But Let's Be Civilized)**

The Licensee is permitted to engage in "Salvage Operations," which is a fancy term for picking through the debris of a fallen nation and hoping to find something shiny that hasn't been melted, mutated, or otherwise rendered useless by the magical equivalent of a toddler's tantrum. Any and all items of even remotely questionable value "salvaged" (i.e., looted, pilfered, or otherwise yoinked) are subject to inspection, appraisal, and potential confiscation by duly authorized representatives of House Tharashk, who will determine a "fair" price, mostly for them, not you.

**Article III: Reporting Requirements (aka Don't Get Any Bright Ideas)**

The Licensee is obligated, under penalty of a disapproving glare from Sheriff Karr (and possibly worse), to report any "significant discoveries" to the Sheriff's office within twenty-four (24) hours. Significant discoveries include, but are not limited to: intact structures, functioning magical items, large deposits of anything that glows ominously, and anything that tries to eat you. Failure to report said discoveries may result in fines, imprisonment, or being forced to listen to Deputy Bron sing.

**Article IV: Fees and Compensation (aka We Get Paid, You Get to Pay)**

In exchange for this incredibly generous license, the Licensee agrees to pay a nominal fee of five (5) gold pieces per person. This fee is non-refundable, even if the Licensee's expedition ends prematurely due to, say, spontaneous combustion, being eaten by a creature that shouldn't exist, or realizing that adventuring is a terrible career choice. Furthermore, House Tharashk is entitled to a completely reasonable and not at all exorbitant seventy-five percent (75%) share of the estimated value of any and all salvaged goods. We're feeling generous, it's usually eighty.

**Article V: Liability (aka Not Our Fault)**

House Tharashk, the town of Salvation, Sheriff Karr, his deputies, their fruit-themed metal companions, and any other entity even remotely associated with this license are hereby absolved of any and all responsibility for the Licensee's safety, well-being, sanity, or continued existence. Venturing into the Mournland is inherently stupid, and the Licensee assumes all risks, including but not limited to: being dissolved, disintegrated, devoured, driven mad, turned into a newt, or subjected to any number of other unpleasant fates. You have been warned.

**Article VI: Code of Conduct (aka Try Not to Embarrass Us Too Much)**

The Licensee agrees to abide by the laws of Salvation, which are mostly made up on the spot by Sheriff Karr but are nonetheless enforced with extreme prejudice. Furthermore, the Licensee will endeavor to avoid causing any unnecessary trouble, blowing anything up (unless absolutely necessary), and generally refraining from activities that might reflect poorly on House Tharashk's already questionable reputation.

**Article VII: Duration of License (aka Don't Overstay Your Welcome)**

This license is valid for one (1) week, starting from the date of issue. After that one week, you will be required to return to Salvation to renew the license. This license may be revoked at any time for any reason, or no reason at all. Good luck. You'll need it.

**Article VIII: Arbitration** In the event of any dispute, the licensee agrees that any complaints will be dealt with by a panel of three judges. The first will be selected by House Tharashk, the second will be selected by House Tharashk, and the third will be selected by House Tharashk to ensure impartiality.

**Article IX: Amendments** House Tharashk reserves the right to amend this contract at any time, for any reason, especially if they think of a way to make more money off of you.
