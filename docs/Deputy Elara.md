**Deputy "Whisper" Elara**

- **Appearance:** A slight, hooded human woman who moves with an almost unnatural grace. Her face is often obscured by the shadows of her hood, and she speaks in a soft, almost inaudible voice. She carries a set of throwing knives strapped to her belt and a whip coiled at her hip.
- **Personality:** Quiet, observant, and deadly efficient, Elara is the most competent of the deputies, but also the most enigmatic. She rarely speaks unless spoken to, and her past is a mystery. She's fiercely loyal to Sheriff Karr, but her methods are often unorthodox.
- **Motivation:** To maintain order in [[npcs]] and protect the innocent, in her own way. She also has a personal vendetta against a particular criminal operating in [[the Mournland]] (a potential plot hook).
- **Secret:** Elara is a former assassin who fled to [[npcs]] seeking redemption. She's trying to leave her past behind, but her skills are still sharp, and she's not afraid to use them when necessary. She is also deaf, but is an expert lip reader.
- **Cannith Connection:** Elara is the [[Cannith]] double agent. She was planted in [[npcs]] to monitor House Tharashk's activities and gather intelligence on [[the Mournland]]. She uses her exceptional observation skills and her ability to move unnoticed to gather information, which she transmits to her Cannith handlers through coded messages left at predetermined drop points.
- **Quote:** "... (nods silently)" or "(in a barely audible whisper) Justice will be served."
