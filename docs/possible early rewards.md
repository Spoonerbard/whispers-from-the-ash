# warlock
**Option 1: Shadowfell Shroud**

- **Description:** A dark, ethereal cloak made of interwoven strands of shadow. It seems to ripple and shift even when there's no wind. When worn, it clings to the wearer like a second skin, almost becoming one with their shadow.
- **Type:** Wondrous Item, requires attunement by a Warlock.
- **Effect:**
    - **Shadow Armor:** While wearing the shroud, [[Gyda]]can use a bonus action to activate its magic. For 1 minute, her AC becomes 16 + her Charisma modifier, unless it is already higher. This effect recharges at dawn.
    - **Shadow Step:** Once per day, while in dim light or darkness, [[Gyda]]can use the shroud to cast the _misty step_ spell without expending a spell slot.
    - **Patron's Blessing/Curse:** The shroud is linked to Gyda's patron. When she activates its Shadow Armor ability, she gains temporary hit points equal to her Warlock level. However, she also suffers disadvantage on her next saving throw against an effect originating from her patron.
- **Upgrade Potential:**
    - **Tier 2:** The Shadow Armor AC bonus increases to 17 + Charisma modifier, and the temporary hit points gained increase to twice her Warlock level. The _misty step_ ability can be used twice per day.
    - **Tier 3:** [[Gyda]]a]]gains resistance to necrotic damage while wearing the shroud. The Shadow Armor now lasts for 10 minutes.
    - **Tier 4:** The shroud grants advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. The Shadow Armor AC bonus increases to 18 + Charisma modifier. [[Gyda]]can now cast _dimension door_ once per day using the shroud.
- **Flavor:** "Woven from the fabric of the Shadowfell itself, this cloak offers protection and a taste of the power that lies beyond."

**Option 2: Amulet of the Raven Queen**

- **Description:** A tarnished silver amulet depicting a raven in flight. It's cold to the touch and seems to absorb light from its surroundings. This could be a relic from her time with the cult or a gift from the warrior who spared her.
- **Type:** Wondrous Item, requires attunement by a Warlock.
- **Effect:**
    - **Raven's Protection:** While wearing the amulet, Gyda's AC increases by 1.
    - **One with Shadows:** Once per short rest, [[Gyda]]can use a bonus action to become partially incorporeal for 1 round. While in this state, she has resistance to all damage except force damage, and she can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. If she ends her turn inside an object, she takes 1d10 force damage and is shunted to the nearest unoccupied space. This would use her bonus action.
    - **Mark of the Raven Queen:** The amulet is marked with the symbol of the Raven Queen. This could attract the attention of her followers (both good and bad) or grant [[Gyda]]a connection to the goddess of death and fate.
- **Upgrade Potential:**
    - **Tier 2:** The AC bonus increases to +2. [[Gyda]]can use the One with Shadows ability twice per short rest.
    - **Tier 3:** [[Gyda]]gains advantage on death saving throws while wearing the amulet. Once per long rest, she can cast the _speak with dead_ spell without expending a spell slot, using the amulet as a focus.
    - **Tier 4:** The AC bonus increases to +3. While using the One with Shadows ability, [[Gyda]]becomes invisible until the start of her next turn.
- **Flavor:** "A token of the Raven Queen's favor, or perhaps a reminder of her watchful gaze."

**Option 3: Bracers of the Hex Warrior**

- **Description:** A pair of dark metal bracers etched with runes of binding and power. They hum faintly when worn and seem to draw strength from the wearer's own life force.
- **Type:** Wondrous Item, requires attunement by a Hexblade Warlock.
- **Effect:**
    - **Hex Warrior's Focus:** These bracers enhance Gyda's connection to her Hex Warrior feature. While wearing them, she can use her Charisma modifier instead of Strength or Dexterity for the attack and damage rolls of any weapon she touches, even if it doesn't have the finesse or thrown property. This effect lasts until she uses this feature again or until she removes the bracers. This would not stack with her Hex Warrior feature on weapons that she is proficient with.
    - **Bound by Shadows:** Once per day, as a bonus action, [[Gyda]]can expend a spell slot to gain a bonus to her AC equal to the level of the spell slot expended. This bonus lasts for 1 minute. She can not do this again until she has finished a long rest.
    - **Price of Power:** When [[Gyda]]uses the Bound by Shadows ability, she also takes necrotic damage equal to the level of the spell slot expended. This damage cannot be reduced or prevented.
- **Upgrade Potential:**
    - **Tier 2:** [[Gyda]]can use the Bound by Shadows ability twice per day. The necrotic damage taken is halved (rounded down).
    - **Tier 3:** The bracers grant resistance to necrotic damage. The bonus to AC from Bound by Shadows now equals the level of the spell slot expended + Gyda's proficiency bonus.
    - **Tier 4:** [[Gyda]]can use her Charisma modifier for any weapon she is proficient with, so long as she is wearing the bracers. She can use the Bound by Shadows ability three times per day.
- **Flavor:** "Forged in darkness, fueled by sacrifice, these bracers offer a path to power, but at a cost."

**Choosing the Right Item:**

- **Consider Gyda's Playstyle:** Does she prefer to be stealthy, elusive, or a more direct combatant?
- **Think About Her Patron:** Which item best reflects the nature of her patron and the type of power they offer?
- **Balance with Other Items:** Make sure the chosen item doesn't make her too powerful compared to the other party members, especially at early levels.
- **Story Integration:** How will the item be introduced into the campaign? Will it be a gift, a discovery, or something she starts with?
