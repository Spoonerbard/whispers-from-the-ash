# **Opening Monologue/Bulletin Announcement:**


**(The speaker, likely Sheriff Karr or another town official, addresses a gathered crowd in Salvation. Their voice is amplified, perhaps by a simple magical device or just by shouting from a raised platform.)**

"Alright, folks, settle down, settle down! Got an announcement, as I'm sure most of you have heard by now. A month. One month to the day since the mists pulled back. The dead-grey, the cursed shroud, the rot-lung air... whatever you want to call it, it's gone. Lifted like a widow's veil, and for the first time in a century, we can see clear to the edge of the Mournland."

**(A murmur rises from the crowd, a mix of excitement, fear, and disbelief.)**

"Now, I know what you're thinkin'. Some of you are thinkin' it's a blessing. A chance to reclaim what was lost. Some of you are thinkin' it's a curse, that nothin' good can come from that blighted land. And you're all probably right, in a way."

**(The speaker pauses, letting the words sink in.)**

"We all heard the stories. Heard 'em from our folks, who heard 'em from theirs. How the Mourning came. How Cyre... vanished. How the grey mist rolled out, chokin' the life from anything it touched. Man, woman, child... even the hardiest beasts. They said even the warforged, built of steel and stone, couldn't withstand it. Corroded 'em from the inside out, they did. Magic itself went haywire. You couldn't even scry on the place, lest the spell turn back on itself. A land swallowed whole, gone from the world."

**(He gestures towards the edge of town, where the Mournland begins.)**

"But now... now the mist is gone. Don't ask me why. Don't ask me how. Maybe the gods finally took pity on us. Maybe whatever dark magic birthed that cursed fog finally ran its course. Truth is, nobody knows. And that's what makes this so damned complicated."

**(The speaker's voice hardens.)**

"This ain't a call to charge in blind, folks. The Mournland ain't suddenly turned into a field of daisies. It's still a dangerous place. Twisted. Scarred. Things live out there... things you don't want to meet. And there's whispers of... other things. Treasures. Lost relics. Secrets best left buried, maybe. But secrets that some folks are mighty eager to dig up."

**(He lowers his voice, almost conspiratorially.)**

"That's why House Tharashk is offerin' good coin for information. For maps. For salvage. They're puttin' their faith, and their gold, in folks brave enough, or foolish enough, to venture out there. And they're not the only ones watching. Keep your eyes open, and you'll see there are others, with their own reasons for wanting a piece of the Mournland."

**(The speaker raises his voice again, addressing the crowd with renewed authority.)**

"So, here's the law in Salvation: You go out there, you do it at your own risk. Get yourself an adventuring license, report your findings, and stay within the bounds of the law. We ain't got the manpower to police every inch of that wasteland. But we will keep order here. This town is a foothold. A beacon. And we aim to keep it that way."

**(He pauses, looking out at the crowd, his gaze lingering for a moment on the party.)**

"The Mournland's open for business, folks. What you do with that... that's up to you. Just remember... some doors are best left closed. And some treasures come at a price you can't imagine."

**(The speaker steps back, signaling the end of the announcement. The crowd disperses, buzzing with a mixture of apprehension and excitement. The adventure begins.)**

**Notes:**

- **Tone:** The tone is a mix of caution, warning, and a hint of opportunity. It reflects the spaghetti western influence, with a tough, no-nonsense delivery.
- **Information:** The monologue provides essential background information about the Mourning, the mist, and the current situation.
- **Hooks:** It introduces several plot hooks: the dangers of the Mournland, the involvement of House Tharashk, the presence of other interested parties, and the potential for both treasure and trouble.
- **Call to Action:** It implicitly invites the players to explore the Mournland while also warning them of the risks.
- **Thematic Resonance:** The speech touches on themes of loss, greed, the unknown, and the choices that people make in the face of both danger and opportunity.
