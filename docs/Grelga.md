[[Salvation NPCs]]


**5. Grelga (Barkeep at [[the crooked tankard]] )**

- **Services:** Runs  [[the crooked tankard]] , the only tavern in Salvation. She serves drinks, food, and provides a place for the locals to gather and socialize. She is an expert at brewing and distilling alcohol. She is also skilled at cooking. She is secretly a skilled fighter.
- 
- **Personality:** Gruff but fair, Grelga is a shrewd businesswoman who knows how to handle herself in a rough town. She's seen her share of trouble and is not easily intimidated. She has a soft spot for underdogs and is fiercely protective of her establishment. She is known for her incredibly strong drinks. She is secretly very lonely.
- **Rumors & Information:**
    - Grelga is a good source of local gossip and rumors. She hears a lot from her patrons and is adept at separating fact from fiction.
    - She might know something about the Steel Stoners' activities or the various factions operating in the region.
    - She could have information about the previous adventuring party that went into the Ashen Wastes, as they likely stopped at her tavern before their ill-fated expedition.
- **Quest Hooks:**
    - **The Rowdy Patrons:** A group of Steel Stoners or other troublemakers are causing problems in  [[the crooked tankard]] . Grelga might ask the party to help her deal with them, either through diplomacy or force.
    - **The Special Brew:** Grelga needs a rare ingredient from the Mournland to create a special brew, perhaps for a local festival or to fulfill a special order. She might offer the party a discount or a unique item in exchange for their help. She might ask the party to help her create a new drink, using ingredients from the Mournland. This could be a dangerous, but potentially profitable, undertaking.
    - **The Debt:** Grelga owes money to a dangerous individual or faction (perhaps a loan shark in a nearby city or even a criminal organization operating in the Mournland). The party might be able to help her pay off the debt or negotiate a solution.
    
- **Secret:** Grelga is a former member of a mercenary company that operated in the area before the Mourning. She's a skilled fighter but has tried to put that life behind her. However, she might be forced to take up arms again if the town is threatened or if someone from her past comes looking for her. She is also secretly in love with Sheriff Karr, but she is too proud to admit it.
