**Sheriff Hardin "Hard-Eye" Karr**

- **Appearance:** A grizzled, imposing human with a weathered face, a steely gaze, and a handlebar mustache that could rival a walrus's. He's rarely seen without his wide-brimmed hat, worn leather duster, and a custom-made, repeating crossbow strapped to his back, a modified version of the one the players find the parts for in their first expedition. His House Tharashk badge, shaped like a stylized pickaxe, is prominently displayed on his chest. He is missing the little finger on his left hand.
- **Personality:** Sheriff Karr is a কঠোর but (mostly) fair lawman, dedicated to keeping order in [[npcs]]. He's a man of few words, but his কথার carry weight. He's seen his share of violence and is not afraid to use force when necessary. He's loyal to House Tharashk, but his primary concern is the safety of the town and its residents. He's also a stickler for rules and regulations, especially when it comes to adventurers.
- **Motivation:** Maintaining order in [[npcs]], protecting the town from the dangers of [[the Mournland]]]], upholding House Tharashk's interests, and keeping his deputies in line.
- **Secret:** Hard-Eye lost his little finger in a card game gone wrong many years ago. He's ashamed of the incident and keeps it hidden, always wearing gloves in public. He also has no time for music or singing of any kind. He will immediately shut down any establishment that has music, and has been known to arrest bards "disturbing the peace".
- **Quote:** "[[npcs]] ain't a town for the faint of heart. You got business here, best make sure it's legal. And keep your weapons holstered unless you intend to use 'em."
