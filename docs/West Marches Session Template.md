Okay, here's a session template designed for a West Marches campaign, specifically tailored to the setting and elements we've been developing. This template provides headings and prompts to help you plan each session, ensuring a good balance of exploration, narrative, and player agency.

**West Marches Session Template**

**I. Session Planning & Setup**

- **Date & Time:** (When the session is scheduled)
- **Players:** (Who is attending/confirmed?)
- **Recap:** (Brief summary of the previous session's events, focusing on unresolved issues, player goals, and important discoveries. Include a tailored recap for the players attending.)
- **Party Location:** (Where are the characters at the start of the session? In Salvation or a specific hex/location in the Mournland?)
- **Party Goals:** (What are the players' stated or implied goals for this session? What do they want to achieve?)
- **Review of Town Status:**
    - **Town Tier:** (Current level of development)
    - **NPCs Present:** (Note any significant NPCs who are in town and available for interaction)
    - **Rumors/News:** (Any new rumors, events, or developments in Salvation or the wider Mournland)
    - **Available Quests/Jobs:** (From the board, NPCs, or ongoing investigations)
- **Review of the Wider World:**
    - **Faction Activity:** (What are the major factions up to? Order of the Broken Sky, House Cannith, House Tharashk, the Steel Stoners, the Lord of Blades, etc.)
    - **Impending Events:** (Any planned events, such as storms, unusual occurrences in the Mournland, or actions by NPCs/factions, that might impact the players)

**II. Session Content**

- **Opening Scene:**
    - **Location:** (Where does the session begin?)
    - **Description:** (Brief flavor text to set the scene and mood)
    - **Hook:** (What immediately draws the players into the action or presents them with a choice?)
- **Exploration & Travel:**
    - **Destination:** (Which hex/location are the players heading towards?)
    - **Travel Time:** (How long will it take to get there, and back to a safe location?)
    - **Planned Encounters:** (Any specific encounters planned for the journey or the destination?)
    - **Random Encounters:** (Roll for random encounters during travel, tailored to the terrain and distance from Salvation)
    - **Environmental Hazards:** (Consider using the hazard tables we created earlier, based on location and weather)
    - **Resource Management:** (Track food, water, spell slots, and other limited resources. Apply encumbrance rules if using the variant.)
- **Location/Encounter Details:** (For each significant location or encounter)
    - **Description:** (Flavor text to describe the area, atmosphere, and any notable features)
    - **Inhabitants/Creatures:** (Who or what is present?)
    - **Challenges:** (Obstacles, puzzles, traps, or social encounters)
    - **Rewards:** (Treasure, information, experience points, reputation gains)
    - **Secrets:** (Hidden details, clues, or connections to the wider plot)
    - **Possible Complications:** (Unexpected events or consequences that might arise)
- **Social Interactions:**
    - **NPCs:** (Which NPCs might the party encounter and interact with?)
    - **Information Gathering:** (Opportunities to learn rumors, gather information, or gain insights from NPCs)
    - **Trading:** (Opportunities to buy, sell, or trade goods and services)
    - **Faction Interaction:** (Potential interactions with members of the Order of the Broken Sky, House Cannith, House Tharashk, the Steel Stoners, or other factions)
- **Downtime Activities (if applicable):**
    - **Crafting:** (Can any players craft or upgrade items with Troxler or other artisans?)
    - **Research:** (Can any players conduct research in the town's library or using their own resources?)
    - **Training:** (Can any players train to improve their skills or learn new abilities?)
    - **Base Improvement:** (Can any players work on upgrading the party's base?)
    - **Other:** (Any other downtime activities relevant to the campaign, such as carousing, performing, or pursuing personal goals)

**III. Session Wrap-Up**

- **Experience Points:** (Award XP for encounters, discoveries, roleplaying, and achieving goals)
- **Treasure & Rewards:** (Distribute any treasure, items, or other rewards earned during the session)
- **Consequences:** (Determine any consequences of the party's actions, both positive and negative)
- **Update Town Status:** (Reflect any changes to Salvation based on the party's actions, new discoveries, or the passage of time)
- **Update Faction Status:** (How have the party's actions affected the various factions? Have they gained or lost reputation? Have they made new allies or enemies?)
- **Cliffhanger/Preview:** (End the session with a cliffhanger or a preview of what might happen next, setting the stage for the following session)
- **Session Feedback:** (Take note of what worked well, what could be improved, and any player feedback that might be useful for future sessions)
- **Log:** Briefly log what happened during the session in your chosen way, for example, a shared document or a campaign log.

**Example Session Outline:**

**Session 3: The Ashen Wastes**

**I. Session Planning & Setup**

- **Date & Time:** 2023-12-28, 7:00 PM
- **Players:** Sarah, John, Ben, Emily
- **Recap:** The party arrived in Salvation, met Sheriff Karr, got their adventuring licenses, and heard about the dangers of the Mournland. They learned about the three nearby areas: the Ashen Wastes, the Jagged Peaks/Silent Grove, and the Stillwater. They decided to investigate the Ashen Wastes, drawn by the story of the failed expedition. They have also secured their base of operations at Shack E.
- **Party Location:** Salvation
- **Party Goals:** Explore the Ashen Wastes, find out what happened to the previous adventuring party, and map the area.
- **Review of Town Status:**
    - **Town Tier:** 1 (Outpost)
    - **NPCs Present:** Sheriff Karr, Deputies Dal, Bron, and Elara, "Old Man" Silas, Mara "Fingers" Keen, "Mama" Elara Thorne, Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight,  [[Grelga]] .
    - **Rumors/News:** Rumors of increased Steel Stoner activity near the mountains.
    - **Available Quests/Jobs:** The Sheriff wants the [[Croydonite]] smugglers found, Sister Arya mentioned a need for specific herbs found in the Mournland for a special healing concoction.
- **Review of the Wider World:**
    - **Faction Activity:** The Order of the Broken Sky is rumored to be sending a team to investigate the Sky Song Tavern. House Cannith is গোপনে monitoring House Tharashk's activities in Salvation.
    - **Impending Events:** A sandstorm is brewing in the Ashen Wastes, predicted to hit within the next few days.

**II. Session Content**

- **Opening Scene:** The party prepares to leave Salvation, gathering supplies and information. They might talk to Anya "Swiftbow" to get more details about the Ashen Wastes. They are approached by Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight, who asks them to collect some uncontaminated soil samples for him while they are out.
- **Exploration & Travel:**
    - **Destination:** The Ashen Wastes.
    - **Travel Time:** Half a day's journey to the edge of the desert.
    - **Planned Encounters:** None planned for the journey.
    - **Random Encounters:** Roll for random encounters on the way to the Ashen Wastes.
    - **Environmental Hazards:** Heat, dehydration, potential sinkholes. The approaching sandstorm could also be a factor.
    - **Resource Management:** Track water consumption carefully.
- **Location/Encounter Details:**
    - **The Ashen Wastes:**
        - **Description:** A desolate expanse of black sand, with the occasional withered plant or animal carcass. The air shimmers with heat, and the silence is unsettling.
        - **Inhabitants/Creatures:** Ashworms or Sand Stalkers (as described in the previous "areas surrounding Salvation" response).
        - **Challenges:** Navigating the dunes, avoiding sinkholes, dealing with the heat and dehydration, and surviving encounters with the creatures.
        - **Rewards:** Map of the area, salvage from the previous party, potentially rare minerals or [[Croydonite]] deposits, information about the creatures' behavior and weaknesses.
        - **Secrets:** The black sand is infused with necromantic energy. The creatures are attracted to movement and sound.
        - **Possible Complications:** The sandstorm arrives earlier than expected, reducing visibility and making travel even more dangerous. The party might discover a hidden cave or a buried structure, but it's guarded by a particularly large or powerful creature. They might also run into another adventuring party, or treasure hunters, who are less than friendly.
    - **The Remains of the Previous Party:**
        - **Description:** The party finds the remains of the previous adventuring party, scattered across a small area of the Ashen Wastes. It's clear they were ambushed and overwhelmed.
        - **Inhabitants/Creatures:** None, but signs of a struggle are evident.
        - **Challenges:** Piecing together what happened, dealing with any traps or hazards that might have been triggered by the previous party.
        - **Rewards:** Salvageable equipment, personal effects that might offer clues about the party's mission or their destination, a partial map, a journal.
        - **Secrets:** One of the dead adventurers might be clutching a small, strangely shaped piece of metal – a component for a modular magic item.
        - **Possible Complications:** The area might be unstable, with a sinkhole about to open. The creatures that killed the previous party might return.
- **Social Interactions:**
    - **Anya "Swiftbow":** If the party talks to her before leaving, she might offer some fragmented advice or warnings, though she's still traumatized.
    - **"Old Man" Silas:** Might offer to buy any rare minerals or salvage the party finds.
    - **Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight:** He will ask for soil samples, and reward the party.

**III. Session Wrap-Up**

- **Experience Points:** Award XP for exploration, encounters, roleplaying, and discovering the fate of the previous party.
- **Treasure & Rewards:** The party might find salvage, minerals, a partial map, and clues about the creatures in the Ashen Wastes.
- **Consequences:** The party's actions might attract the attention of the creatures in the Ashen Wastes, making future travel more dangerous. They might also have used up valuable resources, forcing them to resupply in Salvation.
- **Update Town Status:** The party's report about the Ashen Wastes might influence the town's perception of the Mournland. News of their discoveries might attract other adventurers or traders.
- **Update Faction Status:** If the party encountered any factions, their actions might have affected their relationship with them.
- **Cliffhanger/Preview:** The session could end with the party discovering a hidden cave or structure in the Ashen Wastes, hearing a rumor about the Steel Stoners' activities, or receiving a message from the Order of the Broken Sky. They might also make it back to town as the sandstorm hits.
- **Session Feedback:** Note what worked well, what the players enjoyed, and any areas that need improvement.
- **Log:** Briefly log what happened during the session.

This template and example should give you a solid framework for planning your West Marches sessions. The most important thing is to create a fun and engaging experience for your players, where their choices matter and their actions have consequences.
