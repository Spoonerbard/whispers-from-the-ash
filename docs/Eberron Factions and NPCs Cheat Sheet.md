

# **Major Factions**

- **The  [[dragonmarked houses]] :** Powerful families with hereditary magical abilities, each specializing in a specific area like transportation, healing, or security.
    
    - **[[House Cannith]]:** Forgewrights, creators of magic items and warforged.
    - **[[House Deneith]]:** Guardians and mercenaries, specializing in martial prowess.
    - **[[House Jorasco]]:** Healers and researchers, focused on medicine and magical healing.
    - **[[House Kundarak]]:** Bankers and locksmiths, known for their security and vaults.
    - **[[House Lyrandar]]:** Shipwrights and explorers, specializing in airships and seafaring.
    - **[[House Medani]]:** Spies and information brokers, masters of disguise and infiltration.
    - **[[House Orien]]:** Messengers and transporters, specializing in lightning rails and rapid communication.
    - **[[House Phiarlan]]:** Entertainers and diplomats, known for their artistry and charm.
    - **[[House Sivis]]:** Scribes and message senders, specializing in communication and magical messages.
    - **[[House Tharashk]]:** Finders and prospectors, specializing in tracking and uncovering secrets.
- **The Nations of [[Khorvaire]]:** The five major nations that emerged after the Last War.
    
    - **[[Aundair]]:** A nation of mages and scholars, known for its arcane traditions and floating towers.
    - **[[Breland]]:** A nation of industry and innovation, known for its bustling cities and technological advancements.
    - **[[Karrnath]]:** A nation of militant undead and necromancers, ruled by a king obsessed with immortality.
    - **[[Thrane]]:** A theocratic nation devoted to the Silver Flame, known for its paladins and religious fervor.
    - **[[the Mournland]]:** A blighted wasteland created by a magical catastrophe, filled with dangerous creatures and warped landscapes.
- **Other Factions:**
    
    - **[[The Lord of Blades]]:** A warforged leader seeking to create a nation for his kind.
    - **[[The Emerald Claw]]:** A shadowy organization seeking to restore the ancient Dhakaani Empire.
    - **[[The Cults of the Dragon Below]]:** Malevolent cults worshipping the monstrous entities trapped beneath Eberron.
    - **The Chamber:** A secretive organization manipulating events from the shadows.

**Notable NPCs**

- **[[King Boranel ir'Wynarn]]:** The ruler of Breland, a progressive leader seeking to rebuild after the Last War.
- **[[Queen Aurala ir'Wynarn]]:** The ruler of Aundair, a powerful mage and political strategist.
- **[[Kaius ir'Karrnath]]:** The undead king of Karrnath, obsessed with immortality and expanding his kingdom.
- **[[Jaela Daran]]:** The Keeper of the Flame, the spiritual leader of Thrane and a powerful paladin.
- **[[Merrix d'Cannith]]:** The leader of House Cannith, a brilliant inventor and master of artifice.
- **[[Baron Merrix d'Orien]]:** The leader of House Orien, a shrewd businessman and transportation magnate.
