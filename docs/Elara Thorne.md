**3. "Mama" Elara Thorne (Landlady of the Party's Base)**

- **Services:** Provides the party with their initial base of operations (Shack E), a place to rest, store their gear, and plan their expeditions. She also offers home-cooked meals (of variable quality) and a listening ear. She will offer advice to those she trusts.
- **Personality:** Tough, resourceful, and fiercely independent. She's seen her share of hardship and is not easily intimidated. She has a soft spot for those who are struggling and is willing to help those in need, she has a particular fondness for children.
- **Rumors & Information:**
    - She knows the history of Salvation better than most, having lived there for many years. She might share stories about the town's past, the early days after the Mourning, and the various groups that have tried to make a life in the wasteland.
    - She's heard rumors about the different areas surrounding Salvation and might offer warnings about the dangers the party might encounter.
    - She might know something about the previous tenants of Shack E and their fate.
- **Quest Hooks:**
    - **The Repairs:** Elara needs help repairing or upgrading the party's shack. This could involve finding specific materials in the Mournland, dealing with local wildlife that has taken up residence in the shack, or even defending the shack from an attack.
    - **The Lost Keepsake:** Elara lost a valuable keepsake in the Mournland years ago during an ill-fated expedition. She might ask the party to retrieve it, offering them a reward and a glimpse into her past. This could be an item that belonged to her deceased partner or child.
    - **The Threat:** A group of bandits or a dangerous creature threatens Elara or her property. The party might have to defend her, track down the threat, or negotiate a solution.
- **Secret:** She's a retired adventurer, a former member of a mercenary company that operated in the region before the Mourning. She's skilled with a crossbow and has a hidden stash of weapons and supplies. Her past might catch up with her, creating new challenges for the party. She might be convinced to share her knowledge of combat with the party if they gain her trust.
