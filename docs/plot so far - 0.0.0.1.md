**I. [[the Mournland]]] and its Mysteries:**

- **The Mourning:** The central catastrophic event that destroyed a nation and created the twisted wasteland. Its exact cause and nature are unknown, a mystery for the players to unravel.
- **Croydonite:** This dangerous, magically unstable material is a byproduct of the Mourning. It holds the shattered soul fragments of those who died, making it both powerful and ethically problematic.
    - **The Secret of the Souls:** The players are likely to discover the horrifying truth about Croydonite's origins, forcing them to grapple with the moral implications of its use.
    - **Source of Power:** [[Croydonite]] is a potential source of great power, attracting various factions and individuals.
- **Environmental Hazards:** [[the Mournland]] is filled with bizarre and deadly environmental hazards, making exploration dangerous and unpredictable. These hazards hint at the warped nature of magic in this land.
- **Ruins and Remnants:** The ruins of the lost nation offer glimpses into the past and hold valuable artifacts, knowledge, and resources.

**II. Sanctuary and its Growth:**

- **A Beacon of Hope:** The town of Sanctuary is a fragile haven in the wasteland, slowly growing and evolving based on the players' actions.
- **[[Troxler]]'s Role:** The rookie artificer, [[Troxler]], is a key NPC. They can craft and upgrade magic items using materials found in [[the Mournland]], including the ethically controversial Croydonite.
    - **[[Troxler]]'s Development:** [[Troxler]]'s growing expertise mirrors the players' progress and the town's development, creating a symbiotic relationship.
    - **Moral Choices:** [[Troxler]]'s involvement with [[Croydonite]] will likely force them (and the players) to confront the ethical dilemmas surrounding its use.
- **Player Base:** The party's base in Sanctuary evolves from a simple shack to a powerful stronghold, reflecting their growing influence and providing a tangible symbol of their progress.
- **Economic Engine:** The players' exploration and the resources they bring back fuel Sanctuary's economy and attract new settlers, artisans, and traders.

- **West Marches Style:** The core of the campaign is player-driven exploration and discovery. The players choose where to go, what to investigate, and how to interact with the world.
- **Exploration and Discovery:** The hex crawl format encourages players to explore [[the Mournland]], uncovering its secrets, encountering its dangers, and finding valuable resources.
- **Moral Choices:** The use of [[Croydonite]] and the potential to exploit the shattered souls within it presents players with difficult ethical decisions.
- **Character-Specific Goals:** Each player character has a unique class-specific magic item with limited uses, potentially hinting at personal goals or motivations tied to their class.

- **Unraveling the Mystery of the Mourning:** The players could investigate the cause of the Mourning, seeking to understand what happened and potentially find a way to reverse or mitigate its effects.
- **The Fate of the Shattered Souls:** The players might embark on a quest to free the souls trapped within Croydonite, potentially requiring them to find a powerful artifact, develop a new ritual, or confront a powerful entity connected to the Mourning.
- **The Rise of Sanctuary:** The players could focus on building up Sanctuary, transforming it into a major power in the region and potentially even a new nation.

- **Exploiting or Rejecting Croydonite:** The players will have to decide whether to use [[Croydonite]] for its power, despite its dark origins, or to find alternative solutions, potentially leading to conflict with those who embrace its use.
