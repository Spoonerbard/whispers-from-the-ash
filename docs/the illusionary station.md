#fantasticlocations/mournland 


1. **[[The Ashen Wastes]]:** (Small Desert) with the black sand, the [[Ashworms]]/Sand Stalkers, and the story of the failed expedition. This is where they will encounter the necromantic magic, and is closest to the area where the portal was ripped open.
2. **The Jagged Peaks & The Silent Grove:** (Rocky Mountains & Dead Forest) with the treacherous terrain, mutated wildlife, the eerie silence of the petrified forest, and the ruins of the **Sky Song Tavern** at the edge of the grove. This is where the party will encounter chronomancy magic.
3. **[[The Stillwater]]:** (Lake) with the contaminated water, the Lurker, and the potential for underwater exploration. This is where the party will encounter conjuration and transmutation magic.

The one we haven't detailed yet is a location themed around, and this will be a great place to introduce the players to the last two schools of magic:

4. **The Illusionary Station:** (Ruined City/Ruins)

**Description:**

- This location is a relatively small area to the south of Salvation, perhaps a ruined outpost, a small village, or a district on the outskirts of a larger city that was swallowed by the Mournland. It is a place where the boundaries between reality and illusion have become blurred, remnants of powerful illusion magic lingering in the air.
- **Visuals:** The area is characterized by shimmering mirages, buildings that appear intact from a distance but crumble into dust upon closer inspection, and phantom sounds that echo from nowhere. The architecture is distinctly different from the rest of the ruins found so far, hinting at a different culture or time period.
- **Colors:** This is a great place to introduce the **green** (illusion) and **purple** (divination) color-coding for schools of magic. The area might be shrouded in a perpetual green mist, or objects might shimmer with a strange, ethereal purple light.

**Dangers:**

- **Illusory Terrain:** The ground might appear solid but give way to reveal a pit or a chasm. Paths might lead to dead ends or loop back on themselves. The party will need to use their wits and perception to navigate the area safely. This could be represented by DC 10 Intelligence saving throws, on a failure the creature is subjected to the _confusion_ spell until the start of their next turn.
- **Phantom Guardians:** The area could be guarded by illusory creatures – phantom soldiers, spectral beasts, or even illusory duplicates of the party members themselves. These illusions might be purely visual, or they might be able to interact with the physical world to a limited extent (e.g., dealing psychic damage or causing the frightened condition). These creatures have 5 hit points and are immune to non-magical damage.
- **Sensory Overload:** The constant barrage of illusions could overwhelm the characters' senses, causing headaches, nausea, or even madness. This could be represented by Constitution saving throws, with failures leading to temporary penalties to skills or even levels of exhaustion.
- **The Source:** At the heart of the area lies the source of the illusions – perhaps a damaged artifact, a malfunctioning magical device, or the lingering spirit of a powerful illusionist. This could be a challenging encounter, requiring the party to dispel the illusions or find a way to shut down the source.

**Possible Encounters:**

- **The Illusory Patrol:** The party encounters a patrol of phantom soldiers, remnants of a pre-Mourning military unit. The soldiers might be hostile, or they might simply be re-enacting their last patrol, oblivious to the party's presence.
- **The Mirror Maze:** The party enters a ruined building that has become a maze of mirrors, reflecting and distorting their images. They must navigate the maze while dealing with illusory duplicates of themselves or other creatures.
- **The Tempting Mirage:** The party sees a vision of a lush oasis or a welcoming tavern, a powerful illusion designed to lure them into a trap.
- **The Guardian of Secrets:** The source of the illusions is guarded by a powerful creature, such as a **Shadow** or a **Will-o'-Wisp**, that has been warped by the illusion magic.

**Rewards:**

- **Divination Artifact:** The source of the illusions might be a damaged artifact that can still be used for divination magic, though its visions might be fragmented or unreliable.
- **Illusionist's Spellbook:** The party might find the spellbook of a powerful illusionist who once lived in the area, containing rare or unique illusion spells.
- **Knowledge:** By studying the illusions and their source, the party can learn more about the history of the area, the types of magic used before the Mourning, and potentially gain insights into the nature of the Mourning itself.
- **Components:** The area might be a source of rare components for illusion or divination spells, such as shimmering dust, phantom energy, or pieces of the illusory constructs.
- **Map:** The party can create a map that distinguishes between illusory and real objects and pathways.

**Connections to the Wider Plot:**

- **The Order of the Broken Sky:** This area could be of particular interest to the Order, as it represents a concentration of pre-Mourning magic. They might be searching for the source of the illusions, hoping to learn more about the types of magic used in Cyre before the catastrophe. This is a good place for the party to encounter agents of the Order, such as Kaelen "The Raven" Rhen.
- **House Phiarlan/Thuranni:** The  houses might also be interested in this area, as it could be a source of powerful illusions that they could use for espionage or entertainment. It could also be revealed that this area was once a stronghold for the house, and they are now trying to recover lost assets.
- **The Warrior:** The warrior who spared [[Gyda]]might have passed through this area during his travels, leaving behind clues or warnings about its dangers.

**The Illusory ruins is an excellent addition to your roster of starting locations. It provides a different kind of challenge, emphasizing perception, investigation, and the ability to discern reality from illusion. It also introduces the players to the green and purple color-coding, further expanding their understanding of the Mournland's magical corruption.**
