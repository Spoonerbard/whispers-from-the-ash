**Troxler's Emporium: Tier 2 Wares**

**Note:** Prices are significantly inflated due to Troxler's advanced skill and the rarity of materials in Salvation.

**New Items:**

1. **Mourning-Touched Weapon Infusions (Tier 2):**
    
    - **Description:** Troxler has developed a way to infuse weapons with a small amount of refined Croydonite, granting them temporary magical properties. These infusions are unstable and last for only a short time.
    - **Types:**
        - **Necrotic Infusion (Yellow):** Adds 1d6 necrotic damage to the weapon's attacks. (Cost: 150 gp)
        - **Time-Touched Infusion (Black):** The weapon is considered magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage1 for the duration. If the weapon hits, the target must make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target's speed is halved until the end of its next turn. (Cost: 200 gp)
        - **Illusionary Infusion (Green):** The weapon is covered in illusion magic, making it difficult to track. The weapon's attacks have advantage. (Cost: 250 gp)
        - **Divination Infusion (Purple):** The weapon is infused with divination magic, the first attack made with the weapon each turn has advantage. (Cost: 250 gp)
    - **Duration:** The infusion lasts for 1 minute or until the weapon has made 3 successful attacks, whichever comes first.
    - **Risk:** There's a 10% chance (roll a d100, 1-10) that the infusion backfires, dealing 1d6 damage of the chosen type to the wielder.
    - **Price:** Varies depending on the infusion type (see above).
2. **Elemental Grenades (Tier 2):**
    
    - **Description:** Small, unstable spheres filled with volatile energy, crafted using refined Croydonite and other rare materials. They are essentially single-use, thrown weapons.
    - **Types:**
        - **Fire Grenade:** Explodes in a burst of fire. Deals 2d6 fire damage to all creatures within a 5-foot radius (Dexterity saving throw DC 13 for half damage).
        - **Acid Grenade:** Explodes in a splash of acid. Deals 2d6 acid damage to all creatures within a 5-foot radius (Dexterity saving throw DC 13 for half damage).
        - **Lightning Grenade:** Releases a jolt of lightning. Deals 2d6 lightning damage to all creatures within a 5-foot radius (Dexterity saving throw DC 13 for half damage).
        - **Time Grenade:** Briefly distorts time in a 5-foot radius. All creatures within the area must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, they are slowed, as per the _slow_ spell, for 1 round.
    - **Risk:** There's a 5% chance (roll a d100, 1-5) that a grenade is a dud, or a 5% chance it explodes prematurely in the user's hand.
    - **Price:** 200 gp each.
3. **Enhanced Iron Defender Component (Tier 2):**
    
    - **Description:** Troxler has managed to create a component that can be used to enhance an Iron Defender, giving it a special ability. The component must be installed by Troxler, which takes one hour.
    - **Types:**
        - **Acidic Core:** The Iron Defender's bite attack deals an additional 1d4 acid damage. The Iron Defender gains resistance to acid damage.
        - **Flaming Core:** The Iron Defender's body radiates heat. Any creature that touches the Iron Defender or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 1d4 fire damage. The Iron Defender gains resistance to fire damage.
        - **Shadow Core:** The Iron Defender can cast the _darkness_ spell once per day, centered on itself. The Iron Defender gains resistance to necrotic damage.
        - **Shocking Core:** When the Iron Defender takes the Dash action, it can move up to its speed and make one bite attack as a bonus action. This attack deals an additional 1d4 lightning damage. The Iron Defender gains resistance to lightning damage.
    - **Risk:** There's a 10% chance (roll a d100, 1-10) that the installation process fails, damaging the Iron Defender (1d10 force damage).
    - **Price:** 300 gp per component.
4. **Mournland-Proof Flask (Tier 2):**
    
    - **Description:** A specially designed container, crafted by Dr. Vex and improved by Troxler. It's a sturdy, lead-lined flask with intricate runes etched on its surface, designed to contain hazardous substances from the Mournland.
    - **Effect:** The flask can safely hold liquids and materials from the Mournland, preventing them from leaking or interacting with the outside environment. It can also be used to store a small amount of a substance, which can then be extracted without opening the flask.
    - **Price:** 250 gp
5. **Tinker's Goggles (Tier 2):**
    
    - **Description:** A pair of goggles with adjustable lenses and intricate gears.
    - **Effect:** While wearing these goggles, the user has advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks made to examine objects and they can see in dim light as if it were bright light. They also provide +1 bonus to all crafting checks.
    - **Price:** 200 gp

**Other Items:** Troxler might also have a few **Tier 1** items that they have created, for a lower price. These could be less powerful versions of their current inventions.

**Plot Hooks:**

- **Testing, Testing:** Troxler might offer the party a discount on their wares if they agree to field-test them in the Mournland and provide feedback on their performance. This could lead to some unexpected and potentially dangerous situations.
- **The Missing Ingredient:** Troxler needs a rare component from a specific location in the Mournland to complete a new invention. They offer to share the invention with the party if they retrieve the ingredient for them.
- **The Sabotage:** A rival artisan (perhaps an agent of House Cannith) tries to sabotage Troxler's work, either by stealing their tools, destroying their supplies, or spreading rumors about the dangers of their inventions. The party might be hired to protect Troxler or to investigate the sabotage.
- **The Breakthrough:** Troxler makes a major breakthrough in their research, creating a truly powerful (and potentially unstable) item. They might offer it to the party, or they might decide to keep it for themselves, leading to a moral dilemma for the players.

**Roleplaying Troxler:**

- **Enthusiastic:** Troxler should be genuinely excited about their creations, eager to show them off and explain how they work (even if the party doesn't fully understand).
- **Reckless:** They should also be somewhat reckless and overconfident, downplaying the risks involved in using their inventions.
- **Secretive:** Troxler is still hiding their true motives and their connection to a shadowy organization. They might drop hints about their past or their research, but they will never reveal the whole truth.

**Remember**: These are just examples, and you can adjust the prices, effects, and availability of items based on your party's level, their resources, and the overall balance of your campaign. The important thing is to make Troxler's wares tempting and unique, encouraging players to spend their gold and to take risks in the pursuit of power and innovation. Their inventions should reflect the dangerous and unpredictable nature of the Mournland, offering both great potential and significant risks.
