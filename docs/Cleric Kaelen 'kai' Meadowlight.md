# **Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight (Town Cleric)**

- **Race:** Centaur (an exotic race for a town cleric, visually striking and memorable)
- **Class:** Cleric (Life Domain)
- **Appearance:** Kai is a young centaur with a chestnut brown equine body and a lean, muscular human torso. His human half has warm brown skin and a kind face framed by long, braided, light brown hair adorned with feathers and beads. He wears simple, homespun clothes and a holy symbol of **Arawai**, the Sovereign of Life and Love, a deity of the Sovereign Host often associated with nature, healing, and community. He has a calm and gentle demeanor, but his eyes reflect a deep-seated fear. He has a noticeable limp, and his lower left leg is wrapped in bandages.
- **Personality:** Kai is a gentle and compassionate soul, dedicated to helping those in need. He is soft-spoken and a good listener, always willing to offer a kind word or a healing touch. However, he is also timid and inexperienced, especially when it comes to the horrors of the Mournland. He doubts his own abilities and is easily overwhelmed by severe injuries or magical afflictions.
- **Role:** Serves as the town's primary healer, tending to the sick and injured in Salvation. He has a small, makeshift clinic set up in a tent or a spare room in the inn. He is skilled in basic healing magic and herbal remedies but lacks the experience and power to deal with serious wounds, magical diseases, or the more horrific effects of [[Croydonite]] exposure.
- **Motivation:** To ease the suffering of others and bring hope to the people of Salvation. He believes that everyone deserves compassion and a chance to heal, no matter their past. He also wants to become more confident and to overcome his fears.
- **Secret:** Kai is not originally from Eberron. He arrived in Salvation through a **manifest zone** connected to **Thelanis, the Feywild**, that appeared briefly a few years ago. He was fleeing from something in his homeland (perhaps a powerful archfey, a political conflict, or a natural disaster). The journey through the manifest zone was traumatic, and he was badly injured, resulting in his limp. He is terrified of the Mournland and has vowed never to leave the safety of Salvation.
- **The Limp:** Kai's limp is not just a physical injury. It's a psychosomatic manifestation of his fear and trauma. The injury was healed long ago by his own magic, but the limp remains, a constant reminder of his ordeal. This is why he can't help with more serious conditions, he is afraid to use more powerful magic, afraid that if he does, whatever he is running from will find him.
- **Leveling Up:** Kai can still gain experience and improve his abilities, but his growth will be focused on expanding his healing repertoire and gaining confidence within the confines of Salvation. He might learn new spells, develop new herbal remedies, or even establish a small garden to grow medicinal plants. He will never gain spells more powerful than 3rd level spells, due to his fear.
- **Relationship with the Party:** Kai can become a valuable friend and ally to the party. He will readily offer them healing and support, and he might even provide them with information or advice based on his knowledge of nature and his limited understanding of the Mournland. He will be particularly drawn to the druid character, seeing in them a connection to the natural world he longs for. He might also be wary of the warlock, sensing the darkness within her, but he will ultimately be guided by his compassion.
- **Quote:** "Even in the darkest of times, a little kindness can go a long way. Let me see what I can do."

**Using Kai in the Campaign:**

- **Source of Healing:** Kai provides a reliable source of basic healing for the party, especially in the early stages of the campaign.
- **Moral Support:** He can offer the party a sympathetic ear and a reminder of the importance of compassion and hope in the face of adversity.
- **Quest Hooks:**
    - **The Missing Ingredient:** Kai needs a rare herb that only grows in a specific part of the Mournland to create a more potent healing potion.
    - **The Lost Caravan:** A group of travelers carrying medical supplies for Salvation has gone missing. Kai asks the party to find them.
    - **The Corrupted Well:** The town's well becomes contaminated with Croydonite. Kai needs the party's help to find a way to purify it before the townspeople get sick.
    - **The Plea for Help:** Kai receives a cryptic message from his homeland, hinting that the danger he fled from might be spreading.
- **Character Development:** The party's actions could help Kai overcome his fears and become a more confident healer. They might even help him find a way to return to his homeland or to reconcile with his past.
- **The Manifest Zone:** The presence of a centaur in Salvation could be a clue to the existence of a nearby manifest zone to Thelanis. This could lead to further adventures or complications, as creatures or individuals from the Feywild might start appearing in the area. The manifest zone might even be the source of the Croydonite, and the reason why this area was used for the ritual.


# **4. Kaelen "Kai" Meadowlight (Town Cleric)**

- **Services:** Offers healing magic and basic medical care to the residents of Salvation. He can also provide information about local flora and fauna, as well as spiritual guidance. He is also skilled in creating potions and herbal remedies. He can also identify poisons.
- **Personality:** Gentle, compassionate, but timid and inexperienced. He is devoted to his deity, Arawai, and strives to help those in need. He is also deeply afraid of the Mournland. He prefers to avoid conflict.
- **Rumors & Information:**
    - He might have heard stories about the Stillwater and the creature that lurks within it.
    - He might know about the properties of certain plants and herbs found in the Mournland, including their potential uses and dangers.
    - He could share information about his homeland in the Feywild, if the party earns his trust.
- **Quest Hooks:**
    - **The Missing Herbs:** Kai needs specific herbs from the Mournland to create a healing potion or antitoxin. These herbs might only grow in dangerous locations, such as near Croydonite deposits or in areas inhabited by hostile creatures. He might ask the party to gather them for him.
    - **The Sick Settlers:** A group of settlers has fallen ill after drinking contaminated water or encountering a strange creature in the Mournland. Kai needs help diagnosing and treating the illness, which might require the party to find a cure or capture a creature for study. He will ask for the creature to be captured alive, so that he may study it.
    - **The Lost Shrine:** Kai has heard rumors of a hidden shrine to Arawai located somewhere in the Mournland. He might ask the party to find it, hoping that it can be restored as a place of healing and hope.
- **Secret:** He fled his home in Thelanis through a manifest zone that appeared a few years ago. He is terrified of the Mournland and has vowed never to leave Salvation. He also has a deep fear of his powers, which he believes are the reason he was forced to flee his homeland.
