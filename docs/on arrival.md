**Scene Setup:**

The party arrives in Salvation. Karr eyes them up and down, his hand resting on the butt of his custom crossbow, Lemon whirrs and clicks softly by his side. The office is sparsely furnished, with a কাঠের desk, a few chairs, and wanted posters on the walls. A map of the surrounding area, marked with various symbols and notes, hangs behind the desk. Sheriff Karr sits behind his desk, while the deputy stands near the door, observing the party. Lemon sits on a small stool next to the Sheriff, it's whirring and clicking the only sound in the room. On the desk sits a stack of forms - the "Adventuring Licenses."

**Sheriff Karr's Interview/Questionnaire:**

**(Sheriff Karr gestures for the party to sit, his gaze sweeping over them with a critical eye. He taps a stack of forms on his desk.)**

"Alright, newcomers. Welcome to Salvation. Before we get down to brass tacks, you got to understand somethin'. This here's the edge of the world, and beyond that edge lies the Mournland. Nothin' good ever came outta there, and probably nothin' good ever will. You planning on going out there, you need a license. House Tharashk rules, not mine, but I enforce 'em."

**(He slides the forms across the desk.)**

"Fill these out. And I'll ask you some questions. Answer straight, and we'll get along just fine. Lie to me, and well... Lemon here has a way of sniffin' out trouble, ain't that right, boy?"

**(He pats Lemon, who lets out a hiss of acidic fumes.)**

**Questions:**

1. **"Name and Alias:** First off, state your full name for the record. And if you got any aliases, nicknames, or somethin' you'd rather be called, spit it out. Helps to know who we're dealin' with, especially if you end up face down in a ditch somewhere."
2. **"Race and Origin:** What in the nine hells are you, and where did you crawl in from? Don't care if you're a gnome from the Feywild or a half-orc from the sewers, just so long as it's on the record."
3. **"Skills and Abilities:** (Gestures with his chin at the stack of forms) "Says here you lot fancy yourselves adventurers. Alright then, what makes you so special? Can you swing a sword, sling a spell, pick a lock, or are you just gonna stand there and bleed when things get rough? Be specific, and don't try to impress me, I've seen it all before." This is where they can state their class.
4. **"Reason for Visitin' Salvation:** This town ain't exactly a tourist destination. So, what brings you to this dusty corner of nowhere? Lookin' for work, runnin' from somethin', or just got a death wish?"
5. **"The Mournland's Call:** You ever been in the Mournland before? You have any intentions of headin' out there? And if so, why? Treasure? Glory? Somethin' else? Spill it. And don't think you can pull one over on me, I've heard every lie in the book."
6. **"Past Dealings:** You ever had any dealings with any of the big names around here? House Tharashk, House Cannith, the Order of the Broken Sky? Any other outfits I should know about? Don't hold back, now. Sooner or later, everything comes to light in Salvation."
7. **"The Price of Entry:** This license ain't free, you know. Five gold pieces per person. And that's just the start. You find anything out there, House Tharashk gets a hefty cut. Seventy-five percent to be exact. You got a problem with that?" (He'll wait for an answer, staring intently at the party, daring them to object. If they do object he will sigh, and say 'It's usually eighty')
8. **"One Last Thing:** You see anything out there, anything... unusual, you report it to me. Immediately. The Mournland's full of nasty surprises, and I aim to keep this town from being on the receiving end of one. Understood?"

**During the Interview:**

- **Deputy's Role:** The deputy (Dal or Bron, depending on your choice) will be observing the party closely, either taking notes or simply watching their reactions. They might interject with a question or a comment, depending on their personality.
- **Lemon's Presence:** Lemon, the acid-spitting Iron Defender, serves as a visual reminder of the potential dangers of the Mournland and the seriousness of the situation. Its low whirring and clicking should punctuate the silence.
- **Sheriff's Attitude:** Sheriff Karr will be gruff, direct, and somewhat intimidating. He'll be testing the party, trying to gauge their honesty, their competence, and their intentions. He might use his position to subtly threaten the party.
- **Player Reactions:** Encourage your players to roleplay their characters' reactions to the interview. Are they nervous, defiant, cooperative, or secretive? This is a great opportunity for them to establish their characters' personalities and motivations.

**After the Interview:**

- **License Granted:** Assuming the party fills out the forms, pays the fee, and doesn't do anything to seriously antagonize the Sheriff, they will be granted their "Adventuring Licenses." These documents are stamped with the House Tharashk seal and signed by Sheriff Karr.
- **Warnings:** Karr will likely reiterate the dangers of the Mournland and remind the party of their obligation to report any significant findings.
- **First Impressions:** This encounter sets the tone for the party's relationship with the law in Salvation and establishes the power dynamics of the town. It also provides the players with some initial information about the setting and hints at some of the larger forces at play.
