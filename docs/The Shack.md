**Tier 1: The Shack**

- **Description:** A small, dilapidated shack in a less desirable part of [[npcs]]. It has a leaky roof, a dirt floor, and offers minimal protection from the elements.
- **Starting Features:**
    - **Sleeping Area:** A few straw-filled pallets or bedrolls.
    - **Storage:** A single, small chest for storing personal items.
    - **Hearth:** A crude fire pit for cooking and warmth.
- **Upgrades:**
    - **Patch the Roof (Cost: 50 gp, requires basic materials):** Stops leaks and provides slightly better shelter.
    - **Basic Door (Cost: 25 gp, requires wood and hinges):** A simple wooden door for a modicum of security.
    - **Floorboards (Cost: 75 gp, requires lumber):** Raises the floor off the ground, making it more comfortable.
