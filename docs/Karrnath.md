#fantasticlocations 


- **Geography:** A nation in northeastern [[Khorvaire]], known for its harsh climate, its martial tradition, and its use of necromancy during the Last War. It borders the Mournland to the south. It's capital is Korth.
    - **Culture:** Karrnath is a militaristic and disciplined society, valuing strength, loyalty, and order. They are known for their powerful armies, their skilled tacticians, and their dark, gothic architecture. They are also known for their use of undead soldiers during the Last War, a practice that continues to this day, albeit on a smaller scale.
    - **Post-War:** Karrnath was severely weakened by the Last War, both economically and militarily. They are struggling to rebuild their strength and maintain their position in the new world order. They are ruled by King Kaius III, a powerful and enigmatic figure who is rumored to be a vampire.
    - **Connection to the Campaign:**
        - **Necromantic Interest:** Karrnath's history of necromancy might make them interested in the undead and the necromantic energies found in the Mournland, particularly the yellow Croydonite and its connection to the Conductor Husks.
        - **Military Presence:** Karrnath might have a military presence near the Mournland border, ostensibly to protect their territory but potentially also to conduct their own investigations into the wasteland.

        - **Questlines:** The party might be hired to investigate rumors of undead activity near the border, deal with Karrnathi patrols encroaching on Salvation's territory, or uncover the activities of the Order of the Emerald Claw in the Mournland.
