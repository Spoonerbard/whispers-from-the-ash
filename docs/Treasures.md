#rewards

**I. Mundane Treasures**

Even in a magical wasteland, mundane treasures still hold value, especially in a frontier town like Sanctuary.

- **Pre-Mourning Artifacts:**
    - **Intact Goods:** High-quality tools, finely crafted jewelry, preserved artwork, or other valuable items from before the Mourning. These items would be rare and highly sought after.
    - **Valuable Materials:** Untarnished gold, silver, or platinum, along with precious gemstones, would be valuable for trade or crafting.
    - **Historical Documents:** Intact books, scrolls, or maps from before the Mourning could provide valuable knowledge or clues to lost locations.
- **Mournland Resources:**
    - **Croydonite:** While dangerous, [[Croydonite]] itself is a valuable resource, especially in refined form.
    - **Mutated Flora:** Certain mutated plants might have unique properties useful for crafting potions, poisons, or other alchemical concoctions.
    - **Monster Parts:** Hides, claws, teeth, and other body parts from dangerous[[the mournland]] creatures could be valuable for crafting or as trophies.
    - **Rare Minerals:** The Mourning might have created or exposed rare and valuable minerals, useful for crafting or trade.

**II. Magical Treasures**

These are items imbued with the strange magic of [[the Mournland]]]], offering unique abilities but potentially carrying risks.

- **Weapons:**
    - **Croydonite Weapons:** Weapons forged with [[Croydonite]] might deal extra fire or necrotic damage, or have other unpredictable effects on a hit. They might also be unstable and dangerous to the wielder. (e.g., _Croydonite Dagger_: +1 dagger, deals an extra 1d4 fire damage on a hit. On a critical hit, the wielder takes 1d4 fire damage as well).
    - **Mourning-Touched Blade:** A weapon that survived the Mourning, now imbued with residual magic. It might have a unique enchantment related to the weapon's history or the manner in which it was used during the Mourning. (e.g., _Whispering Blade_: A longsword that whispers secrets of the dead to its wielder, granting advantage on History checks related to the Mourning).
    - **Living Weapon:** A weapon that has become partially sentient due to the Mourning's influence. It might have its own personality, desires, and even special abilities. (e.g., _Ravenous Axe_: A greataxe that craves battle and grants its wielder advantage on attack rolls when below half health, but it also whispers violent urges to the wielder).
- **Armor:**
    - **Croydonite-Studded Armor:** Armor reinforced with [[Croydonite]] shards, offering increased protection but potentially harming the wearer. (e.g., _Croydonite-Studded Leather_: +1 leather armor, grants resistance to fire damage, but the wearer takes 1 fire damage at the start of each turn).
    - **Mourning Shroud:** A cloak or suit of armor that blends into the shadows of [[the Mournland]], granting advantage on Stealth checks but potentially attracting unwanted attention from creatures of darkness.
    - **Shifting Armor:** Armor that adapts to the wearer's environment, changing its appearance and properties based on the surrounding terrain. (e.g., [[Wasteland Walker's Leathers]]: Grants advantage on Stealth checks in [[the Mournland]] and resistance to the dominant damage type of the current environment).
- **Wondrous Items:**
    - **Singing Stones:** Remnants from the Sky Song Tavern, these stones could be used to activate dormant magical effects or be repurposed for new enchantments.
    - **Distorted Lens:** A lens crafted from a mutated creature's eye, allowing the user to see through illusions or perceive the Ethereal Plane, but potentially causing madness with prolonged use.
    - **Mourning Locket:** A locket containing a fragment of a soul trapped in Croydonite. It might whisper secrets or grant the wearer limited communication with the dead, but at a cost to their sanity.
    - **Sky Song Ale (Residual):** Even a few drops of this potent brew could grant temporary benefits, but with unpredictable side effects due to its age and corruption.
    - **[[Troxler]]'s Early Experiments:** Failed or incomplete prototypes of magical items, offering strange and potentially dangerous abilities.
- **Cursed Items:**
    - **Haunted Helm:** A helmet that whispers terrifying secrets to the wearer, granting advantage on Intimidation checks but imposing disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws.
    - **Croydonite Amulet:** An amulet that grants the wearer enhanced strength but slowly corrupts their body and mind.
    - **Bag of Devouring:** A seemingly ordinary bag that consumes any non-living matter placed inside it, slowly growing larger over time.

**III. Wonders of [[the Mournland]]**

These are unique locations or phenomena that offer not just treasure but also knowledge, challenges, and opportunities for discovery.

- **The City of Lost Echoes:** A ruined city where the echoes of the past still linger. Players might witness spectral reenactments of the Mourning or interact with ghostly remnants of the city's inhabitants.
- **The Crystal Fields:** A vast expanse of land where the ground has been transformed into razor-sharp crystals. It's a dangerous but beautiful area, potentially containing valuable minerals or [[Croydonite]] deposits.
- **The Weeping Oasis:** A seemingly fertile oasis where the water is infused with the sorrow of the Mourning. Drinking the water might grant visions or insights but also inflict psychic damage or a curse.
- **The Twisted Forest:** A forest warped by the Mourning, where the trees are contorted into unnatural shapes and strange, mutated creatures roam. It might contain rare herbs or be a source of powerful, corrupted magic.
- **The Monolith of Silence:** A towering structure of black stone that absorbs all sound, creating an eerie zone of silence. It could be a place of power, a prison for a dangerous entity, or a gateway to another dimension.
- **The Shifting Citadel:** A fortress that phases in and out of reality, appearing in different locations within [[the Mournland]]. It could be home to a powerful creature, a repository of lost knowledge, or a trap for the unwary.

**IV. Delivering the Rewards**

- **Hidden Caches:** Treasures could be hidden in ruined buildings, buried underground, or concealed within natural formations.
- **Guarded Locations:** Dangerous creatures or traps might protect valuable items or locations.
- **Puzzles and Challenges:** Players might need to solve puzzles, decipher riddles, or overcome skill challenges to access certain treasures.
- **Reputation and Renown:** Discovering lost wonders or recovering powerful artifacts could increase the party's reputation in Sanctuary and beyond, opening up new opportunities or attracting the attention of powerful factions.
- **Troxler's Expertise:** Troxler can help identify, appraise, and potentially refine or repurpose magical items found in [[the Mournland]]. He might be the only one who can unlock the secrets of certain artifacts or stabilize volatile [[Croydonite]] creations.

**V. Examples of Treasure Distribution**

- **Early Game (Tier 1):** Focus on smaller rewards, such as pre-Mourning artifacts, valuable materials, and low-level magical items. The Sky Song Tavern could contain some of these treasures, along with clues to more significant finds.
- **Mid Game (Tiers 2-3):** Introduce more powerful magical items, unique resources like refined Croydonite, and access to larger wonders like the City of Lost Echoes or the Twisted Forest.
- **Late Game (Tier 4):** Offer legendary artifacts, the potential to reshape [[the Mournland]], and encounters with powerful entities connected to the Mourning.
