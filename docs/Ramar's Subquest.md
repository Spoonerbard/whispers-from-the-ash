# **Subquest: The Corrupted Warden**

**The Bounty:**

- **Name:** [[Roric Ashthorn]]
- **Background:** A former member of the Wardens of the Wood, a druidic sect dedicated to protecting the natural world. Roric was once a respected Warden, known for his skill in tracking and his deep connection to nature. However, he became obsessed with the Mourning, believing that it held a hidden power that could be used to restore the balance of nature, but in a more forceful, dominant way.
- **Crime:** Roric has gone rogue, abandoning the Wardens and embracing a twisted interpretation of their teachings. He's been implicated in several attacks on settlements near [[the Mournland]]], using corrupted nature magic to destroy outposts and terrorize civilians. He believes that civilization is a blight that needs to be cleansed, and that [[the Mournland]] is the key to a new, more "natural" order.
- **Motivation:** Roric seeks to harness the power of [[the Mournland]]], specifically Croydonite, to amplify his druidic magic and spread his vision of a world dominated by nature, even if it means embracing the corruption of the Mourning.
- **House Deneith Connection:** House Deneith wants Roric captured alive. They see him as a dangerous extremist who threatens the stability of the region and potentially gives all druids a bad name. They also want to study his corrupted magic, hoping to find a way to counter it or perhaps even weaponize it.
- **The player character is given two objectives:** Capture Roric and bring him back alive. Discover what you can about his research.

**[[the Mournland]]] Connection:**

- **Croydonite Experiments:** Roric has fled into [[the Mournland]] to continue his experiments with Croydonite. He believes that the shattered souls within the material can be manipulated to enhance his druidic powers, allowing him to control and reshape the natural world on a larger scale. He's set up a hidden base of operations in one of the more dangerous areas of [[the Mournland]]. Perhaps in the Twisted Forest or Weeping Mire.
- **Twisted Creations:** Roric's experiments have resulted in the creation of twisted, corrupted versions of natural creatures and plants. These could be used as guardians or obstacles for the party to overcome. Examples include:
    - **Blights made from Croydonite:** They might have additional abilities, such as exploding into shards of [[Croydonite]] upon death or draining the life force of those they touch.
    - **Animated Trees infused with souls:** These trees could be incredibly strong and resistant to damage, fueled by the tormented souls within the Croydonite.
    - **Corrupted Animals:** Animals that have been exposed to Roric's magic might be larger, more aggressive, and possess unnatural abilities.
- **The Lure:** Roric might be intentionally leaving a trail of corrupted nature magic, hoping to attract other druids or creatures sensitive to the natural world, either to recruit them to his cause or to use them as test subjects. He could have set up a signal to draw in other druids. Perhaps a large tree, similar to the one created by the Seed of the Mourning Treant, but twisted and corrupted, that emits a powerful, alluring psychic call that only druids can hear.

**Tying into Wider Themes:**

- **Nature vs. Corruption:** Roric's story highlights the dangers of unchecked ambition and the corrupting influence of [[the Mournland]]. He represents a dark mirror of the druid's connection to nature, showing how that connection can be twisted and perverted.
- **The Ethics of Croydonite:** Roric's experiments with [[Croydonite]] force the druid character (and the party) to confront the ethical implications of using this material. Is Roric's vision of a "cleansed" world worth the cost of exploiting the shattered souls?
- **House Deneith's Role:** The bounty on Roric's head raises questions about House Deneith's own motivations. Are they truly concerned about the balance of nature, or are they simply trying to eliminate a threat to their authority and potentially acquire a new weapon?
- **Redemption and Choice:** The quest could offer the possibility of redeeming Roric, though it would be extremely difficult. This would force the druid character to make a difficult choice: bring Roric to justice as their House demands, or try to save him from the darkness he has embraced?
- **The Order of the Broken Sky:** This faction might also be interested in Roric's research, seeing it as a potential key to understanding the Mourning. They might try to intervene, either to capture Roric for themselves or to eliminate him before he can reveal his secrets to others.

**Possible Encounters:**

- **The Corrupted Grove:** The party discovers a grove that Roric has twisted into a nightmarish reflection of a natural sanctuary. The trees are animated and hostile, the plants are poisonous, and the air is thick with corrupting energy.
- **The Twisted Guardian:** Roric has created a powerful, corrupted elemental or a mutated beast to guard his base of operations. This could be a challenging boss encounter that requires the party to use their skills and knowledge of nature to overcome.
- **The Captured Wardens:** Roric might have captured other members of the Wardens of the Wood, either to interrogate them for information or to use them in his experiments. The party could have the opportunity to rescue them.
- **The Confrontation with Roric:** The final encounter with Roric could take place in his hidden base, a place where he is at his most powerful. He might try to persuade the party to join him, or he might unleash the full force of his corrupted magic against them.

**Rewards:**

- **Bounty:** House Deneith will pay a substantial reward for Roric's capture (or proof of his death).
- **Knowledge:** The party can learn a great deal about Roric's research, the dangers of Croydonite, and the potential for manipulating the Mourning's magic. They might even gain insights that help [[Troxler]] in his research back in [[npcs]].
- **Magical Items:** Roric's base might contain unique magical items, corrupted by his experiments, or even a powerful artifact he is using to channel the Mourning's energy.
- **Reputation:** Completing this quest will increase the party's reputation with House Deneith and potentially with other druidic circles.
- **Moral Choice:** The way the party handles Roric – capture, redemption, or death – will have lasting consequences and shape their relationship with House Deneith, the Wardens of the Wood, and potentially other factions.

**Roric Ashthorn, The Corrupted Warden**

**Personal Details:**

- **Appearance:** Roric was once a tall, powerfully built human, but the [[Croydonite]] has warped his body. His skin has taken on a sickly greenish-grey hue, and veins of black, crystalline [[Croydonite]] are visible beneath the surface, pulsing with a faint, internal glow. One of his arms has been replaced with a monstrous limb made of twisted wood and sharpened [[Croydonite]] shards. His eyes glow with an unnatural green light, and his hair is a tangled mass of thorny vines. He wears the tattered remnants of his Wardens of the Wood uniform, now adorned with [[Croydonite]] shards and twisted pieces of metal.
- **Personality:** Roric is utterly convinced of the righteousness of his cause. He sees himself as a savior, the one who will cleanse the world of civilization's blight and restore the natural order through the power of [[the Mournland]]. He is fanatical, arrogant, and utterly ruthless in the pursuit of his goals. He views those who oppose him as enemies of nature itself, deserving of no mercy.
- **Motivation:** To harness the power of [[the Mournland]], particularly Croydonite, to reshape the world according to his twisted vision of natural order. He believes that he is the chosen agent of this new order, a prophet of the "true" nature that the Mourning has revealed.
- **আদর্শ:** "The world must be cleansed. Civilization is a disease, and the Mourning is the cure."
- **Bond:** "[[the Mournland]] speaks to me. It guides my hand. I am its instrument."
- **Flaw:** "I am blind to the suffering I cause. My vision justifies all means."
- **Voice:** Roric's voice is raspy and distorted, as if his vocal cords are strained. When he speaks of his vision, his voice takes on a fervent, almost religious tone. He often intersperses his speech with quotes from what he believes to be the "true" teachings of the Wardens of the Wood, twisted to fit his own ideology.

**Tactics:**

- **Spellcasting:** Roric will use his spells to control the battlefield (_entangle_, _spike growth_), enhance himself (_barkskin_, _stoneskin_), and deal damage (_blight_, _call lightning_).
- **Croydonite Claw:** He'll use his [[Croydonite]] Claw to inflict heavy damage, especially to targets he's pulled close with Thorn Whip.
- **Call of the Mourning:** He'll use this ability to damage groups of enemies or to clear an area of vegetation.
- **Legendary Actions:** Roric will use his legendary actions to reposition, make extra attacks, cast spells in the middle of the fight, and debuff his enemies.
- **Hit and Run:** If possible, Roric will use _tree stride_ or _meld into stone_ to reposition and avoid taking too much damage. He will use his mobility to his advantage.
- **Exploit the Environment:** Roric will use the terrain to his advantage, using his spells and abilities to create difficult terrain, block line of sight, or trap his enemies.

**Weaknesses:**

- **Psychic Vulnerability:** The [[Croydonite]] infusion makes Roric vulnerable to psychic damage.
- **Limited Melee Attacks:** He relies heavily on his [[Croydonite]] Claw for melee damage. If he's disarmed or prevented from using it, he's less effective in close combat.
- **Fanaticism:** Roric's fanaticism can be exploited. Clever players might be able to use his beliefs against him, perhaps by tricking him into believing they share his vision or by challenging the logic of his corrupted ideology.

**Roleplaying Roric:**

- **Emphasize his conviction:** Roric truly believes he is doing what's right for the world, even if his methods are horrific.
- **Use corrupted nature imagery:** Describe his spells and abilities with vivid, disturbing imagery that reflects the twisted nature of [[the Mournland]].
- **Show his arrogance:** Roric sees himself as superior to those who cling to civilization. He might mock the party for their weakness or their ignorance of the "true" natural order.
- **Offer a chance at redemption:** Even though he's a villain, consider offering a slim chance for Roric to be redeemed. Perhaps the party can appeal to his former ideals or show him the error of his ways. However, make it clear that redemption will be difficult and require significant effort on Roric's part.
