# Home Base Upgrades

As the party grows in power and resources, they can upgrade their makeshift base of operations to make it more secure, comfortable, and functional.

## Upgrades

| Upgrade | Cost (gp) | Parts Required | Completion Time (days) | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reinforced Doors | 50 | 10 iron ingots, 10 wooden planks | 3 | Reinforces the base's doors with iron to make them more resistant to damage. |
| Barricades | 100 | 20 wooden planks, 10 iron bars | 5 | Creates barricades to block windows and doors, making it harder for enemies to enter. |
| Security Post | 200 | 10 iron ingots, 20 wooden planks, 10 torches | 7 | Creates a security post near the entrance with a raised platform and torches to increase visibility. |
| Watchtower | 300 | 20 iron ingots, 30 wooden planks, 10 ropes | 10 | Builds a watchtower to increase visibility and provide a vantage point for guards. |
| Medical Station | 150 | 10 medical supplies, 5 wooden crates | 5 | Creates a medical station with basic medical supplies and a place for the party to rest. |
| Storage Room | 100 | 10 wooden crates, 5 iron bars | 3 | Creates a storage room for the party to store their gear and supplies. |
| Kitchen | 200 | 10 cooking utensils, 5 food crates | 5 | Creates a kitchen with basic cooking utensils and a place to store food. |
| Latrine | 50 | 10 wooden planks, 5 water bottles | 2 | Creates a latrine to improve sanitation and hygiene. |
| Training Area | 150 | 10 wooden dummies, 5 ropes | 5 | Creates a training area with wooden dummies and ropes to practice combat skills. |
| Library | 200 | 10 books, 5 writing utensils | 5 | Creates a library with basic books and writing utensils for research and study. |
| Armorer | 300 | 10 iron ingots, 20 wooden planks, 10 tools | 10 | Creates an armorer with basic tools and materials to craft and repair armor. |
| Alchemist's Lab | 400 | 10 alchemy supplies, 20 glass vials | 15 | Creates an alchemist's lab with basic supplies and equipment to brew potions. |

## Notes

* The costs listed are for the materials only and do not include labor costs.
* The parts required are the minimum needed to complete the upgrade and can be adjusted based on the party's needs and resources.
* The completion time is an estimate and can vary based on the party's skills and the complexity of the upgrade.
* The upgrades can be combined to create a more comprehensive base of operations. For example, the party could build a reinforced door and add a security post to increase the base's security.
